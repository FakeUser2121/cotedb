      Psychology & competing readings

      • She is Ayanokouji's sole witness-partner: the only one who has seen both sides and stayed (confidence 0.8)
        • Evidence: y1v4.5 p.160 · y1v7.5 p.161 · y1v7.5 p.170
        • Reading: Corroborated by his own claim-voice ('Kei was the person I'd grown closest to'). The open question the corpus leaves standing is her own charge — 'you only view people as tools, don't you, Kiyotaka?' — which the volumes park without answering.
      • The 'new me' is a deliberately manufactured identity — the school was chosen as a site of anonymity (confidence 0.8)
        • Evidence: y1v4.5 p.158 · y1v4 p.200
        • Reading: Stated almost verbatim ('No one here knows about my past…'). Her 'grateful to the people who bullied me, and the school that covered it up' reads as bitterness, not literal gratitude — the cover-up is what she needed, not what she approves of.
      • 'For my own sake' is armour: self-interest as the declared motive keeps the partnership honest (confidence 0.75)
        • Evidence: y1v4 p.222 · y1v7 p.190
        • Reading: She uses the identical formula at her two most exposed moments. Competing: her actual behavior contradicts pure self-interest — saving, promising, waiting ('I'll save you', 'you'll protect me') — so the formula functions as self-permission more than doctrine.
      • Love grew out of the fake relationship — the script expired before the feelings did (confidence 0.75)
        • Evidence: y1v7.5 p.164 · y1v9 p.130 · y1v11.75 p.11
        • Reading: Hesitation to end the 'pretend relationship', the courier-valentine, the girlfriend-voiced wish — one trajectory. Competing: the feelings predate the script (first-name 'Kiyotaka' experiments in y1v5 sit on the borderline).
      • The year-2 break is handled as mutual protection, not rupture — silence chosen for both sides (confidence 0.55)
        • Evidence: y2v12 p.22 · y3v1 p.166
        • Reading: 'I won't say anything more… I don't need a detailed report' plus 'holding back the truth for my sake' — restraint as the surviving mode. Low confidence: the corpus here is thin and the motives behind the separation are asserted by others more than by her.
