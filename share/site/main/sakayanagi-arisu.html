      • Knowledge state: Already fluent in adult doctrine — she argues policy in a child's voice.
      • Changes: 'Hundreds of children are abandoned every year… we should welcome' political action; 'this is also a form of education' — selection as a moral good, stated before she can vote.
      • Why it matters: The root text of everything she later becomes at school.

      • Evidence: y1v5 p.202 · y1v5 p.203

      Psychology & competing readings

      • Her weapon is held secrets: she cold-reads everyone's hidden shame and waits (confidence 0.85)
        • Evidence: y1v9 p.91 · y1v7.5 p.42 · y1v7.5 p.114
        • Reading: Kamuro's shoplifting, Ichinose's opened heart, Masumi's identical problem — one method, three targets. Competing: she is not all-seeing — Hashimoto's betrayal and Ryuuen's traitor rights both surprised her (y2v12 p.197).
      • Her withdrawal was a chosen move, not a suffered defeat — she accepted the loss to honor Ayanokouji's wish (confidence 0.8)
        • Evidence: y2v12.5 p.34 · y2v12.5 p.36 · y2v12.5 p.35
        • Reading: Two readings live side by side: Ryuuen's — 'you self-destructed because of that message' — and hers ('my strength was insufficient… I accepted defeat knowing the content'). Her own 'setting the groundwork' (p.97) hints at further motives. The corpus supports chosen-move as primary with strategic aftertaste.
      • She treats rivalry as courtship — Ayanokouji is the opponent she wants to be seen by (confidence 0.8)
        • Evidence: y1v11 p.433 · y2v9.5 p.110 · y2v12.5 p.97
        • Reading: Face-off satisfaction, formal enemy-declaration, and the reunion wish form one line. The counter-evidence is her own pain when he doesn't want her as opponent (y2v12 p.216 'even hated this thought process') — the courtship reads real precisely because it can wound.
      • Competence expressed as play — she handicaps, torments, and teases rather than simply crushing (confidence 0.75)
        • Evidence: y1v7.5 p.37 · y1v7 p.115 · y2v12 p.199
        • Reading: Boredom-motivated play is her stated mode; even at checkmate she feels 'parental emotion' toward Ryuuen. Competing: the play is a luxury that collapses the moment the stakes stop amusing her (y2v11's flat wager line).
      • She is her father's doctrine made flesh — elite selection as moral good, inherited not rebelled against (confidence 0.6)
        • Evidence: y0 p.38 · y0 p.87 · y1v7 p.69
        • Reading: Childhood and adulthood repeat the same voice (facility tour → admissions doctrine at the party). Competing reading: her final wishes — protecting the betrayer, promising reunion — are personal, not doctrinal, suggesting the inheritance is manners more than belief; corpus cannot fully settle it.
