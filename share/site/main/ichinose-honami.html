      Arc

      y1v2

      • Situation: Early friendship arc: she mishandles a classmate's crush and owns it immediately.
      • Knowledge state: Still just the popular, warm Class B girl — no strategic mask yet visible.
      • Changes: 'I didn't respect Chihiro-chan's feelings. I just wanted to avoid hurting her... That was my mistake' — honesty as first instinct, stated as policy before any exam pressure exists.
      • Why it matters: Establishes the baseline the later deceptions are measured against.
      • Evidence: y1v2 p.127 · y1v2 p.152

      y1v4

      • Situation: Cruise VIP exam: she chairs the mixed-class group, runs trust-building card games, and weighs an alliance with Ryuuen.
      • Knowledge state: Understands the test's math better than most; privately declares her real endgame.
      • Changes: Refuses Ryuuen outright ('you've hurt people in Class B... I can't ally with you'), and tells Ayanokouji 'I want to graduate from Class A. I have a dream I want to make real.'
      • Why it matters: First proof her sweetness is paired with operational sharpness — she reads faction play (Morishige) and phone-swap fraud unprompted.
      • Evidence: y1v4 p.149 · y1v4 p.215 · y1v4 p.233

      y1v6

      • Situation: Ryuuen circulates a forged letter accusing her of fraud.
      • Knowledge state: The accusation is false but designed to detonate her trust-based class.
      • Changes: Meets it with calm refusal to be dragged down — 'I'll report to the teachers tomorrow and prove that this is just a misunderstanding.'
      • Why it matters: Her anti-crisis style: transparency instead of counter-intrigue.
      • Evidence: y1v6 p.171 · y1v6 p.174

      y1v11.5

      • Situation: Class B's points collapse under her leadership; rain, confession of exhaustion to Ayanokouji.
      • Knowledge state: Blames herself alone for the arithmetic of the loss.
      • Changes: 'The only miscalculation was me as the leader. If I took better action, Class B would definitely have more points than now' — plus 'I'm always showing you my weak sides… I'm so lame.'
      • Why it matters: The low point of the first-year arc: guilt, not ambition, is her failure mode.
      • Evidence: y1v11.5 p.223 · y1v11.5 p.230

      y2v4

      • Situation: Her feelings for Ayanokouji surface in speech.
      • Knowledge state: She knows what saying it costs.
      • Changes: The bare confession — '…..I like… you…' — turns the admired strategist into a person with a declared want.
      • Why it matters: Sets up the renunciation that follows; she never uses the feelings as leverage.
      • Evidence: y2v4 p.200

      y2v9

      • Situation: Private meetings with Ayanokouji end under the pressure of her class's standing.
      • Knowledge state: She chooses clarity over comfort.
      • Changes: 'We won't see each other in private anymore… Because it would hurt my heart… I couldn't remain in the dark. I had to change from the ground up.'
      • Why it matters: The break is self-imposed discipline — feelings acknowledged, then fenced.
      • Evidence: y2v9 p.174 · y2v9 p.175

      y2v12.5

      • Situation: Aftermath: her unwitting help got Maezono expelled; class doctrine is being restated.
      • Knowledge state: Guilt over the expulsion collides with the class's no-expulsion ideal.
      • Changes: Owns it ('greatly and intensely disturbed'), then declares openly: 'I certainly respect him. But more than that, I like Ayanokouji-kun' — and defends the ideal ('We have fought so far without expelling a single person… sometimes, these idealisms do bear fruit').
      • Why it matters: The mature synthesis: feelings stated without leverage, idealism restated with its price acknowledged.
      • Evidence: y2v12.5 p.15 · y2v12.5 p.187 · y2v12.5 p.189

      y3v1

      • Situation: Now trading trust as equals with Ayanokouji's camp.
      • Knowledge state: Steady while admitting pressure.
      • Changes: 'Just like Ayanokouji-kun trusts me, I trust him too' — verbal promises, no contract; 'I'm just barely holding up against the pressure' said without pretending otherwise.
      • Why it matters: Closes the loop opened in y1v2: honesty scaled to the weight of year 3.
      • Evidence: y3v1 p.60 · y3v1 p.165

      Psychology & competing readings

      • Guilt and self-blame are her failure mode — she collapses inward, never outward into blame of others (confidence 0.85)
        • Evidence: y1v11.5 p.230 · y2v12.5 p.15 · y1v2 p.127
        • Reading: Three separate crises, identical reaction shape. No serious competing reading in the corpus.
      • Her bond with Ayanokouji moves confession → self-imposed renunciation → equal trust, never leverage (confidence 0.8)
        • Evidence: y2v4 p.200 · y2v9 p.174 · y3v1 p.60
        • Reading: Distinct from every other romance line in the corpus: she ends the private contact herself. Competing: y2v12.5 p.172 ('engrave my existence into your heart') reads possessive on the surface — the dossier treats it as declared intent with restraint as track record.
      • Her kindness is a governing strategy, not naivety — transparency is how she wins (confidence 0.8)
        • Evidence: y1v4 p.151 · y2v4.5 p.151 · y1v11 p.327
        • Reading: She openly states class-first motives ('Everything I do is for the sake of Class B') and runs open-book point funds. Competing reading: y1v4 p.149 suggests she still plays some performance — even she says 'he saw through me' — so the strategy is layered, not pure.
      • The no-expulsion ideal is a real, priced constraint on her class, not a slogan (confidence 0.75)
        • Evidence: y2v12.5 p.189 · y2v12.5 p.15
        • Reading: The corpus shows the price directly: lost class points and the Maezono guilt. Competing: her own self-blame (y1v11.5) implies she sometimes reads the ideal as her personal failing rather than a chosen trade-off.
      • Her cheerful persona is the genuine baseline, not a Kushida-style mask (confidence 0.7)
        • Evidence: y1v6 p.115 · y3v1 p.165 · y1v2 p.127
        • Reading: She shows weakness to others unprompted and admits limits ('I'm really not so wonderful') — the inverse of concealment. Competing: her strategic depths (y1v4 SIM-swap analysis) show the light voice carries heavy equipment; two layers exist, but only one is hidden.

      Relationships

      • Kanzaki Ryuuji — class-b-friction [from y1v4.5]: Class B's ideological splitter: she notes his lack of assertiveness while he pushes harder line than her trust-first doctrine allows.
        • Evidence: y1v4.5 p.219 · y1v11.5 p.197
      • Ayanokouji Kiyotaka — confidant-then-trust [from y1v4]: Told him her Class-A dream (y1v4), confessed (y2v4), cut private contact herself (y2v9), and rebuilt it as declared mutual trust (y3v1).
        • Evidence: y1v4 p.215 · y2v4 p.200 · y3v1 p.60
      • Ryuuen Kakeru — principled-refusal [from y1v4]: Refused his alliance over Class B harm (y1v4) and met his forged fraud letter with calm counter-proof (y1v6) — the moral line he never crosses, she never abandons.
        • Evidence: y1v4 p.149 · y1v6 p.171
      • Horikita Suzune — respectful-peer [from y1v4]: Card-table ally of the VIP exam; later class-leader to class-leader — closeness grew by observation, not scheming.
        • Evidence: y1v4 p.146 · y2v12 p.133
      • Karuizawa Kei — supportive-ally [from y2v9.5]: Offered open support ('I want to support you and Karuizawa-san') — bridge figure across classes without strings.
        • Evidence: y2v9.5 p.38
      • Kushida Kikyou — warm-peripheral [from y1v4.5]: Honorami-chan social ease — Kushida is one of the few across classes who uses her first name; no entanglement with the double life.
        • Evidence: y1v4.5 p.218 · y1v6 p.118
