      Student Council

      governance

      1259 mentions · 29 vols · 3 claims

      Function

      Student self-government: discipline summonses, budget oversight of events, and the annual presidential election.

      Notes

      Manabu Horikita's presidency opens the series (y1v1 p.64-67); the mid-October election ceremony swears in Nagumo Miyabi as next president (y1v6 p.14), with the Horikita-Nagumo race recalled at y1v4.5 p.180. Suzune joins the council in the second year (y2v2 p.350) and the council's budget hands out festival service funds (y2v7 p.7-9); Nagumo-era entanglements peak in y2v9. The council room doubles as the pressure venue (see student-council-room location). 1189 mentions / 28 vols.
