      Mixed Training Camp

      special

      13 mentions · 7 vols · 2 runs · 0 claims

      Rules

      Autumn third-semester opener: the whole school boards buses for a joint road-trip camp — first-, second- and third-years together (announce chapter y1v8 p.11, named 'Mixed Camp' at p.14). Students travel in jerseys with designated seats and no advance briefing on the tasks.

      Points

      Point effects are narrated through the camp chapters rather than a posted results board; the Y2 rerun was explicitly scaled down (y2v11 p.53: 'compared to last year's mixed training camp, this one is a significant step down').

      Conditions

      Participation is compulsory for the entire year group on the bus; format differences between the Y1 and Y2 runs are announced beforehand (y2v11 p.34).

      Organizer

      School-run camp special across all three grades.

      Notes

      13 mentions / 7 vols (y1v8-y2v11). The Y1 run is the first mixed-grade exam of the series; the Y2 announcement stresses the changed nature of the event (y2v11 p.34, p.53). Thin outcomes: no results board appears on mention pages.

      Footprint

      • Span: y1v8 → y2v11
      • Anchored mentions by vol: y1v8 ×5, y1v10 ×1, y2v2 ×2, y2v4.5 ×1, y2v9 ×1, y2v9.5 ×1, y2v11 ×2

      • Participants: All three year groups mixed into inter-class teams; first-year classes under their homeroom teachers.
