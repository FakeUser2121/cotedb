      Unanimous Vote Exam

      special

      90 mentions · 11 vols · 1 runs · 2 claims

      Rules

      Announced same-day for the next day: 'a series of multiple-choice votes which you will have to repeat as a class until you reach a unanimous decision' (y2v5 p.31), with the full overview at y2v5 p.35-41. Decision topics include whether to expel a member (any single no vetoes) and the school-trip destination ballot.

      Points

      Stakes are large: failure modes put 350 class points on the line in the protection vote (y2v5 p.187), and defeated classes in the exam family forfeit protection points (y1v11 p.318).

      Conditions

      Unanimity is the hard gate — the class cannot expel without every yes, and the trip ballot runs in rounds (Okinawa 19 / Hokkaido 17 / Kyoto 3 first round, y2v5 p.91).

      Organizer

      School-run special exam; classes stage the votes internally, the school tallies them.

      Notes

      89 mentions / 10 vols (y2v5-y3v1). The exam that nearly expelled Airi Sakura — Kushida pushed her out, the votes failed to unify (y2v5 p.237) — and the exam whose ballot picked Hokkaido for the trip (y2v8 p.17).

      Footprint

      • Span: y2v5 → y3v2
      • Anchored mentions by vol: y2v5 ×39, y2v7 ×18, y2v8 ×8, y2v9 ×2, y2v9.5 ×4, y2v10 ×8, y2v11 ×2, y2v12 ×2, y2v12.5 ×4, y3v1 ×2, y3v2 ×1

      Runs

      y2v5 p.31 (run #1, conf 0.95)

      • Chronology: Second-year spring: the Unanimous Vote Exam is announced and its rules board goes up in class (y2v5 p.31, p.41).
      • Participants: All classmates must vote; Hirata Yōsuke (pushes for unanimity); Horikita Suzune; Sakura Airi (the proposed sacrifice, y2v5 p.237).
      • Outcome: Five questions, up to four options each, repeated until unanimous, timeout voids the question (y2v5 p.41); the class avoids expelling Airi (y2v5 p.237) while weighing a 350-class-point loss (y2v5 p.187); the later Hokkaido ballot reuses the mechanism (y2v8 p.17).
      • Evidence: “he is showing it more than ever. “You’ll be taking part in what’s known as the ‘Unanimous Voting Exam’.” The monitors light up and the explanation begins with customary visuals”
      • Refs: ["y2v5 p.31", "y2v5 p.35", "y2v5 p.41", "y2v5 p.237", "y2v8 p.17"]

      Claims

      • “The Unanimous Voting Exam runs within each class only — five school questions of up to four options, repeated until the class votes unanimously, with a timeout voiding the question.” (y2v5 p.31) — character_claim, chabashira-sae, conf 0.95
        • evidence: “he is showing it more than ever. “You’ll be taking part in what’s known as the ‘Unanimous Voting Exam’.” The monitors light up and the explanation begins with customary visuals”
        • Verbal announce and written overview agree (y2v5 p.31, p.41).
      • “The winter school-trip destination was chosen by a three-way class ballot (Hokkaido, Kyoto, Okinawa); Hokkaido took three of the class votes.” (y2v8 p.17) — narrator_fact, narrator, conf 0.95
        • evidence: “would be going on a school trip, but we still don't know where we're going. The Unanimous Vote Special Exam offered us three choices. Hokkaido, Kyoto, and Okinawa. Each class”
        • Chabashira reads the same result in scene (y2v8 p.17).
