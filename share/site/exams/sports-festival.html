      Sports Festival

      event

      327 mentions · 19 vols · 2 runs · 1 claims

      Rules

      Annual autumn field event. Y1: physical ability week announced for October (y1v5 p.13) with individual and team scoring. Y2: 'Some rules will differ this time around, but the base concept is still the same' (announce y2v5 p.13; rules y2v6 p.30).

      Points

      Class results move the standings — after the Y2 contests Ryuuen-side classes had won both their matches and sat 100 class points ahead (y2v7 p.29). Private-point side stakes: MVP of each grade earns 100,000 private points (y1v5 p.19) and roster substitutes cost 100,000 per competition (y1v5 p.23).

      Conditions

      Roster rules bill substitutes per competition (y1v5 p.23); the event is compulsory class competition.

      Organizer

      School-run PE event; homeroom and PE staff coordinate.

      Notes

      326 mentions / 18 vols — the highest-count event entity, dense across y1v5 and y2v6. Announced together with the cultural festival each year (y2v5 p.13 covers both slots).
