      Event Selection Exam

      special

      14 mentions · 2 vols · 1 runs · 1 claims

      Rules

      Year-one final special: classes show cumulative ability — knowledge, physical skill, cooperation and luck — with opponents decided 'similar to how it was during the Paper Shuffle exam' (announce y1v11 p.28); schedule set at y1v11 p.33.

      Points

      Runs as the closing exam of the first year (final-special slot from March 8, y1v10 p.31); class outcomes feed the year-end standings discussed in the y1v11.5 aftermath.

      Conditions

      Expulsion stakes from the class-vote era hang over it — after Yamauchi's expulsion students fear being 'next in line' (y1v11 p.28).

      Organizer

      School-run final special of the academic year, announced by Chabashira.

      Notes

      14 mentions / 2 vols only, but narratively it is Year 1's end-of-year special: y1v11.5 calls it 'the final examination of our 1st year, the event selection exam' (p.145), and the y1v11.5 'end-of-year exam' retrospectives (p.19, p.343) refer back to it — those y1v11.5 'end-of-year special' mentions belong to this event, not to the Y2 special.

      Footprint

      • Span: y1v11 → y1v11.5
      • Anchored mentions by vol: y1v11 ×9, y1v11.5 ×5

      Runs

      y1v11 p.28 (run #1, conf 0.95)

      • Chronology: The final special of the first year: announced March 8 with matchups set, event selection locked March 15, exam day March 22 (y1v11 p.33).
      • Participants: First-year classes paired by Paper-Shuffle-style rules (y1v11 p.28); Chabashira Sae (explains); Horikita Suzune (clarifies the bluff mechanic).
      • Outcome: Each class narrows the opponent's ten events to five submissions, five of them bluffs (y1v11 p.33); the conclusion is recalled from the aftermath volume (y1v11.5 p.34, p.145).
      • Evidence: “e may very well be next in line for expulsion. “The special exam is called the ‘Event Selection Exam’, an exam where each class will compete in terms of their comprehensive ab”
      • Refs: ["y1v11 p.28", "y1v11 p.33", "y1v11.5 p.34", "y1v11.5 p.145"]

      Claims

      • “The first year's final special is the Event Selection Exam: matchups are decided like the Paper Shuffle and each class submits five of the opponent's ten events.” (y1v11 p.28) — character_claim, chabashira-sae, conf 0.9
        • evidence: “e may very well be next in line for expulsion. “The special exam is called the ‘Event Selection Exam’, an exam where each class will compete in terms of their comprehensive ab”
        • Naming and matchup rule at y1v11 p.28; the submission rule and timeline at y1v11 p.33, where Horikita adds that five of the ten events are bluffs.
