      Uninhabited Island

      exam-venue · generated view (source: cote_kb.db; regenerate with python3 kb/build_world_dossiers.py)

      367 mentions · 27 vols · 92 co-paged scenes · 1 claims

      Function

      Tropical resort island used for first-year special exams (base camp, lodge, survival zones).

      Notes

      First-year introduction (y1v3): students boat in for a one-week stay at a summer lodge, then cruise onward; the base camp splits into class zones with school-issued safety watches (y1v3 p.30). MTL drift: 'deserted island' (y1v3 p.14) and 'uninhabited island' (y1v3 p.26) are the same place; corpus later keeps referencing it as the template exam venue (367 mentions, 27 vols through y3v1).
