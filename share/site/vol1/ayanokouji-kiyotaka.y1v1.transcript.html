      Ayanokōji Kiyotaka — Year 1, Volume 1 scene transcript

      Scene by scene: the speech lines, narration beats, and inner monologue of Year 1, Volume 1, quoted verbatim with page numbers (e.g. p.247).

      Year 1, Volume 1, p.14–15 — Opening monologue: equality is a lie (Ch.1)

      • [p.14] Ayanokōji Kiyotaka said: “No, we are not equal.”
      • [p.14] Ayanokōji (inner) mused: To be human is to be unequal. Equality does not exist. (narrated thesis, immediately after the quoted line)
      • [p.14] Ayanokōji Kiyotaka said: “So then, education creates an imbalance.” (reading of Gakumon no Sume)
      • [p.14] Ayanokōji (inner) mused: doubts public equality doctrine; abilities differ → roles differ
      • [p.15] Ayanokōji (inner) mused: “Have you ever given serious thought to the future?” — reader address
      • [p.15] Ayanokōji (narrated) narrated: finished compulsory education, became high-schooler; “only felt joy at being nearly free of obligation”; had not considered future/influence of school

      Year 1, Volume 1, p.16–18 — Horikita's coercion in class (Ch.2 open, present)

      • [p.16] Horikita Suzune said: “Ayanokouji-kun, do you have a moment?”
      • [p.16] Horikita Suzune said: “If you do not awaken within three seconds, I will bestow additional punishment upon you.”
      • [p.16] Ayanokōji Kiyotaka said: “What do you mean, 'punishment'?”
      • [p.17] Ayanokōji Kiyotaka said: “That's nothing but harassment, or rather, bullying! And besides, that last one sounds strangely familiar, because you've already stabbed me before!”
      • [p.16] Ayanokōji (inner) mused: “She came. She was here. It was terrifying.” — devil/Shostakovich Symphony No.11 imagery; she is “the devil” seeking “her slave”
      • [p.16] Ayanokōji (inner) mused: “Sleeping Strategy”: pretend not to hear; abandoned instantly under threat
      • [p.17] Horikita Suzune said: “Would you prefer to regret while you suffer or regret while you despair?”
      • [p.18] Ayanokōji Kiyotaka said: “All right, then. What am I supposed to do?”

      Year 1, Volume 1, p.19–20 — Flashback 2.1: entrance-ceremony bus (April)

      • [p.19] Ayanokōji (inner) mused: mind “clear as a passing stream”; seat-finder's luck; forgot the wobbling elderly woman until commotion

      Year 1, Volume 1, p.21–24 — Bus: the uniformed girl intervenes (unnamed Kushida)

      • [p.21] kouenji-rokusuke said: “I have no interest in contributing to society. I care only for my own satisfaction.”
      • [p.24] Ayanokōji (inner) mused: analyzed the bus: two kinds — pretended not to hear / hesitant; none would move; himself included (“like everyone else, I didn't move”)
      • [p.24] Ayanokōji (inner) mused: elderly vs youth value argument — cold utilitarian framing; still didn't act

      Year 1, Volume 1, p.25–25 — Gate confrontation: first AK↔Horikita exchange

      • [p.25] Horikita Suzune said: “You were looking at me. Why?”
      • [p.25] Horikita Suzune said: “I didn't give up my seat because I thought it would be pointless. That's all.”
      • [p.25] Horikita Suzune said: “I don't want to spend any time around people like you.”
      • [p.25] Ayanokōji Kiyotaka said: “I feel the same way,” (his reply, muttered)

      Year 1, Volume 1, p.26–28 — Classroom day one: friendship anxiety + name exchange

      • [p.26] Ayanokōji (inner) mused: rehearsed friend-making scenarios the day before; “completely isolated... ventured alone into a battlefield... do or die” — social inexperience from upbringing
      • [p.26] Ayanokōji (inner) mused: paralyzed analyzing approaches (rotund boy beaten to the punch by glasses boy); “What on earth are 'friends,' anyway?”
      • [p.27] Horikita Suzune said: “That's quite a heavy sigh, considering the school year has only begun. Meeting you again makes me want to sigh.”
      • [p.28] Ayanokōji Kiyotaka said: “Nice to meet you. I'm Ayanokouji Kiyotaka.”
      • [p.28] Horikita Suzune said: “I'm Horikita Suzune.”
      • [p.28] Ayanokōji (inner) mused: “Wow. She was cute. Or rather, she was beautiful... Such a calm, cool beauty.”
      • [p.28] Ayanokōji Kiyotaka said: self-description: “I have no particular hobbies, but I'm interested in just about anything. I don't need many friends, but I think it would be nice to have at least a few.”
      • [p.29] Horikita Suzune said: “Spoken just like someone who avoids trouble. I don't think I could ever like such a person.”
      • [p.29] Ayanokōji Kiyotaka said: “Jeez, I feel like you trashed my entire existence in one second,”

      Year 1, Volume 1, p.29–32 — Chiyabashira + school rules + S System (orientation)

      • [p.31] Ayanokōji (inner) mused: “This school doesn't seem as strict as I thought” — aloud; then agrees with Horikita's “almost too much preferential treatment. It's frightening” — veil of mystery; expects hidden risk

      Year 1, Volume 1, p.32–40 — Class introductions (cast assembly)

      • [p.33] Hirata Yōsuke said: “Starting today, we're all going to be classmates... introduce ourselves and become friends as soon as possible.”
      • [p.35] Kushida Kikyō said: “My first goal is to become friends with everyone... share your contact information with me!”
      • [p.35] Ayanokōji (inner) mused: Kushida is genuine type; then (p.37) “She said it as though she knew I'd been critiquing everyone's introductions. I felt strangely uncomfortable” — first unease re Kushida
      • [p.39] Ayanokōji Kiyotaka said: own intro: “Um. Well, my name is Ayanokouji Kiyotaka. And, uh, I don't really have any special skills or anything. I'll do my best to get along with all of you.”
      • [p.40] Ayanokōji (inner) mused: “I'd failed!” — worst intro, attracts no attention by design instinct; applause “felt somewhat like pity, which strangely pained me. Despite that... I felt kind of glad.”

      Year 1, Volume 1, p.41–45 — Convenience store with Horikita (post-ceremony)

      • [p.41] Horikita Suzune said: “I'm the sort who doesn't know when you might need money,”
      • [p.41] Ayanokōji (inner) mused: surprised she stayed for intros; registers her price choices as revealing
      • [p.44] Ayanokōji Kiyotaka said: razor joke (five blades) — fails colossally
      • [p.44] Horikita Suzune said: “I don't have anything to shave. Not on my chin, not under my armpits, and not down there.”
      • [p.45] Horikita Suzune said: “You don't seem adept at socializing. You're terrible at conversation.”
      • [p.43] Ayanokōji (inner) mused: noticed “Horikita really was cute” — attraction registering despite wariness

      Year 1, Volume 1, p.46–51 — Sudou incident: Class D stigma + cameras

      • [p.46] Ayanokōji Kiyotaka said: “My name's Ayanokouji. I'm from your class. I just asked because it sounded like there was trouble.”
      • [p.46] Ayanokōji Kiyotaka said: “I can pay for you. I mean, it'd be annoying if you had to head all the way back to the dorms. I don't mind.”
      • [p.47] sudou-ken said: “My name's Sudou,” (he said)
      • [p.47] sudou-ken said: “Thanks for helping me out. I owe you.”
      • [p.47] Horikita Suzune said: “You're acting like a pushover right from the start. Do you intend to become his servant?”
      • [p.47] Ayanokōji Kiyotaka said: “I didn't care about making friends. I just wanted to help. No big deal.”
      • [p.47] Horikita Suzune said: “It's mostly defenseless people who stay away from those types. If he acted violently, I could rebuff him.”
      • [p.49] sudou-ken said: “Hey, get over here! I'll smack that smug look off your face!” (at Horikita; AK holds him back)
      • [p.50] second-years said: “You're in Class D, aren't you?” ... “Since you're 'defective,' we'll let you off the hook” / “You guys'll be in hell soon enough”
      • [p.50] Ayanokōji (inner) mused: upperclassmen treat Class D as known trash tier; can't explain how they knew

      Year 1, Volume 1, p.52–53 — Dorm 401: freedom monologue (CORE BACKSTORY ANCHOR)

      • [p.52] Ayanokōji (narrated) narrated: “I'd chosen this school for one fundamental reason... At this high school, people were not allowed to contact students without permission... I greatly appreciated that. I was free.”
      • [p.53] Ayanokōji (narrated) narrated: “No one else's eyes or words would ever reach me. I could start ag—no. I could begin anew entirely. A new life.” (self-corrected “start again” → “begin anew”)
      • [p.53] Ayanokōji (narrated) narrated: admitted before, “thought I would be been fine either way, that the difference between passing and failing would have been trivial. But my true feelings finally welled up. I was glad to have been admitted here.” (corpus reads “would be been” — kept verbatim)

      Year 1, Volume 1, p.54–58 — Lunchtime envy + Kushida's approach

      • [p.54] Ayanokōji (inner) mused: envious of others' groups; “still hadn't managed to befriend a single one”; worries loneliness will make him stand out/bullying target
      • [p.54] Horikita Suzune said: “How pathetic.” / “Your thoughts are like an open book.”
      • [p.56] Kushida Kikyō said: “You're Ayanokouji-kun, right?”
      • [p.56] Kushida Kikyō said: “Ayanokouji-kun, are you on good terms with Horikita-san?”
      • [p.56] Kushida Kikyō said: “Horikita turned me down.” (contact info)
      • [p.57] Ayanokōji Kiyotaka said: “I don't think she's very good at interacting with others. Why are you asking about Horikita, anyway?”
      • [p.57] Ayanokōji (inner) mused: she remembered his lame intro — “Somehow, that made me really happy”; handshake; “Today was probably my lucky day”

      Year 1, Volume 1, p.59–60 — Club fair talk with Horikita

      • [p.59] Horikita Suzune said: “I'm not interested in joining a club.”
      • [p.60] Horikita Suzune said: “Listen, don't count me as one of your friends.”
      • [p.60] Horikita Suzune said: “If you seriously want to make a friend, you should be more insistent.”
      • [p.60] Ayanokōji Kiyotaka said: “I can't, though. I've devoted myself to walking a lonely road.”
      • [p.60] Horikita Suzune said: “I have trouble comprehending your contradictory way of thinking.”

      Year 1, Volume 1, p.61–62 — Violence debate; she joins the club fair

      • [p.62] Horikita Suzune said: “Watching you flail about as you fail to make friends sounds somewhat interesting.”

      Year 1, Volume 1, p.63–68 — Club fair: Horikita Manabu's speech

My name is Horikita Manabu,” — silences 100+ students with presence alone; recruitment speech: no other-club involvement; rejects naive outlook; “council's right and duty to enforce and amend the rules, but the school expects more”
      • [p.67] Ayanokōji (inner) mused: sensed his power is personal, not positional; noticed sibling surname coincidence with Horikita Suzune — “perhaps they just happened to have the same surname. Or, maybe…”

      Year 1, Volume 1, p.72–75 — Swim-class day: betting ring + social foothold

      Year 1, Volume 1, p.76–79 — Pool: girls counter-surveil; Kushida & Horikita bodies noticed
