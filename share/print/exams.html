Class Poll

special

43 mentions · 8 vols · 2 runs · 3 claims

Rules

Class vote: each student casts three praise and three censure votes; praise minus censure is the result, self-voting and blanks are banned, voting repeats until first and last are determined, last place is expelled, and one dedicated praise vote must go to another class's student (full rule box y1v10 p.45; name and basics y1v10 p.37). The February 'provisional' rerun was debated by faculty before announcement (y1v11 p.13-18).

Points

Main run: no class-point changes came from the expulsions themselves (results board y1v10 p.315). Provisional run: class points were expected to fluctuate without any expulsions (y1v11 p.318).

Conditions

First run: last-place students in each class are expelled (y1v10 p.315). Provisional: no expulsions — defeated classes instead forfeit a protection point earned during the exam (aftermath y1v11 p.318).

Organizer

School-run supplementary special exam; faculty debate preceded the provisional rerun (Mashima objects that it risks expulsions, Tsukishiro insists, y1v11 p.15-18).

Notes

43 mentions / 8 vols (y1v10-y2v9.5). The y1v10 run produced three expulsions — Totsuka (A), Yamauchi (C), Manabe (D), none from B (y1v10 p.315) — the series' first mass-expulsion set piece; the provisional rerun is where Ayanokouji obtains a protection point (y1v11 p.37).

Cooperative Comprehensive Written Exam

special

4 mentions · 1 vols · 1 runs · 2 claims

Rules

Official bracket (y2v9 p.13): '[Special exam at the end of the second semester. A cooperative comprehensive written test in which the whole class will solve a total of 100 questions.]' Students solve in predetermined order, at least 2 and at most 5 questions each, 10 minutes per turn; overtime disqualifies; leaving clues terminates the exam with zero points. Time-remaining bonuses: +10 per hour left, +5 over 30 minutes, +2 over 10 minutes; each question scores by the solver's OAA band (A=1 ... E=5, OAA as of December 1).

Points

Class-versus-class results transfer 50 class points winner-takes (y2v9 p.11); pairings come from recent final-exam averages — 1st vs 2nd, 3rd vs 4th (y2v9 p.11-12); December standings A 1250 / B 985 / C 684 / D 655 (y2v9 p.12).

Conditions

No expulsions — explicitly 'not the most feared kind of special exam that will cause students to drop out' (y2v9 p.11); questions cover all regular midterm/final subjects from relatively easy to extremely hard (y2v9 p.12).

Organizer

School-run special exam; Chabashira announces, classes self-organize study strategy.

Notes

4 mentions / 1 vol — smallest footprint of any special. Found late in P9 as the unresolved 'mystery exam': the official name appears only in the p.13 bracket, so alias forms use the dialogue phrasings. Results: Horikita's class beat Sakayanagi's; Ichinose's class beat Ryuuen's despite sabotage (y2v9 p.215).

Cruise Ship Exam

special

22 mentions · 9 vols · 1 runs · 0 claims

Rules

Second week of the first-year cruise trip: the liner becomes a twelve-sign zodiac test. Students form zodiac-named groups (rabbit, dragon, ...) each with a hidden VIP identity puzzle and a traitor embedded in the group; rules are walked through in the private post-trip briefing (y1v4 p.34) and the group/VIP logic is re-derived over the map at y1v5 p.13-14.

Points

Group-level scores plus traitor sabotage decide outcomes; the ship results are accounted together with the island week (y1v4.5 p.69: 'results from the island test, and the test on the ship').

Conditions

Groups must complete the week's tasks while neutralizing their traitor; Koenji unilaterally ended his group's participation (y1v4 p.125).

Organizer

School-run special exam staged aboard the luxury liner during the trip.

Notes

22 mentions / 9 vols — smallest footprint of the trip pair, because the corpus mostly recalls it as aftermath ('Ryuuen defeated us on the zodiac test', y1v7 p.20) rather than restating rules. Forms 'zodiac test' (y1v7, y2v9) and 'zodiac game' (y1v7) added after pack reads; 'double-question' (y1v4 chapter title) folded in here.

End-of-Year Special Exam

special

88 mentions · 7 vols · 1 runs · 1 claims

Rules

Y2's closing special: announced with rules at y2v11 p.203-210 and run in mid-March. Class representatives receive rule sets different from what participants see in the classroom — 'the information that was given between your representatives and the participants waiting in the classroom was different' (y2v12.5 p.11).

Points

Decisive for the standings: the class still gains class points from the end-of-year special under partial-win conditions (y2v12 p.36).

Conditions

Expulsions are possible and did occur — anticipated pre-match ('the loser will be expelled', y2v12 p.41) and confirmed at the close, with PM Kijima noting 'it's truly regrettable that there were expulsions' (y2v12.5 p.9).

Organizer

School-run; Chairman Sakayanagi presides with homeroom teachers; sitting Prime Minister Kijima attends as observer (y2v12.5 p.9).

Notes

86 mentions / 5 vols. The corpus names it both 'end-of-year special' and plain 'end-of-year exam' — both tokens on y2v11 p.203 — so Y2's end-of-year slot IS this special, unlike Y1 where the end-of-year written sat separately (y1v9 p.39, all-pass y1v10 p.31). The two y1v11.5 mentions are event-selection retrospectives. First-years run their own concurrent end-of-year special (y2v12 p.26-28), and Horikita's class ascends to Class A by the third year on the strength of this arc (y3v1 p.9 synopsis).

Admission fixes the starting class-point baseline for the intake (1000 points at enrollment, y2v1 p.19 context), but the exam itself never scores inside the story.

Pre-enrollment only — zero instances by design (see p9_import exclusion log).

Organizer

School admissions (advanced-nurturing entrance testing).

Midterm and Final Written Exams

written

255 mentions · 22 vols · 5 runs · 4 claims

Rules

School-designed written exams sat every semester (midterms, finals, the year-end written). Results are posted class-wide with no privacy (y1v6 p.30). The first-midterm pass line was computed as half the class average (79.6/2 = 39.8, y1v1 p.247); later sitings use a fixed threshold over the class distribution (y1v6 p.30). Finals run two days, four subjects per day (y1v6 p.36).

Points

Results move standing: second-semester finals rank the classes with first place earning 50 class points (y2v8 p.12), and final-exam averages seed later specials — December's head-to-head paired classes by those averages (y2v9 p.11). Private points can buy extra test points: Chabashira sells one for 100,000 (y1v1 p.253).

Conditions

Failing a midterm or final forces drop-out: 'If you fail on a midterm or final exam in this school, then you have to drop out' (y1v1 p.128); the year-end written restates immediate expulsion for a failing grade (y1v9 p.39) and the results post carries the below-the-line expulsion rule (y1v10 p.14). Unexcused absence scores zero (y1v6 p.36).

Organizer

The school designs questions and cutoffs; homeroom teachers (Chabashira for Class D) announce results and enforce consequences.

Notes

Highest-frequency exam entity (256 mentions, 23 vols, y0-y2v12.5). Carries the core academic-arc set pieces: Kushida's old-test leak (y1v1 p.236), Sudou's near-expulsion and Ayanokouji's point purchase (y1v1 p.247, p.253; y1v2 p.16, p.27), the Y1 year-end all-pass (y1v10 p.31), and the Y2 second-semester finals whose averages seeded the December head-to-head (y2v8 p.12). y0 hits are White Room / generic-written senses.

Uninhabited Island Exam

special

265 mentions · 27 vols · 2 runs · 2 claims

Rules

First-years spend the opening week of the summer cruise trip at a lodge on a deserted island; classes run a base camp split into zones with school-issued safety watches (y1v3 p.30). The Y2 return, 'Uninhabited Island Survival', runs about two weeks with student groups working tasks across grid areas such as E7/G3/H5/D8 (overview y2v3 p.17).

Points

Task performance converts to points and class standing; Y2 first place paid 300 class points (y2v5 p.187: 'the 300 class points that Koenji got in the desert island exam'). Y1's version is remembered as the template class victory (y1v7 p.20).

Conditions

Safety-watch failures and rule breaches cost participation; groups move through island zones across the week (rules narrated y1v3 p.27-40). The Y2 rerun adds cross-year competition and task pacing.

Organizer

School-designed survival exam run on the trip's island leg; homeroom teachers observe.
