Class Points

points

640 mentions · 32 vols · 3 claims

Function

Standing currency of the four classes: starts at intake, moves with every exam, sets the monthly private-point payout, and reaches Class A graduation value.

Notes

Intake baseline 1000 points — 'We had been given 1000 class points when we first came here' (y2v1 p.19) — though later intakes differ (this year's first-years started at 800, y2v12 p.28). Outside special-exam windows the index can only be lowered (decay periods, y2v5 p.30). Snapshot at March 1 of the second year: A 1098 (Sakayanagi) / B 983 (Horikita) / C 730 (Ryuuen) / D 654 (Ichinose), y2v12 p.13. Festival awards 100/50 (y2v5 p.14) and island first-place 300 (y2v5 p.187) are typical movement sizes. 616 mentions / 30 vols.

Expulsion Rules

discipline

1702 mentions · 33 vols · 4 claims

Function

Drop-out discipline: failing grades, class votes, eliminations and buybacks all end in the same forced exit.

Notes

The base rule lands in the first weeks — fail a midterm or final and you drop out (y1v1 p.128) — with results posts carrying the below-the-line threat (y1v10 p.14). Instruments beyond written grades: class-vote expulsions (three names y1v10 p.315), elimination-loss dropouts (Kamuro, y2v11 p.11), bounties (20,000,000 to have someone removed, y2v2 p.71) and the 20,000,000 revocation off-ramp (y1v10 p.46). Voluntary and forced-exit counts are quoted in the y1v11.5 aftermath and the Maezono case (y2v12.5 p.81, p.143). 1634 mentions / 30 vols — the largest system footprint after student-council.

Private Points

points

743 mentions · 32 vols · 6 claims

Function

Personal currency of ANHS: issued to every student, worth one yen each, spent on comfort, services, exam points and institutional loopholes.

Notes

Issued at enrollment — 100,000 points at the start (y1v2 p.27) and a monthly allowance tied to standing: 1000 class points = 100,000 points, stated yen-equivalent (y2v1 p.14; y1v2 p.15). Transfers are unrestricted (y1v10 p.46), which enables: buying exam points (sale price 100,000, y1v1 p.253; bought for Sudou, y1v2 p.16, p.27), revoking an expulsion at 20,000,000 (y1v10 p.46), class transfers at 20,000,000 (y3v1 p.19), bounties grey-area-legal (y2v2 p.71), event stakes (festival prep grants y2v5 p.14; sports substitutes 100,000 each y1v5 p.23). Protection points enter as a parallel instrument y1v10 p.38. 722 mentions / 31 vols, present y0-y3v1.

Student Council

governance

1259 mentions · 29 vols · 3 claims

Function

Student self-government: discipline summonses, budget oversight of events, and the annual presidential election.

Notes

Manabu Horikita's presidency opens the series (y1v1 p.64-67); the mid-October election ceremony swears in Nagumo Miyabi as next president (y1v6 p.14), with the Horikita-Nagumo race recalled at y1v4.5 p.180. Suzune joins the council in the second year (y2v2 p.350) and the council's budget hands out festival service funds (y2v7 p.7-9); Nagumo-era entanglements peak in y2v9. The council room doubles as the pressure venue (see student-council-room location). 1189 mentions / 28 vols.
