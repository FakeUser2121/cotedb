      Private Points

      points

      743 mentions · 32 vols · 6 claims

      Function

      Personal currency of ANHS: issued to every student, worth one yen each, spent on comfort, services, exam points and institutional loopholes.

      Notes

      Issued at enrollment — 100,000 points at the start (y1v2 p.27) and a monthly allowance tied to standing: 1000 class points = 100,000 points, stated yen-equivalent (y2v1 p.14; y1v2 p.15). Transfers are unrestricted (y1v10 p.46), which enables: buying exam points (sale price 100,000, y1v1 p.253; bought for Sudou, y1v2 p.16, p.27), revoking an expulsion at 20,000,000 (y1v10 p.46), class transfers at 20,000,000 (y3v1 p.19), bounties grey-area-legal (y2v2 p.71), event stakes (festival prep grants y2v5 p.14; sports substitutes 100,000 each y1v5 p.23). Protection points enter as a parallel instrument y1v10 p.38. 722 mentions / 31 vols, present y0-y3v1.
