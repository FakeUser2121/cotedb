Prologue: Hashimoto Masayoshi's Monologue p.4–8

Setting. monologue; pages 1–7 blank/front matter.

Narrative beats. Hashimoto's worldview: deep mistrust of people; people betray easily; resolve since joining the school to graduate in Class A and take revenge on those who betrayed him; he names Ryūen, Sakayanagi and Ayanokōji as possible opponents; he acts solely for himself; his kanji (Masayoshi) can be read as "justice" — the contrast he despises.

About these pages. Hashimoto's prologue; pages 4–7 blank/watermark only.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. his private resolve (graduate Class A; revenge) is stated outright, not a system rule.

Unattributed lines. none (monologue).

Translation errors, kept as printed. doubled period in Chabashira-sensei's bedhead narration p.13.

Chapter 1: Opening of the Third Term of the Second Year p.9–30

Setting. School; dorm.

Narrative beats. Third term opens; Kei walks him arm in arm to school; Sudō, envious but worn down by studies and sports, warns him about the school email on winter-break first-years and sighs; Chabashira's homeroom notice of the school's first-ever student-teacher discussion (next month, careers/employment, parents' survey done; parent-teacher conferences to come) and her bedhead; class talk about career paths (Hirata: university; Keisei/Ishigami: top-company Class A route; an untagged classmate backs the "aim for the job you want" line); at lunch, Horikita ropes him into the Ibuki cooking wager — seven-dish homemade bento, judged 20/100 by Horikita (compare AK's deserted-island framing), no salt discipline; Kushida joins for free food and damage; Ibuki storms off, accepts the seven-o'clock dinner anyway; Kei + girls file past him; evening — the five-paintings variety show ("Number four."), the untouched sketchbook (White Room taught technique, not purpose), and the empty sketchbook returns to the second drawer.

About these pages. term start; career crossroads; the Ibuki cooking gauntlet.

New names. none (all named students are rostered).

New rules and facts. student-teacher discussions are new to this school (first-ever, next month); ANHS's Class A privileges discussed in class context.

Unattributed lines. several short exchanges are untagged (e.g., the "Yukimura-kun's choice" line p.15; the mini-tomatoes turn p.22; the "out of 20?" verdict chain pp.24–25); none were assigned.

Translation errors, kept as printed. "Kushidasan" printed as one word (p.27); doubled period p.13.

Chapter Two: Survival and Elimination Special Exam p.31–59

Setting. School; dorm.

Narrative beats. Thursday of the third term; Chabashira-sensei unveils the [Survival and Elimination Special Exam] — second-years only; attack/defense rotation A→B→C→D clockwise this half (counterclockwise next half), 10+10 of 20 turns; 16 categories with 3 difficulty levels (extra levels cost accumulated points); nominations of five students per side per three minutes; the leader protects up to five per task; three misses = elimination with a point deducted per person; the leader never fights and cannot be eliminated; a bottom-ranked class with eliminations faces expulsion; rewards 1st +100, 2nd/3rd −50, 4th −100 (extension on ties; −100 all on all-tie); anti-collusion rule (Ryūen–Katsuragi deserted-island contract cited as the exception); phones permitted except during the defensive solve; next Friday; leader due by Monday or randomly assigned — "Do your best not to place last. That’s it." After her exit, Yōsuke recaps calmly; Horikita gathers the class at lunch, then the room settles the leader question — trail of silence, thirty seconds, Yōsuke's prompt, Horikita accepts; Maezono publicly challenges her decisiveness (the unanimous-vote expulsion), Horikita concedes the hesitation, offers the job to anyone raising a hand — nobody does — Maezono withdraws; Kōenji's polite written demand for protection surfaces; Sudō forces the question; Kei asks AK for protection over the Keyaki Mall detour; on the way home Morishita — Class A, friendless until now — intercepts them on a cold bench, interrogates Kei, forces contact exchange; she recognizes him as the watching target.

About these pages. the special exam's rules; the leader selection; Morishita's interest.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. full exam ruleset (categories, difficulty, protection, elimination/expulsion ladder, rewards, phone rule, leader immunity and duty, random-pick fallback, 20-turn structure, 100-point cap per class on bad years); second-years only.

Unattributed lines. the “But what if the opponent’s class is aware of the categories we’re weak in...” line (p.37); the "That's the worst, right!?" line (p.50); the “Eh, it’s allowed...?” line (p.41); the 30-points line (p.22 in Ch.1); none were assigned.

Translation errors, kept as printed. the p.36–37 rule sentence and the orphaned "kun?" tail at the top of page 55 (page-break artifacts) kept as printed.

Chapter Three: The Sender's Identity p.60–80

Setting. School; dorm; Keyaki Mall (convenience store, gym, café).

Narrative beats. One week before the special exam; the Friday after the announcement, Mii-chan (Wang Mei-Yu) corners Ayanokōji about the anonymous food deliveries made during her three-week absence; he helps her trace the source — disguised handwriting (a grocery-bag note with the room number), items all from a convenience store, a newly assigned clerk and a transferred shift leader; she ran from the clerk for six days; his theory that it could be someone outside her class (a worried boy) shakes her; she decides to know; on the walk to the store he gives her the honest advice that he'd want to know first, decide second; the clerk names the buyer — Kōenji; after wavering Saturday, they tail him to the Keyaki Mall gym (Saturday/Sunday mornings; he benches 200 kg), past the practice date with Enoshima Midoriko (class 3-B), and confront him — he admits the gifts outright ("It was me who gave you those items, but why does it matter?"), calls it a trivial repaid goodwill debt; refuses towels and money; refuses to be allied into the class; warns AK off ostracizing him ("they'd better be prepared"); leaves for his "dates"; Mii-chan leaves with nothing but the confirmation.

About these pages. the Kōenji–Mii-chan benefactor mystery resolved.

New names. Wang Mei-yu's moniker "Wang girl" (his coinage); Enoshima Midoriko (class 3-B) appears speaking zero lines.

New rules and facts. student-card purchase tracking at the campus convenience store; the student card contains name and photo; Kōenji benches 200 kg (stated).

Unattributed lines. the clerk's lines are untagged throughout; the “I want to know” page (p.65) is answered by Mii-chan's narration-tagged line; the Enoshima identification exchange (p.75) is untagged; none were assigned beyond what the pages tag themselves.

Translation errors, kept as printed. Kōenji's "Dēto"-spelling "dates" gag (TL Note kept); the page-boundary split between pp.76–77 kept as printed.

Chapter Four: Advice p.81–100

Setting. School; Keyaki Mall (karaoke, café).

Narrative beats. Wednesday, two days before the exam; Ike stands on his desk with a foolproof plan — feign mass illness so 32 Class-D students "drop out", leaving five defenders + leader, guaranteeing 68 points via protection across 20 turns; the class empties; the karaoke room session (Horikita, Yōsuke, Shinohara, Hondō, Miyamoto) dissects it — Ike's arithmetic survives the first pass, then Horikita walks the hypothetical: Ryūen's class would take it, lock 66 points forever, lose the cooperation game; Ichinose and Sakayanagi would co-sign; the anti-collusion rewards make a capped class a loser by definition; Ike deflates, then accepts; she praises the courage anyway; then the one-on-one café arc — blended coffees, the spilled-coffee sparring, the unanimous-vote relitigation; the trap — the three futures (win/lose/draw) tightened to "win with Ibuki Mio expelled from Ryūen's class" — Horikita breaks; AK installs the awareness demand ("Don't fight aimlessly. Be conscious."); she identifies her unfit for the coldness; he reframes her as Class-A-caliber-in-a-garden-miniature; her pick of excluded categories (entertainment, music, subculture) — identical to his; she registers his without question.

About these pages. the 68-point plan; the one-on-one Horikita session; the Ibuki hypothesis.

New names. Enoshima Midoriko is not named here (she is p.75); here: Totsuka named as Sakayanagi's dislike-target, Manabu named as AK's teacher-perspective mentor.

New rules and facts. 68-point scenario mechanics (32 eliminations + 5 defenders + leader); negative rewards as the anti-collusion design; leader immunity to expulsion; Points as difficulty currency; Protection Points can be traded for an elimination immunity.

Unattributed lines. the "I was also surprised when I heard it from Kanji" line (p.84); the "That sounds good..." line (p.81); attribution left open where alternation doesn't decide.

Translation errors, kept as printed. none new; the café and karaoke pages read clean.

Setting. School; dorm; Keyaki Mall (the vibrant cafe), the east-entrance restroom.

Ayanokōji POV: the morning with Kei; Kanzaki's call; the dorm visit with Watanabe and then Ichinose + Amikura — the four-person combination; the collusion question foregrounded before Ichinose opens; Ichinose leaves early and "forgets" her phone; the alone-time admission; the deliberate-phone-forget confirmation; the accidental-lean fiction; "I love you, Ayanokōji-kun. I'm helplessly in love with you."; Watanabe bursts in at the wrong moment; she manages him with pure craft; his junior-high story — the fateful encounter, the two-timing girl, the ruined friendship; Mako-chan revealed as his crush; 100→120 arrival. Hashimoto's POV again: the evening call around 8 o'clock; Sakayanagi's classical-music calm; his info-dump (Kōenji-Horikita deal, Ryūen scheming, expulsion candidates); her indifference; the Maezono reveal — "Are you dating Maezono-san for information, not love?"; threat-discussion audio was hers.

About these pages. the classes pre-exam positioning; Sakayanagi's mask; Ichinose's move.

New rules and facts. student-card tracking at the campus convenience store; Sakayanagi's public "tea party" as a tension-manufacture tactic; ANHS East entrance restroom as a chronic refuge.

Unattributed lines. the parting words at the fourth-floor elevator (p.106); the “See you later / contact me whenever” exchange (p.106) — none assigned.

Chapter Six: The Square of Offense and Defense p.123–188

Setting. School; dorm; Keyaki Mall (gymnasium, café).

Narrative beats. Third-term special exam; Ayanokōji’s class (Class B) plays against Sakayanagi, Ryūen, Ichinose in a chessboard-like rotation; students are nominated to answer questions; the leader nominates five to protect; three wrong answers eliminates a student — and in the bottom class, one eliminated student is expelled. Horikita targets five vulnerable students on Ichinose’s side to keep her from protecting anyone. A mysterious perfectly-protected streak by Ryūen appears in the second half. Kei is nominated repeatedly by Ichinose to send a message. Sakayanagi senses a leak; Morishita names it openly; mood boils.

About these pages. the special exam in full.

New names. Ishikura (Class D) — Class A’s strongest student; Ayanokōji’s class’s strategy.

New rules and facts. the special exam's mechanics — 20 turns, each class attacks each other in rotation; the target nominates five, the leader protects five; difficulty levels 1-3; point values 60-75; answer formats vary by category (literature, arithmetic, sports, gourmet food, entertainment, music, subculture, lifestyle, kanji, news, etc.).

Unattributed lines. several exchanges between Ichinose and Horikita at the start of the exam; several reaction lines in the final stretch.

Translation errors, kept as printed. none new.

Chapter Seven: New Expulsion p.189–216

Setting. School.

Narrative beats. Joint effort collapses; the second half restarts; Sakayanagi, from her POV, watches Ryūen perfectly defend all ten turns, realizes a traitor sits in her class, but allows the exam to continue; Morishita voices the leak; the final ranks are Class D 69, Class C 62, Class B 59, Class A 53. Sakayanagi loses. She expels one of the five previously-eliminated students by drawing lots — the lot falls to Kamuro. Sakayanagi and Ayanokōji meet later by the staff room; Kamuro appears expelled but strangely at peace; she asks for one promise from Sakayanagi (make the traitor go down the same path); she hands Ayanokōji the responsibility of checking Sakayanagi keeps her word.

About these pages. the exam’s collapse, the elimination game, Kamuro’s lottery expulsion.

New rules and facts. the penalty for a class finishing last is one expulsion; tie-breaking by points and then turns.
