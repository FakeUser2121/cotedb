The supermarket trip with Kushida, Horikita and IbukiY2V9.75 · p.4–8notes on Ayanokōji Kiyotaka

On January 3rd he heads to the supermarket alone; Kushida greets him and spots Horikita heading the same way; she ropes them into shopping together; all three put ingredients in their baskets. They spot Ibuki sulking over the ready-made food; Kushida stalks behind her and peeks in her basket (instant ramen, sweet bread); Ibuki yells; Horikita grabs the basket and puts the ready-made items back. Horikita declares war with Ibuki, offers to cook her lunch at her room after learning she doesn't cook; Kushida gets invited too; he accepts; the four go to Horikita's room; the lunch party is famously "extremely difficult and tumultuous."

Horikita at the supermarket and at her own placeY2V9.75 · p.4–8notes on Horikita Suzune

Accepts Kushida's suggestion to shop together on impulse, walks into the supermarket with her; at the end of her shopping, she catches Ibuki in the ready-made food corner. Stalks Ibuki's basket with Kushida; forces Ibuki to put the instant noodles and sweet bread back; in front of everyone, orders her body back into shape for their martial arts match someday. Offers Ibuki lunch at her room; tells Kushida and Ayanokōji they may join; cooking lesson follows; the fire drill goes predictably.
