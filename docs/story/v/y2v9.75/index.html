The First Dissonance of the New Year — the entrance p.4

Setting. Keyaki Mall, January 3rd just past ten in the morning; the supermarket entrance, the mall open again after a two-day New Year break.

Narrative beats. Ayanokōji comes alone to restock a refrigerator emptied during the holiday he spent indoors with Kei. Kushida greets him at the entrance; Horikita passes on the same errand; Kushida calls her back and proposes shopping together; Horikita stops and considers the proposal instead of refusing; he reads the odds of the three of them having fun as close to zero; she accepts; the three enter with baskets.

About these pages. opening scene of a five-page short story, narrated throughout by Ayanokōji.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. the mall closes for two days over New Year and resumes operation on January 3rd; the supermarket is open a little past ten.

Unattributed lines. none — the greeting exchange is bounded by named narration on both sides.

Translation errors, kept as printed. quotation glyphs are mixed on the page (the first greeting opens with a straight mark and closes with a curly one); “Ayanokōji” is printed with the macron here.

The First Dissonance of the New Year — the aisles and the sighting p.5

Setting. supermarket interior, vegetable to meat to fish to the processed-food corner, ending at the ready-made food shelves.

Narrative beats. cooking talk on the way round; Kushida says she can cook “to some extent” and treats the skill as social capital; Horikita spars with her over which of them is the sarcastic one; the three spot Ibuki scowling at her phone in the ready-made food corner; Horikita reads her as checking her point balance, Ayanokōji offers online bulk buying as an alternative explanation, and Horikita dismisses it from what she has seen of Ibuki; the three are still watching when Ibuki notices them; Kushida greets her.

About these pages. the bridge scene; the story's only look at Ibuki before she speaks.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. none — Ayanokōji's bulk-buying line is his own recent discovery, not a school rule.

Unattributed lines. none — Kushida and Horikita are each named in the narration around their lines.

Translation errors, kept as printed. this page prints its dialogue with straight quotation marks throughout, against the curly marks used on pp.4 and 7.

The First Dissonance of the New Year — the basket p.6

Setting. the ready-made food corner, in public, with the three classmates standing over Ibuki's basket.

Narrative beats. Ibuki hides the basket behind her and refuses a greeting; Horikita asks whether she bought something she does not want seen; Kushida circles behind her and reads the basket out loud (instant ramen, instant yakisoba, sweet bread, processed foods); Ibuki swings the basket and misses because Kushida has already stepped back; Horikita calls the diet shockingly unhealthy and starts returning items to the shelves herself, holding the rim so the basket cannot be pulled away; Ibuki protests.

About these pages. the volume's physical comedy; the scene the story is remembered for.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. none.

Unattributed lines. “Ugh…”, “Can't you even say a New Year's greeting properly?” and “Did you buy something you don't want to be seen?” carry no speaker tag. The narration names Ibuki's disgusted expression in the sentence after the first, and turns on what had caught Horikita's attention before the third; Ibuki's refusals bound the exchange. All three are recorded under those characters with the gap noted in their own records.

Translation errors, kept as printed. none observed beyond the glyph mix.

The First Dissonance of the New Year — the invitation p.7

Setting. still at the shelves; Horikita holding the basket, Kushida and Ayanokōji standing by.

Narrative beats. Horikita turns the diet scolding into a condition of their future martial-arts match — imbalanced nutrition costs muscle; Ibuki argues that she cannot cook and asks whether she should buy bentos; Horikita rejects supermarket bentos for their carbohydrates and fats and invites her to her room for lunch and a cooking lesson; Ibuki refuses; Horikita answers with the money saved; Ibuki becomes rigid at the private-points argument and accepts; Kushida invites herself on the grounds that the pairing is interesting; Horikita asks Ayanokōji too, and when he objects that he is unrelated, answers that three or four makes no difference.

About these pages. the pivot of the story — the diet scolding is converted into a social invitation by way of Ibuki's budget.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. none — the private-points squeeze is personal budget, not a school rule.

Unattributed lines. “Uh...”, “Eh…” and “...I see.” carry no speaker tag; each sits inside an exchange bounded by Horikita's named lines and by Ibuki's own tagged answers, and all three are recorded under Ibuki with the gap noted.

Translation errors, kept as printed. “Aren’t I unrelated here?” is printed with a curly apostrophe against the straight marks around it.

The First Dissonance of the New Year — the party of four p.8

Setting. the supermarket closing out the scene; the lunch itself is set in Horikita's room later that day and never narrated.

Narrative beats. Ayanokōji weighs the invitation as a food bill and as a chance to watch someone cook, and accepts; the four agree to meet in Horikita's room; the story closes by calling the lunch party extremely difficult and exhausting.

About these pages. the story's last page; the outcome is withheld and only judged.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. none.

Unattributed lines. none.

Translation errors, kept as printed. none observed.

Postscript and credits p.16–17

Setting. the translator's afterword and the staff credits page, outside the story.

Narrative beats. the postscript notes that this is the first .75 volume since Year 1 Volume 11, says the two panels at the end are manga covers, mentions translated character profiles, and thanks the team, readers and Kinugasa. The credits page lists the Japanese proofreader, two editors, two illustrators and the EPUB maker, with jokes in quotation marks.

About these pages. production matter; no character appears.

New names. none rostered — the credits name staff only.

New rules and facts. none.

Unattributed lines. none — the quoted lines on the credits page are staff jokes, not dialogue.

Translation errors, kept as printed. the credits heading is printed as “Credit”; the EPUB maker's name is styled “SnOrT NeSqUiK™”; the proofreader's joke line reads “Kushida (Kikyō) x Horikita (Suzune) x Ibuki (Mio)”; the postscript's opening sentence runs “for y’all, Definitely been a while” with a comma splice as printed.
