Prologue p.8–12

Setting. Morning of March 31 in Horikita Suzune’s dorm room; she reflects before Horikita Manabu’s graduation departure.

Narrative beats

  • pp.8–9.
    • Suzune looks at her tired reflection after a sleepless night and thinks about how little time she has spent talking with her brother over the past year.
  • pp.9–10.
    • She wonders whether to meet him to say farewell but feels she has not shown him enough growth and fears making him worry.
  • pp.10–11.
    • Rejecting the idea of running away, she struggles with what to do as time runs out before his noon departure and questions who she is.
  • pp.11–12.
    • She reflects that she spent years as an imitation shaped to gain her brother’s recognition, accepts that past self as real, decides what she wants to show him, and resolves to act before he leaves.

About these pages. First-person monologue from Horikita Suzune’s perspective; p.12 is followed by a blank page in the text.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. Horikita Manabu is departing at noon on March 31 (pp.8, 10).

Unattributed lines. none.

Translation errors, kept as printed. "31th" appears on p.8.

Prologue: Before Manabu’s graduationY1V11.5 · p.8–12notes on Horikita Suzune

Morning of March 31 in her dorm room; Suzune looks at her tired reflection in the mirror before her brother’s departure from the school. She reflects that she and her brother spent less than a few hours talking over more than a year and questions whether it is acceptable to part without speaking. Feeling she has not shown her brother enough growth this year, she worries that sending him off would only make him worry about her. With her brother set to depart at noon, she rejects running away but struggles to decide what to do as time runs out. She reflects that she spent much of her life as an imitation shaped to gain her brother’s recognition. Accepting that past self as real, she decides what she wants to show him and resolves to act before his departure.

Ch.1: Graduation ceremony and farewellsY1V11.5 · p.13–120notes on Ayanokōji Kiyotaka

On March 24, Ayanokōji attends the third-year graduation ceremony curious about the outcome of Horikita Manabu and Nagumo’s conflict and the ceremony itself. In the elevator, Yukimura Keisei greets him and talks about Class D’s demotion after the final exam. Keisei blames himself for pushing the Katsuragi recruitment and says he told Horikita about it. Keisei explains that Horikita did not blame him and discusses the risk of Katsuragi betraying Class A. Keisei asks why Ayanokōji did not report his Katsuragi plan to Horikita; Ayanokōji downplays it. Keisei reflects on Horikita’s leadership and how he has changed since enrolling. During the ceremony, graduates are called to the stage; Horikita Manabu gives the representative address as a Class A graduate. Back in class after the ceremony, Chabashira praises the class’s growth despite their demotion. After class, Ayanokōji asks Horikita Suzune whether she will see her brother; she initially says she has no plans. Suzune decides she may see her brother and asks Ayanokōji to wait while he kills time until the graduates’ meeting ends. In a flashback to the previous night, Ayanokōji calls Chairman Sakayanagi from his room. Ayanokōji asks Chairman Sakayanagi about his house arrest and explains he is calling because of circumstances affecting his own status. Ayanokōji tells Chairman Sakayanagi that Acting Director Tsukishiro is acting against him and asks the chairman to return to the school. In the present, Ayanokōji meets Sakayanagi Arisu while waiting and they head to a meeting room. Ayanokōji attends a meeting with Sakayanagi, Tsukishiro, Mashima, Chabashira, Sakagami, and Hoshinomiya concerning the chess exam. Sakayanagi states that Ayanokōji originally won the chess match, prompting a dispute over the process and Tsukishiro’s role. Mashima and the teachers decide the matter must be reported despite Tsukishiro’s denials. After the meeting, Ayanokōji finds Horikita Suzune waiting near the gym for the third-years to emerge from the graduates’ meeting. As more graduates gather, Ayanokōji urges Suzune to approach her brother and pushes her forward. Ayanokōji watches Nagumo Miyabi congratulate Horikita Manabu as Asahina Nazuna and other student council members stand nearby. Nagumo approaches Suzune after parting from Manabu, and their exchange draws attention to her as Manabu’s sister. Ayanokōji listens as Nagumo and Asahina discuss the outcome of Nagumo’s conflict with Horikita Manabu. Ayanokōji observes the farewells as the graduates’ send-off continues around them.
