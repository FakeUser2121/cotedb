Chabashira's monologue: the nightmare of the exam that cost her Class AY2V5 · p.7–8notes on Chabashira Sae

From her own POV, reveals a recurring nightmare she's never told anyone about — the events of the special exam in her third year, which always ends the same way.

with Hoshinomiya Chie

  • Chabashira Sae "I keep having a recurring nightmare."p.7
  • Chabashira Sae "By the time I reached my third-year, the number of classmates who had left me had swelled to six."p.7
  • Chabashira Sae "I believed that I could graduate from Class A without having to leave anyone behind."p.7
6 more notes
  • Narration "My best friend, Chie, approaches me with an angry expression and grabs my chest."p.7
  • Narration "The class, which had been united as one, collapsed in an instant."p.7
  • Narration "The relationship between the two of us ended just as it had begun."p.8
  • Trait Haunted by guilt — she was in Class B, close to Class A, and vowed to graduate without leaving anyone; the graduation special exam destroyed the class and cost her a "companion" (a boy who reached for her hand under the evening sky, whom she called "irreplaceable"). The memory is the source of her drive to protect her current class and her trauma over the coming exam.p.7–8
  • Relationship Hoshinomiya Chie Her best friend "Chie," who angrily grabbed her in the nightmare — they were on opposite sides.p.7
  • Caveat The "companion" is unnamed in these pages; the text describes him as an irreplaceable friend and opposite-sex person — no roster id assigned (no name on the page).p.8
