Prologue p.11–14

Setting. Miyake Akito's monologue, in the school grounds at the end of the sports festival; told after the fact, looking back.

Narrative beats. Miyake describes himself as neither a good guy nor a bad guy — an average person who coasted through life until high school. The comfortable five-person group (Keisei, Kiyotaka, Haruka, Airi) became his life. He falls in love with Haruka, convinced of it when she said during the Unanimous Consent Special Exam that she would drop out of school; he would have abandoned Airi to protect Haruka, and has no regrets. At the festival's end Haruka asks him to join her revenge; he resolves to follow her no matter how many people will hate him.

About these pages. Miyake POV; the invitation in the prologue is the same “revenge” offer the Epilogue shows again from the outside.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. none.

Unattributed lines. the “revenge” inviter is unnamed on p.13 (Miyake narrates her eyes and her walking away; gender inferable from context but the page does not name her).

Translation errors, kept as printed. none observed.

Chapter 1: Introduction p.15–63

Setting. September 20–21 (Monday–Tuesday), dorm, then 2-D homeroom; the Unanimous Consent Special Exam's aftermath.

Narrative beats. AK decides his support for Horikita's “illogical” choice was a calculated watch on her growth; OAA shows Airi gone; Horikita waits outside Kushida's room from dawn in the lobby; homeroom: CB announces the three absences (Kushida, Hasebe, Wang); the Sports Festival's special rules are unveiled — individual-ability focus, parallel events, 5 starting points, ≥5 events required, 1-point participation prizes (none from the 6th event on, which instead costs 1 point), 10-event cap, <5 events forfeits all points, unsigned withdrawal −2 points, fundamental (individual; 5/3/1) vs special (team ≥2, equal split) competitions, class ranking rewards 150/50/0/−150, individual rewards 2M private points or a Limited-Time Class Change Ticket (usable only during second semester)/1M/500k, first come first served pre-booking from 10PM (three cancels), 100m race layout, and the club-restriction clause (Hirata no soccer, Sudō no basketball); Sudō erupts; Horikita preempts the class and apologizes for breaking her promise, "I will bring her back… I'll take responsibility”; Hirata requests the class keep Kushida's secret; Matsushita backs him; Kōenji signs on solo for the money; Kei intercepts Horikita at lunch — Nagumo summons AK through Yagami and Horikita; Nagumo offers him a head-to-head bet at the festival (one head-to-head win settles everything, five Nagumo-specified competitions, miss one and it's AK's loss; one week to answer) and lunches daily with Ichinose; maid-café pause (Satou, Matsushita, Maezono); corridor: Chabashira tells him Airi spent about 5,000 private points post-expulsion before formal processing — he pays it himself.

About these pages. Monday is Ayanokōji's POV (he narrates the whole chapter); Tuesday morning covers homeroom and the corridor.

New names. Yagami Takuya appears (text only); Sports Festival summary screen; OAA app; "Limited Time" Class Change Ticket (Limited-Time); clinic in Keyaki Mall (footnote marker [1], body absent from these pages).

New rules and facts. Sports Festival ruleset as listed above; member restriction for club events; starting handicap for absentee classes (400 vs 380, 20-point deficit); class ranking reward table; individual reward table.

Unattributed lines. the "C-Class change ticket…!?" cry (p.35, speaker untagged; class "erupted"); the two "Limited Time" reaction lines (p.36, Sudō and Ike talking without speaker tags); Chabashira's "What’s with that look."
