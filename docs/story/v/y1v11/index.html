Prologue: "The Monologue of Sakayanagi Arisu" p.7–12

Setting. Sakayanagi Arisu’s recollection of visiting the White Room facility as a child with her father; ends in the present as she wakes from the memory.

Narrative beats

  • pp.7–9.
    • Young Sakayanagi and her father observe children through one-way glass at the facility
    • she argues talent is set at birth and questions whether the experiment can create geniuses.
  • pp.10–11.
    • She notices a boy (Ayanokōji Kiyotaka, the director’s son) excelling calmly and learns he has lived in the facility since birth
    • her father expresses pity for him.
  • p.12.
    • Sakayanagi resolves to prove talent is not decided by education
    • in the present she wakes from the nostalgic dream, thinking of their coming confrontation.

About these pages. monologue/flashback framed by a present-moment awakening on p.12.

New names. none on roster beyond Sakayanagi Arisu and Ayanokōji Kiyotaka (her father appears unnamed in roster terms).

New rules and facts. Sakayanagi visited the White Room as a child and saw young Ayanokōji Kiyotaka there (pp.7–12); she recalls resolving to play chess after seeing him.

Unattributed lines. none.

Translation errors, kept as printed. none observed.

Chapter 1: "The Teachers’ Battle" p.13–26

Setting. Staff room and school corridors, before the Class Vote special exam is announced.

Narrative beats

  • pp.13–20.
    • Acting Director Tsukishiro explains the first-years’ final special exam and new Class Vote system to staff
    • Mashima objects to forced expulsions and the new system, but Tsukishiro insists and ends the meeting mentioning a possible future cultural festival.
  • pp.21–26.
    • Chabashira speaks with Mashima and Hoshinomiya in the hallway about the expulsions and Tsukishiro’s methods
    • after Chabashira leaves, Hoshinomiya and Mashima discuss her rivalry with Chabashira and Class C’s threat.

About these pages. faculty-focused chapter; students are discussed but not present.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. First-years will face a provisional Class Vote exam with expulsion risk and a final special exam with a new system (pp.13–17); Tsukishiro states the 20 million private-point override for forced expulsions will not require class points this time (p.19 context in discussion/p.22 mentions exemption).

Unattributed lines. none.

Translation errors, kept as printed. none observed.

Chapter 2: "The First Years’ Final Battle" p.27–54

Setting. Class C classroom on March 8.

Narrative beats

  • pp.27–29.
    • Chabashira announces the final special exam, the Event Selection Exam, after Yamauchi’s expulsion.
  • pp.29–36.
    • She explains the rules: each class proposes 10 events (with fair rules and tiebreakers), narrows to 5, and 7 are randomly selected on exam day
    • schedule runs through March 22.
  • pp.36–41.
    • Commanders are required and can intervene in events but are expelled if their class loses
    • class matchups are decided by raffle after commanders are chosen
    • class points change by 30 per event plus 100 for the winning class.
  • pp.42–46.
    • Horikita shares the event-rule handout
    • the class reviews participation restrictions and preparation needs while Hirata remains withdrawn.
  • pp.46–54.
    • Sudō and Ike question Ayanokōji’s praise votes
    • Horikita explains Sakayanagi’s plan
    • Ayanokōji volunteers as commander, Shinohara objects, and Horikita supports Ayanokōji taking the role.

About these pages. rule-heavy explanation chapter with class discussion.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. Event Selection Exam: 10 proposed events per class, 5 submitted, 7 randomly selected; commanders required, may intervene, expelled on class loss; ±30 class points per event and +100 to winning class (pp.29–41).

Unattributed lines. none.

Translation errors, kept as printed. none observed.

Chapter 3: "Opponents" p.55–92

Setting. Class C classroom and hallway; multipurpose room in the special building; Ayanokōji’s dorm room.

Narrative beats

  • pp.55–59.
    • At lunch and after school, Class C gathers to prepare for the Event Selection Exam
    • Kōenji and Hirata are absent, Mii-chan chases after Hirata, and Horikita takes the lead.
  • pp.60–71.
    • Commanders meet in the multipurpose room. Kaneda wins the draw and chooses Class B, so Class D faces Class B and Class C faces Class A
    • teachers explain the commander system and event-day interface.
  • pp.72–92.
    • Back at the dorm, Kei visits Ayanokōji’s room
    • she shares information about the girls in Class C and receives a birthday/White Day necklace from him.

About these pages. includes class match-up decision and commander procedures for the Event Selection Exam.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. Event-day commander interface: timed participant selection, random fill/removal on errors, live broadcasts, rule-based interventions, and text-to-speech instructions (pp.67–71); final matchups set as Class C vs Class A and Class D vs Class B (pp.64–65).

Unattributed lines. none.

Translation errors, kept as printed. none observed.

Chapter 4: "What the Class Lacks" p.93–169

Setting. Cafeteria and Class C classroom; school corridor and route to dorms; path to school the next morning.

Narrative beats

  • pp.93–109.
    • The Ayanokōji Group eats lunch and reviews Class C’s slow preparation, Class A’s information advantage, Hirata’s withdrawn state, and low odds against Class A.
  • pp.110–124.
    • After school, Horikita leads a discussion
    • Hirata leaves
    • Kōenji participates briefly but refuses to commit to helping on exam day.
  • pp.125–136.
    • The next morning Horikita and Ayanokōji discuss handling Hirata
    • Mii-chan tries to invite Hirata to lunch and is harshly rejected, and Kei’s attempt to intervene is shut down.
  • pp.137–159.
    • Another class discussion moves planning to a group chat to avoid eavesdropping
    • Mii-chan follows Hirata, Horikita tries to stop her, Ayanokōji goes after Mii-chan and encourages her to reach Hirata
    • Hashimoto is seen watching/eavesdropping and later speaks with Ayanokōji
    • Sudō proposes basketball and Horikita gathers event ideas.
  • pp.159–169.
    • On the way to school, Hoshinomiya talks with Ayanokōji about dorm relationships and the coming Class C vs Class A match
    • Chabashira intervenes afterward.

About these pages. class-preparation chapter showing Class C’s lack of unity, Hirata’s isolation, and continued information pressure from Class A.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. none.

Unattributed lines. none.

Translation errors, kept as printed. Hirata’s line break renders as `“Would you mind not calling me by my given name so intimately like that?` / `You don’t have anything to do with me anymore, alright?”` across pp.133–134 in the text (kept as printed).

Tells her father the experiment cannot create artificial geniuses because potential is set at birth. Ch.1: Staff meeting on the first-years’ examsY1V11 · p.13–20notes on Tsukishiro

As acting director, finishes explaining the first-years’ final special exam and a new system to the staff. Invites questions and responds when Mashima challenges the Class Vote’s risk of expulsions. Smiles and argues that students have always faced expulsion risk under the school’s rules. Dismisses Mashima’s objections as old-fashioned and says the new system will focus on first-years. States he was sent by a higher corporate body backed by the government and will not reconsider the provisional exam. Ends the meeting after mentioning he is considering a cultural festival next year.
