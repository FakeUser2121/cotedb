Prologue: Kanzaki Ryūji's Monologue p.9–10

Setting. Kanzaki Ryūji's monologue; his childhood; Advanced Nurturing High School entrance.

Narrative beats. Kanzaki recalls watching a group of children denounce a liar (an autograph-story fake) while an accomplice shielded him "simply because he was his friend"; an adult's hand on his head delivered the line he still remembers: "If you don't have the power to help, you can run away or ignore it. But if you have the power and don't use it, you are a fool." On entering ANHS he chose to socialize and tried to support Ichinose, the recognized class leader — "However, in the end, I was not able to do so, and my heart was broken." Ayanokōji Kiyotaka's words rescued him; "Truly, fate is a mysterious thing."

About these pages. Kanzaki's orientation toward the events of the volume (his own heartbreak and debt to Ayanokōji).

New names. none (Kanzaki, Ichinose, Ayanokōji all rostered).

New rules and facts. none.

Unattributed lines. none (monologue POV).

Translation errors, kept as printed. the monologue mixes straight and curly quotation marks; kept as printed.

Chapter 1: Know the enemy and know yourself p.11–45

Setting. School; homeroom; late November, second semester finals.

Narrative beats. Final exam results announced in homeroom (Hondō last at 53, no expulsion; Sudō 10th, a personal best; Horikita and Keisei tie 1st at 93.5; Kei ≈56 despite her nerves); Class B takes 2nd place (+25 pts, behind Class A, ahead of Ichinose's Class D and Ryūen's Class C); this year the exam is standardized — last year's Paper Shuffle (Kushida's plans vs Ryuen's influence vs Horikita's strategy) no longer applies; an anonymous 0–37 evaluation task assigns every second-year student (156 students; Horikita/Keisei/Mii-chan rated high; Kōenji rated No. 2 in Class B) under one hour, memory and feelings only, zero OAA use; Hokkaido wins the destination vote (Horikita/Keisei/Ayanokōji voted Kyoto); the trip is five days, four nights, ski resort day 1, day 2 free, day 3 Futsatsu Horoporo, day 4 free with conditions, day 5 return; no special exam promised; groups fixed (one boy and one girl from each second-year class; 18 groups of eight and two groups of six; group behavior mandatory outside the ryokan; phones allowed; cash exchange up to 10,000 yen); Sun Tzu's line sets the theme; lunch talk with Kei, Hirata and Satō smells a setup — Ayanokōji suspects the chart and trip are set pieces for the end-of-year exam; Amasawa and Nanase meet him on a bench by Keyaki Mall (Amasawa decides to stay); he warns Nanase about the trip's open-air danger and probes Tsukishiro's design; Sudō declares he will confess to Horikita on the trip (Ayanokōji helping witness it); Kei eavesdrops, and they bet on the outcome (expensive Christmas present vs. "do whatever I say").

About these pages. final exams, the anonymous evaluation task, the school trip announcement; stakes converging on the trip.

New names. none (all rostered).

New rules and facts. final exam scoring (50/25/−25/−50; 39-average cutoff); anonymous 0–37 evaluation task rules (one repeat number forbidden; own entry typed first; secret from teachers and students; memory-only; one hour); destination vote (Hokkaido 3 of 4 class votes); five-day/four-night school trip; mixed fixed groups (one boy and one girl per second-year class; 18×8 + 2×6; 156 students); group-action mandatory outside the ryokan ("deprivation of freedom" otherwise); cell phones permitted (second-years and emergency contacts only); cash exchange up to 10,000 yen, refundable to private points after; the Sun Tzu theme; no special exam on the trip.

Unattributed lines. the "I was surprised too" / "I don't mind having a lot of free time" / "I guess I'm a little concerned about the group aspect" lines in the lunch scene (pp.25–26) are untagged in the translation; the closing "genuinely scared" / "kind of a bad feeling" lines are left unattributed (shared by Yōsuke and Satō); the "It's kind of like a double date" quip is Satō's, not Kei's (misattributed to Kei in an earlier pass and corrected in this one).

Translation errors, kept as printed. "Okay…?" in Hondō's line p.15 (straight quote marks inside a curly-quoted line in the original); dash style varies (–, —) kept as printed.

Chapter Two: A School Trip that Works Exactly as it Sounds p.46–89

Setting. Haneda Airport → Shin-Chitose; the ski resort; the ryokan (boys/girls wings, outdoor rock bath, Room 203).

Narrative beats. Morning buses split by class (Mashima No.1, Chabashira No.2, Sakagami No.3, Hoshinomiya No.4); the group-6 master list (Kitō + Yamamura, Class A; Ayanokōji + Kushida, Class B; Ryūen + Nishino, Class C; Watanabe + Amikura, Class D); the anonymous-evaluation task seeds the fixed groups. Bus: no assigned seats — Kei sits with Akito, Yōsuke brokers the Ayanokōji/Kei seat swap; Ryūen probes Ayanokōji about Yagami ("I hear there's another girl left, Amasawa"); the etiquette-bag existential confusion. Ski lesson (~60% beginner; Ryūen advanced, fluoro-wax expert); Ayanokōji secretly skis advanced with help; Kushida randori-reads him on the lift ("I can't read you, Ayanokōji-kun."); the knit-hat catch moment. Ryūen's class conclave (Kaneda/Ishizaki/Todo) holds on the advanced course; Yamamura stalks it from above. Ryokan: Ryūen hurls his Boston bag at Watanabe ("Take it to the room."); Room 203 assignment (12 tatami); girls' visit at 10 p.m. (the shampoo mystery); futon politics — Ryūen claims the end spot, Kitō vetoes ("I don't approve."), Watanabe gives up his end; the pillow fight ("Darkness ball!" from Kitō) ends with a pillow burst over the room; skiing decided for day 2. Watanabe lets slip he heard the girls talk about Ichinose ("Well, now I can understand why Ichinose likes you so much…").

About these pages. the school trip begins; group formation; cross-class dynamics in confinement.

New names. Kaneda (Class C), Ishizaki Daichi, Todo Chikon, Rokkaku Momoe — all rostered; Ibuki (Class B), Sakagami-sensei, Hoshinomiya-sensei named on-page.

New rules and facts. phones prohibited on the slopes; ski lesson splits beginner/intermediate/advanced; ryokan lights-out at 11:00 p.m.; dinner 7:00 p.m.; karaoke on the bus; cash kept minimal (school keeps outgoing-call logs); the anonymous evaluation splits the 156 students into 18 groups of 8 + 2 of 6; cell phones allowed during the trip (within the school).

Unattributed lines. "Maybe she's just trying to be nice" (p.48), "Come on, it's a mystery for sure." (p.79), "I can't help but think that boys are really just children, aren't they?" (p.87), "How about you, Mako-chan?" (p.88) — all untagged in the translation; not assigned.

Translation errors, kept as printed. straight/curly quote mixing; dash style varies; Ryūen's dropped f-word kept as printed.

Chapter 3: School Trip: Second Day p.90–129

Setting. The ryokan (morning room), ski resort (lifts, advanced and beginner courses), food court, large public bath.

Narrative beats. Morning TV (former Secretary-Gen. Naona Ee dies; Prime Minister Kijima Onikijima speaks; "Let the man be with you, and the horse with you"); rain delay; snowman; Yamamura watches bare-handed; Sakayanagi boards the sightseeing bus after a cane-slip, Tokitō props her up; split skiing; Ryūen vs Kitō dead heat, Ayanokōji breaks it; Kōenji horseback-rides the slope chased by staff; lunch court (Ryūen's 800M points plan; Kushida's warning text; Sakayanagi's information war); five Gifu visitors yank Yamamura/Nishino — Ryūen and Kitō make them kneel at the girls' feet; bath night — Sudō confesses to Horikita ("I love you! Please go out with me!"), she declines gently but grabs his trembling hand ("I certainly received your feelings. Thank you for liking me."); afterward Ayanokōji finds Sudō collapsed but radiant; Horikita corners him by the guest rooms ("You saw it, didn't you?") and claims tomorrow night as his trial.

About these pages. day two of the school trip; escalation (800M plan; Gifu incident); the confession.

New names. none (all rostered).

New rules and facts. phones prohibited on slopes; group formation confirmed for free days; ski lesson split by level (beginner/intermediate/advanced); 19:00 return-to-ryokan curfew; confession etiquette informal; translation notes on newscast.

Unattributed lines. "I can't help but think that boys are really just children, aren't they?" (p.87); "How about you, Mako-chan?" (p.88) — untagged in the translation; not assigned.

Translation errors, kept as printed. Horikita addresses Ayanokōji as "Sudō-kun" in the p.128 line ("You're not being fair, Sudō-kun.") — kept as printed and flagged in her entries; dash style varies.

Chapter 4: School Trip: Third Day p.130–161

Setting. Sapporo Station; the bus tour of 15 spots; the ryokan dining hall; Horikita's corner; the second-floor rest area (massage chairs); boys' room.

Narrative beats. The group-6 sightseeing test: visit six of fifteen spots (scores 1–8; 20+ points = 30,000 private points; under six spots and tomorrow's free day is lost to study sessions); Ryūen wants the points; Kitō blocks him; Ayanokōji out-thinks Ryūen with the five-eighths majority; the group chooses leisure. Ishizaki crab-binges at dinner; Katsuragi is quarantined by Matoba and Baba; Ryūen menaces him (“‘I don't understand’. That's what it means to betray your class.”). Kushida confronts Ryūen over her true nature; Ayanokōji intervenes ("I'm sorry, Ryūen, but I'm going to have to ask you to back off on this one.") — his quiet brick-face; Kushida collapses against a wall and breaks down; Horikita confronts Ayanokōji about the cultural festival and Yagami; he discloses the trap; Horikita hears the "joker" Daifugō speech from a drunk Hoshinomiya; Kitō announces his dream (fashion designer).

About these pages. the spot-tour test; the Kushida–Ryūen showdown; the festival/Joker talk; Kitō's dream speech.

New names. Baba (Class A, unrostered; Matoba's sidekick; only a name, no given name).

New rules and facts. the spot-tour test (15 spots, 1–8 points each; 20+ points = 30,000 private points; fewer than 6 = lose day 4 free time; cabs prohibited; photo required); group policy by majority vote; "Daifugō" teacher politics (Hoshinomiya's analogy; the "joker" is Ayanokōji to Class B).

Unattributed lines. none flagged on these pages.

Translation errors, kept as printed. Hoshinomiya-sensei speaks drunk — sentences run on with "~" trailing; kept as printed; Horikita's "Mr. Joker" coinage kept.

Chapter 5: School Trip: Fourth Day p.162–186

Setting. The ryokan; the snowfield; the lobby; the hallway; Sakayanagi's corner.

Narrative beats. Snowball fight for private points (1,000 pts entry; ~30+ signups, 42 students, six teams; Ishizaki's boys vs Sudō's boys — Sudō wins; final Horikita vs Ibuki — Ibuki wins (her eighth graze + dispersed fragments clip Horikita's left arm; Yōsuke rules it a hit)); final all-eight beginners' ski; Sakayanagi summons Ayanokōji to the lobby at 8 p.m. — she reveals first-year Ishigami Kyō (Class A) is Ayanokōji Senior's admirer-recommended student who contacted Kiyotaka several times and "indirectly helped with Yagami's expulsion"; Kanzaki arrives and confirms: he's "fascinated with Ayanokōji-sensei"; he saw it in the OAA; Sakayanagi phones Ishigami, who says he chose ANHS on Ayanokōji Senior's recommendation and foresaw the call; Sakayanagi says she'll only interfere if Ayanokōji Senior orders it. Amikura bursts in — Ichinose is missing.

About these pages. the snowball fight; the Ishigami revelation; Ichinose disappears.

New names. Yano (Class A, rostered; the head-shot that eliminated Yamamura); Mii-chan (Matsushita) and Miyamoto — Group 15 disqualified, study session.

New rules and facts. snowball fight format (1,000-pt entry; winners split the pot; hit = self-report or referee); snowball-fight teams drawn from the second year (42 = 6×7); no special exam on the trip.

Unattributed lines. none flagged on these pages.

Translation errors, kept as printed. the Amikura–Ichinose dialogue keeps the romanized "Mako-chan" address; the Gifu boy's ramen-broth complaint keeps the culinary mismatch as printed.

Chapter 6: The Light At The End Of The Tunnel p.187–195

Setting. The hill/wooden deck behind the ryokan; 9 p.m.; snowstorm; curfew.

Narrative beats. Ayanokōji spots Ichinose on the forbidden hill; negotiates entry; she breaks down about her class losing the lead, the Horikita/Ryūen/Sakayanagi bar, the "White Room" fragments she overheard (the former acting chairman and Shiba-sensei); she confesses the gap between the class leader she wishes she were and the one she is; he pulls her into a hug on the porch; they make it back to the ryokan together before curfew.

About these pages. Ichinose's breaking point; the White Room first surfaces on-page.

New names. none (all rostered).

New rules and facts. school-trip curfew (back by 9 p.m.); the deck is off-limits at night.

Unattributed lines. none flagged on these pages.

Translation errors, kept as printed. none observed.
