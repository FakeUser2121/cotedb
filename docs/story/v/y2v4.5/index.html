Depth pass — line-by-line record (2026-11-19)

About these pages. the line-by-line pass appended scene sections and mention rows to the person files (per chapter: ch1 pp.12–35; ch2 pp.36–72; ch3 pp.73–95; ch4 pp.96–158; ch5 pp.159–216; ch6 pp.217–267; Epilogue pp.268–274; SS pp.275–290). Blank pages: pp.1–2, 7–9, 40, 66, 76, 92, 145, 158, 203, 207, 262, 270, 272. Front matter pp.1–9; TOC pp.4–6.

Setting. a luxury cruise ship, August 4–10 (summer vacation week). Facilities: swimming pool, gym, movie theater, concert hall, bathhouse, shopping area, café terrace, private pool (rentable, 20,000 pts/60 min, up to 40 people), store for snacks. Breakfast/dinner buffet included free (7–9am intervals, 60 min limit).

Class points & standings (August, post-exam, announced late). A (Sakayanagi) 1206 · B (Ichinose) 578 · C (Horikita) 571 · D (Ryūen) 551. Kōenji's solo win earned the class 300 class points, lifting Class 2-D to third; one step short of Class B. Kōenji holds an "exempted from all cooperation" card until graduation.

Private points mechanics seen in force. 57,100 private points per person at 571 class points (571 class points = 100 yen each); individual cap before syphoning is 500,000 points — anything beyond is syphoned to Nagumo; tickets to Class A cost 20 million points; Kōenji's solo win earned his class 300 class points; Ryūen acquired about 280,000 points from first-years via 'Free Ride' cards, betting on Nagumo.

Treasure Hunt Game (Aug 8, 10am–5pm, 10,000 points entry). 100 QR stickers placed around the ship; single phone scan per code per person (app); prizes: 5,000×50, 10,000×30, 50,000×10, 100,000×5, 300,000×3, 500,000×1, 1,000,000×1; pairing (any year) doubles the reward of whichever scans; riddles for 3 of the codes; scan app verifies; names registered in a shared roster to prevent proxy plays; participants could scan from stored photos of QR codes (technique used by Ayanokōji & Satō).

Treasure Hunt administration. the teachers and the student council run it together; Horikita's fraud-prevention measure (name registration at payment) was her own suggestion; the council desk is staffed by Yagami and Kushida; the school lets ~200 students join out of ~300.

The next special exam (unnamed in the text). first used 11 years ago (third semester of the third year); the exam that ruined Chabashira's and Hoshinomiya's Class A graduation dream; re-adopted now without mandatory expulsions; used 11 years ago on the same 4-year rotation; the school expects second-years to strain; the name of the exam is never printed.

White Room students (named). Amasawa Ichika (first-year Class A); Yagami Takuya (second-year Class B); both are original students of the White Room. Amasawa was expelled from it; Yagami is still attached to it; both point at Ayanokōji; the White Room itself is never named on-page among this volume's exam events.

Other names (not roster entities). Takatō-sensei (3rd-year Class A homeroom teacher; treasure-hunt MC; added to the roster this pass as `takato`), Kinugasa (author), Hermann Hesse (Beneath the Wheel — literary reference), Miyamoto Musashi (rhetorical reference), Kisarazu (Class B), Kibayashi, Mochizuki, Etō (handwriting candidates), Yamaga (cabin mate of Horikita).

Unattributed lines. Himeno's "annoying" (p.65) — kept untagged. "Another time, Matsushita." (p.173) — untagged, kept unassigned. "But yeah, that's too bad…" (p.176) — untagged, both read plausible by position; not assigned. "A voice she didn't recognise called out" (p.55) — Kobashi, named on the next line.

Translation defects, kept as printed. "senfai" (p.75, Nanase's voice); "Matushita" (p.174, missing u); translator-watermark lines are stripped by verification tooling; curly-quote variance across volumes; the Nanase SS attributes the bow-deck interruption to "Ninomiya" while the main text names Kobashi Yume (both kept as printed); "Hydro" does not appear; blank pages are kept.

Attribution gaps (recorded, not resolved). the origin of the Karuizawa–Ayanokōji information Nagumo wields; the Ryūen–Sakayanagi bargain (terms withheld); Hōsen's grudge against Ryūen (unstated); the expulsion note's author until Horikita re-verifies him (Yagami by his own account); the SS's "Ninomiya" vs the main text's "Kobashi"; the two Tokitōs (Katsumi vs Hiroya, distant relatives, shared surname); the class placement of Yamaga; the award Katsuragi will get for recruiting Hōsen against him; the wording of the unnamed special exam.

System facts. individuals hold up to 500,000 points before excess syphons to Nagumo (Kiriyama's account, p.51); Class A ticket = 20 million points; the third year shifted to individual competition early; all 15 expelled in the exam were third-years; only Kōenji holds "exempted from all cooperation" until graduation; Chancellor Sakayanagi reinstated; Tsukishiro dismissed as Acting Director.
