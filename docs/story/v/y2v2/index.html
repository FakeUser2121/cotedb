Prologue: The White Room p.6–10

Setting. The White Room, in a 1st-year Advanced Nurturing classroom; POV: unnamed White Room 5th-generation student.

Narrative beats. The narrator — a 5th-generation White Room student a year below Ayanokōji — monologues on the 4th-generation “god” who dominated the program; the organization's true goal (mass production, not number-1 individuals); his resolve to make Ayanokōji fail, made possible at last because Ayanokōji broke orders and refused to return to the restarted White Room.

New names. none (the narrator is unnamed; the POV character's identity is not printed).

New rules and facts. 4th-generation Ayanokōji Kiyotaka was the White Room's model; 5th generation now trains separately; refusal to return to the restarted White Room is what creates the narrator's opening.

Unattributed lines. none (all speech is the narrator's monologue; the instructor's cold words are recalled quotation).

Translation errors, kept as printed. none observed.

Chapter 1: School Life Is Changing p.11–63

Setting. Class 2-D classroom (pp.12–22); the reception-room corridor and Chabashira's office route (pp.23–26); the 2-D classroom (pp.27–38); the hallway after class (pp.39–45); Keyaki Mall entrance (pp.46–53); school ground / shoe lockers (pp.53–63).

Narrative beats. Yukimura shakes his leg until Ayanokōji returns to class; the class erupts over the perfect math score (nobody else in the year, not Ichinose or Sakayanagi, got full marks); Sudō shuts down the cheating talk; Hirata disperses the class; Chabashira waits for Ayanokōji, overflowing with joy; he returns to the classroom and faces the group — Keisei leads, Haruka and Airi back him, Miyake absent (club); Horikita improvises the “reserve strategy” cover (seatmate discovery of his talent, the 20-million-point parallel left unspoken, Yamauchi as precedent, Sudō's growth as proof); Yousuke's covering fire closes the topic; the class disperses; the Ayanokōji group corners him after class — Keisei confesses the hurt, Haruka forces the handshake, tutoring resumes; next morning at Keyaki Mall he meets Ichinose and Kanzaki (Class 2-C, fallen from B): Kanzaki hostile, Ichinose loyal; after Golden Week he and Sudō walk and talk at the lockers — Sudō reveals he fought Housen, grapples with the perfect-score mystery, admits he will not tell anyone about the fight, and jokes about Suzune.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. the tablet lets any student see the 2nd-year exam results across classes (noticed by the class on p.15); Hirata usually leads the class but stayed quiet until the end this time.

Unattributed lines. none (Haruka's p.40 line and similar are attributed in context).

Chapter 2: The Flowing Passage of Time p.64–108

Setting. 2-D classroom and lobby/school (pp.65–74); the student-council office (pp.75–82); Ayanokōji's dorm room (pp.83–108).

Narrative beats. End of April/start of May; the second-year OAA evaluates everyone (Ayanokōji: Academics A- 81, Physical B- 61, Adaptability D+ 40, Social Contribution B 68, Overall B- 62; Sudō climbs to Overall B- 63); Horikita walks Ayanokōji to school and they reason through the 20-million-point bounty (special exam vs private contract; the first-years' silence; Nagumo suspected by elimination); after school Horikita joins the student council (her “brother's path” story; Nagumo admits her; sets the presidents-graduate-in-A bar aloud; Kiriyama attends for her); Ayanokōji declines Nagumo's casual invitation; that evening Kei visits — they nearly kiss, the entrance doorbell rings; it is Amasawa, who watched Kei enter; she forces her way in with the plastic bag of ingredients, drops a contraceptive “gift” on the table, confirms aloud she left a trace in the knife plot, and extracts the 20-million-point bounty from Karuizawa's open mouth; when she leaves the atmosphere is dead.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. The OAA updates monthly and can be checked on the school tablet by anyone in the 2nd year; second-year evaluations now count; on the 2nd year's side, Sudō is the year's biggest climber. For the coming exam: Group formation rules, rewards and penalties, and the card system are NOT yet announced (Ch.3); the school does not state the exam is a first-year initiative — it is being traced to private capital.

Chapter 3: Summer's Approaching, Premonition of a Fierce Battle p.109–218

Setting. School route/classroom (pp.111–121); the homeroom briefing (pp.117–141); the classroom and corridors (pp.142–151); Albert's room and Keyaki Mall (pp.152–195); the café/cafeteria (pp.195–218).

Narrative beats. In mid-June, Nanase walks with Ayanokōji and apologizes for the special exam; she reveals the 1st-years' end-of-May exam is over (Class 1-D placed third; Class C lost an A-ranked boy) and that the bounty is still active till second semester. Homeroom: Chabashira announces the Uninhabited Island Survival exam (July 20–Aug 3, all years, cross-class small groups up to 3 pre-exam by July 16, big groups up to 6 in-exam, 2:3 gender ratios small / 1:2 big, relief 6M points, bottom-5 groups expelled); the luxury cruise follows Aug 4–11. Kouenji's sick-leave loophole is priced out; he can't afford it. After school, outstanding students' phones light up for groups; Horikita's stays dead; she asks him to hold. Ishizaki/Albert/Hiyori capture Ayanokōji and pitch a Class B group with 20M points; Matsushita Chiaki stakes him out and offers herself; Shinohara and Komiya drift together; Kushida redirects Ike; Komiya plans to confess on the island; Ike crumbles; Ayanokōji and Horikita rehearse a confession plan and fail — the OAA shows Shinohara grouped with Komiya and Kinoshita Minori; Sakayanagi makes her move on Ichinose (alliance, trust as currency, semi-eliminated position, the More People card, 3M+2M deposits); Ichinose names Sakayanagi's true fear (the three-class alliance) and accepts. Ayanokōji receives the "Trial" card the next morning; he resolves internally to keep casualties minimal if Tsukishiro strikes.

New names. Hatano and Takahashi Osamu appear in Ch.4, not Ch.3 (roster already carries both — see Ch.4). Card holders named: Asakura Mako (Class C, More People), Yano Koharu (Class A, Nullify), Ayanokōji (Trial).

New rules and facts. The exam schedule and full reward/penalty structure (pp.121–137); the group rules and the 8 cards (pp.124–137); groups unswitchable after formation; small groups ≤3 pre-exam (July 16); mixed-gender ratio 2:3 small, 1:2 big; the relief cost 6M points (1M per person for groups of 6); Sakayanagi's “semi-eliminated” position (p.214); Ichinose's 5-person deposit pact (pp.216–217).

Unattributed lines. the front-row strategy circle's “The particularly important part of this exam...” block (pp.142–146) — speakers untagged in the translation; Sakayanagi's “Class B, there are you and Kanzaki-kun, as well as Shibata-kun” (p.215) — Ichinose and Kanzaki are Class 2-C, not Class B; the "why / now" layer is printed but could not be tagged; Ike's p.170 lines cleaned — see his person record. Chapter 5: An Invitation p.293–325

Setting. Keyaki Mall / lobby / classrooms (pp.294–325).

Narrative beats. Less than one week remains to form small groups; Ishizaki emails Ayanokōji to a café meeting on Ryuuen's behalf. Kanzaki stops him at the store and begs for help with Ichinose (who has declined into Class C; he's desperate and apologizes for his own pushes); Ryuuen sits with Ayanokōji and Katsuragi of Sakayanagi's faction and launches a three-class alliance for Class A; Katsuragi stands by Sakayanagi's banner; Ryuuen reveals he knows Ayanokōji beat him; offers 20 million points for Katsuragi's transfer to Class B; the contract nullification costs 5 million points; Ayanokōji sells him the Trial card for 500k points (Half Off card swap from Katsuragi to Class D). Later, Ishizaki reports almost everyone has grouped (Ibuki Mio still alone); Ayanokōji mediates the Horikita–Ibuki summit — it fails completely (their grudge is historical); the 1st-year deadline arrives Friday; his own group question hangs unresolved as the storm approaches.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. Group formation deadline July 16; almost all students are paired; the Trial card sale/win bonus math for Ryuuen (first place = 450 class points); Katsuragi's Half Off card goes to Class D; the “1st-year = friendlier deal” observation on cards.

Translation errors, kept as printed. "Kanazki" for Kanzaki (p.297); no volume-wide project issues on these pages; story-specific defects are recorded in the person files.
