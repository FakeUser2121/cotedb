Amasawa: White Room prologue (her POV)Y2V2 · p.6–10notes on Amasawa Ichika

In her own voice she explains she is a fifth-generation White Room student, that she has always been compared to the fourth-generation "god" Ayanokōji Kiyotaka and found wanting, and that she worships only hatred, not him; she watched him through a visiting room and shivered at his figure.

with Ayanokōji Kiyotaka

4 more notes
  • Caveat This is Amasawa's interior monologue (her POV); no spoken line is quoted.p.6–10
  • Trait Driven by hatred rather than worship — she refuses to revere Ayanokōji and treats him as her enemy, the obstacle to her being recognized as the White Room's success story.p.6–10
  • Goal To prove Ayanokōji is not number one and to be recognized by the organization as the true success; the opportunity comes now that he has refused to return to the restarted White Room.p.6–10
  • Relationship Ayanokōji Kiyotaka The fourth-generation "god" she has been measured against her whole life; she has resolved to bury him.p.6–10

The classroom erupts over his perfect math scoreY2V2 · p.12–22notes on Ayanokōji Kiyotaka

Back in class after Tsukishiro's summons, the Ayanokōji group waits anxiously; Yukimura shakes his leg, Haruka tries to calm him, Sakura and Miyake sit with them. When Ayanokōji returns, Yukimura confronts him about the 100 in math — no one else in the year, not even Ichinose or Sakayanagi, got full marks. The class speculates wildly — cheating, hacking, seeing the paper in advance — while Sudō silences the cheating talk ("there isn't even a shred of evidence"), and Hirata steps in to disperse the club members and defuse the tension.

with Yukimura Teruhiko · Horikita Suzune · Sudō Ken

  • Ayanokōji Kiyotaka "It's not a question of whether it's hard or not. I didn't even understand the meaning of the question during the exam."p.14
