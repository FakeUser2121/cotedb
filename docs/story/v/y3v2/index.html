Coverage notes (Year 3, Vol. 2)
Prologue: Nanase Tsubasa's Monologue (pp. 10–12) — depth read

Setting. A hospital room; Matsuo Eiichirou lies in a vegetative state; Nanase speaks with Tsukishiro.

Beats

  • p.10: Nanase sits beside the unconscious Eiichirou, diagnosed with a prolonged disturbance of consciousness, and blames herself for not noticing something was wrong.
  • pp.10–11: Tsukishiro appears — he knew her and Eiichirou since childhood, references "Shirogane-san," and offers her "an opportunity to exact your revenge." He shows an application form and photo of Ayanokōji.
  • pp.11–12: Tsukishiro explains the target is Ayanokōji Kiyotaka, already at ANHS; he instructs her to mix lies with truth, build a persona around her genuine grief for Eiichirou, and approach Ayanokōji as an "insignificant being" who won't be scrutinized. He chose her because she is in a perfect in-between position.
  • p.12: Nanase accepted. Now her motive has shifted — she wants to help Ayanokōji, so he won't walk the path of pure evil and create more victims like Eiichirou.

Open threads. Nanase's true mission for Tsukishiro vs. her new wish to save Ayanokōji; Ayanokōji Atsuomi as the true target.

Attribution gap. none.

Ch.1: Fitting In (pp. 13–42) — depth read

Setting. The dorm lobby on a rainy Sunday morning; a bookstore and vending machines at Keyaki Mall; the walk back in the rain.

Beats (pp.13–28)

  • pp.13–16: Ayanokōji meets Yoshida and Shimazaki in the lobby. Shimazaki, a studious former Class A student, reveals he studies ~5 hours a day and attends a cram school; he invites Ayanokōji out saying he'll "make you confess all of your secrets."
  • pp.17–21: At the bookstore, Shimazaki asks about Ayanokōji's study methods and study guides. Ayanokōji demurs, giving partial truths (internet videos, browsing guides); they help each other and Yoshida pick suitable guides.
  • pp.21–28: Yoshida buys fashion/romance magazines; the three discuss the "100-man slayer" rumor about Shiraishi. Shimazaki theorizes Nishikawa started it to keep boys away, possibly because Shiraishi likes girls — an unsettling idea for Yoshida.

Beats (pp.29–42)

  • pp.29–33: By the vending machines, they debate drink sizes; Shimazaki presses Ayanokōji again about his study secrets, saying he'll repay the information "in kind someday."
  • pp.34–39: Yoshida is crushed by the possibility Shiraishi likes girls; Ayanokōji advises retreat is also courage, then reveals Shimazaki also likes her. Yoshida declares he wants to go out with Shiraishi.
  • pp.40–42: Ayanokōji admits he envies Yoshida's ability to feel romantic love, which he still does not understand despite dating Karuizawa. He concludes his analysis of Yoshida and Shimazaki as straightforward, indispensable classmates.

Open threads. Shimazaki's debt of information; the Shiraishi "100-man slayer" lie; Ayanokōji's inability to feel romance.

Attribution gap. none.

Ch.2: A Gentle Exam (pp. 43–65) — depth read

Setting. Class 3-C classroom during homeroom; the cafeteria at lunch.

Beats (pp.43–51)

  • pp.43–45: Morishita teases Ayanokōji about fitting in. Mashima abruptly announces a new exam starting "tomorrow," with only rewards given (+50/+20/0/−25 class points), no rules, no questions. Ayanokōji notes the ambiguous wording.
  • pp.45–51: The class debates the meaning — does "starts tomorrow" mean the exam begins or a preparation week? Sanada polls; opinions split (19 vs 12). Shimazaki and Tsukaji suggest a written test; Ayanokōji proposes "attitude in daily life" is being observed, echoing the first-year unannounced conduct exam. The intent, he judges, is to see how long they can maintain tension under vague goals.

Beats (pp.52–65)

  • pp.52–54: Shiraishi invites Ayanokōji to lunch; Yoshida transparently tries to muscle in; Nishikawa and, at Ayanokōji's request, Kitou join.
  • pp.55–65: At lunch, Ike and Hondou avoid them. Yoshida shows Ayanokōji's new OAA (Academic A+ 96, Physical A 81, Adaptability B 66, Social B+ 77, Overall B+ 80) — top-class among third-years. Yoshida cheerfully roots for him as a "comrade"; Nishikawa is skeptical and suspects he's an assassin sent by Horikita; Kitou has no interest in others' OAA but wants to know why he hid his ability and why he got serious now. Shiraishi suggests they share private-life details to know each other. Kitou barely participates and leaves.

Open threads. The ambiguous exam and what it tests; Nishikawa's suspicion; Kitou's incomplete trust.

Attribution gap. none.

Ch.3: Getting to Know the Opponent (pp. 66–125) — depth read

Setting. Class 3-A classroom; the student council room; Keyaki Mall supermarket; Horikita's and Sudō's rooms; Kōenji's room; the dorm hallway.

Beats (pp.66–88)

  • pp.66–68: Class A grumbles about Chabashira's cold refusal to explain the exam; Sudō and Hirata urge calm; Horikita proposes brainstorming. She senses Chabashira's coldness is deliberate.
  • pp.69–74: Horikita chases Kōenji after he walks out of the discussion. He rebuffs her — a "contract" means he owes nothing, and he bluntly says a victory against other classes is impossible while she leads. When she persists, he shows a flicker of killing intent and warns her not to make an enemy of him; she stops.
  • pp.85–88: In the student council room, Horikita and Nanase analyze the first-year fight between Kusanagi and Maki; Horikita deduces a single girl may have beaten both (too embarrassed to admit it) and asks Nanase to help investigate. She voices self-doubt about being "mediocre."

Beats (pp.89–113)

  • pp.89–93: At the supermarket, Horikita asks Karuizawa's opinion; they discuss the exam theories and the likelihood that Ayanokōji is feeding information to Ichinose's Class D. Karuizawa argues Ayanokōji would have gone to Class D and even expelled Ichinose if that were his goal.
  • pp.94–95: Back at her room, Ibuki (who came for a meal) is warned by Horikita not to kick people in the back again.
  • pp.111–113: Kushida, staying after Ibuki, thanks Horikita, asks about the exam, and offers to help think — while saying she "loves the cute me who pretends to be kind." Horikita realizes Chabashira's attitude is the hint: this is likely a repeat of the first-year conduct exam.

Beats (pp.114–125)

  • pp.115–118: Horikita visits Sudō (studying with Onodera) and shares her theory — the exam tests daily conduct/discipline, echoing the first-year month-long exam; they agree to keep it secret and have the class live disciplined starting tomorrow.
  • pp.120–125: Horikita and Sudō visit Kōenji's room. She offers 200,000 points for one week of perfect discipline; he doubles it to 500,000, seeing through her bluff. The deal is struck and kept secret. Sudō agrees to keep quiet; Horikita returns to her room warmed by having reliable allies.

Open threads. The student-council investigation into the first-year fight; whether the exam is really about conduct; secrecy of the Kōenji deal.

Attribution gap. none.

Ch.4: The Quest for Knowledge (pp. 126–151) — depth read

Setting. The dorm elevator/lobby; Keyaki Mall café; the walk back to school.

Beats (pp.126–135)

  • pp.126–128: Ayanokōji tries to leave early for school but is blocked from the elevator by Class A girls; he ends up walking with Hiyori, but can't find words, and Hashimoto, Morishita and Shiraishi intercept him, driving Hiyori away.
  • pp.128–130: Class C's group confirms his group-chat instructions about the conduct exam; Shiraishi defends Sakayanagi's legacy, Morishita calls Hashimoto a "traitor." Kondou and Komiya from Ryūen's class arrive and apply pressure, fishing for hints.
  • pp.131–135: At the café, Hashimoto reports no useful intel from other classes. Shiraishi joins. They tease Ayanokōji about "ogling" Shiraishi; Morishita plays along to make him disliked.

Beats (pp.136–145)

  • pp.136–139: The serious discussion: Ayanokōji says he isn't focused on winning because the reward is small and conduct can't decide the outcome. Shiraishi asks his probability — "over 90%" — and he explains his deduction: he compared the teachers' parting words across all four classes (Mashima "befit a student," Hoshinomiya "your usual selves," Chabashira "different from when you enrolled"), confirming conduct is the focus. He keeps his sources (Ichinose, Horikita) secret.
  • pp.139–145: They discuss the likelihood of a high-stakes, expulsion exam next; Morishita notes third-years face higher expulsion risk (~15 expelled last year). Ike and Hondou arrive and vent — blaming Ayanokōji for Sakura, Maezono and Yamauchi's expulsions. Ayanokōji stays cold, letting them direct their hatred at him to unite Class A against him, and paints Class C as peaceful.

Beats (pp.146–151)

  • pp.146–149: On the walk back, Shiraishi joins him to the school. They discuss why she trusted him early; she reintroduces herself ("I'm Shiraishi Asuka") and asks who he is — a deliberate, teasing reset. He is struck again by how little he understands her.

Open threads. The all-but-confirmed conduct exam; the anticipated high-stakes expulsion exam; Shiraishi's deliberate mystery.

Attribution gap. none. TL note (p.129) on "the righteous" wordplay.

Ch.5: Yamamura's Courage (pp. 152–181) — depth read

Setting. Class 3-C classroom; Keyaki Mall rest area/second floor/south entrance; the school library approach; a karaoke parlor.

Beats (pp.152–167)

  • pp.152–155: Morishita pulls Ayanokōji's hair and says Yamamura wants to see him; she leads him on a deliberately pointless detour (the floor "still warm," the "Ear Gun"), revealing her comic, manipulative style.
  • pp.156–163: Yamamura appears silently outside the south entrance. With Morishita's rough help (blowing in her ear to ease her tension), she tells Ayanokōji she wants to change — to gain confidence and smile in front of others — and asks to help reach Class A. He tasks her with investigating her own classmate Shiraishi.
  • pp.164–167: Hashimoto tracks them down by process of elimination; Morishita rejects him. Alone, Hashimoto insists he has committed fully to Class C and Class A (having given up his escape funds), and Ayanokōji notes this.

Beats (pp.168–174)

  • pp.168–169: On the way to the library, Ayanokōji admits to himself he has been avoiding seeing Hiyori because he doesn't want to see her sadness after rejecting her transfer invitation.
  • pp.170–174: Hasebe stops him, worried her earlier approach caused his transfer; he reassures her it's unrelated. She shows him that the expelled Sakura Airi is now appearing on late-night TV — her "hard work." He agrees to hear it, seeing a chance to win over his former class.

Beats (pp.175–181)

  • Kaneda arrives early to meet Shiina at the karaoke parlor; Ishizaki and Ryūen join. Ryūen asks Shiina her read on Ayanokōji; Ishizaki calls it the "Ayanokouji Filter" (her bias). Ryūen learns Hashimoto has cut contact with Kaneda (committing to Ayanokōji). He discusses the next high-stakes exam and says he'll use any means, while Katsuragi insists on rules. He tells Shiina he'll make her useful — to the class, not to Ayanokōji — exploiting her feelings.

Open threads. Yamamura's investigation of Shiraishi; the next high-stakes exam; Shiina's unresolved feelings.

Attribution gap. none.

Ch.6: Fortune and Misfortune Are Intertwined (pp. 182–210) — depth read

Setting. Class 3-C classroom; the special building; the library; the school exit.

Beats (pp.182–189)

  • pp.182–184: Five days into the exam, all classes maintain perfect conduct; Morishita shoots Ayanokōji with a rubber-band "gun" she built. Satonaka invites him to hang out Sunday (with Yanagibashi, Tsukasaki, Hoashi); Morishita refuses.
  • pp.184–189: Ayanokōji meets Hoshinomiya in the special building (they each claim the other was called by Ichinose). She presses against him to test whether cameras would catch "inappropriate" contact — confirming his conduct-exam theory. He tells her not to interfere to make her class win, invokes Chabashira, and asks her to consider the alliance long-term.

Beats (pp.190–202)

  • pp.190–194: Ayanokōji finally visits Hiyori in the library. She repeatedly hides from him; he asks if she's still bothered by his rejection of her transfer invitation. She apologizes, saying she got her hopes up and had resolved to see him as an enemy. He proposes they talk about the past month; she smiles for the first time that day.
  • pp.195–200: They talk; Kaneda joins (a frequent library visitor now). With Hiyori called away by the librarian, Kaneda speaks at length — praising Ayanokōji as a "hero"/"anti-hero," envying him, disparaging himself, and asking what if Hiyori hated him. Ayanokōji has no answer. Kaneda leaves abruptly. Ayanokōji realizes Hiyori has been making friends (Kaneda) and that she is "a little different from the frameworks" he used — feeling "happiness" reading with her.
  • pp.202+: They leave the library; a flower cart appears.

Open threads. Kaneda's feelings for Hiyori and envy of Ayanokōji; Ayanokōji's emerging feelings for Hiyori; the flower cart.

Attribution gap. none.

Ch.7: The Observer (pp. 211–230) — depth read

Setting. Class 3-C classroom on results day; the gym at Keyaki Mall; the walk home; an unpopular restaurant where Shiraishi and Nanase meet back-to-back.

Beats (pp.211–212). On the reveal day, Rokkaku and Yano are absent (sick after a sneeze from an ill mall employee). Hashimoto worries the absences will cost them in the tight exam; Ayanokōji tells him to ensure no one slanders them. Class C accepts the situation calmly.

Beats (pp.213–220). At the gym, Ayanokōji coaches Watanabe, who wants to join to get closer to Amikura; he devises a plan to bring Kanzaki, Himeno (and initially Hamaguchi) too. Utomiya appears, interested in the gym; Akiyama says he may be concerned about a talented first-year girl he brought (20 km/h treadmill pace), and asks whether Mashima is "a player."

Beats (pp.221–225). Exam results: no ranking change; Class C last (−25) due to the two absentees (Horikita 1240, Ryūen 1081, Ayanokōji 867, Ichinose 864). Ayanokōji asks Ichinose to invite Watanabe, Kanzaki and Himeno to the gym; she sees through his motives. Their hands touch; she apologizes for wanting to touch him "even though we're just friends." He thinks of his blissful library moment with Hiyori and questions whether his new emotion is romance.

Beats (pp.226–230). Shiraishi and Nanase meet back-to-back in a quiet restaurant. Shiraishi reveals she is an "observer" from the White Room — given no role but to spend three years as an ordinary student — and that coincidence put her next to Ayanokōji. Nanase (aligned with Tsukishiro's faction) demands her cooperation/intelligence; Shiraishi refuses, reassuring her only about Hashimoto/Morishita, and warns that if she is expelled before Ayanokōji's fate is decided, things may go badly for Shirogane.

Open threads. The identity of the talented first-year girl Utomiya brought; Ayanokōji's emerging romance (Hiyori vs Ichinose); Shiraishi's observer role and Tsukishiro's faction.

Attribution gap. none. TL note (p.230) on "I am from that side."

Epilogue: Another Story Begins (pp. 231–247) + short stories (pp.249–263) — depth read

Setting. Dorm lobby; Keyaki Mall; the student council room and hallway; the walk back; various short-story settings.

Beats (pp.231–237). Horikita invites Karuizawa out to learn about Ayanokōji's past. Karuizawa agrees to answer what she can. They spot Amasawa and tail her — Karuizawa enjoys the "spy" work. Horikita observes Amasawa keeps to herself. A text summons Horikita to the student council room, cutting the tail short.

Beats (pp.238–247). No one is at the student council room (a possible prank/trap). On her way back, Ishigami Kyou stops her privately. He reveals the person who knows Ayanokōji's past is Nanase (not Amasawa), claims Nanase was in contact with Ayanokōji early on, and asks Horikita to keep his identity secret. He agrees to probe Nanase/Amasawa anonymously while Horikita investigates. The narrator notes this "orchestrated encounter" with Ishigami is the beginning of a great change — Horikita becoming deeply involved in Ayanokōji's life, unaware of the White Room.

Short stories. Ichinose ("To Love Too Much" — her aching desire to touch him), Morishita ("Ear Gun"), Shiina ("A Wavering Heart" — realizes she's fallen in love with him and questions fighting him while in love), Shiraishi ("The Voice Hidden Within"), and the Six First-Year Students (Tsubaki/Utomiya/Takahashi discuss the baffling exam targeting a specific student, Hōsen, Nanase and Ayanokōji's bandaged hand).

Open threads. Nanase's connection to Ayanokōji's past; the exam targeting a specific student; the first-years' investigation.

Attribution gap. none. TL note (p.230 earlier) on "that side."
