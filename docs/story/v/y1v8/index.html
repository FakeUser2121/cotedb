Chapter 1: Horikita Manabu’s Soliloquy p.10

Setting. Framed as Manabu’s retrospective soliloquy at graduation — no scene location; his three high-school years recalled.

Chapter 2: A New Special Exam: Mixed Training Camp p.11–76

none observed in half 1.

Setting. First-year Class C bus to the mountain training camp, then the camp’s wooden main building and gymnasium — boys from every grade assemble while teachers brief and classes negotiate small groups.

Narrative Beats

  • pp.11–21: Chabashira briefs the Mixed Training Camp — 9–15-person small groups inside 15-person large groups; multipliers by class mix (2× three classes, 3× all four) and size (10 base, 15×1.5, 9×0.9); last-place large group faces expulsion of any small group under the average threshold via its representative; solidarity rule lets an expelled representative drag one contributor; no-representative groups are disqualified with all members expelled; class loses 100 class points per expulsion and can go into debt; Revocation of Expulsion costs 20,000,000 private points + 300 class points; phones confiscated for a week; boys and girls live in adjacent buildings with one shared cafeteria hour.
