Chapter 2: A New Special Exam: Mixed Training Camp p.11–76

none observed in half 1.

Setting. First-year Class C bus to the mountain training camp, then the camp’s wooden main building and gymnasium — boys from every grade assemble while teachers brief and classes negotiate small groups.

Narrative beats

  • pp.11–21.
    • Chabashira briefs the Mixed Training Camp — 9–15-person small groups inside 15-person large groups
    • multipliers by class mix (2× three classes, 3× all four) and size (10 base, 15×1.5, 9×0.9)
    • last-place large group faces expulsion of any small group under the average threshold via its representative

    • no-representative groups are disqualified with all members expelled

    • Revocation of Expulsion costs 20,000,000 private points + 300 class points
    • phones confiscated for a week
    • boys and girls live in adjacent buildings with one shared cafeteria hour.

    • Chabashira’s “private points are not pocket money” advice cuts against cohesion
    • Horikita takes the girls’ side with Kushida as relay
    • Ayanokōji texts Kei protective tricks and lists who he will safeguard (Kei, Hirata, Horikita, the friend group) while planning to watch Nagumo.
  • pp.33–43.

    • Kaneda and Kanzaki counter with an all-four-class siege
