Prologue: The Monologue of Ichinose Honami p.6–11

Setting. Ichinose Honami’s first-person retrospective; a one-on-one interview in the student council room on a day off, after she found the letter calling her a criminal.

Narrative beats

  • p.6.
    • Ichinose never saw herself as good or bad
    • comfortable elementary and middle school
    • by third year of middle school she was student council president and offered a private high school scholarship
    • then one unforgivable mistake — her mother collapsed from illness and her little sister was hurt and left a shell of herself.
  • p.7.
    • She wasted half her third year as a shut-in for half a year
    • learning about this school made her resolve to put an end to that and bring back their smiles
    • she swore an oath to face her own answer
    • at school she found a letter and froze — “‘Ichinose Honami is a criminal.’” Classmates turned to her with curious looks
    • the words did not change however many times she read them
    • cut to the student council room interview with Vice President Nagumo.
  • p.8.
    • President Horikita had interviewed Ichinose and rejected her student-council application
    • Nagumo approached her for three reasons — Class B like himself, outstanding academic ability, and extremely good-looking (the last is what he chiefly seeks)
    • the important question is whether she is worth keeping at his side as personal property
    • he cites her middle-school presidency and her entrance-exam results via Hoshinomiya-sensei
    • she accepts the praise but cannot relax.
  • p.9.
    • Nagumo lies that Horikita rejected her out of hand for not being Class A and that the man obsesses over status
    • the narration states Horikita assesses talent from D or A alike
    • Ichinose asks whether she must promote to Class A
    • Nagumo says even promotion may not gain Horikita’s acknowledgment and that she was never considered up to par from enrollment
    • her smile vanishes
    • she stammers that he is also Class B yet vice president.
  • p.10.
    • Nagumo would love her as personal property
    • he claims he confessed the full truth behind his own Class B assignment when applying to the council and that it resulted in his position
    • he refuses to answer what that confession was
    • he presses why a student with her grades, interpersonal skills and middle-school presidency is in Class B
    • Ichinose cannot hide her agitation
    • the hypothesis is built from information he obtained from her homeroom teacher.
  • p.11.
    • “Someone who is unable to grab hold of this opportunity has no place in the student council.”
    • Ichinose harbors a secret — half a year shut in her room floods back
    • “What I say here-” / “It won’t leave this room, of course. Your secret is our secret.”
    • she had held the past without telling a soul
    • because she lost the trust of others she needs to trust in others
    • she opens up about everything — her own ‘mistake’.

About these pages. The volume opens with four pages that carry no text and a table of contents on p.5, so the interview text begins on p.6. Printed page numbers run five lower than the citations used here: the book prints 1 on p.6 and 7 on p.12. Pages 12–13 carry no text, only a page number on p.12.

New names. none — Nagumo Miyabi, Hoshinomiya Chie and Horikita Manabu already on the roster; the little sister and mother are unnamed.

New rules and facts. none — student-council application and interview process only; no point or exam rules stated.

Unattributed lines. none — Ichinose narrates; Nagumo and the letter are tagged on the pages read.

Translation errors, kept as printed. p.7 “ButAt” (missing space); p.10 “how much talent I possess” (likely “how little”); p.10 “Nagumosenpai” (missing hyphen).

Chapter 1: The Student Council President’s Intentions p.14–25

Setting. Early February after the training camp, student council room — Sakayanagi Arisu (Class 1-A) visits second-year Class A president Nagumo Miyabi; she leaves and meets Kushida Kikyō outside.

Narrative beats

  • p.14.
    • The room has become flashy and formal — wallpaper changed, Nagumo’s personal belongings moved in bulk
    • Sakayanagi reads it as a symbol of his might
    • Nagumo asks whether Horikita-senpai recommended her for the council
    • she says she was not invited and is not a fit
    • he says he just doesn’t have a good eye for that sort of stuff.
  • p.15.
    • Sakayanagi asks what would earn his seal of approval
    • he says appropriate talent and invites her to his side
    • she refuses — two leaders in one organization would humiliate the seniors
    • Nagumo does not grow angry and names both her and Ryuuen as interesting first years
    • narration: no student considers making the council their enemy except those like Sakayanagi and Ryuuen.
  • p.16.
    • Nagumo is setting aside time for her and mentions another ‘1st year’ has an appointment
    • Sakayanagi states she will launch an attack against student-council member Ichinose Honami-san of Class B and that it may get stormy
    • Nagumo has heard this before from their previous meeting alone.
  • p.17.
    • “I don’t recall giving you permission to ‘go that far’, Sakayanagi.”
    • Nagumo’s smile vanishes for the first time
    • she says she only had permission to bully Ichinose and intends to play rough
    • “Honami is my personal property I plan on loving. I only gave you permission to weaken her a bit.”
    • Sakayanagi asks whether he minds if Ichinose ends up expelled
    • “You’re a bold woman. Aren’t you afraid of me?” / “It’s just in my nature.”
  • p.18.
    • Sakayanagi could have acted without asking
    • she still came for permission — meaning she does not wish to make him an enemy
    • Nagumo says the information he gave her is proving useful and the student council will turn a blind eye to her actions
    • she catches the word ‘too’
    • he says there is no duplicity and asks what she is planning
    • she leaves it at that
    • narration: Nagumo is not the least bit trustworthy and will throw away someone who could become an asset.
  • p.19.
    • She asks about using violence as a drastic measure
    • Nagumo does not dislike violence, fights are normal, and unlike Horikita-senpai he will not crack down — a skirmish he would laugh off
    • she asks whether he would have sentenced the Class D–Class C first-year fight (Sudou and Ishizaki’s groups) differently from the previous president
    • he will not push them to expulsion and would suspend the parties involved.
  • p.20.
    • Sakayanagi declines an escort and says fighting with the pieces she holds is her modus operandi
    • she wanted to know how far she can take it until it is no longer safe
    • she is satisfied
    • then raises the topic of a student purchasing private points from about-to-graduate third years — a strategy using what the school collects pre-graduation as post-graduation currency, “a surefire way to graduate from Class A.”
  • p.21.
    • It came up during Kōenji and Nagumo’s conversation at the training camp — information only the boys overheard
    • private points are collected at graduation and worthless after, so a third year may entrust them to an underclassman
    • Nagumo realized Kōenji moved early
    • Sakayanagi asks why he boldly declared this to a first year — intending to alter the limiting rule
    • “Kōenji seems to be the only one in possession of an amount greater than what the school permits after all. It’s a form of rule violation.”
  • p.22.
    • Nagumo never liked the school’s rule that allows saving up 20 million points to rise to Class A and would like to revise the system itself — though repeal would not apply to the first years
    • no student has independently saved 20 million so far
    • it is meaningless to save up as a class
    • spy strategies are not realistic because Class A privilege turns spies traitor
    • there is no merit in knocking down a strong class.
  • p.23.
    • Trust as a weapon — betrayal by someone regarded as certain not to betray causes critical damage
    • Sakayanagi says Nagumo was too quick to throw away the trust he established
    • he answers while holding back laughter: he lost Horikita-senpai’s trust and the third-year Class A students’ trust, but nothing changed for the second years and other third years, and the first years will understand right away
    • narration: even breaking the rules he established with Horikita Manabu had been planned from the beginning, perhaps a consensus the second years reached beforehand.
  • p.24.
    • Nagumo asks about Ayanokōji
    • “Ayanokōji…? I am somewhat familiar with that name. He’s a 1st year, is he not?”
    • Sakayanagi offers to investigate him
    • he says he said something unnecessary and to forget it
    • she leaves and runs into Kushida Kikyō of Class C — “Hello, Sakayanagi-san.”
    • Kushida says she was thinking of applying for the student council and asks if Sakayanagi is the same.
  • p.25.
    • Sakayanagi excuses herself
    • she felt doubtful Kushida would want to join the council at a time like this, though an honor student aiming for the council would not be out of place
    • the girls are aware of Nagumo’s actions during the special exam
    • there has not been a single bad rumor about Kushida — “Fufu. It’s exactly that sort of person who’d unexpectedly turn villainous though.”
    • at the very least Sakayanagi does not believe she is purely good.

About these pages. The chapter begins on p.14, after the prologue's blank pages; printed page number 20 appears on p.25; no blank pages occur inside the chapter body.

New names. none — Kushida Kikyō, Kōenji Rokusuke, Ryuuen Kakeru, Sudō Ken, Ishizaki Daichi and Ayanokōji Kiyotaka already on the roster.

Private points are collected at graduation and worthless afterward, which enables pre-graduation point purchases from graduating third years; the 20-million-point Class A promotion rule exists and Nagumo wants it revised; Kōenji holds more private points than the school permits; student council under Nagumo will suspend rather than expel for student fights (contrasted with Horikita Manabu’s stricter posture).

Unattributed lines. none — all speakers tagged on the pages read.

Translation errors, kept as printed. none observed.

Chapter 2: A Changing Relationship p.26–50

Setting. Class C classroom and corridors in early February after the training camp; Ayanokōji Group cafe; gymnasium standoff between Hashimoto and Kanzaki.

Narrative beats

  • pp.28–30.
    • Class C’s morning circles around Karuizawa after the breakup with Hirata
    • Horikita asks Ayanokōji who dumped whom — Karuizawa did it
    • Ayanokōji reasons the fake relationship was Kei’s and Hirata would have engineered the public face to protect her status
    • the girls discuss the split while Ike and Sudō eavesdrop
    • Yamauchi alone teases Hirata about getting dumped and is flanked away by Ike and Sudō
    • Hirata shows no misery (“It’s ok, it’s the truth after all.”).
  • pp.30–32.
    • Horikita brings up slander against Ichinose and shows a note — “‘A history of violence’”, “‘Engaged in compensated dating’”, “‘Engaged in theft and robbery’”, “‘A noted history of drug usage’”
    • Ayanokōji judges it defamation in society but harder to punish inside the school
    • source-tracing is deliberately diffuse
    • narration: “There’s no doubt it’s Sakayanagi pulling the strings behind the scenes”
    • Horikita wonders whether at least one rumor could be true — “you can’t have smoke without a fire” — while allowing the possibility is extremely low
    • she forces the training-camp standings notebook on Ayanokōji.
  • pp.33–38.
    • End of school: Sakayanagi walks into Class C and asks for Yamauchi by name
    • Horikita blocks his exit and warns that being asked out is abnormal and maybe a move to knock Class C down
    • Yamauchi brushes her off with a toast-and-street-corner romance fantasy and leaves
    • the Ayanokōji Group follows to watch
    • Sakayanagi and Yamauchi talk by the stairs — hair-touching read as deliberate act by Haruka (“She’s definitely acting.”) while Ike and Yukimura allow interest as possible
    • the group moves to a cafe.
  • pp.38–42.
    • Cafe: Yukimura refuses romance talk and turns to study — end-of-year exam after the practice test on the 15th, about ten days of past problems
    • failing means expulsion
    • the last special exam of the school year likely in March
    • they notice Ichinose with stiff-faced Class B protectors and no Kanzaki present
    • Miyake phones from club duty: Class A and Class B are quarreling and he may have to stop a fight.
  • pp.43–50.
    • Gymnasium: Hashimoto (Class A) facing off with Kanzaki (Class B) with Miyake standing between them
    • Haruka, Airi and Ayanokōji arrive
    • Kanzaki has been tailing Hashimoto all day, investigating rumors sourced across Class A years
    • Hashimoto admits helping pass rumors along but denies being origin
    • Kanzaki’s investigation names Class A students in every year
    • he asks whether Sakayanagi instructed them — Hashimoto refuses and floats Ryuuen instead
    • Hashimoto presses that Ichinose neither affirmed nor denied the rumors and is not seeking help
    • Kanzaki answers that she hates extreme conflicts and would forgive defamers
    • Ayanokōji concludes from Hashimoto’s manner that the rumors “aren’t all lies” and notes defamation would give Ichinose grounds to sue where truth does not conflict with public interest.

New rules and facts. End-of-year exam is written as usual; a failing grade means immediate expulsion; the last special exam of the school year is expected after the end-of-year exam in March; private defamation outside school still has limits inside the isolated campus.

Unattributed lines. none — speakers tagged on the pages read; the slander note is written text, not speech.

Chapter 3: No Intention of Changing p.51–75

Setting. Gymnasium aftermath; dorm path and Keyaki Mall cafe; dormitory mailboxes and lobby; Horikita’s room at seven; elevator and lobby after.

Narrative beats

  • pp.51–52.
    • Hashimoto leaves the gym after shrugging off Kanzaki
    • Kanzaki bows and heads to the dormitories
    • the group splits. Haruka wants the excitement
    • Miyake asks her to keep the talk private, then admits he was not the top delinquent at his middle school, that Ryuuen went to the school next door, and that their schools clashed several times — “That guy probably never took me very seriously.” Ayanokōji reads Miyake’s ease with confrontation as grown from fighting.
  • pp.53–56.
    • Thursday evening: Ichinose walks alone toward the dorms without her usual ambition
    • Ayanokōji considers calling out, then senses a presence — phone camera shows Hashimoto behind him. Wang Mei-Yu (“Mii-chan”) intercepts him for a consultation
    • Hashimoto passes without a word. They walk to Keyaki Mall’s cafe at her suggestion
    • Ayanokōji confirms at the sugar counter that Hashimoto followed him into the cafe. He reasons Sakayanagi would have used Kamuro, not Hashimoto, if she were monitoring him — so it may be Hashimoto’s own judgment.
  • pp.56–62.
    • Mii-chan’s real ask: whether Hirata likes anyone now. She cites Hirata calling Ayanokōji the most reliable, level-headed person in class. Ayanokōji answers honestly — nobody as far as he knows — and asks when she started liking Hirata: right after the entrance ceremony, one gentle encounter. She fears rivals and inexperience
    • he advises patience because Hirata will likely be careful and reject early confessors. She says he gave the impression of being scary but is actually approachable face-to-face.
  • pp.63–65.
    • Shiina Hiyori (Class D) joins
    • the cover story is China
    • they are “reading buddies”
    • Mii-chan asks if cross-class friendship is great. Ayanokōji notes more students opened hearts after the training camp and that half-baked friendships may cause adverse effects later. He parts ways, then checks his mailbox — he has checked regularly ever since his father visited the school, expecting possible contact.
  • pp.66–68.
    • Hiyori waits for him in the lobby and asks about Ichinose’s rumors
    • she does not want Ichinose “tortured” and recalls Ichinose befriending her in the training camp. She asks him to cooperate
    • he says Class C’s levers are Horikita and Hirata, not him
    • she defers the introduction so as not to spread trouble for Ichinose.
  • pp.68–72.
    • Horikita texts: Ichinose is coming to her room at seven — alliance duty. The three meet. Horikita asks who spreads the rumors
    • Ichinose names Sakayanagi-san as a guess — because she declared war on Class B — then refuses to deny or confirm the rumors: “I’m not able to tell you anything about that.” Silence may read as admission
    • Ichinose chooses not to fight the strategy with energy and asks Class C not to interfere: “I will clean up my own mess.” She learns Kanzaki confronted Hashimoto and says she told him she was alright.
  • pp.73–75.
    • Ayanokōji advises Horikita to do nothing — helping may draw Sakayanagi’s eye to Class C
    • even if Class C is targeted later, Class B’s leader will already be dealt with. Horikita calls him cold-hearted
    • he leaves before eight. His closing note: Horikita is changing but still too extreme, losing sight of herself
    • she must work at getting along with others before reaching her true self. Outside, Ichinose waves him into the elevator and asks him to keep her company for a walk.

About these pages. Page 59 carries no text; printed page numbers 46 and 70 appear on pp.51 and 75.

New names. none — Wang Mei-Yu and Shiina Hiyori already on the roster.

New rules and facts. none — cafe pricing tied to class points restated; mailbox used for possible parental contact; no new exam or point rules.

Unattributed lines. none — all speakers tagged; Horikita’s texts are quoted as messages.

Translation errors, kept as printed. p.63 “began to a few other questions” (verb missing); p.67 “3 classes” (numeral, not spelled out).

Chapter 4: Ichinose’s Secret, Kamuro’s Secret. p.76–100

Setting. Night rest area by the school buildings; Ayanokōji’s room, 1 a.m. call; Friday dorm lobby mailbox scene; Room 401; the walk to the convenience store; Horikita Manabu’s phone call.

Narrative beats

  • p.76.
    • Short night walk with Ichinose after the elevator — she apologises for troubling Horikita, Ayanokōji and Class C, and states the stance she will hold until the rumors subside
    • they part separately so as not to arrive together.
  • pp.77–78.
    • 1 a.m. call from an unregistered on-campus number — a voice he does not know says “Ayanokouji Kiyotaka” and hangs up
    • Ayanokōji reads it as that man beginning his move and as a deliberate threat rather than a surprise attack.
  • pp.79–83.
    • Friday after school — the defamation plan against Ichinose has faded and students start calling the rumors lies
    • then a second letter appears in every first-year mailbox: 『Ichinose Honami is a criminal.』, unattributed and printed. Katsuragi and Yahiko debate reporting it
    • Katsuragi says the sender must expect Ichinose will stay silent as before, so school action depends on her. Ichinose reads the letter in the lobby, declines Amikura’s plea to go to the teachers, and smiles that she is okay
    • Kamuro watches alone and leaves with Katsuragi’s sharp glance after him.
  • pp.84–88.
    • Kamuro follows Ayanokōji into Room 401 under the cold, demands hot chocolate, and steers the talk to the letter
    • Ayanokōji gives his read — some rumors are true, which is why Ichinose will not fight back or the hidden truth would surface. (p.87 blank.)
  • pp.89–95.
    • Kamuro explains why she lets Sakayanagi push her around: a week after the entrance ceremony Sakayanagi caught her shoplifting, refused to report her, and made keeping the secret conditional on becoming her “first friend.” Kamuro states she is a habitual shoplifter, says Ichinose hides the same secret, and asks Ayanokōji to stop Sakayanagi so Ichinose’s heart is not crushed. (p.93 blank.)
  • pp.96–99.
    • Ayanokōji judges the story almost certainly true but withholds admitting it
    • Kamuro leaves her student ID, steals a can of beer in ten minutes as proof, and goes. He reconsiders whether ignoring Ichinose is still right and heads for the convenience store.
  • pp.99–100.
    • On the way, Horikita Manabu calls — Kiriyama reports that Kushida contacted Nagumo asking for his help in expelling Suzune
    • Manabu says he must act, and Ayanokōji notes Kushida broke her non-interference word just as she once used Ryuuen.

About these pages. Pages 87 and 93 carry no text; printed page numbers 71 and 95 appear on pp.76 and 100.

New rules and facts. student phones dial only an approved list and accept only on-campus numbers (p.77); mailbox letters leave physical evidence the rumors did not (p.81); students cannot buy age-restricted goods, so a beer in hand proves theft (p.97–98).

Unattributed lines. none — all speakers tagged.

Translation errors, kept as printed. p.77 “determine whether was a student” (object missing).
