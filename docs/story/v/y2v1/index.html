Prologue: Operating Behind the Scenes p.7–11

Setting. A meeting room in a Tokyo facility, two months earlier (February); Tsukishiro briefs a White Room teenager.

Narrative beats

  • pp.7–8.
    • Tsukishiro shows the teenager data on Ayanokōji Kiyotaka and 156 second-year students
    • orders Ayanokōji expelled and returned to the White Room before the end of April.
  • pp.8–9.
    • He warns against attracting attention and fans the teenager’s rivalry with Ayanokōji, misreading the White Room’s instilled hatred.
  • pp.9–11.
    • The teenager leaves calmly
    • Tsukishiro reviews Ayanokōji’s White Room data and reflects on the matchup.

About these pages. faculty/White Room-focused; no students present except the unnamed teenager.

New names. Nanase Tsubasa (the White Room teenager, unnamed in the prologue text) and Ayanokōji Kiyotaka.

New rules and facts. Ayanokōji must be expelled and returned to the White Room before the end of April (p.7); the plan must stay secret from the government (p.7).

Unattributed lines. The teenager is not named in the prologue; identified as Nanase Tsubasa by later volumes.

Translation errors, kept as printed. none observed.

Chapter 2: A New Stage p.16–65

Setting. Class 2-D classroom (opening ceremony week); hallway; second period (OAA app and special exam briefing).

Narrative beats

  • pp.16–20.
    • Spring break ends
    • students relocate to the second-year classroom
    • Horikita Suzune surprises everyone with short hair
    • Sudō and Kushida react.
  • pp.25–33.
    • Chabashira introduces the OAA app, which numerically rates Academic/Physical/Adaptability/Social Contribution and Overall Ability
    • Hirata and Ike ask about the categories
    • Sudō and Kōenji’s ratings are shown.
  • pp.34–49.
    • Chabashira announces the first special exam: second-years pair with first-years for a written test (5 subjects, 500 points)
    • rules for partnering, 5% late penalty, expulsion if under 500
    • estimated scores by rating
    • expulsions risk for second-years.
  • pp.50–55.
    • Horikita and Hirata calm the class and plan partner-finding
    • Sudō’s basketball-club situation.
  • pp.57–65.
    • Horikita and Ayanokōji discuss the exam in the hallway
    • she trusts him most among independent classmates
    • Ichinose hosts a meet-and-greet in the gym.

About these pages. rule-heavy chapter introducing OAA and the partnering exam.

New names. none (all roster).

New rules and facts. OAA app rates four categories plus Overall Ability (pp.29–33); first/second-year partnering exam with 500-point baseline and 5% late penalty (pp.34–49); Ichinose hosts a meet-and-greet (p.61).

Unattributed lines. none.

Translation errors, kept as printed. none observed.
