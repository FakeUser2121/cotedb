Arcs that move in this volume

Ayanokōji Kiyotaka
  • Situation — Kushida's threats escalate toward an expulsion gambit.
  • What they know — Holds recorded proof of her coercion.
  • What changes — Confronts her openly, drops the pretend-ignorance, and wagers on Horikita defeating her.
  • Why it matters — The mask drops in private: knowing and acting merge.
Horikita Suzune
  • Situation — Kushida's past arrives in the open; the final exam is rigged with malicious questions.
  • What they know — Full picture of Kushida's junior-high destruction and her threat network.
  • What changes — Chooses management over exposure ('This is my responsibility. I can't just abandon her'), then beats the question gambit with a pre-submitted counter-plan and publicly takes blame for the class's failed unity.
  • Why it matters — Her two defining choices in one volume: mercy as responsibility, victory as preparation.
Kushida Kikyō
  • Situation — Final-exam question sabotage exposed; the bet with Horikita settles.
  • What they know — Her junior-high destruction and her addiction to being valued are now spoken aloud to both witnesses.
  • What changes — Confesses the mechanism ('This is what I am'), loses the blazer-swap trap, and accepts a non-aggression pact she refuses to turn into cooperation.
  • Why it matters — The confession reframes her from schemer to addict: visibility is the drug, sabotage the supply.
Ryūen Kakeru
  • Situation — Final exams: he runs the phone-check inquisition to find the traitor.
  • What they know — The X recording proves someone inside his class feeds Horikita protection.
  • What changes — Frames and breaks Kushida with planted evidence ('Evidence trumps theory'), escalates to all-out war on Class D.
  • Why it matters — His surveillance style is rule-bound empiricism, not random cruelty — the method that later fails against X.
Ichinose Honami
  • Situation — Ryuuen circulates a forged letter accusing her of fraud.
  • What they know — The accusation is false but designed to detonate her trust-based class.
  • What changes — Meets it with calm refusal to be dragged down — 'I'll report to the teachers tomorrow and prove that this is just a misunderstanding.'
  • Why it matters — Her anti-crisis style: transparency instead of counter-intrigue.
Karuizawa Kei
  • Situation — Satou's confession lands between her partnership and her cover; exam panic peaks.
  • What they know — She manages both fronts without exposing either.
  • What changes — 'I knew it. Everyone is my enemy' at the study-group blowup — then the same day she softens: cools off, apologizes, covers Kushida's cleaning fee.
  • Why it matters — The two poles of her defended self — siege and repair — in a single volume.
