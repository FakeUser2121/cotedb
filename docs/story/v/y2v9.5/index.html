Kei: bedridden with the fluY2V9.5 · p.11–13, 106notes on Karuizawa Kei

On Christmas Eve Ayanokōji tries to call her; after an hour she calls back, coughing, with one word — "Influenza." She had wanted to keep the 24th promise even crawling, but he urges her to rest and reschedule; he confirms a friend has bought her everything she needs. Satō calls to check on her; Kei has recovered and gone out, and Satō is relieved.

with Ayanokōji Kiyotaka · Satō Maya

  • Karuizawa Kei "Influenza."p.12

  • Trait Dependent and eager to keep promises — sick as she is she still wants to meet, but she accepts rest.p.11–13, 106
  • Relationship Ayanokōji Kiyotaka Reschedules their Christmas promise with him.p.11–13
  • Relationship Satō Maya Her best friend checks on her flu.p.106

Mashima: gym training, and a favor to investigate AkiyamaY2V9.5 · p.15–23notes on Mashima Tomonari

At the gym he is pleased to find Ayanokōji is now a member, shows him his bench-press routine (80–100 kg, using safety bars), trains absorbed for two hours and accepts Ayanokōji's advice to stop. In the break room he asks Ayanokōji to investigate a gym employee, Akiyama — whether she has a boyfriend, her type and hobbies — explaining he dismissed Hoshinomiya and Chabashira as too flirtatious/serious and picked Ayanokōji as discreet and trustworthy.

Shiranami: a heartbreak song and questions about IchinoseY2V9.5 · p.26–35notes on Shiranami Chihiro

Ayanokōji finds her in a rental shop wearing headphones, listening to a melancholic heartbreak song; startled, she takes them off and asks what he wants. She asks to talk somewhere that won't stand out, then leads him to a vending-machine rest area where she keeps a deliberate distance and refuses a drink or a seat. She presses him on his relationship with Ichinose — are they just friends, is there romance — and voices the rumors about the gym and his room; she reveals she feels she has been "an unpleasant girl" for venting. She thanks him for helping her during the uninhabited island exam and stammers that it isn't that she dislikes him.

Ichinose: the 46-photo memory day at Keyaki MallY2V9.5 · p.59–65notes on Ichinose Honami

On Christmas she takes photos with everyone at Keyaki Mall — 43 people by one count, eventually 46 — and shyly asks Ayanokōji for a photo together "to make memories," careful that many photos (not just one with a boy) avoid a rift with Kei. She asks after Ayanokōji's meeting with Chihiro the day before, and is joined by Nanase and Hōsen; she waves him off as she smoothly turns to the next photo request.
