Ch.1: Irreplaceable daily lifeY2V9.5 · p.10notes on Ayanokōji Kiyotaka

Reflects that the second year's winter break has begun; just being able to enjoy an ordinary holiday is best; his remaining time as a student is quietly, surely decreasing; frames the year ahead as a long, extended battle in which every day is irreplaceable.

  • Ayanokōji Kiyotaka "Just being able to live for myself until today, I was satisfied enough."p.10

  • Caveat No other characters appear; the condition of "the time left for me" hangs over the volume.p.10

Ch.2: Christmas Eve morning, Kei's flu, Ichinose's messagesY2V9.5 · p.11–14notes on Ayanokōji Kiyotaka

Wakes on the 24th with a bad dreams; Kei has been out of contact since last night; their Christmas morning date is off after the term's end and the rain. He called Kei last night and got no answer; an hour later she called back, coughing: she said the one word "Influenza." Kei is bedridden; he reschedules the Christmas promise and ends the call; Ichinose texts about others being ill and asks about his plans; he tells her he'll go to the gym around noon.

with Karuizawa Kei · Ichinose Honami

  • Karuizawa Kei "Influenza."p.12

  • Relationship Ichinose Honami She texts at 9 to check on him and on Kei; offers help; he says gym around noon; she proposes "it may seem like we're meeting up" as a joke.p.13–14

Kei: bedridden with the fluY2V9.5 · p.11–13, 106notes on Karuizawa Kei

On Christmas Eve Ayanokōji tries to call her; after an hour she calls back, coughing, with one word — "Influenza." She had wanted to keep the 24th promise even crawling, but he urges her to rest and reschedule; he confirms a friend has bought her everything she needs. Satō calls to check on her; Kei has recovered and gone out, and Satō is relieved.

Ch.2: The gym on Christmas EveY2V9.5 · p.15–19notes on Ayanokōji Kiyotaka

Drops in at the Keyaki Mall gym before noon, intending a solo workout; Mashima-sensei is in the training room doing bench presses in class-2-A gym attire. Mashima walks him through his routine (80 kg now, says 100 kg is possible, safety bars first), lifts three sets, then declines to train him personally; Kōenji arrives and begins training in perfect silence while others watch. Kōenji's form draws unconcealed awe from other members; Ichinose appears, says she was late after talking with a friend; they work 30 minutes side by side on treadmills, then keep training an hour. He notices Amikura has arrived to train with Ichinose; he tells Ichinose he's leaving; Mashima, ~3 hours without break, calls him back to the break room: "Can we talk for a minute?"

with Mashima Tomonari · Kōenji Rokusuke · Ichinose Honami

4 more notes

  • Relationship Mashima Tomonari Mashima welcomes a new face; shows him the bench, eggs him on; notices him leaving after two hours.p.15–19

Mashima: gym training, and a favor to investigate AkiyamaY2V9.5 · p.15–23notes on Mashima Tomonari

At the gym he is pleased to find Ayanokōji is now a member, shows him his bench-press routine (80–100 kg, using safety bars), trains absorbed for two hours and accepts Ayanokōji's advice to stop. In the break room he asks Ayanokōji to investigate a gym employee, Akiyama — whether she has a boyfriend, her type and hobbies — explaining he dismissed Hoshinomiya and Chabashira as too flirtatious/serious and picked Ayanokōji as discreet and trustworthy.
