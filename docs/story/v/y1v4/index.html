Chapter 1: Karuizawa Kei’s Soliloquy p.10–11

Setting. No scene — first-person interior monologue, volume cold open. Karuizawa narrates alone, post-shower, in front of the mirror; no other character is present.

Narrative Beats

  • Opens on stasis and retracts it in the next breath: “In the end, nothing changed even after I came to this school.” — “No, that’s wrong… Maybe I never had any intention of changing.” (p.10)
  • Claims she already knows herself completely — strengths and weaknesses — and that no one liking her no longer hurts; “Because for some reason, I want them to feel that way.” (p.10)
  • The mirror: vomiting at “that old wound on my side” ties her self-loathing to an unnamed past trauma; “My life was destroyed because of that nightmarish day. I lost my youth, my friends, and even myself.” (p.10)
  • Closing thesis puts self-preservation above youth and friends — “The most important thing is protecting myself. I’ll do whatever it takes.” — under the self-assessment “I’m a parasite, a weak creature that can’t survive on its own.” (p.10)
  • Third straight non-Ayanokōji cold-open soliloquy (Sakura in Year 1, Vol. 2, Chabashira in Year 1, Vol. 3, Karuizawa here).

Page map note. p.11 carries no prose (blank/short scan: running footer only). p.10 keeps a duplicated verb as printed: “Why did I have to have to go through”.

New named entities. none — the page names no one.

Attribution gaps. none — first-person monologue with no dialogue.

Chapter 2: The Summer Group Test p.12–62

Setting. The cruise-ship respite three days after the island test — third-deck cabins, the top-deck restaurant, second-deck corridors and Mashima’s Room 204 — ending at the second deck’s 20:40 gathering place on the evening the school’s briefings land.

Narrative Beats

  • Cabin lunch: Hirata recruits Ayanokōji as the bridge to Horikita over Karuizawa’s cancelled plan; she crashes the deck table and reclaims him (pp.13–20).
  • Kōenji’s wet corridor and the bellhop’s fourth warning; Sakura’s roommate email and flight at Kushida’s arrival (pp.21–28).
  • The school message staggers the briefings — Ayanokōji at 18:00, Kushida at 20:40 — and the Horikita chat compares slots (pp.29–31).
  • Mashima’s Room 204 briefing: the Rabbit group, the fourteen-name roster, the VIP handout with four outcomes, the Werewolf detour, the traitor rules, the prohibited-actions list, and the meeting logistics (pp.32–49).
  • The hallway split: Yukimura’s huddle collapses when Karuizawa phones Hirata and the Professor declines for Love Love Alive; Ayanokōji reports to Horikita by chat (pp.50–51).
  • Hirata wakes Ayanokōji and voices the doubt that the scattered briefings are themselves the test (pp.52–53).
  • The 20:40 gathering: Katsuragi pivots from ignoring Horikita to warning her, Kanzaki pulls him off, Ryūen prowls with his lackeys, Kushida arrives puzzled, and Horikita exits cold (pp.54–61).

Page map note. corpus section markers — 2.1 (p.21), 2.2 (p.26), 2.3 (p.32), 2.4 (p.52), 2.5 (p.54); the chapter opening before 2.1 is labelled §2.0 in section headings, the standing convention. pp.59 and 62 carry no prose (blank/short scan: running footer only).

New named entities. the Rabbit roster (p.39) names Takemoto Shigeru, Machida Kōji, Morishige Takurō (Class A); Ichinose Honami, Hamaguchi Tetsuya, Beppu Ryōta (Class B); Ibuki Mio, Manabe Shiho, Yabu Nanami, Yamashita Saki (Class C). Morimiya (Class A, p.54) and Tokitō (Class C, p.54) appear without a first name — which Tokitō is not printed; none of these open files (roster-only names, the Sakagami precedent).

New system facts. the Summer Group Special Exam — twelve zodiac groups of roughly fourteen students mixed across Classes A–D; a VIP message at 8:00 a.m. day one; the test runs to 9:00 p.m. day four with two one-hour meetings daily (1:00 p.m., 8:00 p.m., designated room, no leaving mid-meeting); answers by email only, 9:30–10:00 p.m. the final night, first submission per group counts, one per student, VIPs may not submit, results 11:00 p.m. Outcome #1 pays every member 500,000 private points and the VIP 1,000,000; Outcome #2 pays only the VIP 500,000; Outcome #3 (early correct non-VIP) pays 500,000 plus 50 class points to that student’s class and costs the VIP’s class 50; Outcome #4 (early wrong non-VIP) costs their class 50 and pays the VIP 500,000 plus 50; VIP-classmate early answers don’t count; outcomes and class-point deltas only are announced, temporary IDs allowed for point transfers; prohibitions up to expulsion — phone theft, threats for VIP information, borrowed-phone submissions, cross-class discussion until the exam ends, and tampering with school messages (pp.40–49).

Open threads. the Rabbit group’s VIP identity; Hirata’s promised question to a teacher about the staged briefings; Ayanokōji’s chat strategy with Horikita; the Katsuragi–Ryūen stalemate inside one group.

Attribution gaps. untagged in the translation — “O-one million?! Whoa.” and “Wait, everyone receives 500,000 points? And if you’re the VIP, you get double?” (p.42); “Sensei, what about Outcomes #3 and #4?” (p.42); “Of course, we understand. Please continue.” (p.43); “Roughly speaking, yeah. You’d best mentally prepare yourself.” (p.60); plus the two unassigned Horikita-chat lines of §2.2 — “I’m curious about the difference in start times…” (p.30) and “We can’t know anything for sure at this point.” (p.31).

Chapter 3: An Infinite Variety of Wishes p.63–124

Setting. Day one of the Summer Group Special Exam on the cruise ship — the Blue Ocean café at 8:00 a.m., the second-deck Rabbit room’s first two discussions, the bow deck, the cabin, the night deck, and the first-level vending machines where the teachers drink.

Narrative Beats

  • The 8:00 café: Horikita’s Dragon roster, both messages reporting they were not chosen then Ryūen and Ibuki sit down for an island autopsy, a photo, and a Sakayanagi deflection (pp.63–77).
  • Rabbit §3.1: Yukimura’s hallway scuffle with the Professor’s dialect ranking; Ichinose takes the lead; Machida pitches Katsuragi’s no-discussion plan and Class A withdraws to the corner; Manabe opens the Rika charge and Karuizawa knocks the phone down (pp.78–95).
  • §3.2: the first hour ends with the foot-step exit; Ayanokōji meets Sakura on the bow, reads the Cow roster, and renews the contact offer after checking on Yamauchi (pp.96–102).
  • §3.3: Yamauchi’s neck-grab over Sakura’s email becomes a camera-and-points redirect (pp.103–105).
  • §3.4: the gloomy second gathering — Ichinose baits Machida, Ayanokōji files Karuizawa as a background character, then lies to Manabe at the elevator to light a fuse (pp.106–111).
  • §3.5: the cabin — Hirata’s phone shows Dragon/Kushida and Horse/Minami as known VIPs; Kouenji ends the Monkey group with a traitor submission; Horikita promises a call (pp.112–116).
  • §3.6: the starlit deck and Kushida’s hug; then Chabashira, Hoshinomiya and Mashima at the sofas — Ichinose was put in Rabbit on purpose, Ryuuen into Dragon by evaluation failure, and Ayanokōji is told he is under heavy scrutiny (pp.117–123).

Page map note. corpus section markers — 3.1 (p.78), 3.2 (p.96), 3.3 (p.103), 3.4 (p.106), 3.5 (p.112), 3.6 (p.117); the café chapter opening before 3.1 is labelled §3.0 in section headings, the standing convention. pp.71, 99, 119, 120 and 124 carry no prose (blank/short scan: running footer only).

New named entities. Yashima Mariko (Cow roster, p.100) added to the roster while walking; Hoshinomiya Chie’s first scene as a speaking teacher (pp.121–123); Morishige and Takemoto named in the Rabbit room (pp.95, 108); the Horse VIP is printed only as “Minami-kun” (p.113) — two roster Minamis (Hakuo, Setsuya) both surface on that page, so the first name is not assigned.

New system facts. Horse-group VIP named on Hirata’s phone beside Dragon/Kushida (p.113); Monkey group ends early when its VIP submits as traitor — school notice frees them and forbids disturbing others (pp.114–115); contact between groups is legal (phones, even calls) during discussions (pp.106–107).

Open threads. Karuizawa’s off-pattern behaviour and Ayanokōji’s planted café story; the Minami VIP ambiguity; Hirata’s promised Sakayanagi question; Kushida’s call after the hug; how Hoshinomiya’s Ichinose-in-Rabbit move plays out; the remaining Rabbit VIP.

Attribution gaps. untagged in the translation — “Ugh. No matter how you look at it, we really do have the worst team.” before Karuizawa’s entrance (p.80); “What’s the matter, Ayanokouji? Something on your mind?” (p.80); the teachers’ opening exchange and most of the date/grudge lines (p.121–122), including “What are you planning, Chie?” and “Oh, are you still talking about what happened ten years ago?…” — only the lines tagged to Hoshinomiya, Mashima or addressed by printed name are assigned.
