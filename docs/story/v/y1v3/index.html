Chapter 1: Chabashira Sae’s Soliloquy p.10–12

Setting. No scene — pure first-person interior monologue, volume cold open. Chabashira narrates; the only other presence is an unnamed "certain young boy" she recalls standing before.

Narrative Beats

  • Opens with the Wings of Icarus myth as an interpretive frame: Greek mythology "deals abundantly with human frailty" (p.10); Daedalus's wax wings, Icarus's fall.
  • She connects the myth to a boy she stood before "considering what these past few months had brought" — the comparison because "he resembled Icarus" (p.10).
  • Immediately breaks the comparison: the boy "possessed neither the bravery nor the arrogance of Icarus" (p.11).
  • Confesses her provocation as a forced gamble: "I had been pushed into a corner. I had no choice but to do it." — she chose to "incur this boy's divine wrath" and turn his quiet rage toward herself; "The die cannot be returned once cast. The gamble had already begun." (p.11)
  • Volume's second straight non-Ayanokōji cold-open soliloquy (after Sakura in Year 1, Vol. 2 ch1), this time the homeroom instructor's.

Page map note. p.12 carries no prose (illustration/section-break page).

New named entities. none — the chapter names only the mythological figures (Daedalus, Icarus, King Minos, Minotaur) inside the retelling.

Attribution gaps. the boy is never named on pp.10–11; he stays unnamed in the records.

Year 1, Vol. 3 p.13–99 — Ch.2: The Boundary Between Heaven and Hell

Setting. First day of the two-week summer programme: luxury-liner deck (pp.13–23), beach landing and rules briefing (pp.24–46), Class D river base camp (pp.63–98); the deserted-island special test runs one week to August 7th.

Narrative beats. The vacation reveal collapses into the deserted-island survival test announced by Mashima (pp.24–29); Class D splits three ways over spending the 300-point budget, with the cardboard-toilet war as the first fracture (pp.32–43); the search parties go out and Ayanokōji's team catches Katsuragi revealing himself as Class A's spot-leader at a cave (pp.52–62); the river is chosen as base camp and Horikita is nominated spot-leader (pp.63–67); Ike's bonfire and apology to Shinohara give the class its first working cooperation (pp.85–88); Hirata's 120-point floor speech settles the spending policy (pp.89–90); Ibuki Mio, beaten and expelled by a boy in Class C, is taken in (pp.78–95); the chapter closes on Kōenji's retirement and the class's thirty-point penalty (pp.97–98). p.21, p.37, p.79 and p.99 carry no prose (blank/short scan pages).

Page map note. The chapter's own section numbers §2.1–§2.8 begin at p.24 (§2.1), p.47 (§2.2), p.53 (§2.3), p.63 (§2.4), p.72 (§2.5), p.76 (§2.6), p.83 (§2.7) and p.87 (§2.8). Printed book page footers lag the corpus page markers by ten from p.13 onward.

New named entities. Mashima Tomonari (Class A homeroom, English), Katsuragi Kōhei and Totsuka Yahiko (Class A), Ibuki Mio (Class C), Shinohara Satsuki and Yukimura Teruhiko and Satō Maya (Class D). The sick Class A student who eats the opening thirty-point penalty is never named. The boy who hit Ibuki is never named. "The Professor" (p.51) is a booed nickname without a printed name. The Class A deck bullies (p.15) are unnamed. Miyake Akito is named only on Hirata's phone (p.16); Sakayanagi Arisu only in Totsuka's reported speech (p.60).

New system facts. Special test "deserted island survival," one week to August 7th; 300 points per class (Class A starts at 270 after the illness penalty); two tents, two flashlights, one matchbox, one cardboard toilet per class; penalties — health retirement 30, pollution 20, roll-call absence 5/student, inter-class violence or robbery = disqualification plus private-point forfeiture; watches mandatory, track temperature/pulse/GPS, emergency button; spots with eight-hour exclusive possession, key card held only by a registered leader, 1 bonus point per occupation (provisional), 50-point penalty for using another class's spot; seventh-day leader-guess rule: +50 per correct guess, −50 per wrong one, and a found-out leader forfeits the class's saved bonus points; base camps cannot be moved without justifiable reason; river water and foraged fruit are legal, forest/sea/defecation is not.

Open threads. Horikita's dishevelled hair and cold on p.22 (her afternoon is never explained on these pages); Kōenji's forest knowledge and his "little curious" remark before he retires; why Ayanokōji was sure the camp-location leak to Ibuki "won't be a problem" (p.82); Katsuragi's claim of two more spotted facilities; Ibuki's unnamed assailant and Class C's internal fight; the boys' sleeping arrangement (open air) and the week's remaining economy.

Attribution gaps. Ike's "Whooooaaaaa! Kikyou-chaaaaaaan!" (p.17) is narration-adjacent shouting, not needed as a quote row. Sudou's practice-Horikita gyaru lines (p.19) are Ike performing, already mirrored on both classmates' pages. The Class D "Thirty thousand points next month" chant (p.29) is attributed to "Ike and the others," not to one speaker. The "What the hell, Haruki?" exchange at p.97 keeps both speakers in one quoted block under Ike.
