Coverage notes (Year 3, Vol. 3)
Depth review — prologue & full volume p.12–256

Setting (prologue). Seaside at sunset; Hiyori alone, looking out over the ocean after the special exam's "final phase" is announced.

Narrative beats (prologue). In her own monologue Hiyori admits she never said "I like you" to Ayanokōji because they are now in different classes and enemies; she resolves to defeat him in this exam so Class D can still reach Class A, apologises to both Ryūen and Ayanokōji, and vows to keep watching until the whistle. The chapter frames the exam as "a path to survival and a path to death at the same time."

Setting (Ch.1). Cruise ship crossing to the same uninhabited island used a year earlier; late June.

Narrative beats (Ch.1, pp.14–45). Survival Game rules laid out — four classes, each with VIPs and guards, GPS watches with five-minute updates, designated areas, out-of-bounds zones that expand over the week, 6 PM–9 AM outside exam hours when movement is free but must restart from the 6 PM location, and a penalty where a wiped-out class must select a student for expulsion. Class C travels under Ayanokōji as leader; Morishita proposes that Hashimoto be the sacrifice if penalties fall. Ayanokōji deliberately delays teaching weapon handling so the other classes move away first; on the second morning Ryūen's Class B launches a surprise attack on the stationary Class C.

Setting (Ch.2–3). Island forest and Class C camp (areas around D9–G11).

Narrative beats (Ch.2, pp.46–85). Kitō is hit in the surprise attack and shaken; Hashimoto is unnerved by how calmly Ayanokōji takes the reversal and pledges to follow his orders. Ayanokōji teaches the class to handle the paintball rifles; Kōenji, acting alone, intercepts a Class B supply group near Morofuji and eliminates all eight guards in under thirty seconds, then lets Morofuji take the supplies and leave with a warning. Class C withdraws to area H12.

Narrative beats (Ch.3, pp.86–118). Shiraishi, acting as Class C's recon commander over the radio, expresses total confidence in Ayanokōji despite the failed prediction; he notes this diverges from the impression he had of her and resolves to use that trust. Ayanokōji's underlying goal is revealed: the surprise attack was allowed so that Class C, cornered, would have no choice but to request an alliance with Ichinose's Class D.

Narrative beats (Ch.4, pp.119–150). Ayanokōji identifies Class A's three VIPs by attacking the class at night and watching which students the guards rush to protect: Wang Mei-Yu, Satō Maya and Yukimura Teruhiko. He passes the names to Class B (an eliminated student can still talk) to catalyse an attack on Class A.

Narrative beats (Ch.5, pp.151–166). Camp life on the second night; Ayanokōji wins white rice in rock-paper-scissors; Shibata approaches him about the Class B alliance; Ayanokōji maintains a defensive strategy, waiting for the shrinking play area to force a general engagement.

Narrative beats (Ch.6, pp.167–196). Restricted area expands to a nine-by-nine grid (D4–L12); Ayanokōji and Shiraishi direct Class C to keep distance and avoid engagement while Class A and B exhaust themselves.

Narrative beats (Ch.7, pp.197–232). Final day. Class A loses all three VIPs and is eliminated. Hirata and Hashimoto fight a close-range duel in the forest; Hashimoto is eliminated, then Hirata — consumed by killing intent over Ayanokōji's transfer — raises his gun at the already-out Hashimoto but stops before pulling the trigger. Ayanokōji reveals he fed Class B the VIP names and that the whole engagement served the C–D alliance. With Class A out, he heads for F11 to "adjust."

Narrative beats (Epilogue, pp.233–252). Class B's VIP is Yamashita. Ayanokōji hands spare magazines to Kanzaki and prepares to bluff Ryuuen. On the beach he burns his ammunition against Ibuki and Ryuuen, eliminates Ibuki with a paintball to the thigh, then — out of ammo and apparently surrendered — throws a deliberately damaged paintball that bursts on Ryuuen's abdomen, eliminating both. He explains the true goal: forcing Class C into the C–D alliance, which is now declared. Final standings: Class D first, Class C second, Class B third, Class A last. Mashima-sensei then announces a new uninhabited-island exam built on "trust and betrayal."

Page-map note. Blank or near-empty pages: 132 (short), 225, 243, 255, 257. Chapter 7 ends p.232; Epilogue begins p.233; Author Afterword p.253; translator material pp.255–256.

New named entities. none beyond those already recorded (all major names appear in earlier volumes).

New system facts. Survival-game rules (VIP/guard roles, GPS updates, out-of-bounds expansion, expulsion penalty, personal GPS jammers, weapon/ammunition rules) recorded in Ch.1. Hand-to-hand rule note: taking a dropped weapon is prohibited (p.244). Damaged paintball thrown by hand is not prohibited (p.248).

Open threads. The new "trust and betrayal" exam announced at the end; Hiyori's unspoken feelings; Hirata's unchecked aggression; Tsukishiro's independent enforcer plot (continues from Year 3, Vol. 2).

Attribution gaps. Hirata's internal state in the Ch.7 duel is given only through Hashimoto's observations (the narration stays in Class C's viewpoint); no Hirata line is quoted in that duel, so no `[says:hirata-yousuke]` row is claimed.
