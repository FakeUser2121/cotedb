Prologue: Chie Hoshinomiya's Monologue p.10–11

Setting. Ayanokōji's head — Hoshinomiya's interior monologue before the exam.

Narrative beats. Chie Hoshinomiya (unnamed in the monologue) recounts how Sae-chan (Chabashira) was her best friend and rival; on the day Chabashira failed to graduate from Class A, Chie understood she wanted to become a teacher — to eliminate all hope from Sae-chan's dream. She still will not let Sae's class win; she would bring it down by any means, even if she is branded a failure as a teacher.

About these pages. Hoshinomiya's motive; the translation note prints 吐いた唾は飲めぬ as "words once spoken cannot be taken back" at p.86.

New names. Hoshinomiya Chie named on the page.

New facts. Hoshinomiya is a Class D homeroom teacher (revealed pp.65–86).

Unattributed lines. none.

Translation errors, kept as printed. none observed.

Chapter 1: Unusual End-of-Year Special Exam p.12–25

Setting. Class 2-D classroom; Keyaki Mall.

Narrative beats. End-of-year special exam announced; Chabashira bows and delivers a personal story — her eighth year, never above Class D, until this class "dispelled the defective goods image."; rankings shown (Class A 1098, B 983, C 730, D 654); rules revealed: special building venue, Class A vs Class C and Class B vs Class D; three representatives per class (vanguard 5 lives, middle guard 7, general 10); at least one representative from each gender; substitutes allowed; the school randomly selects three if fewer than three are present; even the teachers were not told the details; the exam is probably not physical; winning class earns 200 class points (250 for this class); losing means no reward, not lost points; the deadline is Sunday. Ayanokōji reflects on the exam's significance; he walks home with Kei; he messages the four leaders to arrange meetings — Horikita (café, Friday), Ryūen (karaoke, Friday), Ichinose (bench, Sunday), Sakayanagi (phone, Sunday).

About these pages. exam announcement and the plan.

New names. none beyond the roster.

New rules and facts. End-of-year special exam — reps 5/7/10 lives, gender rule, substitutes, random selection rule, the 35-person attendance penalty (5 class points per person below/above), A vs C and B vs D, winner takes 200 (this class 250), losers get nothing, unspecified exam content until exam day, deadline Sunday, venue is the special building.

Unattributed lines. none.

Translation errors, kept as printed. none observed.

Chapter Three p.63–86

Setting. Special building; exam room; waiting room; hallway.

Narrative beats. Exam morning; 7:40 AM; Kiryūin-senpai on the path — Nagumo has sent her, not him. The representatives gather; Sakagami and Hoshinomiya perform body checks; Mashima explains the special exam in full; the twelve finalists listed on the monitor; the explanation breaks around step five; Sanada asks how the traitor works; Mashima answers; Hoshinomiya corners Ayanokōji in the hallway — "Give me the win."; he declines; she offers "something in return"; he leaves. About these pages. the exam's rules; the reps assemble; the traitor right.

New names. none beyond the roster.

New rules and facts. Special exam — discussion of five-minute rounds; 14 participants; roles — ordinary students 6–8, honor students 2, teacher 1, graduate 1, underclassman 1, upperclassman 1, traitor 0–2 per class; traitor reveals one random participant's role per round; discussion ends when all ordinary students or all honor students are out; the traitor's reward is 5M private points or 50 class points; representatives can nominate one participant per round as executive role or specific role; if an executive-role nomination is right, the traitor's side cannot move observers; misidentification costs the nominating representative lives; teachers block one honor-student nomination per game; graduates identify one role per round (not traitors); underclassmen recover 1–2 lives; upperclassmen cost the opponent 1 life and reveal 2 roles; honor students can nominate for dismissal; the honor students know each other; interrogation happens at the end of each round; the traitor can only be expelled through confession + determination; misidentification is possible by the traitor's side; class points transfer by exam result.
