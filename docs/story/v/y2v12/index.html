Monologue: her motiveY2V12 · p.10–11notes on Hoshinomiya Chie

A monologue printed before Chapter 1: Chie Hoshinomiya recounts that Sae-chan (Chabashira) was her best friend and rival; she became a teacher for one purpose — to eliminate all hope from Sae's dream of graduating a Class A; she traces her resentment to the day Sae failed to graduate from Class A eleven years ago. She swears that if her class loses the year-end special exam and Sae-chan's class becomes Class A, at worst she will stop it by any means necessary — even if she is branded a failure as a teacher and driven out of the profession.

2 more notes
  • Caveat A TL NOTE printed in-line under her vow explains it literally reads "So as long as I can see."p.10
  • Caveat The monologue reframes everything that follows: her patience and her hostility toward Ayanokōji in Ch.3 both flow from this oath.p.11
