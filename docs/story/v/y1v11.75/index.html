Depth review — Ayanokōji SS: The First Phone Call p.3–14

Setting. Ayanokōji's dorm room on the last night of spring break, 10 PM–11:15 PM.

Narrative beats. Ayanokōji reflects on the coming year (the Horikita bet, the White Room first-year Tsukishiro hinted at). At 10 PM Kei calls — their first contact since the confession. He tries to be a "good boyfriend," clumsily; they talk for about an hour about mundane things. When he hangs up without hesitation she calls back, upset; she demands he end the call "in a way that won't rub me the wrong way." He reasons that ending it eagerly means wanting to see her tomorrow, which satisfies her. They agree to keep the relationship hidden at school for now.

About these pages. first-person Ayanokōji narration; the « » quote glyphs are used throughout.

Attribution gap. none — all dialogue is between Ayanokōji and Kei.

Depth review — Ichinose SS: Spring Vacation p.15–18

Setting. Keyaki Mall on the final day of spring break; Ichinose with Shiranami Chihiro and Amikura Mako.

Narrative beats. Her friends worry she shut herself in her room; she says she was thinking about second-year strategy and insists she is fine. She reflects that revealing her weakness in Ayanokōji's room lifted a weight and restored her will to fight Sakayanagi, Ryūen and Horikita. She catches herself wishing they were in the same class, then stops — they are now rivals who must not let personal feelings interfere. She resolves to keep moving forward as Class B.

About these pages. first-person Ichinose narration.

Attribution gap. none.

Short story: The First Phone CallY1V11.75 · p.3–14notes on Ayanokōji Kiyotaka

On the final night of spring break, Ayanokōji reflects on school resuming and on issues awaiting him in the new year. At 10 PM he receives a scheduled phone call from Karuizawa Kei. Ayanokōji and Kei talk awkwardly at first about their new relationship. He tells Kei he waited for her call, and they continue talking about everyday matters. Ayanokōji shares trivial topics in response to Kei’s request, and their call continues for about an hour. They try to end the call for the night but Kei calls back, upset by how abruptly he ended it. Ayanokōji explains why he ended the call without hesitation, and they end the conversation more calmly. He reflects on their changed relationship and looks ahead to the start of the new school term.

Short story: The First Phone CallY1V11.75 · p.4–14notes on Karuizawa Kei

On the final night of spring break, Karuizawa Kei calls Ayanokōji at 10 PM as they had arranged. She reacts with surprise and embarrassment as they speak as a couple for the first time by phone. Kei admits she delayed calling because she was unsure about their relationship and then talks about everyday topics. When Ayanokōji ends the call abruptly, Kei calls him back and complains. She asks him to end the call in a way that will not upset her. After hearing his explanation, Kei accepts it and they end the call.

Short story: Ichinose Honami’s Spring VacationY1V11.75 · p.15–18notes on Ichinose Honami

On the last day of spring break, Ichinose meets Chihiro-chan and Mako-chan at Keyaki mall. Mako-chan asks whether she is feeling well after spending much of the break alone. Ichinose tells them she has been thinking about strategies for the second year and is feeling better. She reflects on the support Ayanokōji gave her during the break and feels able to fight on as Class B’s leader. Ichinose reminds herself that she and Ayanokōji are rivals in different classes despite her feelings. She resolves to focus on the start of the second year and leading Class 2-B.
