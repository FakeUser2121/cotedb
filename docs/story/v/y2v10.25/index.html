The gym break and the red panda p.1

Setting. the gym's rest area during the first break after the survival and elimination special exam; Ayanokōji has trained alone and is cooling down on a chair.

Narrative beats. he goes to the gym without being told to; after training he takes out his phone and searches a word; the article that comes up convinces him; he is looking at its photos when Ichinose and Amikura arrive.

About these pages. the story's opening beat, narrated by Ayanokōji; the word he searched is never printed, only the red-panda photograph Amikura notices.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. the story is set after the survival and elimination special exam; the exam's questions included material he did not know (the red-panda article and, per p.2, a tapioca question).

Unattributed lines. “Good morning, Ayanokōji-kun.” and “Morning.” carry no speaker tags; the arrivals are named in the next sentence. Both are recorded in the characters' records with the gap noted.

Translation errors, kept as printed. the opening word is split by the drop-cap layout — a lone “A” on its own line, then “FTER THE SURVIVAL and elimination special exam ended”; a bare “:” stands under the title and at the top of every page.

Ichinose and Amikura arrive; the exam questions p.1–2

Setting. the same rest area; Ichinose and Amikura have come to the gym together.

Narrative beats. Amikura notices the red-panda photo; he turns the screen off; Ichinose guesses the search is about the exam question he got wrong; she adds the tapioca question and calls him surprisingly ignorant of the world; he blames hardly watching TV; Amikura doubts the excuse; Ichinose laughs at his confused expression; Akiyama, a member of the gym staff, calls Ichinose to the counter over a paperwork mistake, and the two head there.

About these pages. the story's public scene; the private conversation starts only after Ichinose leaves.

New names. Akiyama (gym staff).

New rules and facts. none beyond the exam references; the page treats the exam as recent and memorable for another class.

Unattributed lines. “Is it related to the exam question? Ayanokōji-kun, you got it wrong, didn't you?” precedes any speech tag; the following narration is “Ichinose seemed to have some insight.” “Honestly, I didn't think not liking TV would backfire like that.” is likewise untagged. Both are recorded with the gap noted.

Translation errors, kept as printed. the page repeats a clause: “Amikura expressed her doubt; perhaps my not-watching-TV excuse wasn't persuasive enough, Amikura expressed her doubt.” The tapioca line reads “Are you surprisingly ignorant of the world?” where the Year 3, Vol. 1 printing of the same story reads “surprisingly that ignorant”.

The change in Honami p.2–3

Setting. the rest area, one empty chair between them; Ichinose is at the counter with Akiyama.

Narrative beats. Amikura sits down and says Honami-chan has changed; he agrees but thinks “a few months” is an exaggeration; she dates the change to just after the second-year deserted island exam and the start of the second semester, when Ichinose seemed unstable and often lost in thought; he connects that period to her confession to him and his telling her about Kei; Amikura asks him to keep the story between them and begins it.

About these pages. the turn from gym small talk to the story's real subject; the page marks the change as observed by those close to Ichinose, not by him.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. none.

Unattributed lines. “Honami-chan's really changed, huh? It's like, unimaginable from a few months… ago…” runs across the page break with no tag; “Is that so?” is untagged; “The deserted island exam, huh?” is untagged. All are recorded with the gap noted.

Translation errors, kept as printed. the first line is split by the page break between “months” and “ago…”.

The misdirected message p.3–4

Setting. the rest area, Amikura telling the story to Ayanokōji alone.

Narrative beats. during Ichinose's unstable spell a boy in her class overheard a rumor that she might like Ayanokōji; the rumor began with a message she sent by mistake; she meant to send something like “I want to calm down and talk. Can we meet in person?”; the accidental recipient was Ishizaki, who came to her class during a break and asked what the message meant while showing the screen; she corrected it as a misdirected message and he went back; the fact that it was missent meant the important message was meant for someone else.

About these pages. the incident at the center of the story; Ishizaki acts openly rather than maliciously, and the page says so.

New names. Ishizaki (named in the retelling only).

New rules and facts. none; the messaging habits described are ordinary phone behavior, not school rules.

Unattributed lines. none in this beat — the account is continuous and its speaker is established by “Amikura began to speak” on p.3.

Translation errors, kept as printed. p.4 prints “Ishizakikun” without a hyphen or space in the contact-list explanation.

The alphabet, the translator's note, and the freeze p.4–5

Setting. the rest area; the story moves from the class's speculation to Ichinose's own memory of standing frozen.

Narrative beats. the boys' suspicion lands on Ayanokōji because his “A” and Ishizaki's “I” sit close in an alphabetically ordered contact list; Amikura explains that Ichinose is composed in public but flusters over matters concerning herself; she turned pale with her eyes on the floor and could not excuse herself; an unlabelled first-person inset states her dilemma — she cannot tell the truth, cannot pin it on anyone, cannot pretend it never happened; Amikura says it was rare to see her like that and that the boys began to wonder whether she meant to confess to someone.

About these pages. the story's emotional low point; the inset is printed without a speaker tag or quotation marks.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. the translator's note explains that the Japanese “Alphabet” follows the gojūon, placing the vowels あ (a) and い (i) next to each other.

Unattributed lines. the inset itself — “I can't tell the truth.” through “...it feels like I've approached a dead end.” — is unlabelled; it is recorded as an inset, not assigned to a speaker.

Translation errors, kept as printed. the translator's note is printed in-line on p.4 as “(TL NOTE: The Japanese “Alphabet” is ordered by the Gojuuon, this places the vowels あ(a) and い(i) directly next to each other.)”, with a comma splice as printed.

The girls' rescue p.6

Setting. the rest area; Amikura continues the account.

Narrative beats. he asks how Ichinose got through it; Amikura says the girls knew who the message was meant for, discussed how to help, and stepped in; the intended recipient was a girl who had sought love advice from Ichinose and was owed a reply; the message was sent during that exchange; Ichinose's silence protected the girl's privacy; testimony from several people corrected most of the boys' misunderstanding.

About these pages. the resolution of the incident; the page credits collective action, not a single rescue.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. none.

Unattributed lines. none — Amikura's account is continuous and bounded by his question and her answer.

Translation errors, kept as printed. none observed.

The warning p.7

Setting. the rest area; the account is over and Amikura turns to the present.

Narrative beats. he thanks her; she deflects the thanks; he says he assumes she is being modest; she says the thanks are irrelevant; he asks why she told him; she says it is a different matter, and her expression stiffens; she tells him Ichinose still sees him as someone very special and that he is the only one in the school who can strongly influence her; she asks him not to make Ichinose sad or hurt her; he says he does not intend to hurt her but that it is a difficult request; she agrees; she adds that she understands his position and does not want Ichinose unnecessarily hurt; she mutters that it is tough for Honami-chan to fall for a guy who has a girlfriend.

About these pages. the story's purpose statement; Amikura speaks as Ichinose's friend, not as an intermediary.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. none.

Unattributed lines. “There was nothing to do but accept it, right?” and “Yeah.” carry no tags at the page break; both are recorded with the gap noted. “Right,” is tagged by “Amikura agreed”.

Translation errors, kept as printed. “Honamichan” is printed without a hyphen in “It's tough for Honamichan, falling for a guy who has a girlfriend.”

Terms, the return, and the messages p.7–9

Setting. the rest area, then the gym exit and the phone screen.

Narrative beats. he calls her straightforward; she says she has a vague idea of him and supposes he is not flustered; he answers “Maybe”; he says he understands and will deal with it well; she apologizes for speaking as a third party; he shows his understanding — “You can't just abandon her as a friend. It's not a bad thing.”; Ichinose returns and apologizes for the wait; Amikura answers, momentarily flustered; Ichinose does not ask what they discussed; he says goodbye and leaves; outside he receives her message asking whether he was having another private talk with Mako-chan about her; he replies that she has a good best friend; she sends a stamp of the utmost happiness.

About these pages. the closing movement; the page withholds whether Ichinose knows the content of the talk and says only that she does not ask.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. none.

Unattributed lines. “You can't just abandon her as a friend. It's not a bad thing.”, “Sorry to keep you both waiting,” and “No, not at all.” carry no tags. They are recorded with the gap noted; the second and third are bounded by “Ichinose returned” and Amikura's fluster.

Translation errors, kept as printed. the two phone lines are printed in square brackets rather than as speech; p.9 ends the story and then prints website navigation (“Home / Our Translations / Side Stories / Partners / Discord / Support Us? / Contact”), the “Royal MTLs” line and a copyright line.

“Ichinose Honami,” huh? — the closing assessment p.9

Setting. outside the gym, after the goodbye.

Narrative beats. he reflects that since the deserted island exam period she has changed a lot, not only recovered but grown; those close to her, Amikura among them, have noticed; he calls her a valuable existence that brings new insights to an analysis he thought finished.

About these pages. the story's last beat; the assessment is his, and the page gives it in his interior voice.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. none.

Unattributed lines. none — the closing lines are narration and interior voice, not dialogue.

Translation errors, kept as printed. p.9's website furniture and credits are printed directly after the story; p.10 is a blank-page scan.
