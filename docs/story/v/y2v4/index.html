Prologue: The Monologue of Amasawa Ichika p.5–7

Setting. Amasawa's thoughts.

Narrative beats. Amasawa reflects on her White Room origins — an in-vitro fertilisation baby, raised by the all-white facility to prove its ideals; she knows nothing of her parents; her existence disproves the facility's premise that environment outweighs genetics; she has decided the White Room's success story, Ayanokōji, is a miracle that shouldn't be worshipped blindly; she has already chosen sides with people she's known for much longer than him (not Ayanokōji), and resolves to watch from a distance.

About these pages. prologue scene.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. none.

Unattributed lines. none.

Translation errors, kept as printed. none observed.

Chapter 1: Hidden Movements p.8–50

Setting. Uninhabited island; forest; heavy rain on day seven.

Narrative beats. The seventh day is washed out by rain (exam suspended; Arrival/Early Bird/Task points doubled on the final day). Amasawa confronts Ayanokōji and Nanase in the rain, reveals she is the White Room student — fifth generation, "interpersonal communications training", Project 5/Project 7 knowledge — and claims admiration, not enmity; Nanase's accusation that she pushed Komiya and Kinoshita down the slope collapses on circumstantial evidence (the broken watch, the larger fleeing footprints, the whittled stick left by someone else). Ayanokōji declines a GPS Search as bait; he retraces the 10-point gap (78 vs 88 expected) to ten undisclosed GPS searches with hourly screenshots; the third-year “school of fish” strategy is revealed (bottom groups conceding Tasks to Nagumo for Class 3-B's Class A ticket) — he stays at 11th place, knowing Kuronaga's 10th-place group blocks him from the public top ten's sabotage magnet.

About these pages. Ayanokōji's POV; Amasawa's confession.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. GPS watch breaking erases the signal with no notice until the next check-in; ranking visibility is top/bottom ten only until day 12; school will double final-day points as weather compensation.

Unattributed lines. none.

Translation errors, kept as printed. none observed.

Chapter 2: Silently with All One's Heart p.51–59

Setting. Island; forest; day eight, clear skies.

Narrative beats. Food shortage on day eight — Ayanokōji is close to empty; Nanase parts at E3 carrying an Arrival Bonus and her own plan to tail Amasawa and Hōsen; he reunites with Horikita at F7 (first time since day one); they coordinate the Shinohara rescue (she is seventh from bottom; Sakayanagi and Class 2-A have already offered a deal; Horikita won first place in a Task rewarding three extra group slots); both divert to a 15-slot food Task; 18 points today; 96 points, 23rd overall; Kuronaga still tenth with 111.

About these pages. Ayanokōji's POV; the rescue coordination.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. none.

Unattributed lines. none.

Translation errors, kept as printed. none observed.

Chapter 3: A Battle Against Solitude p.60–94

Setting. Island; forest; days nine to ten.

Narrative beats. Ayanokōji arrives at D5 with his 11th-place plan intact (112 points, 13th, 11 behind Kuronaga's 123); he detours to a lone one-man tent and discovers Ibuki Mio dying of dehydration — jerky as Class 2-B's class-wide strategy, no friends, Warning Alert already sounded, she trades half her food for his two 500ml water bottles; she storms off unconvinced. At the designated area he meets Fuka Kiryuuin (3rd-year Class B) who jumps from 11th to 9th that morning — she reads the Kōenji–Nagumo deadlock aloud and hints she could be a factor; she provokes him into "force it out" talk. At night he finds Shiranami Chihiro lost in the dark searching for Kobashi "Yume" (Class 2-C); he reunites her with Kobashi and Takemoto; Sakayanagi asks Takemoto to hand Ayanokōji a walkie-talkie — a back-channel line into Class A — for his help.

About these pages. Ayanokōji's POV; the Ibuki trade; Kiryūin's overlook visit.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. The school walkie-talkies have Confidential Mode; GPS Searches cost 1 point; exhaustion/Warning Alerts escalate to Emergency Alerts and forced medical retirement.

Unattributed lines. none.

Translation errors, kept as printed. none observed.

Chapter 4: Encirclement: Koenji VS Free Group p.95–125

Setting. Island; forest; days ten to twelve (movements on day ten).

Narrative beats. Kiriyama Ikuto (3rd-year Class B, vice president) encircles Kōenji with six “Free Groups” (Nagumo's reserve corps of 15 three-person groups, two teams assigned to follow instructions) to stop his top-streak — he fails on day eleven: Kōenji sleeps in broad daylight (short-REM claim), skips the E3 English test (Dodo takes first, Kōenji second with 4 points), walks through the 18-person circle without lifting a hand, and Morooka breaks his arm; Kiriyama concedes the encirclement failed but notes it was deliberate — Kiryūin watches him anyway. Ayanokōji shares camp with Hiyori's merged six (Hiyori, Isoyama, Morofuji, Nishino, Tsutsube, Ishizaki) at I9; Nanase risks her cover at 2:30 a.m. to warn him — the first-years plan a day-12 play on him; he tells her to stop helping so she won't be exposed; day twelve shows no unusual freshman activity; he drops to 16th place.

About these pages. Kiriyama and Kōenji's POV; Ayanokōji's POV.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. the “Free Groups” are Nagumo's reserve three-person groups, two following instructions per group; a three-person team can be blocked by a one-person restriction at one-person Tasks; Morooka is not rostered.

Unattributed lines. “They sure are working very hard.” / “We cannot pretend to be something we are not” (p.109) — printed untagged; Kiriyama or Mikitani; not assigned. “You have done something terrible, Kouenji.” (p.112), “No matter what you say, it doesn't change the fact that you pushed Morooka away.” (p.112), “It seems that you are willing to hit us, your senpai. But if you did, you'd be in big trouble right?” (p.112) — printed untagged; Mikitani or Kiriyama; not assigned.

Translation errors, kept as printed. “atmost two pools” (p.85); “Manuel” (p.67); “senpais” (p.1); Kiryuuin/Kitou/Ryuuen-style ASCII-macross loss throughout.

Chapter 5: Each and Everyone's Opinion p.126–190

Setting. Island; forest; days nine to thirteen.

Narrative beats. The siege of Ayanokōji. On day nine, Tsubaki (Class 1-C) pitches Hōsen a rescue plan for the sinking first-year groups and an attack on Ayanokōji; 500,000-point reward promised to participants; Katagiri delivers a walkie-talkie to Hōsen under threat; Nanase follows Hōsen in. Tsubaki's real plan is exposed in pieces: a “school of fish” of bottom-rank groups + Hōsen as violent muscle + a GPS-fueled encirclement targeting day 12/13; Housen pretends to cooperate while planning to do it alone with violence, because he hates Ayanokōji-senpai; he orders Nanase to stay close and tails Tsubaki — but learns Nanase is on Ayanokōji's side; Ryūen gets the leak via Ayanokōji; he intercepts Hōsen and wins a 3-on-1 with Ishizaki and Albert (GPS disabled) and retires with the unconscious Hōsen — the only way to keep him from going after Ayanokōji again; Sakayanagi runs the countermeasure from the night of day 10 (not told to him): Takemoto's group calls her, she dispatches five groups to freeze the freshmen; Kiryūin laughs and shadows him; Tsubaki's siege collapses as the second-years jam the first; her only consolation is that she caught the group that was protecting him; on day 13 he flees south and ignores six designated areas; Sakayanagi and Ayanokōji's phone call is recorded herein; the third-year containment continues; Kiryūin comes close.

About these pages. Tsubaki and Hōsen's POV; Sakayanagi's countermeasure.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. GPS watches have Confidential Mode for walkie-talkies; a watch broken by its wearer stops transmitting the wearer's signal; expelled-by-withdrawal vs bottom-ten rules restated (p.135); the 20-million-point bounty for Ayanokōji's expulsion; Nagumo's alliance with Tsukishiro is revealed.

Unattributed lines. “They sure are working very hard.” / “We cannot pretend to be something we are not” (p.109) — Kiriyama or Mikitani. “You have done something terrible, Kouenji.” (p.112), “No matter what you say, it doesn't change the fact that you pushed Morooka away.” (p.112), “It seems that you are willing to hit us, your senpai. But if you did, you'd be in big trouble right?” (p.112) — Mikitani or Kiriyama. “That was a bit insistent.” (p.133) — Nanase or Utomiya. “The head, the brain.” (p.133) — Tsubaki. “Can't you trust me?” (p.139) — Hōsen.

Translation errors, kept as printed. “senpais” (throughout); Ayanokouji/Tsubaki/Yagami/Utomiya/Ryūen ASCII-macross loss throughout the chapter; “dwelve” (p.148); “Senpai” capitalization inconsistent; “Housen” vs “Hōsen” variance.

Chapter 6: The Man Known As Tsukishiro p.191–275

Setting. Island; forest; I2's tip; the last day (day thirteen to fourteen).

Narrative beats. Day thirteen on the last day. Sakayanagi reports that Ichinose has disappeared — he can't reach I2; he finds her on H3's path — she walked 4½ hours to warn him that Tsukishiro and Shiba were waiting at I2 and that her watch broke; she confesses “Because I, because I like you, Ayanokouji-kun…!”; he thanks her but cannot reciprocate; she rejoins her group. He walks to I2; President Nagumo is waiting on the beach; they spar — bounty talk (Tsukishiro's order, 20 million points); the superpower guess — Nagumo guesses 11th, misses; the Kuronaga 10th-lock plan; one punch and Nagumo drops with a warning alarm; Ayanokōji hides him under a tree. At I2's tip, Tsukishiro and Shiba wait with a boat at the ready; Tsukishiro reveals the full plan; Kiryūin appears (watch deliberately smashed) and holds Shiba; Tsukishiro fights Ayanokōji one-on-one — he nearly wins; Ayanokōji reads a hidden stun gun from a glance at Tsukishiro's feet; he beats Tsukishiro with a one-punch-stun gun sequence — Mashima and Chabashira arrive on the boat Tsukishiro called for as backup? No — Sakayanagi's walkie-talkie; Sakayanagi's five groups were ready; Tsukishiro concedes; Ichinose's confession saved him; Shiba is spared by Kiryūin's hold; the White Room story ends “for now”.

About these pages. Ichinose's POV section; Ayanokōji's POV; Kiryūin's POV.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. 20 million private points for Ayanokōji's expulsion; the 20-million-point Class A ticket; the White Room's fourth/fifth-generation structure; Tsukishiro's “4” combat rating was disinformation (he's far above it); Shiba “6” is likewise inflated; walkie-talkie Confidential Mode confirmed.

Unattributed lines. none.

Translation errors, kept as printed. “Ayanokouji” ASCII-macross loss throughout the chapter; “Kiryuuin” macross loss; “Senpai” capitalization variance.

Epilogue: And the Results Are In p.276–284

Setting. Ship; evening.

Narrative beats. The ship at last; two weeks of testing done; 6 p.m. call — results announced on the ship; the bottom five groups were called in early to settle expulsion fees; the five withdrawn groups are all third-years — 15 students expelled (Mutō, Kawakami (3-D), Shinonome, Katsumata (3-C), Mikitani (3-B)); Nagumo fails to save his own; Class D celebrates — no second-year withdrew; the results: 3rd place — Sakayanagi's group (261 points); 2nd place — Nagumo (325); 1st place — Kōenji (327, alone) — 300 class points and a Protection Point; Kōenji is granted immunity until graduation; Tsukishiro's “Nothing has occurred here. Ayanokouji-kun and I just had a chat.” to Mashima; Horikita's “I know Ayanokouji-senpai very well” debate; Ayanokōji and Kei; the White Room story is not over — “I just don't think this is the end of the whole White Room thing.”

About these pages. the results; Class D's survival.

New names. none.

New rules and facts. 15 students expelled (p.279); Protection Point for Kōenji; immunity until graduation for Kōenji.

Unattributed lines. none.

Translation errors, kept as printed. “senpais” (p.235); Ayanokouji/Kouenji/Ryuuen ASCII-macross loss throughout.
