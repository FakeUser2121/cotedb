Vol. 1Club Fair debut: absolute presence
Vol. 1 p.66–68
Do your best!
Did you forget to bring your notecards?
Year 1, Volume 1, page 66
10 entries · 3 quoted
  • PostStudent council —

    Student Council President; takes the stage at the club fair.

    p.66–68
  • Scene

    School gymnasium club fair. Representatives from various athletic and cultural clubs deliver lively pitches. Student council president Horikita Manabu steps onto the stage.

  • What happens

    Slender build, sharp analytical gaze behind glasses, neatly trimmed black hair. Merely by stepping up to the microphone, his sheer aura and commanding presence reduce over a hundred chattering first-year students to dead, breathless silence without him saying a single word or raising a hand.

    p.66–67
  • Spoken

    “I’m the student council president. My name is Horikita Manabu.”

    p.67
  • Spoken

    “The student council is looking to recruit potential candidates among the first-year students to replace the graduating third years. Although no special qualifications are required for candidacy, we humbly ask that those considering application not be involved in other club activities. We generally do not accept students involved elsewhere.”

    p.67
  • Spoken

    “Furthermore, we in the student council do not wish to appoint anyone who possesses a naive outlook. Not only would such a person not be elected, he or she would sully the sanctity of this school. It is the student council’s right and duty to enforce and amend the rules, but the school expects more than that. We gladly welcome those of you who understand this.”

    p.67–68
  • What happens

    Delivers his speech in a quiet, eloquent, razor-sharp monotone without pausing once. Immediately hops off the stage and walks out of the gymnasium, leaving the room paralyzed with intimidation.

    p.68
  • Feat

    Unmatched psychological dominance and institutional authority; commands immediate, involuntary deference from the entire freshman class.

    p.66–68
  • Voice

    Austere, aristocratic, intensely disciplined, zero warmth or theatricality; speaks with absolute executive authority.

    p.67–68
  • Rule

    Student Council membership condition: absolute commitment; members are prohibited from participating in any other school club.

    p.67
Vol. 1Night confrontation, martial arts brawl, & sizing up Ayanokōji
Vol. 1 p.192–196
Hmm?
Well, I don’t really need to hide, but…
Year 1, Volume 1, page 192
23 entries · 12 quoted
  • Scene

    Dark pathway outside student dormitories, late night.

  • What happens

    Confronts his younger sister Suzune in the dead of night; displays chilling, emotionless detachment, treating her like an insignificant nuisance.

    p.192–193
  • Spoken

    “Suzune. I didn’t think you’d follow me this far.”

    p.192
  • Spoken

    “I heard you were placed in Class D. I suppose nothing has really changed in the last three years. You’ve always been fixated on following me, and as a result you don’t notice your own flaws. Choosing to come to this school was a mistake.”

    p.192
  • Spoken

    “It’s pointless. You will never reach Class A. In fact, your class will fall apart soon enough. Things at this school aren’t as simple as you think.”

    p.192
  • What happens

    Grabs Suzune roughly by the wrist and slams her back against the rough concrete wall when she insists she will reach Class A.

    p.193
  • Spoken

    “I told you, it’s pointless. You really are a disobedient little sister.”

    p.193
  • Spoken

    “No matter how I try to avoid you, the fact remains that you’re my little sister. If people around here learned the truth, I would be humiliated. Leave this school immediately.”

    p.193
  • Spoken

    “How incredibly stupid. Do you want to relive the pain of the past?”

    p.193
  • Spoken

    “You possess neither the abilities nor the qualities needed to reach Class A. Get that through your head.”

    p.193
  • What happens

    Prepares to violently slam Suzune onto the concrete ground, but his right wrist is suddenly intercepted and gripped from behind by Ayanokōji.

    p.193–194
  • Spoken

    “What? You… Eavesdropping is not an admirable quality.”

    p.193
  • What happens

    Instantly unleashes a vicious martial combination the second Ayanokōji lets go: throws a blinding backhand strike aimed directly at AK’s face, followed immediately by a sharp kick targeting an unguarded opening, and reaches out with an open hand to execute a throw.

    p.194
  • Feat

    Master martial artist: holds **5th dan in karate** and **4th dan in aikido**; strikes with enough lethal force and speed to knock an opponent unconscious in a single blow.

    p.194, 196
  • What happens

    Exhales deeply in slight confusion when Ayanokōji evades all strikes and slaps his throw hand away.

    p.196
  • Spoken

    “Good reflexes. I didn’t imagine you could evade all of my blows so quickly. Also, you seemed to understand quite well what I was trying to do. Have you been taught?”

    p.196
  • What happens

    Directly penetrates Ayanokōji's facade when AK claims he only learned piano and calligraphy. Delivers a biting philosophical diagnosis to Suzune and a parting assessment to AK.

    p.196
  • Spoken

    “You’re in Class D, too, aren’t you? What a unique boy, Suzune.”

    p.196
  • Spoken

    “Suzune, is this boy your friend? I’m honestly surprised.”

    p.196
  • Spoken

    “You continue to confuse independence with solitude. And you, Ayanokouji. With you around, things might get interesting.”

    p.196
  • Trait

    Uncompromising perfectionist; views weakness and familial dependency with icy disgust; highly perceptive judge of martial and psychological capability.

    p.192–196
  • Relationship

    Older brother; regards her as a humiliating, inferior shadow desperately chasing him; demands she withdraw from school.

    Horikita Suzune — p.192–193
  • Relationship

    Immediately recognizes an extraordinary individual capable of reacting to and neutralizing high-dan martial techniques.

    Ayanokōji Kiyotaka — p.193–196
