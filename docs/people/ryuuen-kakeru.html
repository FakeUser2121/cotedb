Vol. 2Ch.7 §7.4: named before he is seenp.241
3 entries
  • Caveat

    The volume names him for the first time in Ichinose’s warning: "Someone named Ryuuen-kun masterminded that one." — the earlier Class B–Class C dispute, and the Sudou setup behind this case. p.241

  • Trait

    As Ichinose describes him: "He doesn’t hesitate to hurt other people for the sake of his own interests. He’s a formidable opponent." p.241

  • Limit

    She adds that he "hasn’t seen any reason to reveal himself just yet," which is why Ayanokōji does not know the name. p.241

Vol. 2Ch.7 §7.6: the boy in the corridorp.254–255
5 entries · 2 quoted
  • Scene

    He stops in front of Ayanokōji and Horikita in the corridor — black hair worn long, about Ayanokōji’s height, a wide grin the page calls ominous, never fully turning to face them. The scene does not name him. p.254

  • Spoken

    "Setting up a camera? You really did something funny, huh?" p.254

  • Spoken

    "Next time, I’ll be your opponent. I look forward to it." p.254

  • What happens

    He walks on without answering Horikita’s "And you are?" p.254

  • Position

    Ayanokōji’s closing inventory names the encounter: "We’d received a declaration of war from some guy apparently known as Ryuuen." p.255
