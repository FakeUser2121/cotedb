- **Caveat** (p.150) — He comes with her already on his mind: “I’d reluctantly planned to ask Hirata and Kushida about Sakayanagi, but those two didn’t seem well informed on the subject.”

- **Spoken · Ryūen Kakeru** (p.223) — “If this wasn’t an honorable deed, then what are you going to do? Did you know that Sakayanagi’s faction has dominated ever since rumors spread that you failed to get on the student council despite your best efforts? This might be your chance, right?”
