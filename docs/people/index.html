Where the pages place them

A post is listed only where a note taken off the page states it beside the page number. That keeps the claim checkable and keeps it dated: a promotion or a transfer adds an entry under the volume where it happens instead of rewriting the earlier ones. Nothing here is inferred from whose classroom a scene happens to be set in.
