#### p.298–300 — The equality proposal is refused

Horikita offers a relationship of equals; he answers with vulgar conduct and asks whether she is the leader before rejecting the premise. He calls the penalty gap irrelevant, demands water and says they will pay. Nanase asks him to let Horikita finish; he calls disobedient students trash and demands to know what he gains.

#### p.301–303 — Nanase's protection-point argument

Nanase argues that enemies made now may deny him partners later; she raises the school's protection points. Horikita confirms that a partner holding a protection point could leave him forcibly expelled; the negotiation's tone shifts. He still refuses equal footing; Horikita holds Sudō back before he can lash out.

#### p.304–305 — The million-point collateral

He calls the trust argument one-sided and asks for a million points as collateral, promising repayment if he ever needs their help. Horikita refuses; he leans forward for the first time.

- **Caveat** (p.304–305) — The deposit would have to be repaid only if Class 1-D were ever asked for help; a written contract would still leave the class exposed.
- **Trait** (p.305) — Not merely a musclehead: the move is calculated and precise, and the narrator compares him to Ryūen.

#### p.306–308 — Collar, water and the broken negotiation

He grabs Horikita's collar; Sudō seizes his arm, and he offers to settle the deal by fighting all three of them at once. Horikita calls the discussion over; he releases her, then throws his cup of water in her face. Sudō charges and Horikita stops him, warning him not to fall into the trap.

#### p.309–311 — The party follows them out

Horikita walks away; Nanase says cooperation would have been better, and the group dissolves with Horikita unbowed. He follows them, admits he had gone looking for Class 2-D and calls the two D classes comrades.

#### p.312–315 — The price doubles, then triples

He demands two million points as the classes reach the dormitory junction; Horikita says she is ready to take the penalty. She counters that Class 1-D only needs 501 points; he says he will flatten them, and then unexpectedly says he will consider her position. He moves the discussion to the back of the first-year dormitory at 10 PM; Horikita allows ten minutes. He raises the price to three million; Sudō and Horikita are dumbfounded.

#### p.316–321 — The brawl with Sudō

He sweeps Sudō's legs and charges; Nanase looks away as if she knows what is coming. He overpowers Sudō against the wall; Nanase explains that Nagumo's student council now tolerates a degree of fighting. He keeps Sudō pinned and beaten; the narrator notes his overwhelming experience and speed. He points at Horikita to break Sudō's guard, then punches him from behind with a fiendish grin.

#### p.322–325 — Horikita is slapped; the knife threat

He asks who is next and says he will beat them until they sign binding agreements. The narrator weighs what he is really after: the violence escalates beyond what a negotiation would require. He catches Horikita's single strike, slaps her across the face and demands five million points. He threatens to hurt the two doormats if the narrator runs, then says he will let Ayanokōji live if they submit.

- **Spoken** (p.322) — “Aight, seems that your trusty bodyguard’s eatin’ the dirt. Who’s next?”

#### p.326–329 — The knife, Nanase's confession and Ayanokōji's charge

He draws a Petty knife and threatens to stab the narrator. Nanase blocks him and tells Horikita that cooperation was never possible: the Class 2-D overture was a ploy, and the class was to profit from the bounty on Ayanokōji. He sweeps Nanase aside and raises the blade; Ayanokōji charges him instead of fleeing.

- **Spoken** (p.326) — “Somethin’ wrong with your eyes? It’s a knife, plain an’ simple!”

#### p.330–333 — The frame is exposed

The narrator lets the blade pierce his palm; the plot was for Hōsen to stab his own thigh with the knife and blame Ayanokōji. The narrator explains the reverse grip, the sheath, and how he identified his own knife; Hōsen's fist is locked in place.

- **Spoken** (p.331) — “Ha! So even though you managed to piece it all together, you still came an’ got stabbed yourself? You outta your mind?”
- **Spoken** (p.333) — “…The hell…? Who the fuck are you…?”
- **Relationship · Ayanokōji Kiyotaka** (p.321–337) — Targets him for the bounty; is completely outwitted and bested, then becomes his partner — “the first opponent who’s gotten my blood boilin’.”
- **Relationship · Nanase Tsubasa** (p.296–328) — Brought her as mediator/block; berates her when she reveals the plot and stops his knife.

#### p.334–337 — Amasawa's part, the offer, and the new rivalry

The narrator traces the knife to Amasawa, who stopped his purchase at Keyaki Mall and later retrieved the copy planted in Ayanokōji's room. He curses having trusted her; the narrator points out the plan could not have taken shape without her. Ayanokōji asks him to be his partner; he refuses to accept that Ryūen is rated above him and is wounded in his pride. He accepts the outcome and looks forward to beating Ayanokōji next time; Nanase stays behind to speak to the second-years, and he leaves first.

- **Relationship · Amasawa Ichika** (p.334) — She helped refine his knife plot and retrieved the knife; he calls her a "random chick" he shouldn’t have trusted.
- **Spoken** (p.337) — “Fuckin’ good shit there, Ayanokōji-sempai! You’re the first opponent who’s gotten my blood boilin’ like this.”
- **Trait** (p.296–337) — Violent, proud and cunning — escalates from posturing to slapping Horikita and sucker-punching Sudō, then draws a knife intending to stab himself in the thigh with Ayanokōji's own Petty knife and frame him for expulsion; outsmarted when Ayanokōji lets himself be stabbed, his plot backfires and he accepts the deal.
