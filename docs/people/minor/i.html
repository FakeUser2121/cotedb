Vol. 1Classroom Day 1: girlfriend hunting intro
Vol. 1 p.38
They’re not a bad bunch. It’s my fault. I was being selfish and made people do this.
No way. You didn’t do anything wrong, Hirata-kun. Let’s just leave those guys be, okay?
Year 1, Volume 1, page 38
  • Scene

    Class 1-D classroom during Hirata's introductions.

  • What happens

    Stands up cheerfully to introduce himself to the class. p.38

  • Spoken

    “I’m Ike Kanji. I love girls, and I hate pretty boys. I’m currently in the market for a new girlfriend. It’s nice to meet you! All the better if you’re a cutie or a beauty!” p.38

  • What happens

    The girls look at him with overt disgust. When one girl deadpans in an emotionless voice that he is "so cool", Ike fails to detect the sarcasm, blushes, and takes it as genuine praise. p.38

  • Spoken

    “Seriously? Seriously? Oh, man. I mean, I thought that I wasn’t bad or anything, but…heh heh.” p.38

  • What happens

    The entire female half of the class erupts into laughter at his gullibility. Instead of taking offense, he jovially plays along with being teased. p.38

  • Trait

    Boisterous, girl-crazy, naive, socially thick-skinned, easily duped by flattery, but cheerful and good-natured. p.38

  • Voice

    Casual, uninhibited, energetic teenage colloquialisms. p.38
