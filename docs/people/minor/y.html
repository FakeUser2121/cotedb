Vol. 1Classroom Day 1: the fabricated athletic recordp.34
  • Scene

    Class 1-D classroom during Hirata's introductions, following Inogashira.

  • What happens

    Steps forward smoothly to deliver his introduction. p.34

  • Spoken

    “I’m Yamauchi Haruki. I competed in table tennis during elementary school, and in junior high I was the ace player on our baseball team. I was number four. I got hurt during the inter-high school championships, though, and I’m undergoing rehab now. Nice to meet you.” p.34

  • Trait

    Habitual bluffer and exaggerator; invents grandiose athletic credentials to impress classmates, unaware that claiming to have played in the "inter-high school championships" (a high school exclusive tournament) while in junior high exposes his lie immediately. Talkative and easily carried away. p.34

  • Voice

    Boastful, overly detailed self-aggrandizement. p.34

    Shameless braggart with remarkably low general knowledge; invents claims out of thin air that immediately collapse under basic scrutiny. p.118

Vol. 10-point bankruptcy & pawning the game consolep.120, 126, 128, 137–138
  • Scene

    Classroom 1-D, May 1st morning homeroom and lunch break.

  • What happens

    Exchanges bewildered glances with Hondou when no monthly points arrive. p.120

  • What happens

    Wails in theatrical, incredible agony upon realizing Class D received zero points, having completely drained his initial 100,000 points in April. p.126

  • Limit

    Academic failure: scores in the red on the short test, placed on the list of seven students facing immediate expulsion at midterms. p.128

  • What happens

    Suddenly appears from under Ayanokōji’s desk, expression deathlike, to pawn off his handheld video game console. p.137

  • Spoken

    “Hey, buy this from me for 20,000 points. I can’t buy anything!” (cried) p.137

  • Spoken

    “How the heck should I know? Come on, it’s good, right? It’s special, so it’s a good deal.” p.137

  • What happens

    Outraged when Ayanokōji counters with a ruthless offer of just 1,000 points; goes teary-eyed, grossing Ayanokōji out. p.137

  • Spoken

    “Ayanokouji! Come on, you’re my only hope!” p.137

  • What happens

    Realizing Ayanokōji isn’t biting, immediately switches targets to Sotomura (“The Professor”) — shamelessly raising the ask price to 22,000 points. p.137

  • Spoken

    “Professor! Your best buddy has a favor to ask! Buy this game system for 22,000 points!” p.137

  • Trait

    Shameless hustler; tries to exploit friendship for personal financial rescue; panics under immediate monetary stress. p.137–138

  • Relationship

    Targets him as an easy mark for pawning goods, only to be shut down by AK's hardball bargaining. — Ayanokōji Kiyotaka p.137–138

Vol. 2Ch.3: one sentence from him undoes Kushida's whole appealp.42–45
  • Spoken

    “Hey, Kushida-chan. I just can’t believe what Sudou said. I think he lied so he could justify what he did. He talked about beating up kids all the time in junior high. He even told us how much fun it was to beat people up.” p.42

  • Feat

    He is the pivot of the homeroom: AK marks the turn as his — "Instantly, silence enveloped the classroom. The first to speak wasn’t the eyewitness, but Yamauchi." and "After Yamauchi voiced his concerns, the rest of the class murmured their dissatisfaction with Sudou in turn." p.42–43

  • Scene

    At the cafeteria table he volunteers first for the case: “I guess it’s just up to us.” — Sudou reads it as loyalty: "I knew for sure you’d understand, Yamauchi. I’m really counting on you too, Ayanokouji." p.45

  • Relationship

    The same boy who supplies the class's proof that Sudou is a habitual bully is then trusted as "one of us" when Sudou needs helpers; AK notes the ranking his own name gets behind Yamauchi's. — Sudō Ken p.42, p.45

  • Relationship

    His accusation is spoken to Kushida by name and lands because the class already half-believes it — he says out loud what Sudou has bragged about to him and Ike privately. — Ike Kanji p.42

Vol. 2Ch.3 §3.1: the other half of the crush councilp.48–52
  • Spoken

    “No way. You seriously think she’ll stoop to your level, Ike?” p.48

  • Spoken

    “H-hey, what are you fantasizing about?! Spill it!” — AK: "Apparently Yamauchi couldn’t stand the fact that Ike could do as he pleased in his fantasies." p.48

  • Spoken

    “Damn it! I won’t be outdone! I’ve also thought of a few things!” p.50

  • Spoken

    “It would be best if Kushida-chan were my girlfriend… It would be the best if she were my girlfriend…” — AK counts the repetition: "Yamauchi said it twice. It must’ve been important to him." p.50

  • Spoken

    “But wait. Since Kushida-chan is so cute, don’t you think she’ll get a boyfriend any day now?” — Ike has to reply "Don’t say that, Yamauchi!" p.50

  • Spoken

    “I see… I feel like I can imagine that. So cute.” — on Sudou's fantasy Horikita: "Yamauchi glanced at Horikita and got lost in his delusional fantasies." p.51

  • Trait

    Competitive rather than romantic: his desire for Kushida is measured against Ike's and voiced as one-upmanship ("I won’t be outdone"), and he can transfer the same script onto Horikita minutes after hearing Sudou describe her. p.48–52

  • Relationship

    She is his stated girlfriend target, named twice in one breath; AK's pity for her is aimed at exactly this pair. — Kushida Kikyō p.50

  • Relationship

    He is lost in a Horikita fantasy while she is, at that same table, refusing Sudou outright — the gap between his version of her and the real one is the joke AK records. — Horikita Suzune p.51

Vol. 2Ch.3 §3.2–3.3: held in the classroom, then frozen in Class Bp.54, p.61–63
  • Scene

  • Scene

    In the doorway of Class B: "Even Ike and Yamauchi appeared to be shaking in their boots. They simply stood frozen in the classroom doorway, unable to move." — "The pair, comrades in vexation, grasped each other’s arms." p.63

  • Spoken

    “Don’t panic, Ike. It’s okay. We’re in Kushida-chan’s class, so we’re one step ahead of them!” p.63

  • Scene

  • Limit

    He is asked to canvass and does stand in a doorway; no witness lead in Chapter 3 is ever traced to him. p.54–63

Vol. 2Ch.4 §4.1: he has the only confident opinion of Sakura, and it is inventedp.79–82
  • Scene

    He is the one surprised by Sudou's blankness about the girl who sits near him: "Yamauchi appeared quite surprised." p.79

  • Spoken

    “Can’t we just go to Sakura’s room right now? We don’t have much time.” — AK: "I thought that Yamauchi’s proposal seemed innocuous, but it all came down to Sakura’s personality and behavior." p.80

  • Spoken

    “She’s plain, that’s it. Talk about a waste, man. Like pearls before swine.” — AK's staging of the line: "While Yamauchi spoke, he gestured with his hands near his chest, to represent Sakura’s breasts." p.81

  • Spoken

    “Uh, ahh…well, I may or may not have said something like that.” — cornered by Ike's memory of a confession to Sakura. p.82

  • Spoken

    “N-no, I wasn’t. I wasn’t lying. It was just a misunderstanding. It wasn’t Sakura; it was a girl from the next class over. Not a gloomy and ugly girl like Sakura. Err, hold on. Sorry, I got a message.” — AK: "Yamauchi dodged the question by whipping out his phone." p.82

  • Trait

    He will say anything about a girl he has never spoken to and retract it in the same breath to stay socially safe; his cruelty is reflexive and his cowardice immediate. p.81–82

  • Relationship

    The volume's only claim that anyone ever confessed to her is a claim he withdraws under pressure — and the withdrawal describes her as "gloomy and ugly" while doing it. — Sakura Airi p.82

Vol. 2Ch.4 §4.8, §4.11: he is a category in someone else's phone etiquettep.129, p.148
  • Feat

    He is ruled out of Kushida's errand before it is offered: "If she’d invited Ike or Yamauchi along, they would have had eyes only for Kushida." p.129

  • Position

    In the narrator's ranking of callers he is in the call-back-later tier: "If it were Ike or Yamauchi, I’d call them back later. But if it were Horikita…I’d have to think about it." p.148

  • Caveat
