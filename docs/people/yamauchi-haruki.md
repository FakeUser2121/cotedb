#### p.34 — Classroom Day 1: the fabricated athletic record

Class 1-D classroom during Hirata's introductions, following Inogashira.

- **What happens** (p.34) — Steps forward smoothly to deliver his introduction.
- **Spoken** (p.34) — “I’m Yamauchi Haruki. I competed in table tennis during elementary school, and in junior high I was the ace player on our baseball team. I was number four. I got hurt during the inter-high school championships, though, and I’m undergoing rehab now. Nice to meet you.”
- **Trait** (p.34) — Habitual bluffer and exaggerator; invents grandiose athletic credentials to impress classmates, unaware that claiming to have played in the "inter-high school championships" (a high school exclusive tournament) while in junior high exposes his lie immediately. Talkative and easily carried away.
- **Voice** (p.34) — Boastful, overly detailed self-aggrandizement.
