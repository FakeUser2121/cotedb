Entrance ceremony bus: the public championY1V1 · p.19–24

Crowded city bus heading to ANHS on entrance ceremony morning. An elderly woman is standing in the aisle, unsteady on her feet. An office lady scolds Kōenji first; most passengers look away or pretend not to notice.

with Kōenji Rokusuke

  • “Um… I think that the lady is right.” (unexpected support, delivered brave and frank)p.21
  • “This poor woman appears to have been suffering for quite some time now. Won’t you offer up your seat? While you might consider such courtesy unnecessary, I think it would contribute greatly to society.”p.21
  • “Everyone, please listen to me for just a moment. Won’t someone give up their seat for this woman? It doesn’t matter who. Please.”p.22
6 more notes
  • What happens An unnamed girl wearing the ANHS uniform — the text never names her here, but she is the one later understood to be Kushida (see [note]) — steps forward from the aisle and directly faces Kōenji, who is lounging in the priority seating.p.21
  • What happens When Kōenji haughtily refuses and declares he cares only for his own satisfaction, she refuses to back down or accept defeat. Instead of retreating, she turns to face the entire crowded bus without fear.p.21–22
  • Feat Extraordinary social courage and charisma; her appeal moves an office lady to stand and surrender her seat out of guilt (“E-excuse me. You can have mine.”), settling the matter.p.22–24
  • Voice Earnest, pleading, gentle, highly moral public register that commands collective attention without sounding aggressive.p.22
  • Relationship Kōenji Rokusuke Direct moral clash; attempts to appeal to social conscience against his absolute solipsism.p.21
  • Caveat The bus text never names the uniformed challenger. Her identification with Kushida is an inference (she later knows AK and Horikita spoke at the gate that day, p.57), not a stated fact in Year 1, Vol. 1.p.19–24

Classroom Day 1: empathetic rescue & friendship declarationY1V1 · p.34–35

Class 1-D classroom after homeroom orientation. Hirata asks students to introduce themselves from the front row.

  • “It’s okay to go slowly. Don’t rush.”p.34
  • “My name is Kushida Kikyou. None of my friends from junior high made it to this school, so I’m alone here. I’d like to get to know all of your names and faces right away and become friends as soon as possible!”p.35
  • “My first goal is to become friends with everyone. So, after we’re finished with introductions, I’d love for you to share your contact information with me!”p.35
7 more notes
  • Post Established as the idol and social anchor of Class 1-D.p.35
  • What happens Inogashira Kokoro freezes at her desk, trembling and pale. Classmates mistakenly shout “Do your best!” which heightens her panic. Kushida steps in with immediate, emotionally intelligent guidance.p.34
  • Feat Instinctive social psychology: understands that “Do your best!” applies forceful pressure, whereas “Just take things slowly. Don’t rush,” removes urgency and restores calm. Inogashira successfully finishes her intro.p.34
  • What happens Steps up cheerfully as the next speaker, glowing with approachable energy.p.34–35
  • Spoken “So, after school or during vacations, I want to make all sorts of memories with lots of people. Please feel free to invite me to lots and lots of events! Anyway, I’ve talked for a long time, so I’ll end my introduction here.”p.35
  • Trait Outward persona: boundless warmth, radical accessibility, open-hearted desire to unite the entire class and befriend every student without distinction.p.35
  • Position Instantly established as the undisputed idol and social anchor of Class 1-D.p.35

Corridor interception: probing for Horikita's secretsY1V1 · p.56–57

On Ayanokōji’s way out of the classroom at lunch on Day 2 (he’d resolved to eat alone). Kushida approaches him with a radiant smile, holding something he can’t identify — possibly a pouch covered in key holders.

with Horikita Suzune · Ayanokōji Kiyotaka

  • “I’m Kushida, from your class. Do you remember me?” she asked.p.56
  • “To tell you the truth, there’s something I wanted to ask you. It’s just one little question. Ayanokouji-kun, are you on good terms with Horikita-san?”p.56
  • “Oh, no. Well, do you remember when I said I wanted to get along with everyone in class? That’s why I wanted everyone’s contact info. But… Horikita turned me down.”p.56
12 more notes
  • What happens Calls out to him cheerfully (“You’re Ayanokouji-kun, right?”); Ayanokōji’s heart pounds like a jackhammer at her beauty (short, straight dyed-brown hair, one of the newly approved short skirt lengths).p.56
  • What happens When he says he barely knows Horikita, she pivots to probing what kind of person she is, then frames her interest as pointed “worry.”p.57
  • Spoken “I was just wondering what kind of person Horikita-san is,” (Kushida continued)p.57
  • Spoken “Is she the type who’ll talk a lot when she’s with a friend?”p.57
  • Spoken “Well, during our introductions, Horikita-san walked out of the classroom, right? It seems like she hasn’t talked to anybody yet, so I’m a little worried about her.”p.57
  • Spoken “Hmm. I see. I thought that you two must have been old friends before starting school here. I’m sorry to have asked you such a strange question.”p.57
  • Spoken “How? You introduced yourself the other day, didn’t you? I remembered.” (she’d listened to his hopelessly lame self-introduction)p.57
  • What happens Closes with a radiant handshake: “Well, it’s nice to meet you again, Ayanokouji-kun.”p.57
  • Trait Relentless social campaigner; frames investigative probing as friendly concern; remembers even the least remarkable classmates’ introductions; warmth weaponized as an information-gathering tool.p.56–57
  • Voice Bubbly, innocent, persistently encouraging, masking an unyielding determination.p.56–57
  • Relationship Horikita Suzune Fixation begins: determined to break through Horikita’s barriers; encodes her as the girl who refused contact info and “hasn’t talked to anybody yet.”p.56–57
  • Relationship Ayanokōji Kiyotaka Recruits him as her bridge and information source to reach Horikita; establishes their first warm real-world contact (handshake).p.56–57

Café trap failure & the compulsion for 100% friendshipY1V1 · p.89, 91–93, 98–101

Classroom 1-D; outside dorms; then Palate Café in Keyaki Mall. Palate Café. Kushida walks in, acting surprised, but Horikita immediately exposes the four-person table and nervous glances as a transparent setup.

with Horikita Suzune · Ayanokōji Kiyotaka

  • “I’ll just ask you outright. Ayanokouji-kun, have you seen Horikita-san smile even once?”p.91
  • “Horikita-san, whatever you say, I really want to be friends with you. When I saw you, I felt like it wasn’t the first time we’d met. I wondered if you felt the same way.”p.99
  • “It’s okay. Thank you, Ayanokouji-kun. It’s true that I wasn’t able to become her friend, but…I was able to learn something important. That’s enough for me.”p.100
12 more notes
  • What happens Smilingly invites Horikita to a café after class; when told not to invite her again because it's a bother, Kushida maintains her bright grin and promises: *“I’ll invite you again.”*p.89
  • What happens Waits outside the dormitory for Ayanokōji. Takes his hand warmly, stepping into his personal space with a floral scent.p.91
  • What happens Conspires with Ayanokōji to stage an ambush meeting disguised as coincidence at Palate Café in Keyaki Mall, directing him to lure Horikita there under the pretense of treating her to tea.p.91–93
  • What happens Faced with Horikita's icy refusal to sit, Kushida mumbles an ominous hint of prior recognition.p.99
  • What happens Horikita cuts her off, drops her bombshell about being alone for nine consecutive years, and leaves.p.99–100
  • What happens Kushida collapses silently into her seat, genuinely stunned and winded by the brutal rebuff.p.100
  • What happens Recovers her trademark sunshine smile in a split-second.p.100
  • What happens Moves to sit beside Ayanokōji at the table. When asked why she is so desperate to befriend Horikita when she already has more friends than anyone else, Kushida reveals her underlying psychological compulsion.p.101
  • Spoken “I wanted to be friends with everyone. Not just the people in Class D, but the students from other classes, too. But if I can’t become friends with one girl in my class, then that means I’ll never achieve my goal…”p.101
  • Trait Terrifying emotional switch: can collapse in despair and snap back to a beaming smile in an instant; driven by an absolute, obsessive psychological need for 100% universal social acceptance—a single holdout (Horikita) invalidates her entire self-worth.p.100–101
  • Relationship Horikita Suzune Suspects or hints at a prior encounter from junior high; fixated on conquering her wall.p.99
  • Relationship Ayanokōji Kiyotaka Deepens alliance; relies on him as a co-conspirator; surprised to learn he was also friendless in junior high.p.91, 100–101
