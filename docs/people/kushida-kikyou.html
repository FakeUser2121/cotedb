Arc

y1v1

  • Situation: School's most social girl systematically befriends everyone while engineering Horikita's isolation.
  • Knowledge state: Already knows Ayanokouji from junior high and is probing how much Horikita remembers.
  • Changes: Recruits Ayanokouji as a Horikita-convergence tool; drops the first genuine mask slip in private ('I really hate you').
  • Why it matters: Establishes the two-layer voice the whole series runs on: the public warmth, the private war.

y1v2

  • Situation: Sudou trial: she plays the tireless class organizer.
  • Knowledge state: Running reconnaissance on who knows what about Sakura and the witness chain.

  • Why it matters: Peak visible usefulness: the mask earns her the class's trust she intends to weaponize.

y1v5

  • Situation: Sports festival leak traced back toward her; Ayanokouji forces the private confrontation.

  • Why it matters: The mask comes off as a choice, not an accident: 'I will have the school expel you... deal with the devil'.

y1v6

  • Why it matters: The confession reframes her from schemer to addict: visibility is the drug, sabotage the supply.
