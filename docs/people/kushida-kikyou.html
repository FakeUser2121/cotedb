    Extraordinary social courage and charisma; her appeal moves an office lady to stand and surrender her seat out of guilt (“E-excuse me. You can have mine.”), settling the matter. p.22–24

    Earnest, pleading, gentle, highly moral public register that commands collective attention without sounding aggressive. p.22

    The bus text never names the uniformed challenger. Her identification with Kushida is an inference (she later knows AK and Horikita spoke at the gate that day, p.57), not a stated fact in Vol. 1. p.19–24
