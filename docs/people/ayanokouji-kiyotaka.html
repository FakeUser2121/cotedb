Arc

y1v1

  • Situation: Enrolled into the school's lowest class; lives as an invisible loner while Horikita's cold war with Kushida's social web begins.
  • Knowledge state: Reads Class D's point deficit and Kushida's junior-high history with him long before either surfaces openly.
  • Changes: Turns down Manabu Horikita's guidance-office challenge; accepts a quiet orbit around Horikita (lunch, the study group as bridge).
  • Why it matters: Establishes the 'no trouble' mask the rest of the series punctures.

y1v2

  • Situation: Sudou's assault accusation threatens the class's first island points.
  • Knowledge state: Understands Kushida's hostility toward Horikita is a personal grudge, not class politics.
  • Changes: Builds the witness strategy and publicly vouches for Sakura's testimony — first real cost-taking.
  • Why it matters: Converts him from observer to reluctant operator while the humility mask ('don't keep overestimating me') holds.
