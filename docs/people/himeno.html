Himeno: the silent dissenter; the explosionY2V7 · p.62–68

Summoned by Ayanokōji to the special wing; surprised to find Kanzaki there too — Kanzaki assumed Himeno would be one of the last people he'd want to see.

with Ayanokōji Kiyotaka · Kanzaki Ryūji · Ichinose Honami

  • “I thought it was impossible. Classes don't change easily. I'd rather not see others besides myself get hurt, and they're all just saying pretty things.” — she began to talk about what she was feeling.p.64
