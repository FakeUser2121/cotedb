Vol. 2Ch.6 §6.1: the secretary who runs the hearingp.193–195
12 entries · 7 quoted
  • Scene

    The student council room on the fourth floor; she receives the parties and opens the proceedings. p.193

  • Post

    Student council — Student council secretary; short-haired, and by her own account handles the bulk of proceedings "as usual." p.193

  • Spoken

    "Of course, considering the magnitude of this dispute, there are times when the student council president will take over. There are several unusual things about this incident. Other than that, the bulk of the proceedings will be handled solely by Tachibana, as usual." p.193

  • Caveat

    Her summary is the hearing's only neutral account: both sides' versions set out "in an easy-to-understand manner," with no need for further explanation. p.194

  • Spoken

    "Based on the aforementioned facts, we would like for you to identify which version of events is true." p.194

  • Spoken

    "Komiya-kun and two other members of the basketball club went to the special building after Sudou-kun called them there. There, they claim to have been beaten up in a one-sided brawl. Is this true?" p.194

  • Caveat

    The question is posed to Class D as a whole; the page does not assign it to a named student. p.194–195

  • Spoken

    "Please calm down, Sudou-kun. Right now, we’re merely listening to what both parties have to say. Komiya-kun, we ask that you too please show some restraint, and not interrupt." p.195

  • Spoken

    "Both parties insist that the other called them over, so the accounts conflict. However, the stories share some commonality. There was a dispute between Sudou-kun, Komiya-kun, and Kondou-kun, correct?" p.195

  • Spoken

    "'Picking fights'?" p.195

  • Spoken

    "Both sides have given their grievances, but now we have to come to a judgment with the collected evidence." p.195

  • Trait

    She keeps the hearing on evidence while the room escalates, and asks the question that turns Komiya's grievance into a concession of shared history. p.195

Vol. 2Ch.6 §6.1: the room's procedurep.199–210
8 entries · 3 quoted
  • Caveat

    Three lines in the witness sequence carry no speaker tag — "1-D, Sakura Airi-san.", "You may begin your testimony, if you wouldn't mind, Sakura-san." and, earlier, "If Class D has no further proof to offer, would you mind if we continued the proceedings?" They sit where the secretary has been speaking, but the translation does not name her, so they are recorded as gaps. p.199

  • What happens

    She walks to Sakura, hesitates, then takes the photographs and hands them to the president. p.203–204

  • Spoken

    "President." p.204

  • Spoken

    "Thank you very much. Please take your seat now," p.207

  • Spoken

    "Will you take your seat now?" p.207

  • Caveat

    The second request is made "as if she suspected that Horikita couldn't hear her," and Horikita still does not sit. p.207

  • What happens

    After the hearing she locks the student council room door while the president speaks to Ayanokōji. p.210

  • Relationship

    She defers the unusual case to him and runs everything else, and she is the one who closes the room behind him. — Horikita Manabu p.193–210
