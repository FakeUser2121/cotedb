### Year 2, Volume 8

#### p.180–184 — Sakayanagi and Kanzaki confer on Ishigami; Sakayanagi phones him

Ayanokōji asks how they know each other; Sakayanagi explains a parental connection through well-known families, then says she wants to know more about Ishigami-kun, the only first-year student at the school with that surname. Ishigami's devotion to Ayanokōji-sensei is discussed: he contacted Ayanokōji several times and indirectly helped with Yagami's expulsion at the cultural festival; Kanzaki of the same cram school calls him undeniably a genius with ideas he could not produce himself. Sakayanagi reasons Ishigami enrolled to check Ayanokōji's ability; she says she will ask him directly and dials his number. Ishigami answers the phone as if he foresaw the call, says he chose the school on Ayanokōji-sensei's recommendation, will not interfere for now, and will only act if Ayanokōji-sensei instructs him.

### Year 2, Volume 9

#### p.61–66 — First-year Class A Ishigami: Horikita's student-council recruit declines

Horikita has been talking with first-year students; Ayanokōji asks who she was meeting and she names "Ishigami-kun," the only first-year with that surname — Amasawa, his classmate, knows him and calls him Class A's de facto strategist rather than its leader. Horikita praises him as academically sound with his classmates' trust; they find him in a small circle of boys by the entrance hall, and he turns to a silent stare at being called, showing no agitation and seeming oblivious to Ayanokōji's presence. Horikita explains she has few first-year contacts and values his OAA standing; he uses honorifics and none of the sass he shows Ayanokōji. He turns his back and walks away; Horikita considers stopping him but realizes she cannot force the council on him.

- **Spoken** (p.65) — "I'm sorry, but I'm not interested in joining."

- **Spoken** (p.65) — "Please look elsewhere."
- **Trait** (p.62–65) — Calm, polite to upperclassmen, and single-minded — refuses Horikita's student-council invitation instantly and without hesitation; Horikita decides she cannot force him, and Ayanokōji notes such motivated students would have applied earlier.
