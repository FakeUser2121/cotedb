Watanabe's confession: he likes AmikuraY2V9 · p.102–105

He follows Ayanokōji to the restroom and, haltingly, asks him to probe Ichinose about whether Amikura likes anyone — then admits, flustered, that he has a crush on her. He reveals he tried to ask Amikura out that morning but couldn't hold a conversation alone, was nervous, and ended up tagging along to the mall with the group; he asks Ayanokōji to find out casually whether she likes someone.
