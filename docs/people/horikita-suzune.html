Classroom coercion (Ch.2 opening, present late May)Y1V1 · p.16–18

Class 1-D classroom after final bell. Ayanokōji is feigning sleep ("Sleeping Strategy") pondering society. Horikita approaches his desk and stands over him like an intimidating presence (described as a "devil" to Shostakovich's Symphony No. 11).

with Ayanokōji Kiyotaka

  • “Ayanokouji-kun, do you have a moment?”p.16
  • “If you do not awaken within three seconds, I will bestow additional punishment upon you.”p.16
  • “See, you are awake after all, aren’t you?”p.16
12 more notes
  • Spoken “Glad to hear it. Well then, may I have a little of your time?”p.16
  • Spoken “Well, even though you have no right to veto such a decision, I suppose I would be exceptionally displeased.”p.17
  • Spoken “And when I am displeased, then I will prove a major obstacle to your school life, Ayanokouji-kun. For example, I might set a great number of thumbtacks on your chair. Or, when you go to the toilet, I might splash water on you from above. Or stab you with the needle of my mathematical compass. Those kinds of obstacles, I suppose.”p.17
  • Spoken “Don’t worry. That was only a joke. I wouldn’t splash water on you from above.” (after his harassment protest)p.17
  • Spoken “Evidence?” (she asked)p.17
  • Spoken “What about the evidence? Did you decide I’m the culprit without evidence?” (demands proof when accused of previously stabbing his arm with a drawing compass)p.17
  • Spoken “Ayanokouji-kun. Would you prefer to regret while you suffer or regret while you despair? Which would you like more? Because if you refuse me and force my hand, it will be your responsibility.”p.17–18
  • Spoken “All right, then. What am I supposed to do?” (agrees under duress, filled with trepidation; the narrative then flashes back two months to the entrance ceremony — the favor itself is named only later, at p.150: “I need your help, Ayanokouji-kun.”)p.18
  • What happens Physical intimidation; stands directly over him; uses cold countdowns; completely denies physical violence (compass stab) on strict legalistic grounds (lack of evidence).p.16–17
  • Trait Dominant, uncompromising, highly articulate, ruthlessly practical, utilizes psychological pressure and legalistic deflection.p.16–18
  • Voice Formal, polite yet chillingly authoritative diction (keigo combined with overt threats: "bestow additional punishment", "prove a major obstacle to your school life", "regret while you suffer").p.16–18
  • Relationship Ayanokōji Kiyotaka Master-slave / coerced partner dynamic; views him as someone she can leverage for assistance through relentless pressure; already comfortable threatening physical harm.p.16–18

Natural rock school gate outside ANHS. As students disembark, Horikita immediately intercepts Ayanokōji before he enters.

  • Relationship Ayanokōji Kiyotaka Eyes lock with Ayanokōji across the aisle for an instant; shared silent mutual recognition that neither sees any reason to sacrifice their seat.p.24
