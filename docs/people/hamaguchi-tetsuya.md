#### p.217–220 — Ch.5 sec. 5.2: the Outcome #1 plan

Arrives with Yukimura and the Professor, then stakes the final hour on a voluntary email show — the plan that matches Ayanokōji’s swap by accident.

- **Spoken** (p.217) — “This is the end, huh? So, have you found any hints?” asked Hamaguchi.
- **Relationship · Ayanokōji Kiyotaka** (p.217-220) — Gentle lead-in; simultaneous “excuse me” with him; then the plan that shatters the swap timing.
- **Spoken** (p.218) — “For the past three days, I’ve been thinking of how we could achieve Outcome #1,” he said.
- **Spoken** (p.218) — “There is a way for everyone here to achieve Outcome #1,” he continued.
- **Spoken** (p.218) — “Yes. I came up with this idea after listening to everyone here, including Ichinose-san and Machida-kun.”
- **Spoken** (p.218) — “I’m going to show you all my phone. Of course, the school sent us all an email. I think you all understand what I’m getting at? Because we’re prohibited from tampering with or misrepresenting the emails we’ve received in any way, there’s no way to deceive one another. That’s why the answer is simple. We show each other our emails, and then we find out who the VIP is. That’s how we’ll discover the truth.”
- **Rule** (p.218) — Email-show logic: school messages cannot be tampered with, so the screen is the only proof.
- **Relationship · Machida Kōji** (p.218-219) — The flat refusal exchange — Machida calls it dumb, Hamaguchi tells him to sit quiet if he won’t participate.
- **Spoken** (p.219) — “It’s certainly true that if the VIP knows they could be betrayed, they wouldn’t show their phone. Suppose there’s a class working together to cover for the VIP. It’s true that none of them would show their phones. But this way, it’s possible to narrow down the list of candidates.”
- **Spoken** (p.219) — “In that case, please be quiet and just watch. If you don’t participate, Machida-kun, it’ll still be fine,” Hamaguchi answered.
- **Relationship · Ichinose Honami** (p.217-220) — His plan is “exactly the same” as hers and Ayanokōji’s — the three-way coincidence of the final hour.
- **Relationship · Beppu Ryōta** (p.219) — Beppu follows his phone first among Class B.
- **Trait** (p.218) — The idea came “after listening to everyone” — Ichinose’s card-hour intelligence put to work.
