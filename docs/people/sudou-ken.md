## Scenes

### Year 1, Volume 1

#### p.37–38 — Classroom Day 1: rejection of self-introductions

Class 1-D classroom after homeroom orientation. Hirata attempts to facilitate class-wide self-introductions.

- **What happens** (p.37) — Student with fiery red dyed hair glares aggressively at Hirata when prompted. Tall, muscular, exudes an overpowering delinquent aura.
- **Spoken** (p.37) — “What, are we a bunch of little kids or something? I don’t need to introduce myself. People who want to do that can go ahead. Just leave me out of it.”
- **What happens** (p.37) — When Hirata bows apologetically and female classmates snap at Sudō for being rude, Sudō snaps right back, completely unfazed by class opinion.
- **Spoken** (p.37) — “Shut it. I don’t care. I didn’t come here to make friends.”
- **What happens** (p.37) — Shoves his chair back and storms out of the classroom, triggering an exodus of several other rebellious students (including Horikita).
- **Trait** (p.37–38) — Volatile, aggressive, solitary, deeply hostile to authority and forced camaraderie; refuses to play along with school social rituals.
- **Relationship · Hirata Yōsuke** (p.37) — Immediate clash with Hirata's harmonious leadership; scorns Hirata's polite approach.

#### p.46–51 — Convenience store: confrontation with second-years

Campus convenience store on entrance ceremony afternoon. Sudō is browsing instant ramen cups in the aisle. Two second-year upperclassmen deliberately bump into him, knocking his cup to the floor.

- **What happens** (p.46–50) — Fists clench; squares up against both upperclassmen instantly, utterly fearless despite being outnumbered.
- **Spoken** (p.50) — “You got a lot of guts, asshole.”
- **Spoken** (p.50) — “Yeah, so what?” (when asked what class he belongs to)
- **What happens** (p.50–51) — When the second-years learn he is in Class D, they erupt into mocking laughter, label him a "defective", and walk away claiming they will let him off the hook because Class D will "be in hell soon enough anyway."
- **Spoken** (p.50) — “Hey, don’t run away! Hey!”
- **Spoken** (p.51) — “Ah, damn it! If those had been nice second-year students, or cute girls, that would’ve been great. Instead, we had to deal with those annoying morons.”
- **What happens** (p.51) — Completely ignores the spilled ramen cup on the floor; thrusts his hands in his pockets and storms off, oblivious to the two security cameras watching above the entrance.
- **Trait** (p.50–51) — Hot-headed, physically combative, lacks foresight; leaves messes behind; completely unaware of institutional surveillance or the ominous meaning behind the "defective" stigma.
- **Relationship · Ayanokōji Kiyotaka** (p.51–52) — Unwitting beneficiary of Ayanokōji's covert assistance (AK cleans up Sudō's mess to prevent disciplinary fallout).

#### p.54, 68–71 — Sleeping in class & the basketball club commitment

Class 1-D classroom (Day 2 morning); then gymnasium club fair.

- **What happens** (p.54) — Spends the vast majority of Day 1's orientation and academic classes fast asleep at his desk, setting an immediate reputation as a delinquent who refuses to pay attention while teachers show zero reaction.
- **What happens** (p.68) — Approaches Ayanokōji at the gymnasium club fair alongside Ike and Yamauchi, forming a clear trio.
- **Spoken** (p.68) — “Yo, Ayanokouji. You came, huh?”
- **Spoken** (p.68) — “Yeah. I’ve been playing basketball ever since elementary school. I thought I’d join the team here.”
- **What happens** (p.68–71) — Confirms he has already committed to joining the school's basketball club. Welcomes Ayanokōji into their informal circle as Ike exchanges contact details for the boys' group chat.
- **Trait** (p.68) — Single-minded dedication to basketball; his athletic ambition is his sole genuine anchor in high school.
- **Relationship · Ike Kanji** (p.68) — Bonds with Ike and Yamauchi as fellow troublemakers.
- **Relationship · Ayanokōji Kiyotaka** (p.68–71) — Casual friendly acquaintance; includes him in the boys' network.

#### p.73, 85–88 — Disapproval of voyeurism & the pool showdown with Kōenji

Classroom 1-D (morning); then indoor swimming pool.

- **What happens** (p.73) — Visibly draws back in disgust when Ike and Sotomura propose taking voyeuristic photos and ranking the girls' breast sizes, showing a line of personal pride he won't cross.
- **Feat** (p.85) — Swims against Hirata in the second boys' heat; uses aggressive, powerful strokes to win narrowly with a fast time of **approximately 26 seconds**.
- **What happens** (p.87) — Shocked by Kōenji's staggering 23.22-second swim time; fiery competitive spirit ignites.
- **Spoken** (p.87) — “I’m not particularly interested in winning or losing…but I don’t like losing.”
- **Spoken** (p.87) — “I’m fired up!”
- **What happens** (p.88) — Races Kōenji head-to-head in the final heat; gives everything he has but is defeated by about five meters.
- **Trait** (p.87–88) — Fierce athletic competitor; hates losing to anyone; recognizes Kōenji's raw superiority while refusing to yield mentally.
- **Relationship · Kōenji Rokusuke** (p.87–88) — Immediate athletic rivalry.

#### p.102 — Classroom tardiness & the Idiot Trio

#### p.127–130, 134–135 — 14-point test crisis & storming out on Hirata

Classroom 1-D, May 1st morning homeroom and subsequent class discussion.

- **What happens** (p.127) — Kicks the legs of his desk with a loud, furious thwack upon hearing Class D is labeled defective failures.
- **Spoken** (p.127) — “Won’t the other classes make fun of us?”
- **What happens** (p.127–128) — Scores dead last in the class on the short diagnostic test with an abysmal **14 points** (class average 65).
- **Limit** (p.128, 130) — Severe academic deficiency: scores 14/100, landing directly on the immediate expulsion danger list for midterms.
- **What happens** (p.130) — Clicks his tongue and hangs his head in deep shame as Chabashira confirms failing the midterm results in immediate expulsion.
- **What happens** (p.134) — Grumbles defensively when Hirata calls him out by name from the podium.
- **Spoken** (p.134) — “Tch, what is it?”
- **Spoken** (p.134) — “Huh? And why do you get to tell us what to do? Besides, that’s supposing our points will increase. If they don’t change at all, then it’s useless.”
- **Spoken** (p.134) — “I’m still not convinced. Besides, even if we get serious and work hard in class, our points won’t necessarily go up.”
- **Spoken** (p.134) — “That’s just your own selfish interpretation. Besides, you don’t know how to increase our points. Try talking to me after you figure that out.”
- **What happens** (p.134–135) — Rejects Hirata’s polite appeal with hostile defiance and storms out of the classroom.
- **Spoken** (p.134–135) — “Do whatever you want. It doesn’t matter. Just don’t involve me in it. Understand?”
- **Trait** (p.127, 134–135) — Prickly defensive pride; lashes out with verbal aggression when confronted with his own failings; flees uncomfortable social accountability.
- **Relationship · Hirata Yōsuke** (p.134–135) — Openly hostile toward Hirata's moralizing leadership; refuses to be directed or lectured by him.

#### p.152–157, 172–178 — Refusal, recruitment, & the collar-grab

- **Spoken** (p.157) — “You serious? If the lessons are a pain in my ass, why would studying after class be any better? Besides, I have club activities, so it’s pointless. Plus, you’re going to be tutoring? Your scores weren’t great, either.”
- **Spoken** (p.157) — “Horikita? I don’t really know anything about her. Sounds fishy; I’ll pass. I’ll be fine cramming for the test the night before.”
- **What happens** (p.172) — Reverses course after Kushida's personal recruitment and shows up at the library study group with Ike, Yamauchi, and Okitani.
- **Spoken** (p.173) — “Scoring lower than thirty-two means failing. Do you fail if you get exactly thirty-two points, though?”
- **Spoken** (p.174) — “Look, I don’t know how to study at all,”
- **Spoken** (p.175) — “So, what, we’re like elementary school kids?”
- **What happens** (p.175) — Throws his mechanical pencil onto the desk and quits before the session has even begun, provoking Horikita's silent fury.
- **Spoken** (p.175) — “Stop. I’m done. This isn’t going to work.”
- **Spoken** (p.176) — “Say whatever you want. Academics will be useless in the future, anyway.”

- **What happens** (p.176) — Gets in close and seizes Horikita by the collar; Kushida grabs his arm before anything happens, while Horikita doesn't flinch.
- **Trait** (p.157, 174–176) — Openly anti-academic; convinced his basketball future makes studying pointless; explosive temper when humiliated, but stops short of striking a girl.
- **Relationship · Hirata Yōsuke** (p.152) — Relationship rocky ever since he stomped all over Hirata's introduction game; pointedly ignores Hirata's recruitment speech.
- **Relationship · Horikita Suzune** (p.174–176) — Direct confrontation: her merciless mockery of his abilities triggers the collar-grab; she remains utterly unflinching.
- **Relationship · Kushida Kikyō** (p.172, 177) — Her recruitment gets him to the library; she physically restrains him when he loses his temper.
