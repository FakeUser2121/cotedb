Year 1, Volume 1

Vol. 1Campus store debut: the broken camera
Vol. 1 p.110–111
Well, you know what they say: ‘seeing is believing,’ right? Just try it. When you start playing, we’ll help you farm for materials. Collecting honey is hard work, you know? So you can buy me some cocoa, then.
For crying out loud…
Year 1, Volume 1, page 110
5 entries
  • Scene

    Campus convenience/electronics store, late April.

  • What happens

    A quiet, bespectacled girl standing alone by the digital camera display rack. Wears glasses with large frames, long hair draped forward to obscure her facial features, wearing the ANHS uniform. p.110–111

  • What happens

    Holds a compact digital camera with a damaged/broken casing in both hands, staring at the replacement models with intense anxiety. p.110–111

  • What happens

    When Ayanokōji steps into the aisle nearby, she flinches in alarm, avoids eye contact completely, clutches her broken camera tightly to her chest, and hurriedly flees down the aisle. p.111

  • Trait

    Pathologically shy, hyper-sensitive to the presence of others, deeply attached to digital photography. p.110–111

Year 1, Volume 2

Vol. 2Ch.1 "Sakura Airi's Soliloquy": the volume opens in her POVVol. 2 p.10
9 entries · 5 quoted
  • Backstory

    Whole first-person monologue chapter — the only non-Ayanokōji POV chapter opening in Vol. 2; foreshadows her role as "An Unexpected Witness" (Ch.4). p.10

  • Inner voice

    "I don't know how to interact with other people. I'm bad at talking while making eye contact. I'm horrible with crowds. I don't remember how or when I became so hopeless." p.10

  • Inner voice

    "However, one thing I do know for certain is that a person cannot live entirely in isolation. No matter how much I may love my solitude, I can't remain totally by myself. So, I came up with a solution. I would adopt a false face and hide my true self. Then, I wouldn't be completely honest, but I could be a version of myself. I could continue to live in this dark, lonely world." p.10

  • Trait

    Self-aware about her condition; frames the false face as a deliberate "solution," not an accident — same masking logic she later applies to photography. p.10

  • Inner voice

    "Is everyone else putting on a façade, just like me? Or do most people show their true selves to the outside world? Since I can't connect with people, I suppose there's no way for me to find out the answer. Therefore, I remain alone." p.10

  • Inner voice

    "I'm all right by myself. I'm all right with being alone." p.10

  • Inner voice

    "I… I want to connect with someone from the bottom of my heart." p.10

  • Voice

    Her interior voice: short declarative confessions, self-questioning, closing cadence "And so I will continue to live quietly, with my eyes downcast. Alone." p.10

  • Trait

    Paradox locked in at volume start: wants a heart-to-heart connection while believing connection is impossible for her — the engine of her Vol. 2 arc. p.10

Vol. 2Ch.2 opening: she stumbles on Sudou's three-on-one brawl (the witness birthed)
Vol. 2 p.12–13
fight
He he, so. Do you really think that this is the end of it, Sudou?
Year 1, Volume 2, page 12
11 entries · 5 quoted
  • Scene

    Evening of June 30, inside a school building with a staircase (the "special building," named Sudou's account p.30–31). While hunting for a good selfie spot, Sakura finds three male students writhing on the floor and a red-haired boy standing over them. p.12

  • Inner voice

    "The timing couldn’t have been worse. While searching for a place to take a good selfie, I stumbled into something. Even a certain famous, diminutive detective would have held his breath when witnessing such a tense situation." p.12

  • Inner voice

    "His right fist was covered in blood from the students he’d pummeled. This was the first brawl I’d ever witnessed." p.12

  • Feat

    Terror overrides etiquette but not habit: "Though I was terrified, I began capturing the scene with my camera. The shutter didn’t make a sound." p.12

  • Inner voice

    Panic freeze: "After taking the pictures, I asked myself what I was doing. I couldn’t think clearly in my panicked state... My legs didn’t obey my command to move, like I was paralyzed." p.12

  • Inner voice

    "I’d heard that that when someone was involved in a crisis, his or her body would lock up, exactly like what was happening now." (sic — doubled "that" in corpus) p.13

  • Inner voice

    "I’d hate it if someone were to spot me and involve me in this mess. If that happened, it would cast a dark cloud over my otherwise peaceful school life." p.13

  • Scene

    Sudou, sensing movement, calls "Is someone there?" toward her hiding spot; she escapes by seconds: "I’d successfully escaped by a hair’s breadth. If I’d lagged a mere two seconds, he probably would’ve seen me." p.13

  • Trait

    Document-first, confront-never instinct: she photographs the fight but flees rather than intervene or report; self-protection through invisibility. p.12–13

  • Relationship

    Unseen witness to his brawl; nearly discovered by him — she does not know his name-to-face link yet beyond "Sudou-kun" heard in his taunts. — Sudō Ken p.12–13

  • Scene

    §2.2 lunch, observed by Ayanokōji: still eating alone, hunched over a homemade bento; the glasses-and-plain-girl reputation ("At one time Ike had made a ruckus about how large her breasts were, but because she was considered plain, everyone quickly lost interest."). p.20
