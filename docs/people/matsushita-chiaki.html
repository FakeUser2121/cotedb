Ch.5: Suspicion and pursuitY1V11.5 · p.296–323, 339–351

During spring break, Matsushita reflects on her suspicions about Ayanokōji Kiyotaka and decides to investigate him. She considers Class D’s situation and concludes that hidden capable students like Ayanokōji could change their prospects. Matsushita recalls observations that led her to suspect Ayanokōji, including his relationships and performance in key events. At Keyaki mall, she meets Shinohara and Satō and steers the conversation toward Ayanokōji. Satō tells Matsushita and Shinohara that she confessed to Ayanokōji and was rejected. Matsushita listens as Satō shares her belief that Ayanokōji and Karuizawa like each other. After spotting Ayanokōji at the mall, Matsushita follows him while pretending to be on her phone. She observes Ayanokōji speaking with Acting Director Tsukishiro from a distance. After Ayanokōji confronts her for following him, Matsushita admits she tailed him and questions him about hiding his strength. She cites the mental arithmetic event and seeing him with Hirata during the Class Vote as reasons for her suspicion. Matsushita accepts Ayanokōji’s explanation for the moment and agrees not to tell anyone about him and Karuizawa.
