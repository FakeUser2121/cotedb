#### p.38 — Classroom Day 1: girlfriend hunting intro

Class 1-D classroom during Hirata's introductions.

- **What happens** (p.38) — Stands up cheerfully to introduce himself to the class.
- **Spoken** (p.38) — “I’m Ike Kanji. I love girls, and I hate pretty boys. I’m currently in the market for a new girlfriend. It’s nice to meet you! All the better if you’re a cutie or a beauty!”
- **What happens** (p.38) — The girls look at him with overt disgust. When one girl deadpans in an emotionless voice that he is "so cool", Ike fails to detect the sarcasm, blushes, and takes it as genuine praise.
- **Spoken** (p.38) — “Seriously? Seriously? Oh, man. I mean, I thought that I wasn’t bad or anything, but…heh heh.”
- **What happens** (p.38) — The entire female half of the class erupts into laughter at his gullibility. Instead of taking offense, he jovially plays along with being teased.

- **Voice** (p.38) — Casual, uninhibited, energetic teenage colloquialisms.
