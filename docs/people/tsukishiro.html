Ch.1: Staff meeting on the first-years’ examsY1V11 · p.13–20

As acting director, finishes explaining the first-years’ final special exam and a new system to the staff. Invites questions and responds when Mashima challenges the Class Vote’s risk of expulsions. Smiles and argues that students have always faced expulsion risk under the school’s rules. Dismisses Mashima’s objections as old-fashioned and says the new system will focus on first-years. States he was sent by a higher corporate body backed by the government and will not reconsider the provisional exam. Ends the meeting after mentioning he is considering a cultural festival next year.

Ch.5: Confrontation at Keyaki mallY1V11.5 · p.324–338

Tsukishiro approaches Ayanokōji at Keyaki mall and dismisses nearby workers before speaking with him. He pressures Ayanokōji to drop out during spring break and return to his father, citing the White Room. Tsukishiro notes that Matsushita is following Ayanokōji. He discusses the history and outcomes of the White Room with Ayanokōji. Tsukishiro proposes a “game,” claiming a White Room student will enroll as a freshman and challenging Ayanokōji to identify that student by the end of April. He ends the conversation after Ayanokōji accepts the challenge on the surface. In Ayanokōji’s reflections on the last night of spring break, Tsukishiro is mentioned in connection with the possibility of a White Room first-year student entering the school.

Prologue: Briefing the White Room studentY2V1 · p.7–11

In a Tokyo facility two months earlier, Tsukishiro briefs a White Room teenager on Ayanokōji Kiyotaka. Orders that Ayanokōji be expelled and returned to the White Room before the end of April, without attracting public attention. Shows data on Ayanokōji and the other 156 second-year students. Fans the teenager’s rivalry by praising Ayanokōji as the White Room’s masterpiece. Is slightly bothered by the teenager’s calm attitude as they leave.
