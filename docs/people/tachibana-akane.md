#### p.193–195 — Ch.6 sec. 6.1: the secretary who runs the hearing

- **Post** (p.193) — Student council secretary; short-haired, and by her own account handles the bulk of proceedings "as usual."
- **Spoken** (p.193) — "Of course, considering the magnitude of this dispute, there are times when the student council president will take over. There are several unusual things about this incident. Other than that, the bulk of the proceedings will be handled solely by Tachibana, as usual."

- **Spoken** (p.195) — "Please calm down, Sudou-kun. Right now, we’re merely listening to what both parties have to say. Komiya-kun, we ask that you too please show some restraint, and not interrupt."
- **Spoken** (p.195) — "Both parties insist that the other called them over, so the accounts conflict. However, the stories share some commonality. There was a dispute between Sudou-kun, Komiya-kun, and Kondou-kun, correct?"
