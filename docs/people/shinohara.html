Shinohara: Ike's clumsy proposal, Komiya's comfortY2V2 · p.168–173

Bumps into Ayanokōji in the elevator; they barely know each other and exchange only a greeting. Ike waits in the lobby, greets her and follows her outside to ask about grouping for the coming uninhabited-island exam; she rebuffs his probing. When Kushida calls Ike away with another girl's invitation, Shinohara coldly tells her to take him; she watches them leave with a lonely look, her eyes red. Komiya notices her red eyes; she hides her hurt behind a "something got in my eyes" excuse, and they talk about his making the basketball team.
