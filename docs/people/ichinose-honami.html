This was the merely the first case. If the party that lied wins, that’d set a bad precedent. Also, I personally can’t turn away now that I know what happened.” (sic — "the merely")p.106

  • Spoken Her reading of Class D's witness problem: “Well, that means there isn’t another witness. I mean, I guess someone from another class might have witnessed it, but it isn’t likely.”p.107
  • Rule She is the source of the class-points half of the club rule, which Class D's own teacher had never given them: “the school evaluates club and philanthropic activities, right? So if he enters a tournament and does well, Sudou-kun could earn points. Those’ll be tied to your class points, too. Wait… Did you guys not know that? Did your teacher not tell you?”p.107
  • Spoken Her diagnosis of the school's teaching staff: “Your homeroom teacher is kind of weird.” / “She doesn’t seem motivated to tell us anything. She’s completely apathetic. Some teachers are like that.” / “Did you know that the school evaluates homeroom teachers when their class graduates?”p.107
  • Trait She arrives with nothing to gain, says so twice, is called a liar's ally to her face, and still ends the afternoon as Class D's first outside partner — the volume's first Class B agent and the counter-example to Horikita's theory that nobody helps for free.p.102–107
  • Relationship Horikita Suzune Horikita suspects her on sight and hires her within the same conversation; the two are the pair who decide, over Sakura's silence, to bring another class in.p.104–107
  • Caveat This is her first direct contact with Ayanokōji in the volume; every fact about her here comes from what she says in one afternoon, and no Class B student corroborates any of it.p.102–107

Ch.4 sec. 4.4–4.6: what Class B's representative actually doesY1V2 · p.108–115

Her contact details are exchanged on her initiative: “How about we exchange contact information, so things can progress smoothly?” — and AK learns her given name in the process: "Also, I learned that Ichinose’s first name was Honami—an unnecessary bit of information." A month after the entrance exam she is already her class's channel to the school's staff: "Thank you very much. It’s been a pleasure." — she has been at the dormitory manager's desk handling other students' complaints: “Some people from my class wanted to make requests about their dorms. I gathered everyone’s thoughts on the matter and told the dorm manager. Stuff like water usage, noise, and so on.” Her physical entrance in this section is written by AK as attraction; it is his narration, not her characterisation: "She had beautiful long, wavy hair and big eyes. The second button of her blazer strained over her large chest. Her upright posture matched her dignified personality. I found myself more attracted to her cool temperament than how cute she was."

with Horikita Suzune · Ayanokōji Kiyotaka

  • “I think it would probably be good for us to meet up and discuss things.” — Horikita's answer, which she takes without offence: “I never thought I’d receive aid from the enemy.”p.108
  • “This seems like a problem we need to address before we can fight. We’re not really on equal footing, are we?” — the clearest statement in the volume of what the class-rank system does between classes.p.108

  • Position Class B's self-organisation is the fact this scene exists to reveal: “Class Rep? Why Class Rep?” — “I’m my class’s representative. It seems like that, anyway.” and “No, that’s just something Class B set up on its own. I think it’s good to have some assigned roles, right?”p.114–115
  • Rule The full informal officer set, in her own words: “Whether or not they serve a function is a different question, but we do have other roles for formality’s sake. Stuff like vice class representative, and secretary. They could be more useful when we have a cultural fair, or sports festival, or something. We could try deciding things on the spot, but that might lead to trouble.”p.115
  • Trait AK's read of why she holds the role: “Most people wouldn’t want to be class representative. They’d be forced to do annoying stuff... However, with Ichinose taking the initiative for Class B, she probably didn’t foist things on others. I’d bet she handled her duties smoothly.”p.115
  • Spoken She refuses the grandeur of it: “Do you think it’s weird? It’s all purely informal. Besides, there are quite a few troublemakers. We have to deal with a lot of stuff.” — AK's note: "As she said, 'We have to deal with a lot of stuff,' Ichinose laughed."p.115
  • Relationship Horikita Suzune She is the first person outside Class D Horikita will negotiate with, and the negotiation is conducted entirely by Horikita's hostility plus Horikita's yes.p.108–110
  • Relationship Ayanokōji Kiyotaka Two weeks in, she greets him first, knows his name, and walks to school with him — while he is still cataloguing her as an asset with unclear motive.p.114–115
