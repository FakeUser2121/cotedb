### Year 1, Volume 1

#### p.55, 103, 117–118 — Status coupling with Hirata & luxury spending

Classroom 1-D; then Keyaki Mall boutique and café.

- **Post** (p.103) — Class 1-D; dating Hirata; introduced at lunch as a “fashionista-type girl” (p.55).
- **What happens** (p.55) — Latches onto Hirata Yōsuke's arm on the second day of school, steering him away from the classroom to lunch with her female friend circle.
- **Position** (p.103) — Formally established as dating Hirata Yōsuke three weeks into the school year, securing the top female status position in Class 1-D. Gazes lovingly at him across the classroom during lessons.
- **Trait** (p.103) — Outward persona: fashionable, assertive, intensely "girly" high schooler; exudes an intimidating aura that repels unassertive students; attaches herself immediately to the class's most popular boy.
- **What happens** (p.117) — Goes on a lavish shopping spree at the Keyaki Mall boutique, purchasing approximately 30,000 points worth of designer clothing while Hirata carries her bags.
- **What happens** (p.117) — At the café, complains that the 100,000 monthly allowance is insufficient for her lifestyle.
- **Spoken** (p.117) — “I just wish we had more points, you know? Maybe 200,000 or 300,000 a month? After buying cosmetics and clothes and stuff, I’ve already used up almost all of my points.”
- **Trait** (p.117) — Total financial recklessness: has completely drained her entire 100,000 point balance (¥100,000) within the first three weeks on clothes, cosmetics, and accessories, assuming the cash flow is guaranteed.
- **Relationship · Hirata Yōsuke** (p.55, 103, 117) — Public boyfriend; relies on his high status and chivalrous accommodation.

#### p.138–139 — Borrowing points from Kushida & social scrounging

Classroom 1-D, May 1st lunch break.

- **What happens** (p.138) — Having completely exhausted her 100,000 allowance, works the classroom borrowing private points from female classmates. Breezily approaches Kushida.
- **Spoken** (p.138) — “Hey, Kushida-san, do you have a minute?… Honestly, I’ve spent way too many points, and I’m seriously running low. Some of the other girls in class have lent me a few points, but I was wondering if you could help me out, too. We’re friends, right? I only need, like, 2,000 points from you.”
- **What happens** (p.138–139) — Laughs happily when Kushida agrees instantly without hesitation; exchanges phone numbers to initiate the mobile point transfer, then immediately bolts off to target Inogashira for more points.
- **Spoken** (p.138) — “Thank you! This is really what friends are for, huh? By the way, here’s my number. Okay, see you later. Ah, Inogashira-san! Hey, to tell you the truth, I used up too many of my points…”
- **Trait** (p.138–139) — Shameless social scrounger; uses cheerful manipulation and peer pressure ("we're friends, right?") to extract money from classmates to sustain her lifestyle.
- **Relationship · Kushida Kikyō** (p.138–139) — Treats Kushida as an easy financial lifeline; borrows 2,000 points without a second thought.
