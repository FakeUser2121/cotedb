### Year 1, Volume 1

#### p.32–34 — Classroom Day 1: initiating class introductions

Class 1-D classroom after Chabashira abruptly leaves. Students are in stunned disarray celebrating their 100,000-point deposits without knowing what to do next.

- **What happens** (p.32–33) — Stands up with calm confidence, smiling warmly to draw the room's attention.
- **Spoken** (p.32) — “Everyone, can you please listen to me for a moment?”
- **Spoken** (p.33) — “My name’s Hirata Yousuke. Back in junior high, lots of people called me Yousuke. Feel free to use my first name! I guess my hobby is sports in general, but I especially like soccer. I’m planning on playing soccer here, too. Nice to meet you!”
- **What happens** (p.33) — Effortlessly breaks the ice; commands widespread admiration; girls in class immediately swoon. Proposes orderly self-introductions starting from the front row so classmates can learn each other's names before ceremony.
- **Trait** (p.32–34) — Natural leader, charismatic, genuinely kind, self-effacing, proactive; instinctively steps forward to build group cohesion.
- **Voice** (p.33) — Warm, gentle, inviting, devoid of ego or condescension.

#### p.37–40 — Mediating Sudō's rebellion & Kōenji's narcissism

Class 1-D classroom during self-introductions.

- **What happens** (p.37) — Encourages Sudō to speak; met with intense delinquent hostility ("Leave me out of it").
- **Spoken** (p.37) — “I can’t force you to introduce yourself, of course. However, I don’t think that getting along with your classmates is a bad thing. If I’ve made you uncomfortable, I apologize.”
- **What happens** (p.37–38) — Bows deeply to Sudō. When class girls snap at Sudō to defend Hirata, Hirata immediately takes the blame onto himself to protect class unity.
- **Spoken** (p.38) — “They’re not a bad bunch. It’s my fault. I was being selfish and made people do this.”
- **What happens** (p.37) — Looks visibly saddened and lonely as Horikita, Sudō, and others walk out of the room.
- **What happens** (p.38–39) — Politely asks Kōenji to introduce himself; bewildered when Kōenji puts his feet on his desk and threatens to punish "ugly things".
- **Spoken** (p.38) — “Excuse me, can you introduce yourself?”

- **What happens** (p.40) — Gently supports Ayanokōji after he stumbles and freezes during his own botched introduction.
- **Trait** (p.37–40) — Peacemaker to a fault; absorbs blame to de-escalate tension; refuses to cast out delinquents or oddballs; desperate to keep everyone together.
- **Relationship · Sudō Ken** (p.37–38) — Patient and forgiving despite Sudō's hostility.
- **Relationship · Kōenji Rokusuke** (p.38–39) — Courteous handling of an uncontrollable eccentric.

#### p.103, 117–118 — Dating Karuizawa & moral unease over point lavishness

Classroom 1-D; then boutique and café in Keyaki Mall.

- **Relationship · Karuizawa Kei** (p.103, 117) — Gentle, accommodating boyfriend; caters to her luxury shopping while maintaining his own level-headed values.

#### p.121–125, 131–137 — Standing up for the class & the podium appeal

Classroom 1-D, May 1st morning homeroom and subsequent class chaos.

- **What happens** (p.121–122) — Takes the initiative on behalf of the bewildered class to question Chabashira regarding the missing points.
- **Spoken** (p.121–122) — “Sensei, may I please ask you a question? I’m afraid I still don’t understand… Can you please tell us why we didn’t receive any points? We won’t completely understand otherwise.”
- **What happens** (p.124) — Politely challenges Chabashira’s omission of point deduction rules.
- **Spoken** (p.124) — “Chiyabashira-sensei. I do not recall hearing you explain that to us before… Of course. There was never any talk about reducing our points. Had that been explained beforehand, I’m sure we would have avoided being late or talking during class.”
- **Spoken** (p.124) — “Well then, could you at least explain in detail how points are added or deducted? We can keep that in mind for future reference.”
- **What happens** (p.125) — Expression darkens as Chabashira reveals that merely eliminating infractions will still leave them at zero points next month.
- **What happens** (p.131) — When the classroom descends into panic during the break, Hirata steps in to soothe tempers. Yukimura grabs his collar in a rage; Hirata remains completely non-violent.
- **Spoken** (p.131) — “I understand that you’re all confused right now, but everyone needs to calm down… Even if I was, isn’t it better for us to band together so we can turn things around?… I understand. However, sitting here whining won’t help us right now.”
- **What happens** (p.132) — After Kushida defuses Yukimura, Hirata takes accountability gracefully.
- **Spoken** (p.132) — “It’s all right. I should have chosen my words a little more carefully.”
- **What happens** (p.133–134) — Takes the teacher’s podium before next period to deliver an impassioned call for class unity.
- **Spoken** (p.133–134) — “Attention, everyone. Before class begins, I want you to listen seriously for a moment. Especially you, Sudou-kun… We didn’t get any points this month. This is a serious problem, and one that will have an enormous impact on our daily lives moving forward. It’s impossible for us to make it to graduation with zero points, right?”
- **Spoken** (p.134) — “Of course. Therefore, we must earn points next month. To do that, we all need to cooperate with each other. So, please, take care not to be late to class or to talk during the lecture. Also, the use of cell phones during class is prohibited, of course.”
- **Spoken** (p.134) — “As long as we continue talking during class and being late, our points won’t increase for sure. Although we can’t go below zero points, disruption will, without a doubt, count as strikes against us.”
- **What happens** (p.134) — When Sudō aggressively rejects his appeal, Hirata bows his head with courteous humility rather than getting angry.
- **Spoken** (p.134) — “I don’t think that there’s anything particularly wrong with what you said, Sudou-kun. I apologize if I made you feel uncomfortable.” (bows his head politely toward the disgruntled Sudou)
- **Spoken** (p.134) — “However, Sudou-kun, it’s a fact that unless we all cooperate, we won’t get any more points.”
- **What happens** (p.135) — Steps down from the podium and stops directly in front of Horikita and Ayanokōji’s desks to personally recruit them into a collective study and improvement effort.
- **Spoken** (p.135) — “Horikita-san, Ayanokouji-kun, do you have a moment? I want to speak with you about how we can increase our points. I’d like you to join me.”
- **Spoken** (p.135) — “This is the first trial we’re facing together as a united Class D. So then—”
- **What happens** (p.135) — When Horikita rejects him outright (“I refuse. I won’t participate.”), Hirata accepts her rebuff with a self-deprecating wry smile rather than anger.
- **Spoken** (p.135) — “I…I see. I’m sorry. If you change your mind, I’d love for you to join us.”
- **Spoken** (p.136) — “What about you, Ayanokouji-kun?”
- **Spoken** (p.136) — “No, I’m sorry for bothering you. If you change your mind, please let me know.”
- **Trait** (p.121–137) — Quintessential servant-leader; absorbs class hostility without retaliation; persistent dedication to total class unity even when facing toxic rebuffs.
- **Relationship · Sudō Ken** (p.134) — Patiently mediates Sudō's outbursts without malice.
- **Relationship · Horikita Suzune** (p.135–136) — Tries earnestly to draw her into the fold; bears her scorn with diplomatic grace.
- **Relationship · Ayanokōji Kiyotaka** (p.135, 137) — Values his quiet presence; extends an open-door invitation without coercion.
