I’m okay. For now, anyway. I’ve used up about half of my points. I kind of lost control the first month and overspent, so it’ll be a little difficult to hold myself back. What about you, Ayanokouji-kun? Are you okay?
Year 1, Volume 1, page 138
7 entries · 2 quoted
  • Scene

    Classroom 1-D, May 1st lunch break.

  • What happens

    Having completely exhausted her 100,000 allowance, works the classroom borrowing private points from female classmates. Breezily approaches Kushida.

    p.138
  • Spoken

    “Hey, Kushida-san, do you have a minute?… Honestly, I’ve spent way too many points, and I’m seriously running low. Some of the other girls in class have lent me a few points, but I was wondering if you could help me out, too. We’re friends, right? I only need, like, 2,000 points from you.”

    p.138
  • What happens

    Laughs happily when Kushida agrees instantly without hesitation; exchanges phone numbers to initiate the mobile point transfer, then immediately bolts off to target Inogashira for more points.

    p.138–139
  • Spoken

    “Thank you! This is really what friends are for, huh? By the way, here’s my number. Okay, see you later. Ah, Inogashira-san! Hey, to tell you the truth, I used up too many of my points…”

    p.138
  • Trait

    Shameless social scrounger; uses cheerful manipulation and peer pressure ("we're friends, right?") to extract money from classmates to sustain her lifestyle.

    p.138–139
  • Relationship

    Treats Kushida as an easy financial lifeline; borrows 2,000 points without a second thought.

    Kushida Kikyō — p.138–139
