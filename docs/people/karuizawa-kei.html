Year 1, Volume 1

Vol. 1Status coupling with Hirata & luxury spendingp.55, 103, 117–118
10 entries · 1 quoted
  • Post

    Class 1-D — Class 1-D; dating Hirata; introduced at lunch as a “fashionista-type girl” (p.55). p.103

  • Scene

    Classroom 1-D; then Keyaki Mall boutique and café.

  • What happens

    Latches onto Hirata Yōsuke's arm on the second day of school, steering him away from the classroom to lunch with her female friend circle. p.55

  • Position

    Formally established as dating Hirata Yōsuke three weeks into the school year, securing the top female status position in Class 1-D. Gazes lovingly at him across the classroom during lessons. p.103

  • Trait

    Outward persona: fashionable, assertive, intensely "girly" high schooler; exudes an intimidating aura that repels unassertive students; attaches herself immediately to the class's most popular boy. p.103

  • What happens

    Goes on a lavish shopping spree at the Keyaki Mall boutique, purchasing approximately 30,000 points worth of designer clothing while Hirata carries her bags. p.117

  • What happens

    At the café, complains that the 100,000 monthly allowance is insufficient for her lifestyle. p.117

  • Spoken

    “I just wish we had more points, you know? Maybe 200,000 or 300,000 a month? After buying cosmetics and clothes and stuff, I’ve already used up almost all of my points.” p.117

  • Trait

    Total financial recklessness: has completely drained her entire 100,000 point balance (¥100,000) within the first three weeks on clothes, cosmetics, and accessories, assuming the cash flow is guaranteed. p.117

  • Relationship

    Public boyfriend; relies on his high status and chivalrous accommodation. — Hirata Yōsuke p.55, 103, 117

Vol. 1Borrowing points from Kushida & social scroungingp.138–139
7 entries · 2 quoted
  • Scene

    Classroom 1-D, May 1st lunch break.

  • What happens

    Having completely exhausted her 100,000 allowance, works the classroom borrowing private points from female classmates. Breezily approaches Kushida. p.138

  • Spoken

    “Hey, Kushida-san, do you have a minute?… Honestly, I’ve spent way too many points, and I’m seriously running low. Some of the other girls in class have lent me a few points, but I was wondering if you could help me out, too. We’re friends, right? I only need, like, 2,000 points from you.” p.138

  • What happens

    Laughs happily when Kushida agrees instantly without hesitation; exchanges phone numbers to initiate the mobile point transfer, then immediately bolts off to target Inogashira for more points. p.138–139

  • Spoken

    “Thank you! This is really what friends are for, huh? By the way, here’s my number. Okay, see you later. Ah, Inogashira-san! Hey, to tell you the truth, I used up too many of my points…” p.138

  • Trait

    Shameless social scrounger; uses cheerful manipulation and peer pressure ("we're friends, right?") to extract money from classmates to sustain her lifestyle. p.138–139

  • Relationship

    Treats Kushida as an easy financial lifeline; borrows 2,000 points without a second thought. — Kushida Kikyō p.138–139

Vol. 2Ch.3: Hirata's vote, cast out loudp.43, p.54
6 entries · 2 quoted
  • Spoken

    “I agree!” p.43

  • Spoken

    “If it were a false charge, that’d be a problem, wouldn’t it? In any case, you’d feel bad for him if he were innocent, right?” p.43

  • Backstory

    Introduced by AK in the middle of the uproar as "heroic Hirata's girlfriend", "brushing her bangs aside with her hand as she spoke". p.43

  • Trait

    AK's paired contrast of the two girls who moved the class: "If Kushida lived by the softness of her heart, then Karuizawa lived by the strength of her will. Perhaps it was because of Kushida and Karuizawa’s influence that many of the girls started to express their support." p.43

  • Scene

    After school she works the canvassing with him: "Hirata and Karuizawa led the Hero & Gal Team". p.54

  • Relationship

    Seconding his stance is enough to open the flood of support; she moves the girls as a block, which is where her influence in Class D actually lives. — Hirata Yōsuke p.43

Vol. 2Ch.4 §4.0 & §4.2: she wants Class A out loud, and is judged too blunt for Sakurap.66–67, p.83
7 entries · 2 quoted
  • Spoken

    “I think what you’re saying is reasonable, Hirata-kun, but I still want my points. The students in Class A get nearly 100,000 points every month. I’m super envious of them. There are girls in their class who buy lots of stylish clothes and accessories. Aren’t we just the bottom of the barrel compared to them?” p.67

  • Scene

    Her posture while the class pleads: "Karuizawa’s legs dangled over her desk. People looked bitter when she pointed out the stark difference between our classes." p.67

  • Spoken

    “Why couldn’t I have been in Class A from the start? If I were in Class A, I’d probably be loving every second of my student life.” p.67

  • Trait

    She says the class's unsayable arithmetic in front of everyone and then makes it about her own life — the strength-of-will half of the pair AK set against Kushida's softness now has a policy position. p.66–67

  • Scene

    Her status in the Sudou case is relational before it is moral: "Karuizawa wore a particularly proud expression, perhaps because she was the one he’d chosen to be his girlfriend." p.66

  • Limit

    Hirata rules her out of the witness interview in advance, and AK agrees with the reasoning: "Also, if I asked Karuizawa-san to talk with her, that might cause a few problems." — "A conversation between the super assertive Karuizawa and Sakura-san was difficult to imagine." p.83

  • Relationship

    She answers his speeches with her own terms rather than opposing them, and her bluntness is treated by him as a liability to be routed around instead of an asset to be used. — Hirata Yōsuke p.67, p.83
