  • Position With him on the entrance, Class D's only option is retreat — he has stripped every other one away.p.129

10 more notes
  • Relationship Sakura Airi Apologizes for startling her — he has seen where she ran to behind his back.p.140
  • Spoken “You’re the one who doesn’t seem to understand the situation, Yahiko. Don’t dismiss the danger of moving through the forest alone. It’s difficult enough in groups of men, but when men and women are together, no matter what, there will be certain limitations to what you can do.”p.141
  • Feat Will not go into the forest alone — groups of men already strain, mixed company more; the narration gives it as the reason he never went alone.p.141
  • Spoken “We’ll help you.”p.141
  • Spoken “I see. We can’t force you. However, can you trust us? After you leave here, there’s a chance we’ll make off with all of this, right?”p.141
  • Relationship Totsuka Yahiko Silences his objections with a look after the head-thump; partnership is offered over his visible refusal.p.140–141
  • Relationship Ayanokōji Kiyotaka The offer and the trust-question both land on him; he refuses the first and gives up the spot rather than accept the second, and Katsuragi withdraws without force.p.141
  • Tactic Offer refused, he pivots to asking to be trusted instead — then quietly clears a path for them.p.141
  • What happens Stays at the corn after they leave so their harvest runs under his eye; whether that was good or bad intent is left open.p.142
  • Position Class A now knows the spot and watches it from close by.p.142

Muttering over the announced results on the beach, then named through the lounge retelling as the cautious holder of the Class A cave.

  • “Something is strange… What does this mean?”p.245
6 more notes
  • Position Class A takes third with 120 points, behind Class B’s 140 and Class D’s 225.p.244
  • Caveat The question follows the narration that he muttered it; recorded as his. The Class A cry “What’s the meaning of this, Katsuragi?!” comes from unnamed voices and is left out as a chapter attribution gap.p.245
  • Relationship Ayanokōji Kiyotaka The one who read his cave display as insurance rather than carelessness — the leader beside him was Yahiko, not him.p.251

  • Fact Class A’s internal schism is the reason Katsuragi could not rely on other people — why the methods revealed Class A so easily.p.253
  • Inference, not stated The retelling’s reading of his class: 270 points at the end of the test, not one spent — bought through Class C’s sacrifice — and still third, Ayanokōji’s own label for it intuition with no evidence.p.256
