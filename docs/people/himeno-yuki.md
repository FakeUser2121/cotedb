#### p.62–72 — Himeno leaves the party with a faked headache; the stern and the scream

Mid-party, she raises her hand: “The usual headache” — can she go back to her room? Ichinose and the girls fuss; she snaps about their overprotective behavior. Ayanokōji, slipping out behind her, catches a glimpse of her distinctive hair color turning away from the guest cabins — and follows her to the stern of the ship, where her OAA scores (p.65) are Academic B- (63), Physical C (51), Adaptability C+ (58), Social Contribution C+ (58), Overall C+ (57). He screamed first into the ocean ("Ah─────"), a feeble “your voice didn’t have a shred of volume” critique follows, then she puts her whole body into it: She heads back to her room, telling him “If you know that you were in the wrong, that's better yet.”

- **Spoken · Ayanokōji Kiyotaka** (p.65) — “Is your headache gone?” — she mutters mostly drowned by the wind, the word “annoying” discernible.
- **Spoken** (p.67) — “It's because I get migraines.”
- **Spoken** (p.69) — “Strange? If you ask me, the people in my class are too good-natured. Our class tends to get together in large groups to do everything. Well, I don't mind that in and of itself, but the problem is that these meetings take too long, and nobody ever wants to leave.”
- **Spoken · Ayanokōji Kiyotaka** (p.70) — “Not many students come to the back of the ship, and when you consider the sound of the engine and the wind, even if you screamed, it wouldn't be heard.”
- **Spoken** (p.71) — “WA────────────!!!”
- **Voice** (p.70–71) — Her usual speech is low and subdued, yet her scream rolls "a voice, twice as loud as I had imagined"; the ship does not move, but he feels it rock.
- **Spoken** (p.71) — “Hah…That felt refreshing.”
- **Relationship · Ichinose Honami** (p.62–69) — Her classmate; semi-forced into the party by her classmates; Ichinose would never tolerate bullying, so the exclusion is informal and gentle, not cruel.
- **Relationship · Ayanokōji Kiyotaka** (p.65–72) — Screams with him into the ocean; lets her voice rip out after all; he is the only witness.
- **Caveat** (p.65–69) — AK's read: her flat tone and distance are armor to keep others away; the class's big-group culture is what grinds her down; screaming together was the one honest exchange of the night.

#### p.108–113 — Himeno's night walk: doubt after the karaoke meeting

Ayanokōji finds her standing alone on the street at night; she had declined to keep hanging out with the group, preferring to be alone, and admits she went to the store and movie theater "in a daze." She confesses the karaoke meeting hit her hard — she realized she had baseless confidence that teaming with Kanzaki made her special, and feels her attempts to change the class are going haywire; she asks if Class A is still reachable if the class changes.

- **Spoken** (p.112) — "Do you think there's still a chance for us to reach Class A if our class changes?"
- **Trait** (p.108–113) — Introspective and still uneasy in groups — she is honest about her doubts and feels her efforts are starting to go wrong, but is reassured when Ayanokōji says change is coming and agrees to help; she ends with a small, embarrassed victory pose.
