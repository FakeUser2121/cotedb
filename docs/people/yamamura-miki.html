Yamamura: tailing AyanokōjiY2V9.5 · p.128–143

Ayanokōji catches her hiding in a blind spot near a vending machine; she admits, by silence, that she has been following him for about ten minutes — and the day before as well — but he tells her Ryūen and Katsuragi did not notice. He catches her hiding by a vending machine; she's been following him for ten minutes — yesterday too; he tells her he won't report her to Ryūen. Ryūen reappears, applauding; she's Sakayanagi's spy, not a Ryūen plant; Katsuragi figures it out too; Ryūen lets her go, telling her to visit his room alone anytime. He finds her by the vending machines; she nearly drops a bottled tea; denies GPS; he gives her the same deal he gives everyone in the group chat; he points her to the truth. She confesses self-worth concerns; he encourages her; she overhears Kei and Satō; his promise not to let her be expelled finally breaks her guard; he tells her to report it honestly. She types the message "I've been caught by Ryūen-kun and Katsuragi-kun. I'll tell you the details by call." and sends it; excuses herself in a hurry.
