Group 6: the girl no one noticesY2V8 · p.46–64

Boards with Class A; on the master group list she shares Group 6 with Kitō and the others. She approaches downcast, ignores Kushida's greeting with a short nod; she doesn't warm to Watanabe's chattiness either. Watching Ayanokōji fall on the beginner slope ("Are you okay?"); she slips onto the wrong (advanced) lift, following Kitō's back by accident or on sight. She hovers above the Ryūen group (Kaneda, Ishizaki, Todo gathered below); she steps onto the steep slope alone and disappears down it; she lets no one see her struggle.

Girls' night visit: the silent attendeeY2V8 · p.78–89

She accepts the girls' visit but doesn't lean in like Amikura; she sits behind Watanabe. She half-steps back when Watanabe slides next to her — the only one bothered by proximity. During "Here we are~", "Darkness ball!" and the pillow fight she remains an observer; she passes on speaking. Asked to pick ski plans: "I'm okay with anything." — her non-preference is taken as fitting in.

Day two: the silent shadow detectiveY2V8 · p.92–117

The snowman morning: she refuses to participate; she observes the builders bare-handed and frozen; she tries to need nobody. She declines the shop browse; stands quietly in the cold; Ryūen spots her; he pulls her arm out of the pocket to prove she has no gloves; she gives up nothing; Ayanokōji offers his gloves; she takes them; she doesn't want to feel anything about this; she asks if she can pay him back; he teases out why she didn't go back to get them earlier — she has no answer that satisfies her either. Soup curry lunch; everyone covers Yamamura's embarrassment; nobody mentions it; she sits darker than the rest; she says nothing. She returns to the table; quietly leaves it; Kushida and Nishino track her; five Gifu students swarm her and Nishino; Yamamura apologizes for the spill — the boys ridicule her; Kitō shows up barking silence; his relief is silent; she survives; Kushida watches the boys get dragged off by Ryūen and Kitō.
