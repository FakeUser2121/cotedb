Vol. 1The old-test pipeline (the intended pass route)
Vol. 1 p.229–235
Where are you going?
It’s lunch. I thought I’d go to the cafeteria.
Year 1, Volume 1, page 229
  • Fact

    Post-admission mock papers are **verbatim identical across years** ("Every single word is identical") — the final three problems (2nd/3rd-year difficulty) repeat exactly. p.232–233

  • Inference

    Chabashira's earlier certainty that "there was a way for us to pass this test" = old-test access as the designed escape hatch; explains how academically weak students (Sudou/Ike/Yamauchi) were admitted. p.234

  • Tactic

    AK buys old papers for 15,000 points, withholds them from the trio (protect motivation), and plans a day-before internet release to force an overnight class-wide cram — targeting universal pass, not perfect scores. p.233–235

  • Backstory

    Buying past papers is not against the rules (point transfers and study aids unregulated). p.229–231

Vol. 1Results, the hidden pass rule & the buyback
Vol. 1 p.246–254
Sensei. We were told that the results would be announced today, but when?
There’s no need for you to get so worked up, Hirata. You should have passed quite easily.
Year 1, Volume 1, page 246
  • Fact

    Results posted on blackboard: 10+ students hit perfect 100s in math, Japanese, and social studies. p.246

  • Fact

    Sudou: 60×4 subjects, **39 English** → red-marked. The class pass line was **40, not 32**. p.246–247

  • Rule

    True pass-grade algorithm: per-class threshold = class average ÷ 2, **rounded** (down 32 on the previous test from 39.8/64.4-derived inputs; up to 40 this midterm from 79.6 ÷ 2 = 39.8). Red mark below threshold = expulsion (withdrawal form + legal guardian). p.247–248, 250

  • Fact

    Sabotage attempt: Horikita scored 51 in English on purpose (vs perfect in four other subjects) to drag the English average below 39.8 so Sudou would pass — defeated only by the rounding rule. p.249–250

  • Fact

    The earlier scope change meant Class D tested under documented unfair conditions — acknowledged by Chabashira as "a microcosm of our unfair society"; appeal route declared closed "at this stage." p.251–252

  • Fact

    Resolution: one English test point purchased for 100,000 points (AK + Horikita combined) → Sudou passes; expulsion rescinded. p.253–254

  • Fact

    Class D passes en masse otherwise — the old-test operation delivered; everyone clears except Sudou's single red line. p.246, 248
