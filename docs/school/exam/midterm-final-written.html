Every time it was run

  • Year 1, Volume 1 — First-semester midterm for the first-year classes, sat and graded inside the first volume; Chabashira posts results class-wide.
    • Outcome — Sudou's English score of 32 sits under the 39.8 pass line (79.6 class average divided by two, y1v1 p.247); Chabashira sells him a test point for 100,000 private points (y1v1 p.253) and Ayanokōji pays for it (y1v2 p.16, p.27).
    • Who took part — All first-year students; Chabashira Sae (posts Class D results); Sudou Kakeru (failing grade); Ayanokōji Kiyotaka (buys the make-up point).
  • Year 1, Volume 3 — The third volume opens on the summer cruise, after the first-semester finals of the first year have already been graded.
    • Outcome — Yamauchi taunts Ayanokōji about finishing with the lowest score on the final — the deliberate last place that keeps his profile unremarkable (y1v3 p.13).
    • Who took part — First-year students aboard the cruise; Yamauchi Haruki; Ayanokōji Kiyotaka.
  • Year 1, Volume 6 — Second first-year midterm: results day falls in Chapter 3 ('The Paper Shuffle'), with Chabashira restating the fail-and-expel rule before reading the board.
    • Outcome — Sports-festival bonuses and penalties adjust the posted scores and nobody fails — 'no one has to drop out of school due to their midterm scores this time' (y1v6 p.33).
    • Who took part — All first-year students; Chabashira Sae; Sudou Kakeru (passed after Horikita's tutoring); Yamauchi Haruki (bottom of Class D).
  • Year 1, Volume 9 — The end-of-year written exam is announced late in the first year — roughly ten days out, with a practice test on the 15th (y1v9 p.39).
    • Outcome — Every first-year passes: recorded on March 2, 'we passed the end-of-year exam without any problems' (y1v10 p.31).
    • Who took part — All first-year students; school administration; Class D under Chabashira.
  • Year 2, Volume 8 — Late November of the second year: results of the second-semester final examinations are posted before the December special exam.
    • Outcome — First place gains 50 class points, second +25, third -25 and fourth -50, and students below the cutoff (subject averages of 39) are expelled (y2v8 p.12); the averages fix the December head-to-head pairings — 1st vs 2nd, 3rd vs 4th (y2v9 p.11-12).
    • Who took part — All four second-year classes; Chabashira Sae (announces to Class B).
