Entrance Exam

entrance

Rules

Admission test that sorts applicants into ANHS classes. It predates enrollment, so the novels only ever discusses it in retrospect or by analogy; no rules box exists.

Points

Admission fixes the starting class-point baseline for the intake (1000 points at enrollment, y2v1 p.19 context), but the exam itself never scores inside the story.

Conditions

Pre-enrollment only — zero instances by design (see p9_import exclusion log).

Organizer

School admissions (advanced-nurturing entrance testing).
