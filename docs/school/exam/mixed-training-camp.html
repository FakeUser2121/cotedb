Exams & School/Mixed Training Camp
Exam
Special exam

Autumn third-semester opener: the whole school boards buses for a joint road-trip camp — first-, second- and third-years together (announce chapter y1v8 p.11, named 'Mixed Camp' at p.14). Students travel in jerseys with designated seats and no advance briefing

First seen
Year 1, Volume 8
Last run
Year 2, Volume 11

Every time it was run

  • Year 1, Volume 8 — 'A New Special Exam: Mixed Training Camp' opens the vacation — all year groups board buses (y1v8 p.11) and Chabashira names the exam in class (y1v8 p.14).
    • Outcome — Eight days and seven nights of inter-class group activities with senior students, positioned as 'a step beyond the Sports Festival' (y1v8 p.14).
    • Who took part — All three year groups mixed into inter-class teams; first-year classes under their homeroom teachers.
  • Year 2, Volume 11 — Second-year Mixed Training Camp: nine buses instead of the expected twelve (y2v11 p.34); the term 'special exam' is deliberately avoided.
    • Outcome — Repositioned as an exchange meeting — no expulsion risk and not even a penalty specified, 'a significant step down' from the previous year's camp (y2v11 p.53).
    • Who took part — Second- and third-year students; Nagumo Miyabi (laments the downgraded stakes, y2v11 p.53).

Points

Point effects are narrated through the camp chapters rather than a posted results board; the Y2 rerun was explicitly scaled down (

Conditions

Participation is compulsory for the entire year group on the bus; format differences between the Y1 and Y2 runs are announced beforehand (
