Every time it was run

  • Year 1, Volume 10 — Named and explained in March of the first year ('The name of the special exam is the Class Poll', y1v10 p.37), then run to the volume's close.
    • Outcome — The posted results board records three expulsions — Totsuka Yahiko (Class A), Yamauchi Haruki (Class C) and Manabe Shiho (Class D), none from Class B — and no class-point changes (y1v10 p.315).
    • Who took part — First-year classes; Chabashira Sae (rules: three praise and three censure votes, y1v10 p.45); every voting member of Class D.
  • Year 1, Volume 11 — February of the first year: a provisional Class Vote is imposed on the first-years over the faculty's objection.
    • Outcome — Implementation is already decided (y1v11 p.18) despite the warned expulsion risk (y1v11 p.15); the vote ends without expulsions, defeated classes forfeit a protection point (y1v11 p.318), Ayanokōji takes a protection point (y1v11 p.37) and Ryuuen's class survives (y1v11 p.381).
    • Who took part — First-year classes; Mashima (protests to Tsukishiro); Chabashira (calls Mashima off); Tsukishiro, Acting Director (decides).
