Every time it was run

  • Year 1, Volume 6 — First-year first-semester final: pairs are fixed by a mock quiz (y1v6 p.35), then the two-day paired written exam runs under the Paper Shuffle name (chapter 3, y1v6 p.30).
    • Outcome — Eight subjects, 400 questions; a pair under sixty in any subject, or under the ~700-point overall bar (43.75 average across sixteen scores), loses both members — 'one or two pairs are usually expelled… most of them are from Class D' (y1v6 p.36). The result narrows the gap to Class C (y1v7 p.19).
    • Who took part — First-year students in fixed pairs; Chabashira Sae (explains the rules); Hirata, Horikita, Yukimura and Sudou among the pairs.
