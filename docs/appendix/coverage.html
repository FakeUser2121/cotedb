How far the evidence reaches, volume by volume.

Evidence by volume

The archive is built in two layers. Records come from the indexing pass and cover every volume: exam runs, arc states, relationships, quoted lines with page numbers. Transcription is slower and finer — a volume read chapter by chapter, with every line and beat captured. Only transcribed volumes can support the scene-by-scene documents.

What this does not do

  • It does not settle attribution beyond what the novel states. Where the speaker is only implied, the entry says so.
  • It does not summarise from memory. If a page cannot cite a page number, it does not assert the fact.
  • It is not complete yet: transcription has reached the first volume, and each new volume widens every section at once.
