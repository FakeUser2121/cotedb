The citation key

A citation on these pages is written the way a reader says it: Vol. 1 p.247 — Year 1, Volume 1, page 247. Each volume also has a short key used inside the archive, y1v1, so Year 1 Volume 1; a range (pp. 118–121) means the passage runs across those pages. Side stories are numbered between the main volumes — y1v4.5 is the interlude that sits after Volume 4 — and the Year 0 material is cited y0.

Pages follow the printed volumes, so they match the English ebook and the physical book rather than any fan transcription.

How to read a quote here

Everything between quotation marks is the novel's own text, copied from the file rather than recalled. Click a citation chip to see the words the archive found on that page. Where the archive is inferring something — that an unnamed girl is Kushida, for instance — it says 'inference' in the sentence that makes the claim.
