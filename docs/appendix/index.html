How this archive was made

36 translated volumes were converted to text with their page breaks intact and indexed, then read through one at a time. For each volume it is worked through chapter by chapter, writing down scenes, spoken lines, inner voice, numbers and rules — every entry pinned to the page it was read on. The prose pages you are reading are assembled from those notes and nothing else; a volume that has not been read that way shows only its mechanical record (who is named on which page) and says so plainly. Every statement carries the page it came from so you can check it rather than trust it.
