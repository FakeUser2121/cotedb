      Arc

      y1v1

      • Situation: Sorted into Class D against expectations; refuses the class and everyone in it while privately enraged at the grading she believes she deserves.
      • Knowledge state: Understands the point system's logic ('points are just a byproduct') before anyone around her.
      • Changes: Relents just enough to tutor Sudou and Ike — grudging entry into class life; declares she will drag herself to Class A regardless.
      • Why it matters: Sets the pride engine and the isolation armor that later softens under load.
      • Evidence: y1v1 p.98 · y1v1 p.155 · y1v1 p.196

      y1v2

      • Situation: Sudou's expulsion threat forces a public stand.
      • Knowledge state: Reads that the trial is winnable only with Sakura's testimony and class coordination.
      • Changes: Vouches for Sakura publicly and refuses to retreat ('I will keep fighting to support your testimony until the end').
      • Why it matters: First proof she will spend her own standing for others — the isolation armor cracks.
      • Evidence: y1v2 p.94 · y1v2 p.210 · y1v2 p.216

      y1v3

      • Situation: Island exam; resource collapse and Ryuuen's reckless point-burning.
      • Knowledge state: Maps the class's structural weakness ('if the person on top is incompetent, those below him will suffer').
      • Changes: Takes operational command of search parties and cooperation with Class A.
      • Why it matters: Leadership stops being a slogan and becomes practice.
      • Evidence: y1v3 p.111 · y1v3 p.116 · y1v3 p.197

      y1v4

      • Situation: VIP phone test; an analytical duel with Ayanokouji across group strategies.
      • Knowledge state: Deduces the VIP selection system and catches Ryuuen's frame on Karuizawa.
      • Changes: Outs her dependence openly — 'the closest I have would be Ayanokouji-kun' — while deflecting with barbs.
      • Why it matters: The partnership becomes visible even to her.
      • Evidence: y1v4 p.65 · y1v4 p.73 · y1v4 p.239

      y1v5

      • Situation: Sports festival: traitor leaks, Kinoshita frame-up, her injured leg.
      • Knowledge state: Forecasts preparation gaps in advance; diagnoses the frame mid-event.
      • Changes: Runs on an injured leg ('I'll participate, even if I have to crawl'), accepts last place without excuse, sets Sudou's non-violence penance; grants Ayanokouji first-name rights only on a win.
      • Why it matters: Defeat becomes her legitimacy: she leads by taking the loss honestly.
      • Evidence: y1v5 p.55 · y1v5 p.115 · y1v5 p.192

      y1v6

      • Situation: Kushida's past arrives in the open; the final exam is rigged with malicious questions.
      • Knowledge state: Full picture of Kushida's junior-high destruction and her threat network.
      • Changes: Chooses management over exposure ('This is my responsibility. I can't just abandon her'), then beats the question gambit with a pre-submitted counter-plan and publicly takes blame for the class's failed unity.
      • Why it matters: Her two defining choices in one volume: mercy as responsibility, victory as preparation.
      • Evidence: y1v6 p.45 · y1v6 p.49 · y1v6 p.184

      y1v7.5

      • Situation: Nagumo's expulsion machine and student-council politics surface.
      • Knowledge state: Tracks Nagumo's cross-year influence and the one second-year opposing him.
      • Changes: States her leadership creed outright: a leader should help the whole body graduate, not win by expulsion.
      • Why it matters: Her morality is stated as policy, not just instinct.
      • Evidence: y1v7.5 p.88 · y1v7.5 p.90

      y1v8

      • Situation: A challenge built on principles she rejects.
      • Knowledge state: Sees the opponent's character gap before the match.
      • Changes: Refuses the terms and calls the betrayal of direct competition what it is ('I trusted you... I was wrong').
      • Why it matters: Values become a weapon she is willing to wield in public.
      • Evidence: y1v8 p.58 · y1v8 p.60 · y1v8 p.235

      y2v5

      • Situation: Kushida's threat resurfaces at close range.
      • Knowledge state: Done diagnosing — she names the destruction outright.
      • Changes: Confronts Kushida without cushion: 'you are fundamentally stupid... you broke the class' — the responsibility posture hardens into direct confrontation.
      • Why it matters: The mercy-without-illusions stance reaches its limit.
      • Evidence: y2v5 p.212

      y2v9

      • Situation: Student-council orbit; Nagumo's pressure campaign.
      • Knowledge state: Learns that standing comes from relationships, not office.
      • Changes: Swallows pride for the class ('If me prostrating is beneficial for the class, then it's worth it') — a mirror of her y1v5 loss-acceptance.
      • Why it matters: Pride is re-tuned from personal honor to class utility.
      • Evidence: y2v9 p.55 · y2v9 p.56

      Psychology & competing readings

      • She leads by absorbing blame, not by shadow control — the deliberate opposite of Ayanokouji's model (confidence 0.8)
        • Evidence: y1v5 p.192 · y1v6 p.180 · y1v6 p.187
        • Reading: Competing: her pre-submitted exam counter-plan (y1v6 p.184) is pure concealed preparation — Ayanokouji's method. So the difference is emphasis, not kind: she prepares in shadows, but claims failure in public.
      • Her brother complex is admiration fused with unresolved inadequacy, and it fuels both her ceiling and her blind spots (confidence 0.75)
        • Evidence: y1v5 p.55 · y1v5 p.61 · y1v5 p.96
        • Reading: Counter-reading: the text frames Manabu purely as benchmark — she wants to equal the standard, not to be seen by him. The first-name gate (permission tied to beating him in results) reads competitive admiration either way.
      • Keeping Kushida managed rather than exposed is principled responsibility, not naivety or calculation (confidence 0.7)
        • Evidence: y1v6 p.45 · y1v6 p.49 · y2v5 p.212
        • Reading: Competing readings: (a) moral — she refuses to abandon someone whose destruction she witnessed; (b) instrumental — Kushida's network is too useful to burn. Her later direct confrontation (y2v5) undercuts pure calculation but doesn't prove pure mercy either.
      • Her friendships form through usefulness first and warmth second (confidence 0.65)
        • Evidence: y1v1 p.98 · y1v6 p.113 · y1v6 p.180
        • Reading: Counter-reading: the Sudou-tutoring relenting (y1v1 p.207) and her rage at being pitied (y1v6 p.50) predate any utility — attachment is present early, buried under pride.
      • She believes goodness is performed for reward, which is why she distrusts Ichinose's generosity (confidence 0.55)
        • Evidence: y1v6 p.113 · y1v6 p.115 · y1v6 p.116
        • Reading: Contested: could equally be strategic caution dressed as philosophy — she tests Ichinose with a loaded question, then admits she is 'special' and must be extra careful. Motive (envy vs. analysis) is not settled by the corpus.
