      Arc

      y1v1

      • Situation: Enrolled into the school's lowest class; lives as an invisible loner while Horikita's cold war with Kushida's social web begins.
      • Knowledge state: Reads Class D's point deficit and Kushida's junior-high history with him long before either surfaces openly.
      • Changes: Turns down Manabu Horikita's guidance-office challenge; accepts a quiet orbit around Horikita (lunch, the study group as bridge).
      • Why it matters: Establishes the 'no trouble' mask the rest of the series punctures.
      • Evidence: y1v1 p.99 · y1v1 p.150 · y1v1 p.162

      y1v2

      • Situation: Sudou's assault accusation threatens the class's first island points.
      • Knowledge state: Understands Kushida's hostility toward Horikita is a personal grudge, not class politics.
      • Changes: Builds the witness strategy and publicly vouches for Sakura's testimony — first real cost-taking.
      • Why it matters: Converts him from observer to reluctant operator while the humility mask ('don't keep overestimating me') holds.
      • Evidence: y1v2 p.112 · y1v2 p.211 · y1v2 p.253

      y1v3

      • Situation: Island survival exam: cave occupation politics around Katsuragi and Yahiko.
      • Knowledge state: Maps Ryuuen's aggression style and the keycard/coercion game as they happen.
      • Changes: Settles the cave dispute forensically from what he saw, without revealing how much he watches.
      • Why it matters: First full proof that his 'bystander' claim is a pose: he investigates even when uninvolved.
      • Evidence: y1v3 p.251 · y1v4 p.109

      y1v4

      • Situation: Phone-VIP game; Ryuuen's camp targets Karuizawa's bullying past.
      • Knowledge state: Identifies Karuizawa as VIP and reads Ryuuen's endgame.
      • Changes: Records Karuizawa's 'baptism' and binds her to him — the series' most morally contested act.
      • Why it matters: Creates the Karuizawa instrument while framing it as fear-management.
      • Evidence: y1v4 p.173 · y1v4 p.201 · y1v4 p.232

      y1v5

      • Situation: Sports festival: predicted traitor, Kinoshita frame-up, Sudou's injury leverage.
      • Knowledge state: Forecasts the class leak before it happens and names the traitor by festival end.
      • Changes: Substitutes into events, counter-frames Kinoshita, settles the million-point deal.
      • Why it matters: Proves the forecasting is operational, while he still visibly sandbags his own results.
      • Evidence: y1v5 p.47 · y1v5 p.141 · y1v5 p.155

      y1v6

      • Situation: Kushida's threats escalate toward an expulsion gambit.
      • Knowledge state: Holds recorded proof of her coercion.
      • Changes: Confronts her openly, drops the pretend-ignorance, and wagers on Horikita defeating her.
      • Why it matters: The mask drops in private: knowing and acting merge.
      • Evidence: y1v6 p.124 · y1v6 p.195 · y1v6 p.199

      y1v7

      • Situation: His father Atsuomi appears while Ryuuen closes in on X's identity.
      • Knowledge state: Fights both fronts at full exposure.
      • Changes: Defines freedom against the White Room, reveals as X, defeats Ryuuen's crew, spares Ryuuen on stated utility.
      • Why it matters: Centerpiece volume: self-definition plus the first stated long-term manipulation of rival classes.
      • Evidence: y1v7 p.57 · y1v7 p.64 · y1v7 p.195

      y1v8

      • Situation: Student-council president Nagumo's attention and expulsion games.
      • Knowledge state: Maps Nagumo's cross-grade network.
      • Changes: Engages the second-year war by proxy; gives Ichinose a partial, framed admission.
      • Why it matters: Shifts the threat model from first-years to institutional power.
      • Evidence: y1v8 p.176 · y1v8 p.195 · y1v8 p.212

      y2v4

      • Situation: Tsukishiro's pressure and White Room students surface inside the school.
      • Knowledge state: Confirms the acting-director's hostility.
      • Changes: Names Tsukishiro's approach to his face — stops hiding from institutional foes.
      • Why it matters: He moves from evading his past to containing it inside the school's rules.
      • Evidence: y2v4 p.20 · y2v4 p.252

      y2v8

      • Situation: Yagami's designs press on Sakura and the class internally.
      • Knowledge state: Reads Yagami's pattern through Amasawa's warnings.
      • Changes: Declares he is not running away — first explicit refusal of withdrawal.
      • Why it matters: Marks concealment turning into standing ground for classmates.
      • Evidence: y2v8 p.61 · y2v8 p.64

      y2v12

      • Situation: Late second year: Karuizawa's position weakens; Horikita's class unity strains.
      • Knowledge state: Anticipates the relationship collapse and the coming separation.
      • Changes: Frames secrets as shackles while advising Horikita to rely on classmates — preparing both to stand without him.
      • Why it matters: Sets up the third-year independence arc.
      • Evidence: y2v12 p.53 · y2v12 p.151

      Psychology & competing readings

      • His terminal goal is self-determination, not Class A victory (confidence 0.8)
        • Evidence: y1v5 p.47 · y1v7 p.59 · y1v7 p.64
        • Reading: Competing: he optimizes class outcomes constantly. Plainest synthesis: Class A is the arena in which self-determination is proven, not the goal itself.
      • The trouble-avoider persona is a White Room survival reflex he inherited, not his genuine preference (confidence 0.75)
        • Evidence: y1v5 p.15 · y1v4 p.109 · y1v1 p.150
        • Reading: Counter-reading: he repeatedly chooses engagement when stakes are personal (y1v6 p.124; y2v8 p.64), so the mask may be inherited but no longer chosen. Both readings fit the corpus; the text never settles it.
      • His relationships shift from instruments to obligations across y1 to y2 (confidence 0.7)
        • Evidence: y1v1 p.73 · y1v2 p.211 · y2v8 p.64 · y2v12 p.151
        • Reading: Counter-reading: no scene shows him acting against interest for a peer without side-benefit; the 'obligations' may be strategic consistency rather than attachment growth.
      • Sparing Ryuuen was utility, not mercy or respect (confidence 0.65)
        • Evidence: y1v7 p.185 · y1v7 p.195
        • Reading: Competing: the 'fear experiment' framing (p.185-186) hints at personal curiosity toward Ryuuen; utility is only the stated reason.
      • The Karuizawa 'baptism' was necessity-driven instrumentation, not sadism (confidence 0.6)
        • Evidence: y1v4 p.201 · y1v7 p.124 · y1v7 p.190
        • Reading: Contested: the method's cruelty (y1v4 p.195-199) supports a colder reading — that he reaches for White Room techniques because they are the only tools he was given. Readers split here; surface evidence supports both.

      Relationships

      • Horikita Suzune — ally-core [from y1v1]: Strategic partner from y1v1, evolving to first-name intimacy by y2v6 (p.343 'Suzune…right?'). Co-scene count in the corpus: 93 — his strongest link.
        • Evidence: y1v1 p.99 · y1v2 p.112 · y2v6 p.343
      • Horikita Manabu — authority-benchmark [from y1v1]: Guidance-office refusal (y1v1 p.150); Manabu's unannounced observations keep shaping the path Horikita and he both walk.
        • Evidence: y1v1 p.149 · y1v1 p.150
      • Ayanokouji Atsuomi — family-adversary [from y1v7]: White Room father; y1v7 meeting inverts the contract: orders bind only inside the White Room, and he claims the right to choose his path.
        • Evidence: y1v7 p.57 · y1v7 p.59 · y1v7 p.64
      • Chabashira Sae — keeper-of-secret [from y1v1]: Homeroom channel for his origin; she presses and conceals in equal measure (handwriting test y2v7).
        • Evidence: y1v1 p.140 · y2v7 p.153 · y2v7 p.167
      • Sakayanagi Arisu — rival-a-class [from y1v7]: Long-game opponent; he deliberately steers Ryuuen against her (y1v7 p.195) instead of engaging directly.
        • Evidence: y1v7 p.195 · y2v8 p.168
      • Kushida Kikyou — rival-secret [from y1v1]: He holds her junior-high history; mutual blackmail from y1v5-6 converts her social mask into a managed threat.
        • Evidence: y1v5 p.195 · y1v6 p.199 · y1v6 p.200
      • Ryuuen Kakeru — rival-tolerated [from y1v4]: Wariness first (y1v4 p.67 'wary of Ryuuen by a mile'), X-endgame defeat y1v7, then kept alive as Class-D's useful enemy against Sakayanagi and Ichinose.
        • Evidence: y1v4 p.67 · y1v7 p.195
      • Karuizawa Kei — ward-to-fracture [y1v4→y2v12]: Created via the recorded baptism (y1v4), managed through y1v7-8 protection, separation prepared in y2v12 ('secrets become shackles').
        • Evidence: y1v4 p.201 · y1v7 p.190 · y2v12 p.53
      • Kouenji Rokusuke — wary-unknown [from y1v4]: The one classmate he declines to model: quantifies everyone else, keeps Kouenji as an unquantified variable.
        • Evidence: y1v4 p.67 · y1v5 p.95
