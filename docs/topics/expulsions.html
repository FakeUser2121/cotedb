Year 2, Volume 5Unanimous Vote Exam

Second-year spring: the Unanimous Vote Exam is announced and its rules board goes up in class (y2v5 p.31, p.41). Five questions, up to four options each, repeated until unanimous, timeout voids the question (y2v5 p.41); the class avoids expelling Airi (y2v5 p.237) while weighing a 350-class-point loss (y2v5 p.187); the later Hokkaido ballot reuses the mechanism (y2v8 p.17).

Year 2, Volume 8Midterm and Final Written Exams

Late November of the second year: results of the second-semester final examinations are posted before the December special exam. First place gains 50 class points, second +25, third -25 and fourth -50, and students below the cutoff (subject averages of 39) are expelled (y2v8 p.12); the averages fix the December head-to-head pairings — 1st vs 2nd, 3rd vs 4th (y2v9 p.11-12).
