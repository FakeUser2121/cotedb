Every figure the records carry

Prices, payouts and point swings, in story order. A number only appears here if a record cites the page it was stated on.

Year 1, Volume 1Entrance Exam — rules

Admission fixes the starting class-point baseline for the intake (1000 points at enrollment, y2v1 p.19 context), but the exam itself never scores inside the story.

Year 1, Volume 1Midterm and Final Written Exams — rules

Results move standing: second-semester finals rank the classes with first place earning 50 class points (y2v8 p.12), and final-exam averages seed later specials — December's head-to-head paired classes by those averages (y2v9 p.11). Private points can buy extra test points: Chabashira sells one for 100,000 (y1v1 p.253).

Year 1, Volume 2Sports Festival — rules

Class results move the standings — after the Y2 contests Ryuuen-side classes had won both their matches and sat 100 class points ahead (y2v7 p.29). Private-point side stakes: MVP of each grade earns 100,000 private points (y1v5 p.19) and roster substitutes cost 100,000 per competition (y1v5 p.23).

Year 1, Volume 4Cruise Ship Exam — rules

Group-level scores plus traitor sabotage decide outcomes; the ship results are accounted together with the island week (y1v4.5 p.69: 'results from the island test, and the test on the ship').

Year 1, Volume 4Uninhabited Island Exam — rules

Task performance converts to points and class standing; Y2 first place paid 300 class points (y2v5 p.187: 'the 300 class points that Koenji got in the desert island exam'). Y1's version is remembered as the template class victory (y1v7 p.20).

Year 1, Volume 6Paper Shuffle — rules

Paired finals shift class standing through the combined scores; the format is cited as last year's contrast when standardized school-designed finals return (y2v8 p.12).

Year 1, Volume 8Mixed Training Camp — rules

Point effects are narrated through the camp chapters rather than a posted results board; the Y2 rerun was explicitly scaled down (y2v11 p.53: 'compared to last year's mixed training camp, this one is a significant step down').

Year 1, Volume 10Class Poll — rules

Main run: no class-point changes came from the expulsions themselves (results board y1v10 p.315). Provisional run: class points were expected to fluctuate without any expulsions (y1v11 p.318).

Year 1, Volume 11.5End-of-Year Special Exam — rules

Decisive for the standings: the class still gains class points from the end-of-year special under partial-win conditions (y2v12 p.36).

Year 1, Volume 11Event Selection Exam — rules

Runs as the closing exam of the first year (final-special slot from March 8, y1v10 p.31); class outcomes feed the year-end standings discussed in the y1v11.5 aftermath.

Year 1, Volume 11School Festival — rules

100 class points to places 1-4, 50 to places 5-8, 9th-12th unchanged, no penalty below 8th (y2v5 p.14); final standings posted in the aftermath (y2v7 p.202).

Year 2, Volume 5Unanimous Vote Exam — rules

Stakes are large: failure modes put 350 class points on the line in the protection vote (y2v5 p.187), and defeated classes in the exam family forfeit protection points (y1v11 p.318).

Year 2, Volume 9Cooperative Comprehensive Written Exam — rules

Class-versus-class results transfer 50 class points winner-takes (y2v9 p.11); pairings come from recent final-exam averages — 1st vs 2nd, 3rd vs 4th (y2v9 p.11-12); December standings A 1250 / B 985 / C 684 / D 655 (y2v9 p.12).

Year 2, Volume 10Survival and Elimination Exam — rules

Class points swing on placement; the losing side of the final standings absorbs the damage (Sakayanagi's class took the loss, y2v11 p.11).

Year 3, Volume 1Comprehensive Academic Ability Exam — rules

Scoring is out of 100 points per the overview; class-point consequences sit beyond the novels horizon (y3v1 is the final volume).

Quotes are verbatim from the Classroom of the Elite light novels. A citation such as y1v1 p.247 means Year 1, Volume 1, page 247; page numbers follow the printed volume.
