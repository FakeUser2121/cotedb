      School Festival

      event

      92 mentions · 10 vols · 1 runs · 1 claims

      Rules

      First cultural festival in school history (announce y2v5 p.13): an all-year sales competition. Outline box (y2v5 p.14): prep budget as private points — 2nd-years 5,000, 1st-years 5,500, 3rd-years 4,500 — extra funds for student-council or club service, unused funds confiscated.

      Points

      100 class points to places 1-4, 50 to places 5-8, 9th-12th unchanged, no penalty below 8th (y2v5 p.14); final standings posted in the aftermath (y2v7 p.202).

      Conditions

      Budget discipline: initial cost and additional funds are excluded from final sales and confiscated if unused (y2v5 p.14). An unexpected expulsion surfaced during the festival (y2v7 p.193).

      Organizer

      School-run festival; classes compete as sales booths across all three grades.

      Notes

      92 mentions / 10 vols. Footprint starts y1v11 p.19 (first-years propose holding one 'next year'); the Y2 event anchors y2v5 p.13. Horikita's class took first place (y2v7 p.200).

      Footprint

      • Span: y1v11 → y2v12
      • Anchored mentions by vol: y1v11 ×2, y2v5 ×27, y2v6 ×4, y2v7 ×48, y2v8 ×5, y2v9 ×1, y2v9.5 ×2, y2v10 ×1, y2v11 ×1, y2v12 ×1

      Runs

      y2v5 p.13 (run #1, conf 0.95)

      • Chronology: The first cultural festival in the school's history is announced with the sports festival (y2v5 p.13) and its sales outline is briefed (y2v5 p.14); it runs across y2v7.
      • Participants: All three years of classes competing on total sales with guests on campus; Ryuuen and Horikita trade a matching-stall declaration of war (y2v7 p.29).
      • Outcome: Horikita's class takes first place — 'we did manage to get first place at the school festival' (y2v7 p.200) — with 100 class points for 1st-4th and 50 for 5th-8th (y2v5 p.14); an unexpected expulsion lands on the first-years' side (y2v7 p.193).
      • Evidence: “the first year, which is also a concern. “And in November, we will be holding a cultural festival, which will be our first cultural festival in the history of Advanced Nurturing”
      • Refs: ["y2v5 p.13", "y2v5 p.14", "y2v7 p.19", "y2v7 p.200"]

      Claims

      • “Cultural-festival preparation grants are 5,500 / 5,000 / 4,500 private points for first / second / third years, and placements pay 100 class points for 1st-4th and 50 for 5th-8th, with no penalty below eighth.” (y2v5 p.14) — narrator_fact, narrator, conf 0.95
        • evidence: “ll explain more about the sports festival later, but first let’s talk about the cultural festival.” In terms of order, the sports festival came first, but Chabashira-sensei bega”
        • Bulleted outline on-page (y2v5 p.14); 9th to 12th place stand unchanged.
