What the text says

  1. The place On-campus student library inside the main school building; designated by Horikita as the neutral ground for tutoring the failing students ("Bring them to the library, and I’ll tutor them"). Multi-table open floor; a long table at the far end serves as the group's base. Closes to casual use after hours; roof access stairs above it lock after class. Y1V1 p.156, 171–180
