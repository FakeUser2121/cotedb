Vol. 1Horikita's midterm study group & its collapse
Vol. 1 p.156
What if you also held a study group like Hirata’s, specifically for helping Sudou and Ike?
Sure. I wouldn’t have any objections to that. You probably find that rather surprising, though, don’t you?
Year 1, Volume 1, page 156
, 171–180
  • The place

    On-campus student library inside the main school building; designated by Horikita as the neutral ground for tutoring the failing students ("Bring them to the library, and I’ll tutor them"). Multi-table open floor; a long table at the far end serves as the group's base. Closes to casual use after hours; roof access stairs above it lock after class.

    school-library — p.156, 171–180
  • Scene

    Setting for the rise and collapse of Horikita's rival study group: Horikita and Ayanokōji secure the far-end table; Kushida arrives with Sudou, Ike, Yamauchi, and Okitani; the session dies within minutes when Sudou, then Ike (with Yamauchi and Okitani) walk out; Horikita, Kushida, and Ayanokōji remain for the "dead weight" confrontation, until only Horikita and Ayanokōji are left in complete, oppressive silence.

    p.171–180
  • Relationship

    Directly after the collapse, Ayanokōji pursues Kushida from here across campus and up to the locked rooftop — her desperate state starts at this door.

    Kushida Kikyō — p.181
