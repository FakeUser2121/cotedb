What the text says

  1. The place Massive, state-of-the-art climate-controlled indoor aquatic complex on campus, featuring an Olympic-length heated pool allowing year-round swimming even during early spring (April). Y1V1 p.75–88
  2. Rule Physical education curriculum: co-ed swimming classes. Students wear school-issued navy swimsuits (girls) and trunks (boys); tight athletic speedo briefs are technically permitted under dress regulations (as demonstrated by Kōenji). Y1V1 p.75–76
  3. Rule Students may use the pool only once during those three days after the first-day crowd forced extra restrictions. Y1V4.5 p.156
  4. Rule Separate locker rooms by grade level; entry without uniforms is allowed since it isn't the main building. Y1V4.5 p.170
  5. Rule Fifteen points per set, three sets, first team to two sets wins; the scorer earns the right to serve again, with service decided by rotation. Y1V4.5 p.183
  6. Rule Public use runs 9:00 A.M. to 5:00 P.M. for the last three days of summer vacation. Y1V4.5 p.207
  7. Rule The men’s and women’s locker rooms sit at opposite ends of the hallway with diverging paths, and the cameras cover the approaches. Y1V4.5 p.208
  8. Fact The connecting vent is fifteen centimeters high and forty centimeters wide, divided so first-year rooms link to first-year rooms. Y1V4.5 p.208
