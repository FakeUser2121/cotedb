      campus-zone · generated view (source: cote_kb.db; regenerate with python3 kb/build_world_dossiers.py)

      Function

      Shopping-and-dining complex on the school grounds; the standard off-duty meeting spot.

      Notes

      Defined at y1v4.5 p.12 as 'a complex on the school grounds that students often went to'. Because students live on campus, the mall functions as the town square: fortune-tellers, casual dates, class meetings on neutral ground (309 mentions, 24 vols).
