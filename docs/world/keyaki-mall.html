What the text says

  1. The place Primary off-academic commercial shopping and entertainment complex for ANHS students and faculty, located on campus grounds. Houses retail stores, cafes, bookstores, cinemas, and restaurants. Y1V1 p.94–101
  2. The place Popular student café inside Keyaki Mall. Features a chic, modern aesthetic with indoor and terrace seating, serving espresso beverages, lattes, and desserts payable exclusively via Private Points. Y1V1 p.94–101
  3. The place The complex draws the crowd out of season — elevator banks backed up, pairs all over the floor, solo students a minority. Y1V4.5 p.18–19
  4. Rule The booth’s written notice and the attendant’s answer: guidance is for couples only; Ukon-sensei reads fortunes for couples. Y1V4.5 p.19–20
  5. The place The car sits in the Keyaki Mall’s external wall — the narration notes it only once the air conditioning dies: under the sun the interior runs to heatstroke danger. Y1V4.5 p.43
  6. Rule AK observes the school gave the points in the first place, so spending them back is not really a loss. Y1V4.5 p.52
  7. Fact Students may drink there but are prohibited from bottling water and taking it home. Y1V4.5 p.108
