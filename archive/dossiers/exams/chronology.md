# Master Chronology

22 exam runs in story order — volume and page for every attempt

## y1v1

- **p.128** — Midterm and Final Written Exams #1 (conf 0.95): First-semester midterm for the first-year classes, sat and graded inside the first volume; Chabashira posts results class-wide.

## y1v3

- **p.13** — Midterm and Final Written Exams #2 (conf 0.9): The third volume opens on the summer cruise, after the first-semester finals of the first year have already been graded.

## y1v4

- **p.34** — Cruise Ship Exam #1 (conf 0.9): The two-week luxury-liner trip doubles as a special exam: Class D gets a private briefing on the Zodiac grouping before activities begin (y1v4 p.34-35).
- **p.69** — Uninhabited Island Exam #1 (conf 0.9): First-year uninhabited-island test, run and graded before the summer cruise; its result is still being argued over a volume later.

## y1v5

- **p.13** — Sports Festival #1 (conf 0.9): First-year sports festival: PE preparation is announced from September to early October (y1v5 p.16); the festival finishes before mid-October (y1v6 p.14).

## y1v6

- **p.30** — Midterm and Final Written Exams #3 (conf 0.95): Second first-year midterm: results day falls in Chapter 3 ('The Paper Shuffle'), with Chabashira restating the fail-and-expel rule before reading the board.
- **p.30** — Paper Shuffle #1 (conf 0.95): First-year first-semester final: pairs are fixed by a mock quiz (y1v6 p.35), then the two-day paired written exam runs under the Paper Shuffle name (chapter 3, y1v6 p.30).

## y1v8

- **p.11** — Mixed Training Camp #1 (conf 0.95): 'A New Special Exam: Mixed Training Camp' opens the vacation — all year groups board buses (y1v8 p.11) and Chabashira names the exam in class (y1v8 p.14).

## y1v9

- **p.39** — Midterm and Final Written Exams #4 (conf 0.95): The end-of-year written exam is announced late in the first year — roughly ten days out, with a practice test on the 15th (y1v9 p.39).

## y1v10

- **p.37** — Class Poll #1 (conf 0.95): Named and explained in March of the first year ('The name of the special exam is the Class Poll', y1v10 p.37), then run to the volume's close.

## y1v11

- **p.13** — Class Poll #2 (conf 0.9): February of the first year: a provisional Class Vote is imposed on the first-years over the faculty's objection.
- **p.28** — Event Selection Exam #1 (conf 0.95): The final special of the first year: announced March 8 with matchups set, event selection locked March 15, exam day March 22 (y1v11 p.33).

## y2v2

- **p.119** — Uninhabited Island Exam #2 (conf 0.95): Announced in the second year as the next special exam during summer vacation (y2v2 p.119) and run across y2v3.

## y2v5

- **p.13** — School Festival #1 (conf 0.95): The first cultural festival in the school's history is announced with the sports festival (y2v5 p.13) and its sales outline is briefed (y2v5 p.14); it runs across y2v7.
- **p.13** — Sports Festival #2 (conf 0.9): Second-year sports festival announced alongside the cultural festival (y2v5 p.13) and explained after the Unanimous Vote Exam closes (y2v6 p.30).
- **p.31** — Unanimous Vote Exam #1 (conf 0.95): Second-year spring: the Unanimous Vote Exam is announced and its rules board goes up in class (y2v5 p.31, p.41).

## y2v8

- **p.12** — Midterm and Final Written Exams #5 (conf 0.95): Late November of the second year: results of the second-semester final examinations are posted before the December special exam.

## y2v9

- **p.11** — Cooperative Comprehensive Written Exam #1 (conf 0.95): Announced after the second-semester finals in December: Chabashira introduces a one-on-one academic competition between classes (y2v9 p.11) and the official outline follows (y2v9 p.13).

## y2v10

- **p.32** — Survival and Elimination Exam #1 (conf 0.95): The [Survival and Elimination Special Exam] overview is issued (y2v10 p.32) and the exam runs across the volume to its close (y2v10 p.144, p.197).

## y2v11

- **p.34** — Mixed Training Camp #2 (conf 0.95): Second-year Mixed Training Camp: nine buses instead of the expected twelve (y2v11 p.34); the term 'special exam' is deliberately avoided.
- **p.203** — End-of-Year Special Exam #1 (conf 0.9): Announced late in y2v11 (Hashimoto commits to making Ryuuen win 'no matter what the content of the end-of-year special exam is', y2v11 p.203); opens in y2v12 (y2v12 p.11) and concludes on the calendar's end — March 14 (y2v12.5 p.9).

## y3v1

- **p.9** — Comprehensive Academic Ability Exam #1 (conf 0.95): The third year opens with the announcement of its first special exam (y3v1 p.9) and the overview board in class (y3v1 p.100).
