# Expulsion Rules

**discipline**

`1702 mentions · 33 vols · 4 claims`

## Function

Drop-out discipline: failing grades, class votes, eliminations and buybacks all end in the same forced exit.

## Notes

The base rule lands in the first weeks — fail a midterm or final and you drop out (y1v1 p.128) — with results posts carrying the below-the-line threat (y1v10 p.14). Instruments beyond written grades: class-vote expulsions (three names y1v10 p.315), elimination-loss dropouts (Kamuro, y2v11 p.11), bounties (20,000,000 to have someone removed, y2v2 p.71) and the 20,000,000 revocation off-ramp (y1v10 p.46). Voluntary and forced-exit counts are quoted in the y1v11.5 aftermath and the Maezono case (y2v12.5 p.81, p.143). 1634 mentions / 30 vols — the largest system footprint after student-council.
