# White Room

**off-site**

`505 mentions · 26 vols · 53 co-paged scenes · 1 claims`

## Function

Underground rearing/education facility where Ayanokouji Kiyotaka and other selected children were trained into engineered elites.

## Notes

The single most referenced off-site location (508 mentions, 24 vols, present from y0 through y3v1) despite never being a campus site: it is discussed, remembered and used as a threat far more than it is visited. y1v11.5 p.159 frames it as a temporary residence no different from a hotel — the place Ayanokouji's father keeps returning to as a lever.
