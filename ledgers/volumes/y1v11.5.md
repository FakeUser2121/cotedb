### Prologue (pp. 8–12)
- **Setting:** Morning of March 31 in Horikita Suzune’s dorm room; she reflects before Horikita Manabu’s graduation departure.
- **Narrative beats:**
  - pp.8–9: Suzune looks at her tired reflection after a sleepless night and thinks about how little time she has spent talking with her brother over the past year.
  - pp.9–10: She wonders whether to meet him to say farewell but feels she has not shown him enough growth and fears making him worry.
  - pp.10–11: Rejecting the idea of running away, she struggles with what to do as time runs out before his noon departure and questions who she is.
  - pp.11–12: She reflects that she spent years as an imitation shaped to gain her brother’s recognition, accepts that past self as real, decides what she wants to show him, and resolves to act before he leaves.
- **About these pages:** First-person monologue from Horikita Suzune’s perspective; p.12 is followed by a blank page in the text.
- **New names:** none.
- **New rules and facts:** Horikita Manabu is departing at noon on March 31 (pp.8, 10).
- **Unattributed lines:** none.
- **Translation errors, kept as printed:** "31th" appears on p.8.

### Chapter 1: Graduation Ceremony (pp. 13–120)
- **Setting:** March 24 graduation ceremony and aftermath on campus; includes flashback to the previous night (Ayanokōji’s call to Chairman Sakayanagi) and post-ceremony confrontation/farewells.
- **Narrative beats:**
  - pp.13–33: Ayanokōji attends the graduation ceremony, talks with Yukimura Keisei about the final exam and class leadership, hears speeches including Horikita Manabu’s, and later speaks with Horikita Suzune about seeing her brother after the graduates’ meeting.
  - pp.34–44: Flashback to the previous night: Ayanokōji calls Chairman Sakayanagi to report Acting Director Tsukishiro’s interference and ask him to return.
  - pp.45–87: Ayanokōji meets Sakayanagi Arisu, joins a meeting with Tsukishiro and teachers (Mashima, Chabashira, Sakagami, Hoshinomiya), and the group confronts Tsukishiro over manipulation in the chess exam; teachers agree to report the matter.
  - pp.88–120: After the graduates’ meeting, Ayanokōji and Suzune observe third-year farewells; Suzune meets Horikita Manabu, and Ayanokōji witnesses exchanges involving Nagumo Miyabi, Asahina Nazuna, Kiriyama Ikuto, and Tachibana Akane as graduates depart.
- **About these pages:** Ayanokōji’s first-person narration with an embedded “Yesterday” flashback (pp.34–44).
- **New names:** none confirmed as new roster additions in this pass.
- **New rules and facts:** Chairman Sakayanagi is under house arrest/suspicion and Ayanokōji asks him to return (pp.37–44); teachers confront Acting Director Tsukishiro about exam interference in chess (pp.47–87).
- **Unattributed lines:** some graduation/farewell remarks in the crowd remain unattributed in this pass.
- **Translation errors, kept as printed:** date written as “March 24th. The graduation ceremony” on p.13.

### Chapter 2: Date Hiyori (pp. 121–143)
- **Setting:** Spring break begins on campus; Keyaki mall café; later a brief encounter near the mall.
- **Narrative beats:**
  - pp.121–123: Ayanokōji reflects on spring break, his point-transfer contract with Kushida, and heads out after arranging meetings.
  - pp.124–128: He meets Shiina Hiyori at Keyaki mall; she brings newly arrived library books and they sit at a café.
  - pp.128–135: Ike Kanji and Shinohara Satsuki sit nearby; Ayanokōji and Hiyori overhear them discuss Class D, grades, and the fear of future expulsion before the pair notice them and leave after arguing.
  - pp.135–141: Hiyori asks Ayanokōji about Ryūen’s change after the end-of-year exam and about Class C’s strategy against Class B, then asks him to pass a message to Ryūen.
  - pp.142–143: After parting with Hiyori, Ayanokōji meets Karuizawa Kei, who is wary of rumors and leaves upset.
- **About these pages:** Ayanokōji’s narration.
- **New names:** none.
- **New rules and facts:** Ayanokōji states his contract with Kushida requires him to hand over half his private points to her every month (p.122).
- **Unattributed lines:** none noted in this pass.
- **Translation errors, kept as printed:** “However, there are still students who encounter trouble.” / “However, there were still students that had encountered some difficulties.” repetition appears on pp.121–122.

### Chapter 3: Lost Lamb (pp. 144–236)
- **Setting:** Spring break on campus and Keyaki mall, including café, corridors, outdoor rain, and dormitory; includes scenes at Keyaki mall’s second-floor men’s toilet.
- **Narrative beats:**
  - pp.144–148: Ayanokōji arranges to meet Ichinose Honami and Horikita Suzune to discuss the end-of-year exam and the future of their classes’ cooperation.
  - pp.149–159: On the way to the meeting, Ayanokōji encounters Ibuki Mio and Ishizaki Daichi; Ishizaki suggests Ayanokōji transfer to Class D, and Ayanokōji infers Ryūen’s return is being prepared.
  - pp.160–193: Ayanokōji meets Horikita and Ichinose at the café; Ichinose discusses her class’s defeat and the future, and Horikita later challenges Ayanokōji to a written-exam contest.
  - pp.194–205: In the men’s toilet, Hashimoto Masayoshi meets Ryūen Kakeru and brings in Kanzaki Ryūji, who warns Ryūen about Class B’s response to his tactics.
  - pp.206–214: Ayanokōji meets Ryūen; Ryūen states his intent to return and target other classes, and the two clash briefly before parting.
  - pp.215–236: In heavy rain, Ayanokōji finds Ichinose alone, brings her to his room to warm up, and they talk about her doubts and make a promise to meet again a year later.
- **About these pages:** Mostly Ayanokōji’s narration; Parts 3 and 5 present the toilet meeting among Ryūen, Hashimoto, and Kanzaki.
- **New names:** none.
- **New rules and facts:** Horikita and Ayanokōji agree to compare scores on a subject of Horikita’s choosing in a written exam after April (pp.191–193).
- **Unattributed lines:** none noted in this pass.
- **Translation errors, kept as printed:** “commander tower” appears in place of “commanding tower” on p.207.

### Chapter 4: From a Brother to a Sister (pp. 237–295)
- **Setting:** March 31 at the school’s main entrance/gate area, followed by the path back toward the dormitories.
- **Narrative beats:**
  - pp.237–258: Ayanokōji meets Horikita Manabu before his departure; they discuss Suzune’s absence, Nagumo and the school system, and Ayanokōji’s future approach before Manabu prepares to leave.
  - pp.259–270: Horikita Suzune arrives late with her hair cut short; she and Manabu reconcile, discuss her past and growth, and share a final farewell as he departs.
  - pp.271–276: Ayanokōji and Suzune talk after the farewell about her lateness, her haircut, and what it means to her.
  - pp.277–283: Suzune breaks down after holding back her feelings; Ayanokōji stays with her until she recovers.
  - pp.284–293: They discuss Suzune joining the student council as Ayanokōji’s proposed request and Ayanokōji’s thoughts about Kushida.
  - pp.294–295: Ayanokōji reflects alone on the coming year and Horikita Manabu’s parting words.
- **About these pages:** Ayanokōji’s narration.
- **New names:** none.
- **New rules and facts:** Horikita Manabu departs the school on March 31 and says he will wait for Suzune outside the main entrance two years later (pp.237, 270).
- **Unattributed lines:** none.
- **Translation errors, kept as printed:** “the elder Hroikita” appears on p.238.
