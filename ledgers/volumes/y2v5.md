- **Setting:** POV frame — Chabashira's recurring nightmare of the graduation special exam her third-year class (Class B) took 11 years ago; the events end the same way every time.
- **Narrative beats:**

By third year, "the number of classmates who had left me had swelled to six" (p.7), yet she had accumulated class points in her final year without a single expulsion, believing she could graduate from Class A "without having to leave anyone behind" (p.7).

"The class, which had been united as one, collapsed in an instant." Then: "It's... over." — printed as his line ("He muttered, his face full of resignation and realization." p.7).

He reached out his hand to her "under the evening sky, somewhat shyly." Holding back tears, she said, "I look forward to working with you..." — "The relationship between the two of us ended just as it had begun." (p.8)
