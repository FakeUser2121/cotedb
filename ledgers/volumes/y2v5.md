### Chapter One: Sae Chabashira's Monologue (pp. 7–8)
- **Setting:** POV frame — Chabashira's recurring nightmare of the graduation special exam her third-year class (Class B) took 11 years ago; the events end the same way every time.
- **Narrative beats:**
  - The nightmare and its invariant shape (p.7): the events of "that day" — her class had momentum, "well within reach of Class A" (p.7). By third year, "the number of classmates who had left me had swelled to six" (p.7), yet she had accumulated class points in her final year without a single expulsion, believing she could graduate from Class A "without having to leave anyone behind" (p.7).
  - The nightmare's climax (p.7): "The scene changes and I am screaming in class." Her best friend, Chie, approaches with an angry expression and grabs her chest; she screams in agony. "The class, which had been united as one, collapsed in an instant." Then: "It's... over." — printed as his line ("He muttered, his face full of resignation and realization." p.7).
  - The ending (p.8): "He had been my companion for the past three years" — irreplaceable friend, irreplaceable person, in the vein of "a bit of a jerk, but he was serious, kind, and more reliable than anyone else." He reached out his hand to her "under the evening sky, somewhat shyly." Holding back tears, she said, "I look forward to working with you..." — "The relationship between the two of us ended just as it had begun." (p.8)
- **New names:** none. "Chie" (her best friend) is printed without a surname; no roster id is confirmed by these pages.
- **Unattributed lines:** none. "It's... over." is tagged "He muttered" (p.7) — the he-companion; who exactly "he" is (the classmate whose expulsion ended the relationship) is not named on these pages.
- **Translation errors, kept as printed:** none observed.
- **Open threads:** identity of the "he" companion and of Chie; the exam at the heart of the nightmare (the Unanimous Voting Exam of Ch. Four/Eight/Nine); the 100 vs 50 class-point decision hinted by "well within reach of Class A."

### Chapter Two: Footsteps of Turmoil (pp. 9–28)
- **Setting:** ANHS, first day of the second semester (early September); dormitory, walk to school, classroom 2-D, special-wing classroom (maid-café audition).
- **Narrative beats:**
  - Sudō's diligence (p.9): staying up all night studying to bring his OAA Academics up to B; now "both literary and military arts"; envies Ike's relationship with Shinohara and asks Ayanokōji whether he's kissed her — Ayanokōji suggests asking Ike directly (Ike would brag).
  - The dating reveal (p.10–12): Ayanokōji confesses his first girlfriend; it's Karuizawa Kei; Sudō is floored ("Wow, my bad. But, no, eh! Hey, why is it Karuizawa?" p.11); Kei catches up with them ("We were supposed to go to school together..." p.10), pouting; the two of them casually use her first name in front of Sudō — he pieces it together ("We're dating." p.11); Sudō is the first outsider told; he whispers about Kei's "flashy" past (dating Hirata; junior-high rumours) as "too much of a hurdle for your first girlfriend" (p.12).
  - Chabashira's second-semester announcement (p.13–16): an October sports festival (similar to last year's; rules differ), a November cultural festival (the school's first — total-sales competition across all years; 5,000 second-year private points per student; 1st–4th place get 100 class points, 5th–8th get 50, 9th–12th unchanged; unused funds confiscated; Main Gate 1 stall = 10,000 points; conflicts settled by bidding war to the school; stall details secret until the day; information leaks penalized), plus midterm/final exams and a school trip.
  - Festival brainstorming (p.17–18): Hirata builds the orthodox event list on the monitor (food, haunted houses, mazes, cafes, live music, plays) — 195,000 points of budget for 39 students; Horikita proposes test-running ideas at personal expense with a planner reward paid from the 100-class-point windfall (390,000 private points/month split); absolute confidentiality required; Matsushita warns some students won't pay out of pocket.
  - The proposal contest (p.19–21): Horikita is swamped with proposals for the reward; Hondō's bare fried-chicken pitch is rejected (no substance); Satō, confident, walks into Horikita's seat with a winning idea and sets up a secret demonstration.
  - The maid-café audition (p.22–25): special-wing classroom, shaded windows, Maezono on guard; Satō + Matsushita + Mii-chan (Chinese-style maid) perform "Maimai, the maid café"; four days' preparation, 13,200 private points spent (3,300 each); 800-point tea; a clear-file three-page proposal (three rental quotes, expected headcount, capacity differences); Ayanokōji honestly praises it ("head and shoulders above any proposal I've seen so far" p.22); Horikita interrogates the budget (outfits 4,000 each = 40,000 for ten, snacks 50,000, ≈90,000 before decorations) and agrees to seriously review it pending cost reduction — her visible approval is a first for Ayanokōji.
  - Horikita drafts Ayanokōji (p.25–26): asks for his help with the girls' project; he protests (Ike/Hondō are better suited and "light on their feet" to leak); she hands him the boys'-side selection, budget management and people arrangement; he accepts ("I suppose so." p.26).
  - Chabashira's interest (p.26–27): runs into them at the special building; teases him about leading the maid café ("Ayanokōji leading a maid café...?" p.27; "Horikita makes some pretty interesting suggestions." p.27); asks him for a few minutes of his time next time he's free — reason not given.
- **New names/entities:** "Maimai" (maid café name on the whiteboard); Maezono (guard); the Main Gate 1 stall location (10,000 points); the 100-class-point windfall = 390,000 private points/month split formula.
- **Open threads:** why Chabashira wants his time (p.27); the relationship going public (it goes public in Ch.3, p.45–46); whether the maid-café bid wins the location; the secret relationship's fallout.
- **Unattributed lines:** the expository budget lines at p.17 ("The event will be held from 10 a.m...", "It's just a matter of budget...", "We have 39 students...") are printed untagged in Hirata's presenting sequence — recorded as notes, no speaker assigned (see hirata-yousuke.md). p.25's "I'm kind of surprised how well that went." is untagged (probably Maezono; adjacent narration only).
- **Translation errors, kept as printed:** none newly observed.

### Chapter Three (pp. 29–75)
- **Setting:** ANHS, the day before and the weeks leading into the Unanimous Voting Exam — homeroom, the walk-home, the classroom, his room, a first-year dormitory room; afterword material begins the same evening as the announcement (p.29 → evening calls).
- **Narrative beats:**
  - The announcement (p.29–44): Chabashira announces a surprise special exam for the second-years only, tomorrow — the "Unanimous Voting Exam": 5 questions, up to 4 options each; 39 students vote anonymously on tablets; not unanimous → 10 minutes free discussion → revote; 5 hours, 50 class points for clearing, −300 for failing; protection points temporarily disabled (expulsion unappealable except by paying 20,000,000 private points); binding agreements/money exchanges strictly forbidden; thorough school monitoring ("even people like Ryūen would be unlikely to make any overt moves"); phones collected during the exam.
  - The mechanics taught by example (p.32–42): Example 1 — "You lose 5 class points, but all your classmates gain 10,000 private points": round one 3 in favour / 36 opposed; second round 2 / 37 (Ike is curiously baffled). Example 2 — "1 million private points to one person in the class" (nomination removes the chosen student and their vote; majority nomination, then 38 others vote for/against; a unanimous "against" removes the candidate and re-picks; volunteering allowed first-come-first-served, one candidate per question): Ike is chosen for the demo; the class unanimously votes against giving it to him (0 for / 38 against); Sudō delivers the verdict. Example 3 — Keyaki Mall facility (4 options; restaurants/shops/recreation/medical: 20/4/15/0 first round, 23/2/14/0 second round); the school-vote rule: if this class is unanimous on one option and the other three classes are unanimous on another, the three-vote choice wins. Named unanimous win condition: 39 in favour or 39 against clears the question.
  - The practice votes (p.33–35): Ayanokōji deliberately votes 'for' both times to prevent an accidental unanimity and teach the class how the exam works; Sudō does the same; 2 in favour in round two. The pair of "who's still voting yes" lines printed untagged — attribution gap recorded (see sudou-ken.md).
  - The emergency leader (p.45–46): Kushida proposes a clear leader in case choices don't add up; Hirata backs her; Horikita accepts the role; Airi and Haruka mutter as Kei claims Kiyotaka in the lunchroom ("Sorry, but I'm his girlfriend and I'm his priority, you know?" p.46) — the relationship goes fully public in one stroke.
  - The fallout (p.48–55): the pair hits the whole school within hours; Ike and Shinohara's own coming-out is treated as expected; Haruka confronts Ayanokōji on the walk home — Airi has cried through lunch; Haruka agrees to talk to Airi about the maid café once she's more relaxed; she calls him out on underestimating the depth of Airi's feelings.
  - Horikita's consultation (p.56–61): she catches him at the dormitory elevator and visits his room (Room 401) — asks about the relationship (he's evasive), then asks for exam advice; proposes the pre-agreed-leader strategy; rejects Ryūen-style coercion because the vote is anonymous; warns him the class is not united (Kushida will "lie about everything"; Kōenji might vote his own way); he says he'll say it when she's ready to hear it; she exits after writing a mysterious long message to someone.
  - The pre-exam war council (p.62–65): Ayanokōji gathers Yōsuke and Kei in his room; rice-or-bread debate; academic-versus-physical question; he assigns them shadow-support roles for Horikita (secret cues, pre-agreed signs); tells Yōsuke that if less than 2 hours remain and the exam isn't clearing, "you might have to resort to forceful measures".
  - The Chairman's call (p.66–69): shortly after 10 p.m., Chairman Sakayanagi reveals government officials and their families will attend the sports and cultural festivals — his father may be involved; asks him to sit out the sports festival on sick leave while trusted observers from the school watch him; Ayanokōji asks for a week; Sakayanagi gives it.
  - The conspiracy (p.70–75): Kushida visits Yagami, a first-year, in the first-year dormitory; he has a recording of Chabashira's briefing; pressure-recipe for crushing Horikita and Ayanokōji — he expects the exam to hold a question that divides the class and wants her to guide the conversation; he grips her shoulders; she walks out determined.
- **New names/entities:** the "Unanimous Voting Exam" rules; "the specific student"; protection points disabled; Sakayanagi–Ayanokōji father angle; Yagami's recording; the November cultural festival guests (government officials, unannounced).
- **Attribution gaps:** the p.35 call-and-response lines are untagged (recorded in sudou-ken.md); p.38's "What? Does that mean my chances of getting them are now zero?" is untagged; p.17's expository lines are untagged (see Ch.2 notes).
- **Translation errors, kept as printed:** p.37 "No ones gonna give you a million private points." (doubled-letter typo); p.62's drop-cap pages; p.29 "the word \u201cspecial exam\u201d probably didn't come to mind" style keeps as printed; stray watermarks stripped by tooling. Translator credit differs within this volume: pp.1–28 "PrinceYG#6558", pp.29+ "Rhymar#7433".
