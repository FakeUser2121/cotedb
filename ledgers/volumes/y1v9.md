### Prologue: The Monologue of Ichinose Honami (pp. 6–11)
- **Setting:** Ichinose Honami’s first-person retrospective; a one-on-one interview in the student council room on a day off, after she found the letter calling her a criminal.
- **Narrative beats:**
  - p.6: Ichinose never saw herself as good or bad; comfortable elementary and middle school; by third year of middle school she was student council president and offered a private high school scholarship; then one unforgivable mistake — her mother collapsed from illness and her little sister was hurt and left a shell of herself.
  - p.7: She wasted half her third year as a shut-in for half a year; learning about this school made her resolve to put an end to that and bring back their smiles; she swore an oath to face her own answer; at school she found a letter and froze — “‘Ichinose Honami is a criminal.’” Classmates turned to her with curious looks; the words did not change however many times she read them; cut to the student council room interview with Vice President Nagumo.
  - p.8: President Horikita had interviewed Ichinose and rejected her student-council application; Nagumo approached her for three reasons — Class B like himself, outstanding academic ability, and extremely good-looking (the last is what he chiefly seeks); the important question is whether she is worth keeping at his side as personal property; he cites her middle-school presidency and her entrance-exam results via Hoshinomiya-sensei; she accepts the praise but cannot relax.
  - p.9: Nagumo lies that Horikita rejected her out of hand for not being Class A and that the man obsesses over status; the narration states Horikita assesses talent from D or A alike; Ichinose asks whether she must promote to Class A; Nagumo says even promotion may not gain Horikita’s acknowledgment and that she was never considered up to par from enrollment; her smile vanishes; she stammers that he is also Class B yet vice president.
  - p.10: Nagumo would love her as personal property; he claims he confessed the full truth behind his own Class B assignment when applying to the council and that it resulted in his position; he refuses to answer what that confession was; he presses why a student with her grades, interpersonal skills and middle-school presidency is in Class B; Ichinose cannot hide her agitation; the hypothesis is built from information he obtained from her homeroom teacher.
  - p.11: “Someone who is unable to grab hold of this opportunity has no place in the student council.”; Ichinose harbors a secret — half a year shut in her room floods back; “What I say here-” / “It won’t leave this room, of course. Your secret is our secret.”; she had held the past without telling a soul; because she lost the trust of others she needs to trust in others; she opens up about everything — her own ‘mistake’.
