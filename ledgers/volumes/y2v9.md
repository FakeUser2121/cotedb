### Chapter 1: Signs of Momentum (pp. 8–43)
- **Setting:** School; student council room; late November/December, end of the second semester.
- **Narrative beats:** Nagumo summons Ayanokōji and proposes an election for the next student council president between Horikita and Ichinose; Ayanokōji and Nagumo secretly bet — expulsion vs 20 million points — on who will become president. Chabashira announces the last special exam of the semester: a cooperative 100-question written test, Class B vs Class A, winner takes 50 class points; points are scored inversely to OAA academic rating, with time bonuses and random unanswerable questions for absences. Kiryūin bursts in and exposes that a third-year, Yamanaka, tried to frame her for shoplifting on Nagumo's order; Nagumo refuses to acknowledge it. The "criminal" remark shatters Ichinose, who withdraws from the election and resigns from the student council, leaving Horikita unopposed. Nagumo tasks her (and Ayanokōji) with recruiting one second-year and one first-year member, then hands over the presidency.
- **About these pages:** exam setup and the student-council succession.
- **New names:** Yamanaka Ikuko (Class 3-D); Anazai (Class D).
- **New rules and facts:** cooperative written test rules (one student at a time, max 5 questions/min 2, 10-minute limit, no clues, points inverse to OAA rating A=1…E=5, 10 pts/hour remaining, 2 unanswerable questions per absence); Class A 1250 pts vs Class B 985 pts.
- **Unattributed lines:** the "Was it Hamaguchi who was supposed to join us later?" question in Ch.3 is left untagged in the translation.
- **Translation errors, kept as printed:** none observed.

### Chapter Two: New Student Council Member (pp. 44–78)
- **Setting:** School corridors; first-year building; Keyaki Mall café; student council room.
- **Narrative beats:** Horikita recruits members — Kushida (her own class) and Nanase (Class 1-D) — while Ayanokōji tags along; she approaches the first-year Ishigami (Class A's strategist) but he bluntly declines. At the café study group Sudō appears in reading glasses and explains his matured attitude to studying, impressing Shinohara and Ike. Horikita, Kushida and Nanase are formally sworn in; Nagumo gives a wistful farewell, proud of the OAA and transfer-ticket system he introduced. Afterwards Kiryūin stops Ayanokōji and pressures him into investigating the shoplifting case in her place.
- **About these pages:** student-council recruitment and the shoplifting-investigation handoff.
- **New names:** the first-year Ishigami (distinct id ishigami-kyo-1a).
- **New rules and facts:** student-council eligibility (B+ academics, no club).
- **Unattributed lines:** none.
- **Translation errors, kept as printed:** none observed.

### Chapter Three: How to Spend Time with Ichinose's Classmates (pp. 79–117)
- **Setting:** Keyaki Mall and karaoke room; Saturday afternoon; night street.
- **Narrative beats:** Kanzaki calls Ayanokōji to a secret karaoke meeting with Himeno, Watanabe and Amikura; they worry Ichinose has lost the will to reach Class A and plot to save her class, recruiting Hamaguchi and swearing secrecy. Watanabe confesses to Ayanokōji that he likes Amikura and asks him to probe Ichinose. Ayanokōji calls Ichinose to set up a date. ### Chapter Five: The Approaching Special Exam (pp. 141–176)
- **Setting:** School classroom/cafeteria; Keyaki Mall café.
- **Narrative beats:** The class grinds through daily lunch study sessions — Sonoda is exhausted, Shinohara newly motivated, Sudō and Ike encouraged by Hirata. Ryūen and Ishizaki barge in to taunt Ichinose about quitting the council, but she now endures it calmly. At a café Sakayanagi explains to Hashimoto and Kamuro why she won't copy Ryūen's sabotage, and tests Kaneda's loyalty. Ayanokōji investigates the shoplifting case: a grieving Asahina reveals Nagumo's secret contract with the lower third-year classes and helps him contact Yamanaka; Tachibana Kento, an intermediary, confesses his role but refuses to be recorded. Ichinose visits Ayanokōji's room, declares her broken resolve restored, and leaves telling him to make up with Kei.
- **About these pages:** exam preparation and the shoplifting investigation.
- **New names:** Tachibana Kento (Class 3-D, distinct from tachibana-akane).
- **New rules and facts:** Nagumo's third-year contract — transfer 75% of monthly private points, obey his instructions, earn tickets by recognized points, funds due the day before class finalization, tickets revocable, final lottery.
- **Unattributed lines:** none.

- **Setting:** School exam rooms; student council room; school entrance.
- **Narrative beats:** On exam day Horikita places the unpredictable Kōenji last and uses hand signs to direct which question each student solves; Class B wins on total points (72% correct vs Class A's 86%). In the council hearing into Kiryūin's complaint, Kushida supplies testimony, Horikita reasons that the accusers feared someone more than Nagumo, and Kiriyama confesses he framed Kiryūin — deliberately choosing her — using Nagumo's job message as cover. Horikita tells Ayanokōji to make up with Kei. At the entrance Sakayanagi congratulates Ayanokōji and Ichinose, then warns Ichinose that idolising Ayanokōji will bring painful repercussions.
- **About these pages:** the exam, the hearing, and Sakayanagi's ultimatum.
- **New names:** none.
