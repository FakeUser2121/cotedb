### Prologue: The Monologue of Nanase Tsubasa (pp. 7–8)
- **Setting:** Nanase's first-person interior monologue — an evening-lit old apartment, a long shadow swinging like a pendulum.
- **Narrative beats:** Nanase recalls the shock and the cruel reality that presented itself without warning — the warm hand, the gentle smile, the sincere gaze, the blank expression — and rejects the “justice” others fight under. She resolves that she, with her own fragile heart, will fight: to defeat Ayanokōji Kiyotaka for real, to send him back to the place he is meant to be, because otherwise there will surely be a second or even third victim. Her true objective lies far beyond defeating him. The island is where her time comes: “It's right here. Right now.”

### Chapter 1: Different Strategies Amidst Different People (pp. 9–42)
- **Setting:** Onboard the Saint Venus (12-story luxury liner, third-largest Japanese-registered ship, 700+ capacity; movie theater, pool, gym, cafes, restaurants, panoramic bath, game corner, clinic/sickbay); then the movie-theater briefing.
- **Narrative beats:** July 20th; the ship approaches the island under nearly 40°C heat; Hirata Yōsuke clenches the railing and steels himself for unavoidable class casualties; he nods once when Ayanokōji tests his resolve. July 19th (flashback-in-scene, 12:36 PM): Ayanokōji meets Karuizawa Kei on the aft deck; their hidden-relationship rendezvous doubles as an exam recruitment; she leaves alone to avoid suspicion. At the 1:00 PM movie-theater briefing (no assigned seating), Mashima-sensei explains the island exam: groups of up to three (expandable to six via day-4 Tasks, 20–30% of groups), the five lowest groups face expulsion nullifiable by points (6M solo / 2M per member), class-point deduction for the bottom three, points decide rankings; a fully retired group is disqualified with points voided. The “Basic Movement” rule: four interval changes a day (three on first/last), 7–9/9–11/13–15/15–17, areas within 2 lateral/1 diagonal cells three times per day plus one random shift daily (never twice in a row); the first three groups to an area earn Early Bird 10/5/3 points, everyone arriving earns 1-point Arrival Bonus, ≥3 consecutive missed areas cost escalating points; unreachable ocean cells B1, C1, F10, G10; single member arriving saves the group, but Early Bird needs all members (last arrival ranks the bonus). Advance-warning watches: a staff-fastened wristwatch monitors body temperature, heart rate, blood pressure, blood oxygen, sleep, stress; Warning Alert (5s) repeats after 10 min, then Emergency Alert (continuous; 24-hour checkup or retirement; >5 min triggers a GPS-guided medical dispatch); forced removal stops the point tally; damage disables scoring until swapped at the start. Twelve “Tables” pre-program each watch's area sequence (Table A: D8, D7, C6 vs Table B example D10, E9, F8); linked members take the main group's table. Tasks: appearance 7:00–17:00 as tablet red dots (Math Test 5/3/1 + provisions; Shot Put 10/5/3 + prize; Fishing 15 pts; categories 40% Academic / 30% Physical / 30% Other; staff on standby for registration; standings top/bottom-ten plus own group visible days 4–12; GPS search unlocks day 6 at 1 point per search); linking averages points (Main Link / Pair Link, ~ten seconds to approve, cancelable). Tsukishiro (Acting Director) cautions on sexual misconduct (expulsion + police) and openly refuses Mashima's retraction demand on his “look the other way” line for disputes — tension with Mashima is visible; the warning reads as a challenge aimed at Ayanokōji. Mashima wheels out a provisioned cart: 5,000 base points (+2,500 Head Start), purchases until 6:00 AM, island-shop double price, in-bottle purifier 4,000 points (~150L), tents 1,000/1,500/2,500 (boys and girls may not share), GPS search and tablets overview; an exhibition room runs until midnight. At the exhibition, Ibuki (2-B, stuck beside AK after Kitō takes her escape seat) finally pushes into the crowd; Kitō flips the manual; Ishizaki (2-B) draws a wail out of everyone with the biggest backpack and an obviously flawed water-hauling plan; Ayanokōji re-stacks every rule before choosing provisions.
