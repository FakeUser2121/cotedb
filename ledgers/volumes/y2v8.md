### Prologue: Kanzaki Ryūji's Monologue (pp. 9–10)
- **Setting:** Kanzaki Ryūji's monologue; his childhood; Advanced Nurturing High School entrance.
- **Narrative beats:** Kanzaki recalls watching a group of children denounce a liar (an autograph-story fake) while an accomplice shielded him "simply because he was his friend"; an adult's hand on his head delivered the line he still remembers: "If you don't have the power to help, you can run away or ignore it.
