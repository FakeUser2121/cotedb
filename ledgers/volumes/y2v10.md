### Chapter Two: Survival and Elimination Special Exam (pp. 31–59)
- **Setting:** School; dorm.
- **Narrative beats:** Thursday of the third term; Chabashira-sensei unveils the [Survival and Elimination Special Exam] — second-years only; attack/defense rotation A→B→C→D clockwise this half (counterclockwise next half), 10+10 of 20 turns; 16 categories with 3 difficulty levels (extra levels cost accumulated points); nominations of five students per side per three minutes; the leader protects up to five per task; three misses = elimination with a point deducted per person; the leader never fights and cannot be eliminated; a bottom-ranked class with eliminations faces expulsion; rewards 1st +100, 2nd/3rd −50, 4th −100 (extension on ties; −100 all on all-tie); anti-collusion rule (Ryūen–Katsuragi deserted-island contract cited as the exception); phones permitted except during the defensive solve; next Friday; leader due by Monday or randomly assigned — "Do your best not to place last. That’s it."
