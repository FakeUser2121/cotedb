### Depth review — prologue & full volume (pp. 12–256)
- **Setting (prologue):** Seaside at sunset; Hiyori alone, looking out over the ocean after the special exam's "final phase" is announced.
- **Narrative beats (prologue):** In her own monologue Hiyori admits she never said "I like you" to Ayanokōji because they are now in different classes and enemies; she resolves to defeat him in this exam so Class D can still reach Class A, apologises to both Ryūen and Ayanokōji, and vows to keep watching until the whistle. The chapter frames the exam as "a path to survival and a path to death at the same time."
- **Setting (Ch.1):** Cruise ship crossing to the same uninhabited island used a year earlier; late June.
- **Narrative beats (Ch.1, pp.14–45):** Survival Game rules laid out — four classes, each with VIPs and guards, GPS watches with five-minute updates, designated areas, out-of-bounds zones that expand over the week, 6 PM–9 AM outside exam hours when movement is free but must restart from the 6 PM location, and a penalty where a wiped-out class must select a student for expulsion. Class C travels under Ayanokōji as leader; Morishita proposes that Hashimoto be the sacrifice if penalties fall. Ayanokōji deliberately delays teaching weapon handling so the other classes move away first; on the second morning Ryūen's Class B launches a surprise attack on the stationary Class C.
- **Setting (Ch.2–3):** Island forest and Class C camp (areas around D9–G11).
- **Narrative beats (Ch.2, pp.46–85):** Kitō is hit in the surprise attack and shaken; Hashimoto is unnerved by how calmly Ayanokōji takes the reversal and pledges to follow his orders. Ayanokōji teaches the class to handle the paintball rifles; Kōenji, acting alone, intercepts a Class B supply group near Morofuji and eliminates all eight guards in under thirty seconds, then lets Morofuji take the supplies and leave with a warning. Class C withdraws to area H12.
- **Narrative beats (Ch.3, pp.86–118):** Shiraishi, acting as Class C's recon commander over the radio, expresses total confidence in Ayanokōji despite the failed prediction; he notes this diverges from the impression he had of her and resolves to use that trust. Ayanokōji's underlying goal is revealed: the surprise attack was allowed so that Class C, cornered, would have no choice but to request an alliance with Ichinose's Class D.
- **Narrative beats (Ch.4, pp.119–150):** Ayanokōji identifies Class A's three VIPs by attacking the class at night and watching which students the guards rush to protect: Wang Mei-Yu, Satō Maya and Yukimura Teruhiko. He passes the names to Class B (an eliminated student can still talk) to catalyse an attack on Class A.
- **Narrative beats (Ch.5, pp.151–166):** Camp life on the second night; Ayanokōji wins white rice in rock-paper-scissors; Shibata approaches him about the Class B alliance; Ayanokōji maintains a defensive strategy, waiting for the shrinking play area to force a general engagement.
- **Narrative beats (Ch.6, pp.167–196):** Restricted area expands to a nine-by-nine grid (D4–L12); Ayanokōji and Shiraishi direct Class C to keep distance and avoid engagement while Class A and B exhaust themselves.
- **Narrative beats (Ch.7, pp.197–232):** Final day. Class A loses all three VIPs and is eliminated. Hirata and Hashimoto fight a close-range duel in the forest; Hashimoto is eliminated, then Hirata — consumed by killing intent over Ayanokōji's transfer — raises his gun at the already-out Hashimoto but stops before pulling the trigger. Ayanokōji reveals he fed Class B the VIP names and that the whole engagement served the C–D alliance. With Class A out, he heads for F11 to "adjust."
- **Narrative beats (Epilogue, pp.233–252):** Class B's VIP is Yamashita. Ayanokōji hands spare magazines to Kanzaki and prepares to bluff Ryuuen. On the beach he burns his ammunition against Ibuki and Ryuuen, eliminates Ibuki with a paintball to the thigh, then — out of ammo and apparently surrendered — throws a deliberately damaged paintball that bursts on Ryuuen's abdomen, eliminating both. He explains the true goal: forcing Class C into the C–D alliance, which is now declared. Final standings: Class D first, Class C second, Class B third, Class A last. Mashima-sensei then announces a *new* uninhabited-island exam built on "trust and betrayal."
- **Page-map note:** Blank or near-empty pages: 132 (short), 225, 243, 255, 257. Chapter 7 ends p.232; Epilogue begins p.233; Author Afterword p.253; translator material pp.255–256.
- **New named entities:** none beyond those already recorded (all major names appear in earlier volumes).
- **New system facts:** Survival-game rules (VIP/guard roles, GPS updates, out-of-bounds expansion, expulsion penalty, personal GPS jammers, weapon/ammunition rules) recorded in Ch.1. Hand-to-hand rule note: taking a dropped weapon is prohibited (p.244). Damaged paintball thrown by hand is not prohibited (p.248).
- **Open threads:** The new "trust and betrayal" exam announced at the end; Hiyori's unspoken feelings; Hirata's unchecked aggression; Tsukishiro's independent enforcer plot (continues from y3v2).
- **Attribution gaps:** Hirata's internal state in the Ch.7 duel is given only through Hashimoto's observations (the narration stays in Class C's viewpoint); no Hirata line is quoted in that duel, so no `[says:hirata-yousuke]` row is claimed.
- **Coverage note:** Page-locator cross-check for roster characters listed for this volume is recorded in each character's volume block; passing mentions are marked explicitly there.

### Ch.1: Opening. Survival Game Special Exam. (pp. 14–45) — depth read
- **Setting:** A cruise ship crossing to the same uninhabited island as last year; the beach and assembly area.
- **Beats:**
  - pp.14–17: Third-years arrive on the same island; Nakanishi (Class D) is absent with a fever. New gym clothes with sensors are distributed; all belongings/phones left on ship.
  - pp.18–28: Rules explained via rulebook: 3 nights/4 days, paint guns, five roles (Commander ×1, VIP ×3, Guard unlimited, Analyst ×2, Scout ×1), GPS updates every 5 minutes during 9–6, tactics (Global/Individual GPS shutdown, Identity Check), total points (VIP 100 each + Guard 1 each), rewards (+150 to −150), and the annihilation penalty — first class wiped out must select one student for expulsion. New prototype paintball guns and sensor watches explained by Kishinami.
  - pp.29–32: Starting positions drawn by lot: Class C (Ayanokōji) at E12, Ryūen's B at C12, Horikita's A at G12, Ichinose's D at I12. Initial food is meager (two supplement blocks + water each), forcing reliance on event supplies. Class C debates strategy — Sugio wants to cross the mountain to monopolize northeast events; Shimazaki objects.
- **Open threads:** The expulsion penalty; the food shortage forcing combat; Class C's strategy.
- **Attribution gap:** none.

### Ch.2: A Surprise Attack (pp. 46–85) — depth read
- **Setting:** The uninhabited island, moving from E12 north through E11/F12; the forest where the ambush occurs.
- **Beats (pp.46–68):**
  - pp.46–50: Class C advances north toward G8; commanders revealed (Matsushita A, Kaneda B, Ichinose D). Reports via VIPs show all classes initially heading away from each other. Ayanokōji refuses to quicken pace (the 5-min GPS update would reveal it).
  - pp.51–60: Classmates urge learning to handle weapons first; Ayanokōji agrees to let Class A go ahead. He assigns a formation with VIPs spread across front/middle/rear (Shiraishi front, Takemoto middle, Nishikawa rear), and has a guard rotate a (magazine-removed) weapon to each VIP as misdirection so enemies can't identify VIPs by lack of a gun.
  - pp.64–68: Ayanokōji reveals the alliance plan — aim for second place and give first to Ichinose's Class D in exchange for full cooperation. Classmates (Matoba, Motodoi, Morishita) object to throwing away first place; he postpones the decision until after the exam.
  - pp.68–74: A surprise attack hits from the rear — Ryūen's Class B used the one-time Global GPS Shutdown to approach invisibly. Kitou counterattacks from cover, taking out an enemy and allowing the front line to retreat. Class C flees to F12.
  - pp.72–74: Ayanokōji explains the tactic (fake retreat + GPS shutdown + commander directing the charge); admires Ryūen's decisiveness. He stops so lagging students (Morishita, the girls) can catch up and rehydrate.
- **Open threads:** The alliance vote deferred; Class B's next move.
- **Attribution gap:** none.

### Ch.3: The Unseen Pressure (pp. 86–118) — depth read
- **Setting:** Ryūen's Class B camp; the forest patrol; Class A's route north; Class C's base near F12–G12; the tents at night.
- **Beats (pp.86–90):** From Ryūen's POV — he explains the surprise attack was a deliberate counter to Ayanokōji (strike before he can think). Katsuragi reports 15 Class C casualties (including Kitou and VIP Nishikawa), 3 Class B losses. Ryūen orders a 10-person search party (with VIP Yamashita) to avoid revealing the VIP via Identify Person, and calls off the pursuit — the key target (Ayanokōji) survived, and being overtly cautious of him is wise. He gains the psychological proof that Ayanokōji is not infallible.
- **Beats (pp.91–95):** First event locations announced; Class C targets G13 (ammo) and D14 (necessities), postponing food. Ayanokōji reassures the anxious Yamamura that simply surviving longer is useful. Class A (Horikita) observes the ambush result with disbelief — she doesn't want to acknowledge Ryūen got the better of Ayanokōji — and keeps moving north, feeling an "invisible pressure" while he remains.
- **Beats (pp.100–107):** Classes gather supplies; Class C boxed in by Class B at G9–G10 ("stalkers"), collects only 5 sets by day's end. Ayanokōji decides to go alone to I10 for food, sending Takemoto/Hashimoto to D12, with rendezvous points F12 (primary) and H12 (secondary). He argues against Matoba's group-up plan (it would just let other classes ignore them and gather supplies freely).
- **Beats (pp.108–118):** Class C sets up camp; Ayanokōji explains small-portion eating. He tells Hashimoto he actually enjoys the island exam for the chance to see new expressions, though he intends to win. Hashimoto cheers up the gloomy class. In the tent at night, Hashimoto tells a funny middle-school bike story; Ayanokōji slips out.
- **Open threads:** Class B's continued harassment; food scarcity; the "invisible pressure" Ayanokōji exerts.
- **Attribution gap:** none.

### Ch.4: A Chance Encounter (pp. 119–150) — depth read
- **Setting:** Ryūen's Class B camp at dawn; Class C base; Class D's area; the E9 supply point; the G11 approach.
- **Beats (pp.119–122):** Ryūen wakes early deliberating whom to attack; rules out Class A/D, focuses on Class C. Katsuragi advises staying defensive (time/hunger will force Class C's hand) and turning attention to Class A. Ryūen is obsessed with Ayanokōji but agrees there's no need to attack yet.
- **Beats (pp.123–125):** Day 2 begins; GPS updates resume; Kōenji is still alive alone in D6. Ayanokōji finds this "creepy" (why hasn't he retired?) but decides to keep monitoring him.
- **Beats (pp.126–127):** At 11:00, Class D sees all outer squares marked as restricted — the map will shrink inward each event, forcing confrontation. Kanzaki realizes fighting is unavoidable; Ichinose tells him not to push for O14.
- **Beats (pp.128–134):** Kōenji ambushes Class B's E9 supply group (led by Komiya with VIP Morofuji), taking out six guards in under 30 seconds with unseen precise shots. He appears, spares Morofuji, lets her take the supplies, and warns them off — bored, doing it for novelty.
- **Beats (pp.135–150):** Class C sees the perimeter restriction too; Ayanokōji deduces the endgame will be a chaotic firefight and decides to go on the offensive for G11. He twice has the class wait five minutes before advancing, saving tactics and using the enemy's need to stay vigilant — knowing the outcome already.
- **Open threads:** The shrinking map; the coming G11 fight; Kōenji as a wild card.
- **Attribution gap:** none.

### Ch.5: The Alliance (pp. 151–166) — depth read
- **Setting:** Class C's retreat east; the L13/M13 border; Class D's standby point; the combined C–D campsite; evening campfire.
- **Beats (pp.151–154):** The 1:00 event adds supplies without shrinking the map; 3:00 shrinks another ring. Class A converges on center; Class D returns toward center. Class C's class accepts the alliance pragmatically; Hashimoto remains privately doubtful. Ayanokōji explains Ichinose can be trusted and that betrayal would collapse Class D. He and Hashimoto go unarmed to talk.
- **Beats (pp.155–157):** Kanzaki and ten Class D guards form a wall with guns; a tense negotiation about GPS jamming tactics. Ayanokōji argues that taking second with an alliance is better than a doomed last-place fight. Kanzaki, already briefed by Ichinose, lowers his gun — Ichinose had warned Class D not to attack first.
- **Beats (pp.158–159):** Flashback — Ichinose watched the G11 battle, saw Ayanokōji's "uncharacteristic carelessness," and instructed Kobashi/Kanzaki to accept Class C without attacking, believing "Ayanokouji-kun won't lose."
- **Beats (pp.160–166):** The alliance forms; Class D treats Class C as equals, lowering their guard. Shiraishi is impressed by Class D's charm. Ayanokōji cooks white rice; Shibata tries to greet him but is cut off by Kanzaki. Kanzaki questions why Ayanokōji proposed the "unprofitable" alliance; Ayanokōji frames it as insurance and to divide Horikita/Ryūen's attention, and tells Kanzaki to understand Class D's trust and visit the gym to know Ichinose.
- **Open threads:** Kanzaki's lingering doubts; the top two classes' response to the C–D alliance.
- **Attribution gap:** none.

### Ch.6: The Real Goal (pp. 167–196) — depth read
- **Setting:** Class B beach camp at E12; the shoreline; the C–D alliance base at K13/H10; the shrinking play area.
- **Beats (pp.167–173):** Ryūen's Class B assesses the C–D alliance (now ~50 people vs their ~20 after Kōenji took out nine). Katsuragi analyzes that Ayanokōji likely orchestrated the timing of Kōenji's clash with Komiya's group. They reject allying with Class A. Ryūen deliberates alone on the beach, then speaks with Shiina — he declares he'll win "with my own damn power." She uses a novel-ending metaphor to urge mental preparation for any outcome and pledges to follow him even if he forces a cruel choice.
- **Beats (pp.174–181):** At the alliance base, supplies run low and some students (Sumida, Minamikata) feel unwell. Ayanokōji explains his strategic reasoning to Kanzaki/Hashimoto — Ryūen attacked Class C because Ayanokōji is his arch-rival — and chooses a defensive strategy, waiting for the shrinking area to force a general firefight. The area shrinks to 9×9 (D4–L12), then shifts southwest.
- **Beats (pp.182–196):** Ninomiya falls ill with a fever and is told to retire; Ayanokōji suspects a cold spreading (aggravated by sharing food with Class C). The area shrinks again to 6×6 (D7–I12). Ayanokōji goes with Takemoto/Moriyama to retrieve G9 supplies, then instructs Takemoto to disable his personal GPS at the next update — he isn't returning; the plan for if he's "taken out" is in place. Class A arrives at G8, puzzled that Ryūen didn't intercept the supply group; they identify Morofuji as Class B's VIP.
- **Open threads:** Ayanokōji's solo plan (GPS disabled, not returning); the final-day confrontation; the spreading illness.
- **Attribution gap:** none.

### Ch.7: The Time of the Decisive Battle (pp. 197–232) — depth read
- **Setting:** Class A's camp at G8; the alliance base at H10; the forest battlefield; the shrinking final area (5×5 D8–H12, then 4 squares D11–E12).
- **Beats (pp.197–203):** Final day. Remaining: A (3 VIPs + 27), B (3 VIPs + 24), C (2 VIPs + 12), D (3 VIPs + 35). Ayanokōji had infiltrated Class A the previous evening after 6 PM, taking out three guards and provoking a rule-violation elimination — he camped beside them overnight. Horikita and Sudō discuss the attack; Class A is bloodthirsty but Horikita keeps a meter-wide path open for him to avoid a restraint penalty. At 9:00 Ayanokōji walks the path; she predicts he'll flee, not fight, and stops Sudō from chasing.
- **Beats (pp.204–216):** Ayanokōji returns to H10, having shaken Class A. He notes Class A's lack of tension (Horikita's Protection Point declaration). He sends two people toward Class B. The final-day restrictions are announced all at once (16 squares at 1 PM → four at 3 PM → E11/E12 at 3:30, expulsion for entering restricted zones after 3 PM).
- **Beats (pp.217–232):** The battle erupts. Sudō saves Satō (Class A's VIP, whom Class B targets — they'd learned the VIPs' identities from Ayanokōji's info the previous night). Ayanokōji had identified Class A's VIPs (Wang, Satō, Yukimura) by observing who the guards protected, and fed the names to Class B. The C–D alliance advances; Class A split its forces (some around back). Hashimoto duels Hirata in a tense close-range firefight and loses; Hirata raises his gun on the already-out Hashimoto but stops before firing. Ayanokōji explains his plan to sow chaos and escape the VIPs; he heads to F11 for "adjustments."
- **Open threads:** Class B's pressure at F11; the final standings.
- **Attribution gap:** none.


### Epilogue: The Final Showdown (pp. 233–252) — depth read
- **Setting:** The shrinking center of E11; the forest pursuit; the coverless sandy beach; the disembarkation beach.
- **Beats:**
  - pp.233–235: With Class A eliminated, Ayanokōji confirms Class B's VIP is Yamashita, hands his spare magazines to Kanzaki, and walks with Beppu to the center of E11 to bait a confrontation. He notes Class B and Class C are tied on 102 points and Class D leads on 201.
  - pp.236–240: Ryūen, Ibuki and Yamashita emerge. Ayanokōji burns his ~20 rounds against Ibuki's submachine-gun suppression, reads her predictable movements and eliminates her with a paintball to the thigh — then finds his rifle empty and flees.
  - pp.241–248: On the beach he surrenders with his hands raised. Ryūen, obsessive about a certain victory, refuses to shoot immediately and demands to see how Ayanokōji can escape. Ayanokōji lures him onto the coverless sand, bluffs that Yamamura is behind him, and — as Ryūen turns to check — throws a deliberately damaged paintball that bursts on Ryūen's abdomen, eliminating both.
  - pp.249–252: Ayanokōji reveals the true goal: he allowed the surprise attack so cornered Class C would be forced into an alliance with Class D, now declared. Final standings: Class D first, C second, B third, A last. On the beach he spots Hiyori talking with Kaneda. Mashima-sensei then announces a *new* exam built on trust and betrayal.
- **Open threads:** The new trust-and-betrayal exam; Ryūen's vow of no mercy from here on; Hiyori's unspoken feelings.
- **Attribution gap:** none. TL note on the phrasing of "I am from that side" in y3v2 carries over; no new defect here.
