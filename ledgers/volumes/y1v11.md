### Prologue: "The Monologue of Sakayanagi Arisu" (pp. 7–12)
- **Setting:** Sakayanagi Arisu’s recollection of visiting the White Room facility as a child with her father; ends in the present as she wakes from the memory.
- **Narrative beats:**
  - pp.7–9: Young Sakayanagi and her father observe children through one-way glass at the facility; she argues talent is set at birth and questions whether the experiment can create geniuses.
  - pp.10–11: She notices a boy (Ayanokōji Kiyotaka, the director’s son) excelling calmly and learns he has lived in the facility since birth; her father expresses pity for him.
  - p.12: Sakayanagi resolves to prove talent is not decided by education; in the present she wakes from the nostalgic dream, thinking of their coming confrontation.
- **About these pages:** monologue/flashback framed by a present-moment awakening on p.12.
- **New names:** none on roster beyond Sakayanagi Arisu and Ayanokōji Kiyotaka (her father appears unnamed in roster terms).
- **New rules and facts:** Sakayanagi visited the White Room as a child and saw young Ayanokōji Kiyotaka there (pp.7–12); she recalls resolving to play chess after seeing him.

- **Setting:** Staff room and school corridors, before the Class Vote special exam is announced.
- **Narrative beats:**
  - pp.13–20: Acting Director Tsukishiro explains the first-years’ final special exam and new Class Vote system to staff; Mashima objects to forced expulsions and the new system, but Tsukishiro insists and ends the meeting mentioning a possible future cultural festival.

- **New names:** none.
