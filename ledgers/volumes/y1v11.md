### Prologue: "The Monologue of Sakayanagi Arisu" (pp. 7–12)
- **Setting:** Sakayanagi Arisu’s recollection of visiting the White Room facility as a child with her father; ends in the present as she wakes from the memory.
- **Narrative beats:**
  - pp.7–9: Young Sakayanagi and her father observe children through one-way glass at the facility; she argues talent is set at birth and questions whether the experiment can create geniuses.
  - pp.10–11: She notices a boy (Ayanokōji Kiyotaka, the director’s son) excelling calmly and learns he has lived in the facility since birth; her father expresses pity for him.
  - p.12: Sakayanagi resolves to prove talent is not decided by education; in the present she wakes from the nostalgic dream, thinking of their coming confrontation.
- **About these pages:** monologue/flashback framed by a present-moment awakening on p.12.
- **New names:** none on roster beyond Sakayanagi Arisu and Ayanokōji Kiyotaka (her father appears unnamed in roster terms).
- **New rules and facts:** Sakayanagi visited the White Room as a child and saw young Ayanokōji Kiyotaka there (pp.7–12); she recalls resolving to play chess after seeing him.

### Chapter 2: "The First Years’ Final Battle" (pp. 27–54)
- **Setting:** Class C classroom on March 8.
- **Narrative beats:**
  - pp.27–29: Chabashira announces the final special exam, the Event Selection Exam, after Yamauchi’s expulsion.
  - pp.29–36: She explains the rules: each class proposes 10 events (with fair rules and tiebreakers), narrows to 5, and 7 are randomly selected on exam day; schedule runs through March 22.
  - pp.36–41: Commanders are required and can intervene in events but are expelled if their class loses; class matchups are decided by raffle after commanders are chosen; class points change by 30 per event plus 100 for the winning class.
  - pp.42–46: Horikita shares the event-rule handout; the class reviews participation restrictions and preparation needs while Hirata remains withdrawn.
  - pp.46–54: Sudō and Ike question Ayanokōji’s praise votes; Horikita explains Sakayanagi’s plan; Ayanokōji volunteers as commander, Shinohara objects, and Horikita supports Ayanokōji taking the role.
- **About these pages:** rule-heavy explanation chapter with class discussion.
- **New names:** none.
- **New rules and facts:** Event Selection Exam: 10 proposed events per class, 5 submitted, 7 randomly selected; commanders required, may intervene, expelled on class loss; ±30 class points per event and +100 to winning class (pp.29–41).
- **Unattributed lines:** none.
