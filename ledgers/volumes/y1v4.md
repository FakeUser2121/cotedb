### Chapter 1: Karuizawa Kei’s Soliloquy (pp. 10–11)
- **Setting:** No scene — first-person interior monologue, volume cold open. Karuizawa narrates alone, post-shower, in front of the mirror; no other character is present.
- **Narrative beats:**
  - Opens on stasis and retracts it in the next breath: “In the end, nothing changed even after I came to this school.” — “No, that’s wrong… Maybe I never had any intention of changing.” (p.10)
  - Claims she already knows herself completely — strengths and weaknesses — and that no one liking her no longer hurts; “Because for some reason, I want them to feel that way.” (p.10)
  - The mirror: vomiting at “that old wound on my side” ties her self-loathing to an unnamed past trauma; “My life was destroyed because of that nightmarish day. I lost my youth, my friends, and even myself.” (p.10)
  - Closing thesis puts self-preservation above youth and friends — “The most important thing is protecting myself. I’ll do whatever it takes.” — under the self-assessment “I’m a parasite, a weak creature that can’t survive on its own.” (p.10)
  - Third straight non-Ayanokōji cold-open soliloquy (Sakura in y1v2, Chabashira in y1v3, Karuizawa here).
- **About these pages:** Page 11 carries no prose (blank page, page number only). Page 10 keeps a duplicated verb as printed: “Why did I have to have to go through”.
- **New names:** none — the page names no one.
- **Unattributed lines:** none — first-person monologue with no dialogue.

### Chapter 2: The Summer Group Test (pp. 12–62)
- **Setting:** The cruise-ship respite three days after the island test — third-deck cabins, the top-deck restaurant, second-deck corridors and Mashima’s Room 204 — ending at the second deck’s 20:40 gathering place on the evening the school’s briefings land.
- **Narrative beats:**
  - Cabin lunch: Hirata recruits Ayanokōji as the bridge to Horikita over Karuizawa’s cancelled plan; she crashes the deck table and reclaims him (pp.13–20).
  - Kōenji’s wet corridor and the bellhop’s fourth warning; Sakura’s roommate email and flight at Kushida’s arrival (pp.21–28).
  - The school message staggers the briefings — Ayanokōji at 18:00, Kushida at 20:40 — and the Horikita chat compares slots (pp.29–31).
  - Mashima’s Room 204 briefing: the Rabbit group, the fourteen-name roster, the VIP handout with four outcomes, the Werewolf detour, the traitor rules, the prohibited-actions list, and the meeting logistics (pp.32–49).
  - The hallway split: Yukimura’s huddle collapses when Karuizawa phones Hirata and the Professor declines for Love Love Alive; Ayanokōji reports to Horikita by chat (pp.50–51).
  - Hirata wakes Ayanokōji and voices the doubt that the scattered briefings are themselves the test (pp.52–53).
  - The 20:40 gathering: Katsuragi pivots from ignoring Horikita to warning her, Kanzaki pulls him off, Ryūen prowls with his lackeys, Kushida arrives puzzled, and Horikita exits cold (pp.54–61).
