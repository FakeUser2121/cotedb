[says] (p.205) "I see. Well, you do seem to be telling the truth about witnessing the incident. That much I simply must accept. However, I can’t ascertain how the situation started from this picture. This doesn’t prove that you saw the entire incident."
[says] (p.205) "So, what do you think, Chabashira-sensei? Why not look for a compromise here?"
[says] (p.205) "I’m convinced that Sudou-kun lied in his testimony."
[says] (p.205) "No matter how long we go back and forth, we’ll never reach an agreement. We won’t change our testimony, and your side won’t give up or admit that you conspired with the witness. Therefore, I suggest we compromise. I do think that the students from Class C are responsible for some of the blame here. There were three students up against Sudou, and one of them has a history of fighting, which is a problem. So how about two weeks of suspension for Sudou-kun, and one week of suspension for my students? What do you think of that? [says] (p.205) "Chabashira-sensei. What do you think?"
[trait] (p.205) The proposal is a settlement that still assigns Sudō the larger share; the narrator calls it "asking for less than half" of a month and therefore "a considerable concession" for Class C.
