[action] (p.20) Ignores the wobbling elderly woman standing near him. When Kushida approaches and politely asks him to give up his seat, he smirks and rebuffs her without shifting an inch.

[action] (p.21) Flashes a pointlessly vigorous smile, slides his earphones in, and begins listening to cacophonous music; his smug, self-important attitude remains fixed as the office lady clenches her teeth.

Unfortunately, I have no interest in contributing to society. I care only for my own satisfaction. Oh, and one more thing. You’re asking me, the one in the priority seat, to give up his spot, but couldn’t you simply ask one of the other people seated on this crowded bus?
