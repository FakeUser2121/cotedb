### y1v2 p.10 — Ch.1 "Sakura Airi's Soliloquy": the volume opens in her POV
[back] (p.10) Whole first-person monologue chapter — the only non-Ayanokōji POV chapter opening in y1v2; foreshadows her role as "An Unexpected Witness" (Ch.4).
[thinks] (p.10) "I don't know how to interact with other people. I'm bad at talking while making eye contact. I'm horrible with crowds. I don't remember how or when I became so hopeless."
[thinks] (p.10) "However, one thing I do know for certain is that a person cannot live entirely in isolation. No matter how much I may love my solitude, I can't remain totally by myself. So, I came up with a solution. I would adopt a false face and hide my true self. Then, I wouldn't be completely honest, but I could be a version of myself. I could continue to live in this dark, lonely world."
[trait] (p.10) Self-aware about her condition; frames the false face as a deliberate "solution," not an accident — same masking logic she later applies to photography.
[thinks] (p.10) "Is everyone else putting on a façade, just like me? Or do most people show their true selves to the outside world? Since I can't connect with people, I suppose there's no way for me to find out the answer. Therefore, I remain alone."
[thinks] (p.10) "I'm all right by myself. I'm all right with being alone."
[thinks] (p.10) "I… I want to connect with someone from the bottom of my heart."
[voice] (p.10) Her interior voice: short declarative confessions, self-questioning, closing cadence "And so I will continue to live quietly, with my eyes downcast. Alone."
[trait] (p.10) Paradox locked in at volume start: wants a heart-to-heart connection while believing connection is impossible for her — the engine of her y1v2 arc.

### y1v2 p.12–13 — Ch.2 opening: she stumbles on Sudou's three-on-one brawl (the witness birthed)
[scene] (p.12) Evening of June 30, inside a school building with a staircase (the "special building," named Sudou's account p.30–31). While hunting for a good selfie spot, Sakura finds three male students writhing on the floor and a red-haired boy standing over them.
[thinks] (p.12) "The timing couldn’t have been worse. While searching for a place to take a good selfie, I stumbled into something. Even a certain famous, diminutive detective would have held his breath when witnessing such a tense situation."
[thinks] (p.12) "His right fist was covered in blood from the students he’d pummeled. This was the first brawl I’d ever witnessed."
[feat] (p.12) Terror overrides etiquette but not habit: "Though I was terrified, I began capturing the scene with my camera. The shutter didn’t make a sound."
[thinks] (p.12) Panic freeze: "After taking the pictures, I asked myself what I was doing. I couldn’t think clearly in my panicked state... My legs didn’t obey my command to move, like I was paralyzed."
[thinks] (p.13) "I’d heard that that when someone was involved in a crisis, his or her body would lock up, exactly like what was happening now." (sic — doubled "that" in corpus)
[thinks] (p.13) "I’d hate it if someone were to spot me and involve me in this mess. If that happened, it would cast a dark cloud over my otherwise peaceful school life."
[scene] (p.13) Sudou, sensing movement, calls "Is someone there?" toward her hiding spot; she escapes by seconds: "I’d successfully escaped by a hair’s breadth. If I’d lagged a mere two seconds, he probably would’ve seen me."
[trait] (p.12–13) Document-first, confront-never instinct: she photographs the fight but flees rather than intervene or report; self-protection through invisibility.
[rel:sudou-ken] (p.12–13) Unseen witness to his brawl; nearly discovered by him — she does not know his name-to-face link yet beyond "Sudou-kun" heard in his taunts.
[scene] (p.20) §2.2 lunch, observed by Ayanokōji: still eating alone, hunched over a homemade bento; the glasses-and-plain-girl reputation ("At one time Ike had made a ruckus about how large her breasts were, but because she was considered plain, everyone quickly lost interest.").
