### y1v2 p.10 — Ch.1 "Sakura Airi's Soliloquy": the volume opens in her POV
[back] (p.10) Whole first-person monologue chapter — the only non-Ayanokōji POV chapter opening in y1v2; foreshadows her role as "An Unexpected Witness" (Ch.4).
[thinks] (p.10) "I don't know how to interact with other people. I'm bad at talking while making eye contact. I'm horrible with crowds. I don't remember how or when I became so hopeless."
[thinks] (p.10) "However, one thing I do know for certain is that a person cannot live entirely in isolation. No matter how much I may love my solitude, I can't remain totally by myself. So, I came up with a solution. I would adopt a false face and hide my true self. Then, I wouldn't be completely honest, but I could be a version of myself. I could continue to live in this dark, lonely world."
[trait] (p.10) Self-aware about her condition; frames the false face as a deliberate "solution," not an accident — same masking logic she later applies to photography.
[thinks] (p.10) "Is everyone else putting on a façade, just like me? Or do most people show their true selves to the outside world? Since I can't connect with people, I suppose there's no way for me to find out the answer. Therefore, I remain alone."
[thinks] (p.10) "I'm all right by myself. I'm all right with being alone."
[thinks] (p.10) "I… I want to connect with someone from the bottom of my heart."
[voice] (p.10) Her interior voice: short declarative confessions, self-questioning, closing cadence "And so I will continue to live quietly, with my eyes downcast. Alone."
[trait] (p.10) Paradox locked in at volume start: wants a heart-to-heart connection while believing connection is impossible for her — the engine of her y1v2 arc.

### y1v2 p.40–65 — Ch.3: the witness sits in the room and says nothing
[scene] (p.41) Chabashira puts the ask to the classroom: "If anyone here witnessed the fight, please raise your hand." — "No students raised their hands in response to her question."
[note] (p.41) This is inference, stated as inference: Sakura was in that homeroom (pp.40–43 is Class D's classroom, and she is a Class D student), but Chapter 3 never singles her out, never names her, and never says whether she looked up. All this page can assert is that no hand went up.
[scene] (p.42) Kushida re-asks the same question in a form a witness could answer privately — "If anyone knows someone who saw what happened, whether it’s someone in class, a friend, or an upperclassman, please tell me. You can contact me at any time." — the one line in the chapter that would have been safe for her to answer, and she does not.
[status] (p.40–65) She holds the only neutral visual record of pp.12–13 — silent-camera photographs she has not shown anyone — and Chapter 3 closes with it still hidden.
[limit] (p.41) The window that her silence consumes is fixed and public: "We’ll make our final judgment next Tuesday, taking into account any eyewitnesses and evidence," and "In order to check for witnesses, every teacher will inform their classes of the details of this incident." The school is now actively canvassing every class in the year for her.
[note] (p.62) AK, in a classroom with "about one third of our class remained in the room": "Suddenly, I felt a sensation, like someone was watching me. I turned around." The text never says who watched him and never resolves it inside the chapter. Do not read it as Sakura — it is an unanswered beat, not a link.
[trait] (p.40–65) Her Chapter 1 creed ("And so I will continue to live quietly, with my eyes downcast. Alone.") is what the whole chapter quietly costs Sudou: invisibility works, and it works on the class's behalf too.

### y1v2 p.77–82 — Ch.4 §4.1: she is named, and stays a rumour to the people who sit near her

[rel:horikita-suzune] (p.77) The only Class D student who ever looked at her closely is the one who does not like being looked at; Horikita's read of Sakura's downcast eyes is the chapter's hinge and Sakura never learns it happened.

[note] (p.77–82) All of this is Class D talking about her in a room she is not in. Chapter 4 gives her no line of her own until her own section later in the chapter; nothing here is her choice.
