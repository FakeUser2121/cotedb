### y0 p.49–54 — Sakayanagi Narimori weighs Atsuomi's proposal
[scene] (p.49) Receives Atsuomi in his family's Shirokane mansion; a slender, gentle man who feigns humility.
[says] (p.49) "I'm Sakayanagi. I've heard about you, Ayanokouji-sensei, several times from Naoe-sensei."
[says] (p.49) "Not at all. I was told you're a very capable person. Moreover, when I heard that we're the same age, I felt quite embarrassed."
[says] (p.49) "No, I still have a long way to go. It was just my father who was remarkable. That's really all there is to it."
[says] (p.50) "I see. You're not asking for investment money, then."
[says] (p.51) "You don't know whether I'm on Kijima-sensei's side, Naoe-sensei's side, or neutral. Yet, you're willing to share such information with me?"
[says] (p.51) "Many politicians will sooner or later join some faction. No matter which faction, once you decide to support someone in particular, you must believe in them to the end without any doubt."
[says] (p.51) "I respect my father, but at the same time, I see him as a goal."
[says] (p.52) "Indeed, hundreds of children are abandoned every year in Japan."
[says] (p.53) "However… even so, you're a Diet member, Ayanokouji-sensei. You're someone who should confront and fight with the country."
[says] (p.54) "Don't lie. Expose all of your thoughts and objectives."
[trait] (p.49–54) Polished, cautious and probing — tests Atsuomi's loyalties, refuses flattery, empathizes with abandoned children yet calls it the government's problem, and finally agrees to read the proposal after sensing his passion and conviction.
[rel:ayanokouji-kiyotaka] (p.49–54) Receives his pitch; agrees to act as liaison to the financial world.
[rel:naoe-sensei] (p.49–51) Holds a principle of fully trusting him; deliberately approached him to learn from a rival to Kijima.
[rel:sakayanagi-arisu] (p.51) His daughter; the "remarkable" father she refers to is him — see note.
[rel:kijima] (p.50–51) His father is close with Kijima, but he distances himself by learning from Naoe.
[note] (p.51) In saying his father was remarkable, he means his own father (Arisu's grandfather, the ANHS founder); Arisu refers to Narimori as her remarkable father. The family chain is: grandfather (ANHS founder) → Narimori → Arisu.
