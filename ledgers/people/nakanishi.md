### y2v12 p.91–98 — Ch.4: The panic in the discussion
[scene] (p.91) Named in the first-discussion participant list for Class D — one of the seven D participants called into the opening discussion of the exam.
[scene] (p.93) Yukimura names him first; “Why me all of a sudden? Why would I be an honor student?”
[says] (p.93) “Why me all of a sudden? Why would I be an honor student?”
[scene] (p.95–96) Under Yukimura’s relentless remarks directed at him, he is cornered, verges on panic; Hamaguchi, who knows him, takes his overreaction as proof of an honor student at stake, but misjudges; Hirata judges his actions forced and takes him as NOT the honor student; Hamaguchi nominates him — and is right: he is an honor student.
[note] (p.96) His nomination by Hamaguchi “successfully” identifies him as an honor student — the only nomination hit of the whole discussion; it leaves Hirata with only two lives.
[scene] (p.98) After the honor student’s removal, the discussion stalls; only six participants remain and he is already gone.
[rel:hamaguchi-tetsuya] (p.96) Identified by Hamaguchi as an honor student; Hamaguchi calls the nomination “quite a reckless” gamble that paid off.
[rel:hirata-yousuke] (p.96) Hirata reads him as not an honor student — the only time the two vanguards read the same person differently.
