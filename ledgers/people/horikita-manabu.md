### y1v1 p.66–68 — Club Fair debut: absolute presence
[role:student-council] (p.66–68) Student Council President; takes the stage at the club fair.
[scene] School gymnasium club fair. Representatives from various athletic and cultural clubs deliver lively pitches. Student council president Horikita Manabu steps onto the stage.
[action] (p.66–67) Slender build, sharp analytical gaze behind glasses, neatly trimmed black hair. Merely by stepping up to the microphone, his sheer aura and commanding presence reduce over a hundred chattering first-year students to dead, breathless silence without him saying a single word or raising a hand.
[says] (p.67) “I’m the student council president. My name is Horikita Manabu.”
[says] (p.67) “The student council is looking to recruit potential candidates among the first-year students to replace the graduating third years. Although no special qualifications are required for candidacy, we humbly ask that those considering application not be involved in other club activities. We generally do not accept students involved elsewhere.”
[says] (p.67–68) “Furthermore, we in the student council do not wish to appoint anyone who possesses a naive outlook. Not only would such a person not be elected, he or she would sully the sanctity of this school. It is the student council’s right and duty to enforce and amend the rules, but the school expects more than that. We gladly welcome those of you who understand this.”
[action] (p.68) Delivers his speech in a quiet, eloquent, razor-sharp monotone without pausing once. Immediately hops off the stage and walks out of the gymnasium, leaving the room paralyzed with intimidation.
[feat] (p.66–68) Unmatched psychological dominance and institutional authority; commands immediate, involuntary deference from the entire freshman class.
[voice] (p.67–68) Austere, aristocratic, intensely disciplined, zero warmth or theatricality; speaks with absolute executive authority.
[rule] (p.67) Student Council membership condition: absolute commitment; members are prohibited from participating in any other school club.
