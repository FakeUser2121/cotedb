### y1v2 p.193–195 — Ch.6 §6.1: the secretary who runs the hearing
[scene] (p.193) The student council room on the fourth floor; she receives the parties and opens the proceedings.
[role:student-council] (p.193) Student council secretary; short-haired, and by her own account handles the bulk of proceedings "as usual."
[says] (p.193) "Of course, considering the magnitude of this dispute, there are times when the student council president will take over. There are several unusual things about this incident. Other than that, the bulk of the proceedings will be handled solely by Tachibana, as usual."
[note] (p.194) Her summary is the hearing's only neutral account: both sides' versions set out "in an easy-to-understand manner," with no need for further explanation.
[says] (p.194) "Based on the aforementioned facts, we would like for you to identify which version of events is true."
[says] (p.194) "Komiya-kun and two other members of the basketball club went to the special building after Sudou-kun called them there. There, they claim to have been beaten up in a one-sided brawl. Is this true?"
[note] (p.194–195) The question is posed to Class D as a whole; the page does not assign it to a named student.
[says] (p.195) "Please calm down, Sudou-kun. Right now, we’re merely listening to what both parties have to say. Komiya-kun, we ask that you too please show some restraint, and not interrupt."
[says] (p.195) "Both parties insist that the other called them over, so the accounts conflict. However, the stories share some commonality. There was a dispute between Sudou-kun, Komiya-kun, and Kondou-kun, correct?"
[says] (p.195) "'Picking fights'?"
[says] (p.195) "Both sides have given their grievances, but now we have to come to a judgment with the collected evidence."
[trait] (p.195) She keeps the hearing on evidence while the room escalates, and asks the question that turns Komiya's grievance into a concession of shared history.
