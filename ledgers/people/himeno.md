### y2v7 p.62–68 — Himeno: the silent dissenter; the explosion
[scene] (p.62) Summoned by Ayanokōji to the special wing; surprised to find Kanzaki there too — Kanzaki assumed Himeno would be one of the last people he'd want to see.
[note] (p.64) Himeno was silent and turned over.
[says] (p.64) “I thought it was impossible. Classes don't change easily. I'd rather not see others besides myself get hurt, and they're all just saying pretty things.” — she began to talk about what she was feeling.
[says] (p.64) “I felt that Kanzaki-kun's resistance was simply a waste of time. So I said so…That I wanted that painful time to end quickly and that I wanted it to be over for good.”
[says:ayanokouji-kiyotaka] (p.65) “Why is it that Ichinose's class dropped in the rankings and is now facing a major crisis?” — Ayanokōji's question to Kanzaki; Himeno interjects on Ichinose soon after.
[says] (p.65) “Don't make it sound so easy.”
[says] (p.65) “You don't have to answer, but you know what I mean. There's only strong peer pressure in our class. Even if I think it's white, if many say it's black, it's black. It doesn't matter if I'm right or wrong. In such a class, there's no point in a minority speaking up. It's just painful to be surrounded by people who go out of their way to say what they think is black until they're persuaded to say it's white. That's why I've never said anything and I never will.”
[says] (p.65) “But still, the color I think of in my mind will remain white because it is.” — "That's fine. I accept the black claims that others concluded on their own." — her stance on the white/black question.
[says] (p.65) “I wish you would stop expecting me to be like your friend here. I'm sorry, but I can't be as passionate as Kanzaki-kun.”
[says] (p.66) “Whether good or bad, it's more important for me to protect myself. I can't be best friends with anyone, but I can't be nasty with anyone either. Sometimes they ask me out, sometimes they don't. I don't want to destroy that level of distance and atmosphere.” — her insistence that things stay quiet.
[says] (p.66) “If Kanzaki-kun's argument gains momentum and exceeds the majority of the class, I will join your side, too. That's fine, right?”
[says] (p.66) “Damn it!” — the words conveyed her true intentions and unwillingness.
[says] (p.66) “Can I go now? I won't tell anyone about this. It will only get me into trouble if I do.”
[says] (p.67) “That's not fair. That's a threat.” — Himeno became upset; her reaction was different from her previous scoffing attitude.
[says] (p.67) “I don't want to stay.”
[says] (p.67) “But I am…” — Himeno, biting her lower lip, closes her eyes tightly.
[says] (p.67) “Even… I'd like to… win if I could.” — she had not given up the possibility of changing classes and winning in no small measure.
[says] (p.67) “I understand what you're saying… But still…” — Kanzaki's words stung her strongly.
[says] (p.68) “If I could graduate from Class A, I would want to!” — a loud, taut voice echoed through the hallway, several times louder than expected.
[says] (p.68) “But if we don't do it now, there's no way we will be able to, no matter how we think about it! It can't be done!” — Himeno shouted, exploding with emotion.
[says] (p.68) “Even Kanzaki-kun knows that!” — her shout echoed down the hallway.
[trait] (p.62–68) Quietly perceptive — she privately thinks Kanzaki's resistance was right but stayed silent due to peer pressure (white is black when the class says so); refuses to stand in a minority; Kanzaki's transfer threat — leaving the class without its only capable leader — shakes her until she finally explodes: she wants to graduate from Class A.
[rel:ayanokouji-kiyotaka] (p.62–68) He draws out her true feelings by letting Kanzaki unload first and by confronting her with Kanzaki's resolve — “Even if you don't say it, I see you.”
[rel:kanzaki-ryuuji] (p.62–68) His transfer threat hurts her; he gives her every opening to speak; her explosion is the breakthrough.
[rel:ichinose-honami] (p.62–68) She is critical of the class's "pretty things" and Ichinose's direction but stays silent — until she can't.

### y2v7 p.70–71 — The spark; "a girl she is curious about"
[scene] (p.70–71) After the meeting, Himeno and Kanzaki begin to plan real change; Ayanokōji urges them to find students like them and to keep building the circle — a small spark into a big flame.
[says] (p.71) “We have the same desire to graduate from Class A. Until now, I couldn't tell anyone, but…” — Himeno confides her goal to Kanzaki.
[says] (p.71) “Yes, yes. I guess our goals haven't changed at all since the beginning.” — Kanzaki.
[says] (p.71) “You know… After listening to Ayanokōji-kun's story, there's a girl I'm a little curious about. Would you like to go see her after this?” — Himeno; Kanzaki nodded vigorously at her suggestion.
[note] (p.71) Ayanokōji's closing read: "it looks like they may be more successful than expected. They may really beat Horikita's class in the end-of-year exam. Either way, it doesn't hurt my plans."
[trait] (p.70–71) Passionate and decisive once roused — she reveals her long-hidden wish to graduate from Class A, takes a “childlike step forward,” and recruits Kanzaki for a girl she is curious about.
