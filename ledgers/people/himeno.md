### y2v7 p.62–71 — Himeno: the silent dissenter of Ichinose's class
[scene] (p.62) Summoned by Ayanokōji to the special wing; surprised to find Kanzaki there too.
[says] (p.64) "I thought it was impossible. Classes don't change easily. I'd rather not see others besides myself get hurt."
[says] (p.65) "There's only strong peer pressure in our class. Even if I think it's white, if many say it's black, it's black."
[says] (p.65) "But still, the color I think of in my mind will remain white because it is."
[says] (p.66) "If Kanzaki-kun's argument gains momentum and exceeds the majority of the class, I will join your side, too."
[trait] (p.62–67) Quietly perceptive — admits she privately thinks Kanzaki's resistance was right but stayed silent due to peer pressure (white is black when the class says so); refuses to stand in a minority, but Kanzaki's threat to transfer classes (leaving the class without its only capable leader) shakes her — she leaves conflicted, a potential ally for change.
[rel:ayanokouji-kiyotaka] (p.62–67) He draws out her true feelings by confronting her with Kanzaki's resolve.
[rel:kanzaki-ryuuji] (p.62–67) His transfer threat upsets her — she doesn't want the class to lose a chance to improve.
[rel:ichinose-honami] (p.64–65) She's critical of the class's "pretty things" and Ichinose's direction but stays silent.
[scene] (p.68) She suddenly shouts in a voice several times louder than expected, exploding with emotion — for the first time showing her true self; Ayanokōji is convinced more than a few students in Ichinose's class only socialize with her on the surface.
[says] (p.68) "If I could graduate from Class A, I would want to!"
[says] (p.68) "But if we don't do it now, there's no way we will be able to, no matter how we think about it! It can't be done!"
[says] (p.68) "Even Kanzaki-kun knows that!"

[says] (p.71) "We have the same desire to graduate from Class A."
[scene] (p.71) She and Kanzaki take a "childlike step forward"; she suggests going to see a girl she is curious about after hearing Ayanokōji's story, and Kanzaki agrees eagerly.
[trait] (p.68–71) Passionate and decisive once roused — she reveals her long-hidden wish to graduate from Class A, flinches in fear at Kanzaki's unexpected shout, and secretly confides that her goal never changed; her shift convinces Ayanokōji she may really change her class.
