### y1v1 p.229–235 — The old-test pipeline (the intended pass route)
[fact] (p.232–233) Post-admission mock papers are **verbatim identical across years** ("Every single word is identical") — the final three problems (2nd/3rd-year difficulty) repeat exactly.
[inference] (p.234) Chabashira's earlier certainty that "there was a way for us to pass this test" = old-test access as the designed escape hatch; explains how academically weak students (Sudou/Ike/Yamauchi) were admitted.
[tactic] (p.233–235) AK buys old papers for 15,000 points, withholds them from the trio (protect motivation), and plans a day-before internet release to force an overnight class-wide cram — targeting universal pass, not perfect scores.
[back] (p.229–231) Buying past papers is not against the rules (point transfers and study aids unregulated).

### y1v1 p.246–254 — Results, the hidden pass rule & the buyback
[fact] (p.246) Results posted on blackboard: 10+ students hit perfect 100s in math, Japanese, and social studies.
[fact] (p.246–247) Sudou: 60×4 subjects, **39 English** → red-marked. The class pass line was **40, not 32**.
[rule] (p.247–248, 250) True pass-grade algorithm: per-class threshold = class average ÷ 2, **rounded** (down 32 on the previous test from 39.8/64.4-derived inputs; up to 40 this midterm from 79.6 ÷ 2 = 39.8). Red mark below threshold = expulsion (withdrawal form + legal guardian).
[fact] (p.249–250) Sabotage attempt: Horikita scored 51 in English on purpose (vs perfect in four other subjects) to drag the English average below 39.8 so Sudou would pass — defeated only by the rounding rule.
[fact] (p.251–252) The earlier scope change meant Class D tested under documented unfair conditions — acknowledged by Chabashira as "a microcosm of our unfair society"; appeal route declared closed "at this stage."
[fact] (p.253–254) Resolution: one English test point purchased for 100,000 points (AK + Horikita combined) → Sudou passes; expulsion rescinded.
[fact] (p.246, 248) Class D passes en masse otherwise — the old-test operation delivered; everyone clears except Sudou's single red line.
