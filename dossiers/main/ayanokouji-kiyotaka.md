## Arc

### y1v1

- **Situation:** Enrolled into the school's lowest class; lives as an invisible loner while Horikita's cold war with Kushida's social web begins.
- **Knowledge state:** Reads Class D's point deficit and Kushida's junior-high history with him long before either surfaces openly.
- **Changes:** Turns down Manabu Horikita's guidance-office challenge; accepts a quiet orbit around Horikita (lunch, the study group as bridge).
- **Why it matters:** Establishes the 'no trouble' mask the rest of the series punctures.
- **Evidence:** y1v1 p.99 · y1v1 p.150 · y1v1 p.162

### y1v2

- **Situation:** Sudou's assault accusation threatens the class's first island points.
- **Knowledge state:** Understands Kushida's hostility toward Horikita is a personal grudge, not class politics.

## Psychology & competing readings

- **His terminal goal is self-determination, not Class A victory** (confidence 0.8)
  - Evidence: y1v5 p.47 · y1v7 p.59 · y1v7 p.64
  - Reading: Competing: he optimizes class outcomes constantly. Plainest synthesis: Class A is the arena in which self-determination is proven, not the goal itself.
- **The trouble-avoider persona is a White Room survival reflex he inherited, not his genuine preference** (confidence 0.75)
  - Evidence: y1v5 p.15 · y1v4 p.109 · y1v1 p.150
  - Reading: Counter-reading: he repeatedly chooses engagement when stakes are personal (y1v6 p.124; y2v8 p.64), so the mask may be inherited but no longer chosen. Both readings fit the corpus; the text never settles it.
- **His relationships shift from instruments to obligations across y1 to y2** (confidence 0.7)
  - Evidence: y1v1 p.73 · y1v2 p.211 · y2v8 p.64 · y2v12 p.151
  - Reading: Counter-reading: no scene shows him acting against interest for a peer without side-benefit; the 'obligations' may be strategic consistency rather than attachment growth.
- **Sparing Ryuuen was utility, not mercy or respect** (confidence 0.65)
  - Evidence: y1v7 p.185 · y1v7 p.195
  - Reading: Competing: the 'fear experiment' framing (p.185-186) hints at personal curiosity toward Ryuuen; utility is only the stated reason.
- **The Karuizawa 'baptism' was necessity-driven instrumentation, not sadism** (confidence 0.6)
  - Evidence: y1v4 p.201 · y1v7 p.124 · y1v7 p.190
  - Reading: Contested: the method's cruelty (y1v4 p.195-199) supports a colder reading — that he reaches for White Room techniques because they are the only tools he was given. Readers split here; surface evidence supports both.
