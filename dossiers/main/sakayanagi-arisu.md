## Arc

### y0

- **Situation:** Childhood: touring an institution beside her father, the future chairman.
- **Knowledge state:** Already fluent in adult doctrine — she argues policy in a child's voice.
- **Changes:** 'Hundreds of children are abandoned every year… we should welcome' political action; 'this is also a form of education' — selection as a moral good, stated before she can vote.
- **Why it matters:** The root text of everything she later becomes at school.
- **Evidence:** y0 p.38 · y0 p.87 · y0 p.145

### y1v5

- **Situation:** First direct exchange with Ayanokouji in their shared history.
- **Knowledge state:** She already knows the word 'White Room'.
- **Changes:** Names it aloud where no one else would — establishing that their game began before the school year.
- **Why it matters:** Opens the only rivalry in the series built on prior knowledge of each other's origins.
- **Evidence:** y1v5 p.202 · y1v5 p.203

### y1v7

- **Situation:** Ayanokouji Sr. attends the party; the school's admissions machinery is discussed openly.
- **Knowledge state:** Handles the White Room architect with policy deflection, and knows Class D's island strategy through Masumi and Hashimoto.
- **Changes:** Meets Sr.'s pressure ('You intend to make him withdraw') with school doctrine — 'we only admit students we believe deserve placement' — while teasing Ryuuen aside.
- **Why it matters:** She treats the series' darkest adult as a counterparty to be managed, not feared.
- **Evidence:** y1v7 p.66 · y1v7 p.69 · y1v7 p.115

### y1v7.5

- **Situation:** Chess-phase probing of Ayanokouji, plus target-selection of Ichinose.
- **Knowledge state:** Runs a cold reading of Ichinose's hidden side and diagnoses Masumi's secret as the same shape as Kamuro's.
- **Changes:** 'You think the White Room made you remarkable, don't you?' — she argues with him as an equal on his own origin; 'I like messing around with Class B to stave off boredom.'
- **Why it matters:** Peak of the observer-era: everything is analysis and play.
- **Evidence:** y1v7.5 p.39 · y1v7.5 p.42 · y1v7.5 p.37

### y1v9

- **Situation:** Kamuro Masumi's secret — shoplifting — sits in Sakayanagi's keeping.
- **Knowledge state:** Holds the blackmail and the rumour war's levers at once.
- **Changes:** 'You were shoplifting, were you not?' — the quietest display of power in the year: one line, total control.
- **Why it matters:** Defines her method: secrets acquired, then spent at leisure.
- **Evidence:** y1v9 p.91 · y1v9 p.171

### y1v11

- **Situation:** The first real face-off with Ayanokouji arrives.
- **Knowledge state:** She has wanted exactly this.
- **Changes:** 'I was simply looking forward to facing off against you. And now, that wish has come true. I'm satisfied.'
- **Why it matters:** Rivalry declared as satisfaction, not grievance.
- **Evidence:** y1v11 p.433

### y1v11.5

- **Situation:** Post-exam: she reconstructs the manipulated instruction machine.
- **Knowledge state:** Works out that Horikita's read input differed from Ayanokouji's real instruction — and that the school's interference cut the other way.
- **Changes:** 'If she had followed Ayanokouji-kun's real instructions, I would have lost instead'; 'I won't allow the school to repeatedly interfere with the outcome of our battles.'
- **Why it matters:** First time she is beaten by a channel she didn't control — the grievance that fuels y2's wager.
- **Evidence:** y1v11.5 p.68 · y1v11.5 p.83

### y2v12

- **Situation:** The duel vs Ryuuen: discussion-game mastery on the field.
- **Knowledge state:** She reads him to checkmate — and receives Ayanokouji's message through Hashimoto mid-endgame.
- **Changes:** Beats Ryuuen outright (he admits 'checkmate'), then chooses the other path: 'Sakayanagi Arisu is not desired by Ayanokouji Kiyotaka from now on' — she foregoes the victory she earned.
- **Why it matters:** The pivot of her entire series: winning the battle and conceding the war in the same hour.
- **Evidence:** y2v12 p.199 · y2v12 p.216 · y2v12 p.217

## Psychology & competing readings

- **Her weapon is held secrets: she cold-reads everyone's hidden shame and waits** (confidence 0.85)
  - Evidence: y1v9 p.91 · y1v7.5 p.42 · y1v7.5 p.114
  - Reading: Kamuro's shoplifting, Ichinose's opened heart, Masumi's identical problem — one method, three targets. Competing: she is not all-seeing — Hashimoto's betrayal and Ryuuen's traitor rights both surprised her (y2v12 p.197).
- **Her withdrawal was a chosen move, not a suffered defeat — she accepted the loss to honor Ayanokouji's wish** (confidence 0.8)
  - Evidence: y2v12.5 p.34 · y2v12.5 p.36 · y2v12.5 p.35
  - Reading: Two readings live side by side: Ryuuen's — 'you self-destructed because of that message' — and hers ('my strength was insufficient… I accepted defeat knowing the content'). Her own 'setting the groundwork' (p.97) hints at further motives. The corpus supports chosen-move as primary with strategic aftertaste.
- **She treats rivalry as courtship — Ayanokouji is the opponent she wants to be seen by** (confidence 0.8)
  - Evidence: y1v11 p.433 · y2v9.5 p.110 · y2v12.5 p.97
  - Reading: Face-off satisfaction, formal enemy-declaration, and the reunion wish form one line. The counter-evidence is her own pain when he doesn't want her as opponent (y2v12 p.216 'even hated this thought process') — the courtship reads real precisely because it can wound.
- **Competence expressed as play — she handicaps, torments, and teases rather than simply crushing** (confidence 0.75)
  - Evidence: y1v7.5 p.37 · y1v7 p.115 · y2v12 p.199
  - Reading: Boredom-motivated play is her stated mode; even at checkmate she feels 'parental emotion' toward Ryuuen. Competing: the play is a luxury that collapses the moment the stakes stop amusing her (y2v11's flat wager line).
- **She is her father's doctrine made flesh — elite selection as moral good, inherited not rebelled against** (confidence 0.6)
  - Evidence: y0 p.38 · y0 p.87 · y1v7 p.69
  - Reading: Childhood and adulthood repeat the same voice (facility tour → admissions doctrine at the party). Competing reading: her final wishes — protecting the betrayer, promising reunion — are personal, not doctrinal, suggesting the inheritance is manners more than belief; corpus cannot fully settle it.
