## Rules

Announced same-day for the next day: 'a series of multiple-choice votes which you will have to repeat as a class until you reach a unanimous decision' (y2v5 p.31), with the full overview at y2v5 p.35-41. Decision topics include whether to expel a member (any single no vetoes) and the school-trip destination ballot.

## Points

Stakes are large: failure modes put 350 class points on the line in the protection vote (y2v5 p.187), and defeated classes in the exam family forfeit protection points (y1v11 p.318).

## Conditions

Unanimity is the hard gate — the class cannot expel without every yes, and the trip ballot runs in rounds (Okinawa 19 / Hokkaido 17 / Kyoto 3 first round, y2v5 p.91).

## Organizer

School-run special exam; classes stage the votes internally, the school tallies them.
